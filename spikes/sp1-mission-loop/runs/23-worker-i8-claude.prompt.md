# First Offer — Price Point & Structure

## PRIMARY: "First-Call Guarantee" for small PI firms

**Claim:** You will never lose another case to a slow phone or an unanswered web form. If you do, you don't pay that month.

### Three steps, each signable by the owner alone

**1. Free diagnostic (day 0-2)** — Submit their own web form and call their own line after hours. Deliver a one-page timestamped record: time-to-first-response, calls unanswered, forms never replied to. Self-verifying evidence, not a pitch deck. (27% of firms never respond to online leads at all.)

**2. 21-day paid pilot — $500 fixed, credited to month 1** — Live 24/7 capture on one number + one form, <5-minute callback SLA, qualified-lead summaries into inbox or case management. Single invoice, card payment, no contract beyond the pilot.

**3. Month-to-month retainer — $1,000/mo**, cancel anytime, **plus $75 per qualified lead above 15/mo** against a written qualification rubric the firm controls.

### Why these numbers
- $1,000/mo sits inside the $300–$2,500 human-intake retainer band and replaces a service also billing $1.50–$4.00/min → reads as substitution, not a new line item.
- At $284/lead, break-even is 3.5 recovered leads/month; at $468/signed case, ~2 signings.
- Low fixed retainer + per-qualified-lead is the structure buyer-side guidance itself calls cleanest — you propose what an informed buyer would ask for.
- $500 first commitment stays under the sub-$1,000 fast-close threshold and under any plausible owner signature limit.

### What NOT to sell
The build. Fixed-price "build me an AI receptionist" jobs transact publicly at $500; gigs start at $35. Never quote a build fee.

### 30-day path
Days 1-10: run diagnostics on 30 FJA-directory firms. Days 8-20: send each firm its own timestamped failure record + pilot offer. Days 15-30: close 1-2 pilots at $500. Target: first dollar by day 30, first retainer by day 45.
