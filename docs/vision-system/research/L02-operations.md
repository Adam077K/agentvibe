# L02 — Company operations across the business lifecycle

Research date: 2026-09-12. Independent lane; repository inputs were `AGENTS.md` and `docs/vision-system/inputs/THE-VISION-AND-THE-FIELDS.md`. No other lane reports or prior architecture were consulted.

**Finding — inference:** the useful unit of company operations is a commitment with an owner, evidence, resources, counterparties, and an eventual disposition. Departments are one possible way to organize people around these commitments; their names do not establish which work requires an agent. A complete system must connect deciding, learning, producing, selling, fulfilling, maintaining, and closing while preserving what was actually promised.

**Labels:** **C** identifies a source-supported claim, including a vendor's description of its product. **I** identifies an inference. **P** identifies a proposal for later evaluation. None selects a final architecture. Product capabilities below were checked in documentation, not tested in deployed accounts. Source dates and institutional incentives appear in the register.

## Organization and decision rights

**C:** Galbraith's organization-design framework covers strategy, structure, information processes, rewards, and people; it explicitly rejects a universally appropriate organizational form. Bain's RAPID distinguishes recommending, contributing input, agreeing, deciding, and performing. These are different contributions to a decision, not necessarily different jobs. [Galbraith][S1]; [Bain][S2].

**I:** preserving founder intent requires more than naming a responsible owner. A sales commitment can satisfy a revenue target while violating intended customer treatment. Likewise, giving every decision to the founder preserves formal authority while making informed authority impractical.

**P:** for consequential decisions, preserve the original request, intended beneficiary, exclusions, decision owner, delegated bounds, alternatives, supporting and conflicting evidence, approved commitment, and conditions for reconsideration. Separate permission to recommend from permission to bind the business. For routine decisions, retain compact rules and exceptions. Have recipients explain the intended outcome before expensive execution; record later corrections alongside the original decision.

**C:** EOS combines vision, people, data, issues, process, and traction, with scorecards, documented processes, quarterly priorities, and weekly meetings. Scaling Up centers people, strategy, execution, and cash, supported by planning tools. These commercial management systems organize management practices. [EOS][S3]; [Scaling Up][S4].

**I/P:** borrow explicit priorities, issue ownership, and a review cadence; test their attention cost. Neither vendor page establishes that its method maintains truthful evidence or works for one owner supervising automated production. Reject importing whole meeting schedules, leadership seats, or branded success claims without a demonstrated need.

## Discovery, design, and the first real transaction

**C:** Camuffo and colleagues' 2024 replication covered 759 firms across four randomized trials. Training in scientific entrepreneurial decision-making increased idea termination; pivot results were consistent with a nonlinear effect, with treated firms making limited rather than absent or repeated radical changes. This is evidence about trained entrepreneurs, not proof of autonomous entrepreneurship or guaranteed commercial returns. [Study abstract][S5].

**P:** discovery should produce a falsifiable business hypothesis: whose problem, current alternative, proposed benefit, price, acquisition route, delivery cost, and what observation would change the decision. Preserve interview context and recruitment method. Distinguish stated interest, observed behavior, paid commitment, repeated use, and renewal. An agent can compare sources and draft tests; recruiting appropriate participants and interpreting ambiguous human behavior may need an experienced researcher and direct owner contact. Synthetic customers cannot supply evidence of actual demand.

**C:** the UK Service Standard recommends research into users' wider circumstances, disposable prototypes, and analytics. Mailchimp's working style guide separates relatively stable brand voice from tone adapted to the reader's situation. [Service Standard][S6]; [Mailchimp][S7].

**P:** product, service design, content, and brand should share a promise and evidence base. Evaluate the complete customer journey, including purchasing, onboarding, failure, support, and cancellation. Use prototypes and actual customer observation for usability; use human taste for identity and positioning; use deterministic checks for accessibility properties and asset requirements where checks are reliable. Agents can create alternatives and locate inconsistencies. A visually coherent campaign is not evidence that the offer is useful or its claims justified.

**P:** before taking money, assemble a readiness record covering the selling entity or person, authorized signatory, payment and refund arrangements, delivery capacity, ownership of assets, and applicable obligations. Local professionals establish relevant legal, tax, and employment requirements; software can track their determinations, deadlines, and completion evidence. The vision's sector labels should not become universal licensing rules. No jurisdiction or business form has been specified here, so this report supplies operational questions rather than legal instructions.

## Distribution, content, sales, and support

**C:** HubSpot's CRM represents contacts and companies using lifecycle stages, records stage changes, and supports automated updates. Its default automatic progression moves forward; returning to an earlier stage through several tools requires clearing the existing value. [HubSpot documentation][S8].

**I/P:** a CRM stage is a process label, not proof of intent or payment. Preserve acquisition source, consent where applicable, original conversation, qualification evidence, promised terms, decision process, next action, and lost-deal reasons. Keep content production, publication authority, audience response, qualified conversations, paid conversions, and retention distinguishable. Compare channels using actual contribution and owner effort, with attribution uncertainty visible. A referral and a cold approach can require different relationship handling even if both enter the same pipeline.

**C:** Zendesk distinguishes solved tickets from closed tickets; customers can generate follow-up requests linked to closed tickets. [Ticket lifecycle][S9]. **P:** support should connect the customer's actual problem to the promised remedy, escalation owner, next update, and acceptance evidence. Track recurrence and unresolved harm alongside response speed. Agents can retrieve known answers and draft responses; workflows can route and remind; people handle sensitive relationships, ambiguous remedies, and promises beyond standing authority. Repeated complaints should reopen product and sales assumptions.

**C — observed failure:** in its 2016 Wells Fargo action, the CFPB reported unauthorized accounts created under sales targets and compensation incentives. This is a documented organizational failure, not evidence about agents. [CFPB][S10]. **I:** an accurate count of activity can coexist with false business progress. **P:** counterbalance growth measures with consent, cancellations, refunds, complaints, and sampled customer accounts of what happened.

## Money, bookkeeping, and procurement

**C:** Odoo documents purchase-order billing based on ordered or received quantities, three-way matching, and exception flags. Its documentation also permits edits that create discrepancies and manual changes to the payment-status field. Its bank reconciliation supports matching transactions against invoices, bills, and payments using fixed rules and configurable models. [Purchase controls][S11]; [Reconciliation][S12].

**I:** accounting software provides useful transaction structure; a displayed status is not sufficient evidence that a payment restriction is enforced. A recorded payment and independently observed settlement are different facts.

**P:** use established accounting and payment software for arithmetic, transaction identities, ledgers, reconciliation, and scheduled obligations. Keep invoices, receipts, bank evidence, corrections, and uncertain classifications connected. Professionals determine accounting treatment and review material exceptions. Agents may extract invoice fields or explain variances; they should not manufacture missing receipts or settle uncertainty by inventing a category.

**P:** procurement needs evidence of need, alternatives, authorized price and duration, supplier identity, receipt or acceptance, renewal date, and exit terms. Verify changed payment details through an independent channel. Budget purchases using committed and recurring costs as well as immediate cash. Portfolio economics should include acquisition, refunds, fulfillment, support, maintenance, shutdown, and owner time; low production cost alone cannot establish viability.

## Work management, people, and partnerships

**C:** Asana supports approval outcomes, dependencies, automated routing, and contextual feedback. Its documentation says someone other than the assignee can complete an approval unless project permissions restrict this; downgrading can turn different approval outcomes into ordinary completed tasks. [Asana approvals][S13].

**I/P:** work-management software can coordinate work without being the authoritative source for financial settlement, customer consent, or binding authorization. Test the actual permissions and export semantics. Completion should identify the accepted deliverable and evidence, with unfinished obligations retained. Reject equating a green task, an approval icon, or an agent's success message with business completion.

**C:** GitLab's onboarding procedure assigns a human buddy through the manager and provides for backup coverage. Its channel-partner process registers individual opportunities, applies qualification rules, and expires registrations. [Onboarding][S14]; [Partner operations][S15].

**P:** hiring or engaging contractors requires an outcome definition, competence assessment, availability, compensation terms, access, onboarding, feedback, and departure arrangements. Scheduling and access changes suit workflows; assessment, negotiation, coaching, conflict, and sensitive employment decisions need accountable people and relevant professional expertise. Partnership records should include mutual obligations, relationship owner, opportunity attribution, conflicts, renewal, and termination. Software can remember an agreement; maintaining trust requires the parties' participation. Do not turn every human responsibility into a permanent agent persona.

## Operation, incidents, growth, pause, and closure

**C:** Google's incident-management practice separates command, operational changes, communication, and planning, and maintains a live incident record and explicit handoffs. It warns against coordinating recovery through the very system that has failed. [Google SRE][S16].

**P:** adapt these responsibilities to missed deliveries, billing mistakes, supplier failures, misleading campaigns, and service outages. Name an incident owner, affected parties, containment action, known facts, unknowns, communication schedule, and recovery criteria. Deterministic controls can halt relevant actions; agents can organize evidence; an authorized person owns consequential remedies. One person may cover several responsibilities, but their available capacity remains a constraint. Rehearse absence and recovery, including access to outside specialists.

**P:** growth should follow evidence that delivery, support, cash, and attention can withstand additional demand. Track maintenance work and aging obligations separately from experiments. At portfolio level, compare each effort against its own stage: discovery learning, early customer value, repeatable economics, or sustainable maintenance. Limit concurrent experiments by potential obligations and decision load. Cheap generation does not make a hundred customer promises cheap.

**C:** the SBA's US guidance treats closure as a sequence involving agreement, dissolution where applicable, financial and employment obligations, and records. It explicitly directs owners toward qualified advisers. These details are jurisdiction-specific. [SBA closure guidance][S17].

**P:** distinguish paused discovery, maintenance-only service, product retirement, and entity closure. Pausing needs an owner, restart conditions, retained obligations, and carrying costs. Winding down needs a funded plan for customers, suppliers, staff, refunds, access removal, data handling, records, and remaining liabilities. Archive the original hypothesis, evidence, expenditure, decision, and unresolved uncertainties. Require proof of canceled renewals and completed obligations; deleting a project board is not closure. Sale or transfer additionally needs a successor who accepts the commitments.

**P — concrete failure exercises, not reported incidents:** rehearse a payment retry after an uncertain bank response; an interested prospect mistakenly counted as revenue; a paused project whose supplier renewal remains active; an edited asset published under an earlier approval; and a customer complaint suppressed by a favorable aggregate score. Recovery should reveal duplicate-action risk, distinguish observation from expectation, preserve remaining obligations, bind authorization to the actual action, and expose the contrary evidence. A month of owner absence should reveal which existing promises fail even when new work stops. These exercises test business consequences across tools, beyond whether individual tasks complete.

## Competing interpretations, assumptions, and unanswered questions

**I:** three interpretations remain plausible. A management-first approach emphasizes priorities and owner judgment but may leave transactions fragmented. An ERP/CRM-first approach supplies established records but can lock reasoning into vendor categories. An experimentation-first approach maximizes learning but can underfund ongoing obligations. These can coexist; the evidence does not establish their correct balance.

**P:** choose implementation mode per action. Use deterministic software when a rule and authoritative inputs suffice; durable workflows for waiting, routing, retries, and deadlines; agents for bounded synthesis and generation; people for intent, taste, relationships, and accountable tradeoffs; professionals for determinations requiring specific expertise or authority. Physical delivery still requires physical capability. Test these assignments against real work before generalizing.

**P:** preserve owner competence through sampled direct customer contact and reconstruction exercises: explain a recent price decision, trace a cash discrepancy, challenge rejected research, or handle an unfamiliar complaint. Measure explanation quality and changed judgments, not meeting attendance. Reserve attention for this work and decline new commitments when that reserve disappears. The effective frequency remains unknown.

**Assumptions:** a founder can set direction and access underlying records; counterparties sometimes require people; specialists can be engaged. None is established for a particular venture. No source here proves that a single owner can responsibly oversee unlimited scale.

**Missing questions:** Which business and jurisdiction starts first? Which promises and existing obligations already exist? How many independent entities and partners are involved? What owner absence must be survivable? What counts as adequate market evidence? Which spending and outward actions have standing authorization? Who can overturn the founder's factual belief? What maintenance and shutdown reserve is affordable? Which system's record prevails in a disagreement? Can a successor reconstruct the business without its original operator?
