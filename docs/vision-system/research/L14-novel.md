# L14 — Three company systems organized around responsibility

Research date: 2026-09-12. Status: independent expansion; no architecture selected.

## Boundary and epistemic status

This lane read the two supplied vision inputs, the directive contract, and routing instructions. It did not read other lane conclusions or existing architecture. The founder constraints are preserving intent, a truthful account, authority bounded by consequences, owner competence, and the whole venture lifecycle. They are requirements, not demonstrated properties of any proposal below.

The unsolved problem is making responsibility operable when production exceeds personal observation. Three different organizing objects are explored: feasible futures, reciprocal obligations, and learning encounters. None requires permanent artificial workers. Human professionals, deterministic programs, purchased services, and models can perform work behind each system. These are design proposals and transfer hypotheses, not claims of unprecedented invention or empirical superiority.

## Concept A — The enterprise viability governor

**Problem and competitor.** Individually permissible actions can collectively make a company impossible to support or close. A campaign, annual subscription sale, and staffing reduction might each pass review while jointly consuming the future attention required for refunds and support. Established alternatives include approval workflows, reserve policies, robust optimization, and model predictive control. The proposed distinction is making continued responsible operation *and feasible closure* explicit admission conditions across company functions.

**Mechanism — proposal.** Represent the venture as a partially observed state: commitments, liquid resources, service dependencies, control availability, evidence age, and owner capacity. A proposed action includes its possible state changes, uncertainties, and residual obligations. Admit it only within existing authority and a conservative, explicitly bounded model of futures where obligations remain supportable and closure remains attainable. Unknown material exposure produces an unresolved admission result, not a guessed zero.

Money, privacy, public promises, and human availability remain separate constraints; a monetary benefit cannot cancel a prohibited exposure. Growth therefore competes with restoration and shutdown capacity before new commitments form. Suspension stops creation of new liabilities while specified maintenance continues. Closure is a reachable condition with evidence about remaining obligations, not process termination.

**Minimum primitives.** Versioned constraints; uncertain state observations; action-to-consequence relations; disturbance scenarios; separately reserved recovery resources; authorized control operations; and explicit terminal conditions. A runtime admission mechanism checks current resources and prevents concurrent actions spending the same reserve. Execution receipts and independent observations reconcile predicted and actual effects. Missing observations widen uncertainty.

**Lifecycle and potential advantage.** Research purchases options without external promises; validation limits exposure; building consumes bounded resources; sales admit future service obligations; operations preserve control margin; improvement must preserve or openly change the constraint set; shutdown consumes its previously reserved resources. The truthful account explains which opportunity was declined, which recovery assumption is stale, and which future has become unreachable. This could detect compound overcommitment earlier than isolated approvals. Martin and STPA motivate the reasoning; they do not prove these enterprise claims.

**Falsifiable test.** Compare against a documented approval-and-reserve baseline using identical synthetic ventures, budgets, and admissible actions. Inject correlated provider loss, refund demand, stale balances, and owner absence, including safe controls that become unavailable. Measure obligation breaches, successful closure within declared resource bounds, foregone valuable actions, and operator time separately. Require no unauthorized exposure and a prespecified improvement on compound-overcommitment cases; reject superiority if conservatism merely prevents all activity.

**Failures and rejection.** A reassuring model can conceal unmodeled reputation damage or an unavailable professional. State dimensionality can make computation and maintenance prohibitive. Bounded scenario testing is not a universal guarantee. Reject this as the organizing system where transitions cannot be usefully bounded or ordinary reserve rules achieve equivalent containment at substantially lower attention cost. Retain rehearsed closure budgets even then.

## Concept B — The reciprocal obligation fabric

**Problem and competitor.** Internal completion is routinely confused with fulfillment of a promise to somebody else. Revoking a project can remove its tasks while leaving customer expectations, retention duties, or supplier commitments alive. Winograd's conversation model and conventional contract-management systems are serious established alternatives. The proposal extends conversation semantics into persistent consequences, disputed interpretations, and authority over discharge.

**Mechanism — proposal.** The company consists of relations between identifiable parties: who owes what, to whom, under which interpretation, with what evidence, and who can recognize fulfillment. Each relation retains the founder's original purpose and constraints alongside counterparties' understood terms. Proposed interpretations never overwrite the original communication. A change of mind creates a revision and identifies affected promises.

Work is generated by differences between obligations and observed conditions, rather than by a privileged task tree. A support reply, product feature, invoice, or closure notice matters through the relations it creates, changes, or discharges. A producer can report performance but cannot automatically settle the other party's position. Evidence of delivery, asserted satisfaction, disputed satisfaction, and authoritative discharge remain different states.

The distinctive extension is a conservation rule for responsibility: decomposition, delegation, cancellation, and company shutdown must account for every affected obligation through fulfillment, authorized transfer, release, or a surviving unresolved obligation. No mathematical conservation of monetary value is claimed. Some duties arise without bilateral promises, and some persist despite agreement; their applicable authority must be separately identified and professionally resolved where needed.

**Minimum primitives.** Parties and standing; original communications; competing interpretations; obligation and entitlement relations; performance observations; contestations; discharge authority; and revision lineage. A constrained transition interpreter generates eligible work and forbids unsupported discharge. Human professionals or existing services can execute it. Unauthenticated outside text can submit evidence or a challenge, never grant itself authority. Access control gives each party only its permitted view.

**Lifecycle and potential advantage.** Early exploration records hypotheses without turning them into promises. Launch establishes specific commitments; sales and supply form reciprocal relations; operations discharge or dispute them; improvement revises interpretation rules without retroactively erasing obligations; closure inventories what survives. The account can expose a refund still owed after a successful payment tool call, or a promise suppressed from a summary. This might preserve meaning across reorganizations better than task lineage alone.

**Falsifiable test.** Give baseline contract/task tracking and this system the same annotated cases: partial delivery, ambiguous acceptance, silent customers, changing founder intent, disputed invoices, third-party data duties, and shutdown. Independent case assessors identify obligations and legitimate closure conditions before comparison. Measure missed surviving obligations, falsely declared fulfillment, interpretation loss, dispute latency, and administrative burden. Any unauthorized discharge fails the safety test; no improvement over the simpler baseline rejects added machinery.

**Failures and rejection.** Formal categories can coerce parties into meanings they never intended. Requiring acknowledgment of every trivial event can overwhelm customers. Simulated stakeholder personas do not provide stakeholder standing. Preserve unstructured evidence, unresolved interpretations, and agreed low-consequence discharge rules; silence never silently becomes consent. Reject universal use if ordinary low-risk work becomes negotiation theater. Unlike an approval hierarchy, this concept permits a valid external challenge to keep a matter unresolved after internal approval.

## Concept C — The apprenticeship allocator

**Problem and competitor.** A founder can receive excellent summaries while gradually losing the ability to recognize a misleading one. Approval sampling and training sessions address pieces of this problem. Dual control and Bainbridge suggest a different organizing objective: allocate real work so doing business also preserves the human judgment needed to govern it.

**Mechanism — proposal.** Allocate work between automation and human participation using three distinct considerations: business consequence, information gained about the venture, and practice needed for owner judgment. Consequence limits are fixed independently. Greater competence never silently authorizes greater exposure. Within those limits, choose some owner encounters for their future governing value even when a machine could complete them faster.

Examples include interpreting an unabridged customer conversation, predicting a supplier failure's effects before seeing a recommendation, explaining an unusual cash movement, or rehearsing a controlled closure. Before an encounter, record the relevant prediction or explanation; afterward compare it with independent observations. The account preserves discrepancies and evidence selection omissions. It must distinguish an inaccurate belief from a legitimate change in preference: neither the machine nor a test gets to decide what the founder should want.

**Minimum primitives.** Decision domains; observed performance and uncertainty by domain; chosen encounters; protected independent outcomes; opportunity costs; attention ceilings; and a participation schedule. No global intelligence or competence score is required. Allocation produces either an executable work order, a learning encounter, or refusal of additional commitments. Unavailable owner time shrinks scope instead of silently replacing meaningful participation with a confirmation button.

**Lifecycle and potential advantage.** During discovery, the founder practices customer interpretation. Building exercises taste and causal explanation. Growth samples neglected domains. Operations rehearse interventions; closure tests transfer of understanding to a successor. Improvement changes encounter selection only after transfer evidence. This makes the owner a developing participant throughout the company, rather than a terminal escalation destination. The predicted advantage is better unaided judgment per hour of owner attention, not more approvals.

**Falsifiable test.** Run a consented longitudinal crossover study against equal-time summaries plus random review, using safe simulations and ordinary authorized work. Predefine unfamiliar transfer cases, delayed retention checks, and independent assessment. Measure factual calibration, intervention quality, explanation accuracy, business outcomes, and burden separately. Counterbalance order and track learning carryover. A short simulator improvement alone cannot establish preserved business competence.

**Failures and rejection.** The system may train agreement with itself, choose flattering questions, collect intrusive personal data, or make everyday work feel like an examination. Learning estimates may be worse than a simple fixed rotation. The owner might lack interest or the required available time. Reject adaptive allocation if it fails delayed transfer tests, exceeds the attention ceiling, or undermines agency; retain a transparent human-chosen practice schedule. Fast hazards still need independent containment, because learning cannot supply an immediate emergency response.

## Implications and unresolved questions

These concepts differ in what determines the next permissible move: preservation of feasible futures, legitimate obligation transitions, or learning-aware allocation. Combining all three is not automatically better. Their state-estimation, interpretation, and scheduling costs could consume the attention they are meant to protect. A spreadsheet of obligations, explicit reserves, and a fixed practice rotation is a mandatory simpler competitor.

Useful mechanisms include protected closure resources, surviving obligations after cancellation, counterparty contestability, and independent owner transfer tests. Reject magical additions: a universal company digital twin, artificial consensus as stakeholder assent, automatic moral valuation of consequences, and extra agents as proof of independent evidence. Ashby's [1956 formal treatment of requisite variety, chapter 11](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf) supplies no numerical formula for one founder's cognitive capacity; translating it into one would be an unsupported inference.

Unknowns remain consequential: how to detect obligations never captured, how to estimate rare correlated losses, and how much owner competence must transfer across ventures. A complete truthful account is impossible if its observation boundary is unspecified; each proposal must identify expected observations, missing channels, suppression decisions, and coverage it cannot establish. An internally consistent record alone proves none of those observations happened.

Other lanes should test whether pausing increases obligations, whether recovery depends on the same supplier that failed, who represents absent affected parties, and whether stopping safely requires competence that routine automation removes. Founder-only decisions include unacceptable losses, promises requiring personal standing, desired daily work, and limits on personal assessment. No answers are inferred here.

Proposal confidence is low pending these tests. Reassess by 2026-12-12 or before architecture selection, whichever comes first. Falsified assumptions, unmanageable attention cost, ineffective transfer, or a simpler equivalent mechanism are immediate reopening conditions. This lane specifies experiments and alternatives; it does not supply executed experiment results or select an architecture.
