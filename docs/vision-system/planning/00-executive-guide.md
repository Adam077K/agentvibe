# What the system is and how to read this package

**Current status:** S1.0 is the selected logical architecture. Complete contract consolidation and independent review remain active. This guide explains the intended system; it does not declare planning accepted, implementation started, provider accounts admitted or the system operational. Read [durable state](../state.json) for the exact continuation point.

## The intended transformation

The founder directs a portfolio of businesses and complex projects through explicit purpose, constraints, commitments and accountable work. The system helps investigate opportunities, create products, conduct permitted marketing and sales, deliver actual service, support customers, manage money and institutional duties, learn, change direction and close responsibly. It carries work the founder cannot personally perform or observe while retaining the evidence and unresolved consequences needed to understand and stand behind it.

The unit of organization is a responsibility with an intended result, relevant authority, resources, evidence and surviving duties. A model is one possible performer. Deterministic software handles checks, arithmetic, state and scheduling; bounded model work helps investigate and produce; actual humans and professionals perform work requiring their competence, presence or legitimate authority. A generated report about delivery is never delivery.

Founder decisions, inferred preferences, system proposals and unknowns have distinct meanings. Original intent, changes of mind, rejected options and omissions remain inspectable. Accounts include attempted, failed, skipped, stale and unverified work. The system preserves uncertainty about why something happened instead of converting a plausible rationale into established cause.

## How ordinary work proceeds

| Operator need | Intended behavior and evidence |
|---|---|
| Explore an uncertain opportunity | Bound a question and resources; inspect customers and sources; preserve rival explanations and negative evidence. Authorized research can occur before a product or offer exists. |
| Create and offer something | Produce and assess actual artifacts; establish capacity for the precise promise; assemble current acceptance evidence; release only permitted exposure. A limited pilot does not claim scale demand. |
| Sell and fulfill | Distinguish agreement, payment, actual delivery and acceptance. Customer support, recurring service, refunds and records continue after an internal workflow ends. |
| Understand unobserved work | Reconstruct an account from authoritative records, context manifests, original evidence, judgments and omissions. The interface exposes what remains unknown or disputed. |
| Remain a capable owner | Reserve authentic customer, taste, finance and intervention practice within consented attention limits. Test unfamiliar takeover; a declined practice is not a diagnosis of incapacity. |
| Pause, recover or close | Stop new permitted effects, reconcile uncertain ones and continue due duties. Verify actual successor access, capacity and performance. Cancellation and exported packets do not discharge obligations. |
| Improve the system | Propose an evidenced change, assess risks and independent tests, stage its rollout and preserve a current rollback path. Ordinary producers cannot rewrite their own authority or acceptance history. |

These paths are specified outcomes. Runtime conformance, sustainable attention, real customer value and reliable human continuity remain to be demonstrated.

## Architecture and why it was selected

The selected design combines common work and obligation control with bounded native production and protected authority, evidence and recovery mechanisms. Nine logical components have separate responsibilities; this does not require nine agents, people, services or computers.

| Component | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| C01 Direction and responsibility | Preserve purpose, choices, commitments, constraints and legitimate ownership |
| C02 Cases and scheduling | Manage work, dependencies, waiting, resources, interruption and continuation |
| C03 Production | Perform bounded work through appropriate deterministic, model, human or external routes |
| C04 Authority and release | Check current consequence limits and exact intended effects before permitted execution |
| C05 Context and validity | Supply authorized, current context with provenance, contradictions and omissions |
| C06 Evidence and acceptance | Distinguish attempts and observations from supported acceptance; preserve independent criteria and dependency invalidation |
| C07 Human and external responsibility | Establish actual mandates, professional and service capacity, remedies, owner practice and continuity |
| C08 Recovery and continuity | Preserve independently recoverable acknowledged state, reconcile uncertain effects and verify restored control |
| C09 Operator surfaces | Project authoritative state into decisions, work, evidence, capacity, people, incidents and terminal interaction |

The [selection record](02-architecture-selection.md) explains the evidence, trade-offs and reopening conditions. The [component contracts](specification/10-components-authority-traceability.md) define owners, interfaces, state, boundaries, failures, evaluation, versioning and replacement.

The serious alternatives were a federated responsibility system and adaptive coordination, including a stronger candidate with compulsory inquiry rights. Their distinct control and information structures were independently attacked. S1 retains useful field responsibility, continuity, bounded discovery and context mechanisms without adopting a permanent departmental agent hierarchy. A smaller manual/native arrangement remains an eligible comparator. The selected design must lose if the simpler arrangement achieves the same duties and outcomes with less owner burden or if the added machinery cannot support useful ordinary work.

## Authority, money and actual operation

Autonomy follows possible consequence, reversibility, exposure, customer impact and repairability. Successful history can adjust review depth within an admitted boundary; it cannot silently raise the maximum consequence. Current authority is checked at use, and untrusted source material cannot become instruction or grant. Potential external effects keep their holds and reconciliation duties when acknowledgement is lost.

The internal model primarily uses authorized native Claude Code and Codex subscription environments. Provider identity, actual allowance, reset windows, scarce capacity and billing mode are distinct from money. Unknown internal provider usage stays unknown; observable job, process, time, output and network bounds remain enforceable construction targets. There is no silent switch to metered APIs or extra credits.

The [economics worksheet and profiles](specification/07-integrations-capacity.md#7-deployment-economics-and-comparators) include the reference AWS arrangement, a VPS alternative, a smaller comparator, storage, restore work, communications and professional/administrator/owner labor. These are dated estimates and explicit unknowns, not affordability findings or permission to purchase. Actual accounts, funding, jurisdiction-specific standing, service engagements and independent custodians are operating prerequisites. They do not require the founder to design the architecture or approve routine local construction.
