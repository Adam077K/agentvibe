## F7-distributed-01: SendClaim not bound to EffectIdentity allocated→claimed

- `contracts/command-registry.json#authority.attempt.claim`: `target_types` now includes
  `EffectIdentity`. `transaction_contract.cas` now states that P4 owns the effect-winner decision.
  In one GroupBody it reads the EffectIdentity by its natural key `(effect_class, counterparty,
  payload_digest)` at its exact head, then does one of two things:
  - it CASes the identity `allocated → claimed`, naming this attempt as the first claimant;
  - if the identity is already claimed or observed, it commits no SendClaim, debits nothing, and
    returns the prior claimant's outcome so the caller can join it without new authorization.

  Either both the winner transition and the claim commit, or neither does.
- `contracts/record-registry.json`: `EffectIdentity:allocated->claimed` now lists
  `allowed_command_ids: ["authority.attempt.claim"]`. It no longer lists `kernel.record.transition`,
  so no separate path can pick a winner.
- Prose updated to agree: the P4 row in `02-authority-recovery.md` §6.2 and the `EffectIdentity`
  specification in `05-work-agents-skills.md` §6.
- Residual: the guard `command.authority.attempt.claim.v1` does not yet add an EffectIdentity
  conjunct, and the command payload has no explicit `effect_identity_ref` (the identity is
  resolved by natural key). Adding either one changes the schema or the guard, so it is left as
  an open question rather than done as a repair.

## F7-accountant-01: JournalBatch.balanced carried ResourceAccount meaning

- Cause: the kind-level `balanced` spec in `tools/phase_content.py` held the reservation equation
  and cited `a-reservation`. JournalBatch is the only record with a `balanced` phase, so that spec
  only ever applied here. `resource_equation` was dropped as not applicable, which left an AR/AF
  body whose `meaning`/`requires` described the wrong rule.
- Repair: the generic spec is deleted. A `("JournalBatch", "balanced")` override now requires the
  following:
  - each entry has an account, source references, and exactly one nonzero debit or credit;
  - debits equal credits per currency, exponent and ledger, with no cross-currency netting and no
    invented exchange rate or balancing entry;
  - coverage by source documents and treatment under one ledger authority and period;
  - every unreconciled item is identified with its disposition;
  - arithmetic is attested and accepted on this criterion id only, so a reservation attestation
    or a judgment on another predicate cannot satisfy it.

  Body: `nonempty_fields` over all six payload fields, plus `accepted_for` and `attested_result`.
  New source citations: journal-balance and bank-reconciliation, from the specification's source register (03 §native business authority); these are not claim-ledger ids.
  `predicate-registry.json` and `phase-content-gaps.json` were regenerated by
  `tools/author_phase_content.py`, and `--check` reports no findings.
- Residual: the kernel has no grouped-sum primitive, so the per-currency sum is attested rather
  than computed by the interpreter. A `journal_balance` primitive would be a kernel change and
  needs a decision.

## F7-cfo-01: Fulfillment.refunded had no acceptance content

- Repair: `gap-Fulfillment-refunded` is closed by a `("Fulfillment", "refunded")` override. It
  requires the following:
  - an entitlement anchored in the agreement, receipts and surviving duties;
  - amount and currency exactly as entitled, full or partial, never more than was received and
    never paid twice;
  - the refund is its own operation linked to the original payment;
  - a qualified native observation, exactly correlated to that provider refund, shows the refund
    **settled**, plus an accepted judgment on this criterion.

  An acknowledgement, a pending or unknown status, an unrelated payment, or a case closure is not
  enough. A pending or unknown refund effect stays held. A reservation or hold is released **only
  against the settled entitlement and only for the amount settled**.

  Body: `nonempty_fields` over agreement_ref, receipts and remaining_duties, plus
  `native_correlated(observed_applied)`, `accepted_for` and `attested_result`. Citations: CAP-17
  (`c-refund-conflict`), the two IC-PAY refund sentences, and `a-reservation`.
- Prose updated to agree: the `Fulfillment.refunded` row in `03-company-capabilities.md` and the
  meaning of `edge.Fulfillment.failed.refunded.v1`. Gaps registered went from 68 to 67.
- Residual: Fulfillment has no refund-amount or refund-operation field. Amount/currency matching
  is carried by the attested judgment and by the correlated observation's request digest, not by
  a payload path. Binding it to a field is a schema change.
