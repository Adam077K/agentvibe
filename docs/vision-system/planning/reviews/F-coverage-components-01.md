## Findings and semantic coverage judgment

**No new severity-ranked adverse finding is established.** The additions consistently preserve important distinctions: proposed work versus authority, selected route versus available capacity, performed service versus a packet, assessed evidence versus an accepted subject, and stopping work versus discharging responsibility.

The following are substantive observations supporting that bounded judgment.

**The compound company question receives actual differentiated treatment.** F25-Q01 has fifteen named job routes covering deciding, design, building, testing, operation, distribution, conversation, sales, support, books, institutional work, market research, suppliers, hiring and worker allocation. Each identifies intake, procedure, execution route and substantive acceptance. The design route requires a produced medium; bookkeeping requires supported reconciliation and applicable actual filing; hiring distinguishes selection, engagement and accepted capacity. This answers the compound question more meaningfully than assigning all its clauses to “orchestration.” See [09](../specification/09-company-human-traceability.md), lines 31–37, and [capabilities.json](../specification/capabilities.json), F25-Q01 beginning at line 11617.

**The component matrix mostly constrains responsibility rather than multiplying entities.** Its nineteen attributes describe logical responsibilities without creating nine new state machines or requiring nine agents. Component ownership depends on actual mandates and accepted assignments. Production cannot accept its own protected evidence, and the operator surface cannot manufacture authoritative state. Replacement and removal preserve current restrictions and surviving duties. See [10](../specification/10-components-authority-traceability.md), lines 9–13, 27–40, 102–120, 180–198 and 258–276.

**The sector treatment preserves distinct performance requirements.** Shipping needs actual movement and receipt; clinical work needs actual competent care; manufacturing needs conforming inspected goods; subscriptions need each recurring delivery and cancellation reconciliation. The shared language is repetitive, but its sector-specific facts and false-success conditions are not interchangeable. The catalog expressly leaves new machines, actuators and provider endpoints subject to admitted extensions. See [12](../specification/12-scope-lifecycle-traceability.md), lines 7–13, and [scope-lifecycle-contracts.json](../specification/scope-lifecycle-contracts.json), subscription boxes at line 933, clinical work at line 1098 and logistics at line 1296.

This is a specification of reusable coordination and performance boundaries. It is not a complete industrial implementation for every listed sector, nor does it claim one.

**Human attention is treated as a real resource and competence remains distinct from ownership.** The answers name actual batch minutes, interruptions, substitutes and unavailable-state behavior. They do not substitute an approval count for capacity or agreement for understanding. A competence gap affects the relevant action rather than taking over the person’s values. See [09](../specification/09-company-human-traceability.md), lines 59–85; [capabilities.json](../specification/capabilities.json), F21-Q07 at line 9110, F22-Q09 at line 10402 and F46-Q08 at line 15570.

These choices still need outcome evidence. They do not establish that the combined demands fit any actual founder’s life.

## What final 11 review must resolve

The pending full schema/predicate catalog and known exact-acceptance revision issue **FI12** are declared active work. I have not treated them as newly discovered omissions or closed them through these prose traces.

Final review must demonstrate, at minimum:

1. **Compatible exact subject and transition semantics.** Ordinary report, assessment, acceptance, activation, subsequent revision and invalidation must compose without circular evidence, stale acceptance or silent substitution. Negative and inconclusive assessments must remain representable.
2. **Canonical identities and composite bindings.** Resolve every imported alias and role, including capture artifacts, evaluations, handoffs, signed response values and projections, without creating competing authorities.
3. **Executable domain predicates.** A sector or capability cannot pass through “actual service” as an unchecked string. Bind the required performer, authority, evidence, subject, scope and accepted outcome; provide meaningful valid, unavailable, partial, disputed and invalid examples.
4. **Authorized command ownership and bootstrap.** Every creation, amendment, transfer and retirement route must have compatible actor, mandate, revision and failure rules. Logical component ownership cannot substitute for actual standing.
5. **Physical storage and current-use consequences.** The schemas must preserve the declared sensitivity, copy, retention and deletion behavior, including short fields, relationships, late derivatives and restored material.
6. **Cross-contract conformance cases.** Assemble complete positive and adverse journeys using actual schema instances and exact guards. The same rules must permit a legitimate routine path without needless founder or professional intervention, and reject the corresponding unsupported consequential path.

A remaining choice that forces an implementer to invent who may act, what acceptance means or how a duty survives would block full-plan acceptance. Unknown actual funding, professionals, account eligibility and measured human burden can remain deployment or empirical prerequisites only while their admission and unavailable-state behavior are complete.

The reviewed additions introduce no independently established new foundational blocker. They support continued consolidation and later full-plan review. Prior scoped FIR/EPT closures remain scoped; no runtime, deployment, fulfillment or Phase G acceptance follows from this report.
