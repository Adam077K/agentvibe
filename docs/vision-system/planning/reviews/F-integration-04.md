A human-supplied source or the admitted search/fetch adapter supplies captured evidence; retrieval preserves source scope, omissions and current permissions. Native reasoning can consume supplied authorized context through the no-tool profile. A separately admitted mediated profile can request bounded work through the broker and isolated build worker. The resulting attempt remains evidence for assessment. Workflow advancement and whole-run acceptance are separate from the worker’s report. Company offer and launch conditions retain the earlier exact-subject acceptance requirements. Pre-sale capacity can be established through an admitted bounded pilot or other relevant evidence; the new integration track does not restore the previously rejected requirement that the first sale already exist.

Checkout creation then carries a continuing exposure. Capacity remains held until native expiry or verified cancellation; an unknown creation does not release that hold. Actual product delivery requires observed public behavior, functional and accessibility evidence, export/recovery and beneficiary acceptance. Human service requires actual accepted performance. Statement reconciliation distinguishes processor activity, bank cash and professional closure. These are substantive fulfillment criteria, rather than labels around a generated packet. See [07](../specification/07-integrations-capacity.md), lines 48–51 and 99.

The positive falsifier remains demanding: a complete legitimate first-venture cycle must finish within admitted resources, including due service and finance. “Everything was safely refused” cannot satisfy it.

### Lost acknowledgement, closure and grievance

The selected adapters preserve a workable adverse interpretation:

- A possibly sent mutation retains an unknown effect, holds and reconciliation work. Changing provider or operation wording cannot reset its business identity.
- Stopping an offer expires or accounts for outstanding Checkout objects; stopping a connection does not discharge customer duties.
- A public form initially returns a staged receipt. It does not claim independently witnessed company acceptance.
- Quota failure must produce actual failure and an available alternative, rather than silently lose intake.
- Continuity requires actual custodians, independent access and exercised performance. A named alternate or exported packet alone does not perform the service.

These meanings are stated in [07](../specification/07-integrations-capacity.md), lines 35–37, 48–57, 67 and 160. The discriminating rehearsal must combine a real complaint, quota exhaustion or lost ingress, an unavailable primary operator and an unresolved effect. Success requires actual permitted continuation or an honestly recorded service failure with surviving duties.

The selected providers still share some external administrative, billing and infrastructure roots. The contracts disclose those dependencies; they do not demonstrate that a purchased arrangement survives every combination of loss.

### Private context, deletion and reconstruction

The privacy rules extend beyond text fields and obvious secrets. They cover relationship metadata, short values, queries, logs, canonical storage, replicas, restored backups and model-directed output. Protected lexical retrieval uses a bounded ephemeral path; persistent full-text indexing is limited to explicitly admitted nonprotected material. An index or summary cannot outrank current restrictions. See [06](../specification/06-knowledge-evidence-evaluation.md), lines 58–70.

A useful paired test is essential: place a private value in build output, a short enum, a correction and a restored copy; revoke its scope during late publication; require forbidden disclosure and stale reuse to fail while unrelated authorized research succeeds. Retained permitted linkage or unknown provider copies must leave deletion visibly incomplete.

The account contract also avoids promising unavailable causation or deterministic provider replay. Record reconstruction, captured-response replay, live rerun and distribution replication have different meanings. Current deletion and authority restrictions remain controlling. See [06](../specification/06-knowledge-evidence-evaluation.md), lines 84–88.

### Protected evaluation and controlled change

Evidence closure includes capture, parser, mappings, selection, denominator, labels, evaluator and acceptance rule. Corrupting a shared dependency must invalidate affected use before acceptance; a later background sweep alone is insufficient. Review completion and inconclusive assessment do not satisfy the product predicate.

The evaluation design includes known-defect controls, sham changes, omitted-attempt checks and clean valid controls. Thus an always-pass evaluator and an always-reject evaluator are both challengeable. Model diversity is not asserted to establish independent error. See [06](../specification/06-knowledge-evidence-evaluation.md), lines 80–82 and 111–127.

Protected change adds bounded trials, staged rollout, monitoring and rollback constraints. Rollback cannot restore old grants, deleted data or consumed allowance, and cannot undo an external consequence by changing software state. See [08](../specification/08-improvement-implementation.md), lines 25–43.

These contracts are meaningful only if the producer lacks the relevant write authority in the deployed arrangement. No such isolation was executed or demonstrated during this review.

## Remaining limits and disposition

The strongest countermodel is a perfectly recorded company that cannot afford or perform its ordinary obligations: accurate reports, disciplined refusals and protected evaluation consume the available people and resources while customers still receive no service. The contracts explicitly reject calling that success. Actual matched outcome and burden trials must determine whether the proposed arrangement avoids it.

No executed evidence presently establishes native flag composition, all-channel isolation, deployed credential separation, protected storage erasure, provider admission, load capacity, human continuity or complete venture fulfillment. Those remain empirical or deployment prerequisites where their intended meaning is already specified.

The earlier FIR and EPT closures remain scoped closures. This review identifies no demonstrated regression in them and grants no broader acceptance. The frozen subset can proceed to complete binding, conformance and whole-plan review; it cannot yet be called an implemented or accepted company system.
