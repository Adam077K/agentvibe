**Review method and decision boundary**

Freeze the eventual review subject and this protocol before evaluating the drafts. Preserve amendments separately with their source requirement and reason; do not change a criterion because the implementation produces an inconvenient result.

Judge the plan’s specified behavior, enforceable boundaries and implementation path. Rename its components mentally into ordinary business terms. Neither nine components, particular record types, centralized storage, federation, persistent agents nor a particular interface receives credit by existing.

For each probe, record the governing requirement, precise specification passage, expected behavior, counterexample, evidence needed and disposition. Distinguish:

- A specification sufficiently concrete to implement and test.
- A documented mechanism whose deployment remains unverified.
- A future empirical hypothesis with an adequate evaluation protocol.
- A missing decision without which implementation would choose the system’s meaning.

The twelve dimensions receive separate judgments and findings. There is no aggregate score, majority-vote override or presumed pass.

**Twelve directive dimensions**

| Dimension | Concrete review probe | Evidence needed and failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Completeness** | Follow an unfamiliar business need from discovery through performance, support and retirement. Where does necessary work enter before anyone requests or sponsors it? | All 24 deliverable families, source concerns and capabilities have substantive dispositions. Missing fulfillment, formation, maintenance, grievance or closure behavior blocks completeness even when infrastructure is detailed. |
| **Traceability** | Pick an original promise, rejected option and correction; follow each into work, outputs, acceptance and subsequent change. Trace a finished artifact backward independently. | Exact meaning-preserving references, versions and transformation rules. Links to a broad chapter or provenance identifiers without their operative relationship do not suffice. |
| **Internal coherence** | Give independently implementing teams adjoining contracts. Can they agree on who acts, what each status means, and what remains owed after failure? | Compatible inputs, outputs, transitions, authority and failure semantics. A local “accepted,” “paid” or “closed” state must not silently establish another domain’s satisfaction or discharge. |
| **Evidence quality** | Keep the reported rationale fixed while changing a planted influence; corrupt a shared parser or denominator while leaving evaluator files unchanged. | Separate observation, endorsement, rationale and causal hypothesis; transitive evidence dependencies and independent expected meanings. Authentic logs and agreeing models cannot establish unsupported causation or independence. |
| **Alternative depth** | Remove each major mechanism or replace it with native tools, fixed grants and scheduled work. Which named problem becomes materially worse? | Preserved credible comparators, opportunity costs and falsifiers. No reward for ontology, document volume or a comparator deprived of its legitimate adaptive capacity. Apply EAS-R1 below. |
| **Vision preservation** | Give the plain-language account to a reader unfamiliar with the architecture. Can they explain the company, beneficiaries, refusals, owner’s day and costs? | Complete production and responsible experimentation alongside a truthful account. An account-only system, coding organization or permanent founder review job does not satisfy the expanded directive. |
| **Unknown integrity** | Replace every “later,” “adapter,” “professional,” “policy” and “configurable” with its actual unresolved choice. What downstream behavior depends on it? | Named settlement method, authority, deadline, bounded interim behavior and dependent admission restrictions. An unanswered foundation cannot become nonblocking through a status label. |
| **Adversarial robustness** | Use a noncompliant worker, hostile tool metadata, poisoned memory, compromised evaluator dependency and colluding reviewers. Can the system still prevent or contain the claimed harm? | Explicit trust/failure boundaries, protected enforcement and adversarial/negative-control plans. Prompts, tool annotations, credentials hidden from display or multiple reviewer names are not enforcement evidence. |
| **Operational feasibility** | Run the proposed company through ordinary demand, unfamiliar exceptions, provider exhaustion and joint owner/supplier absence. Who actually works, with what capacity? | Separate money, allowance, infrastructure, professional/service capacity and human labor. Hidden founder translation, unavailable continuity or routine refusal of authorized work defeats the operating claim. |
| **Buildability** | Ask an implementer to construct one vertical journey using only the package. Which essential decisions must they invent? | Selected supported technologies/topology, concrete contracts, repository/module map, dependency order, integration and failure tests. Foundational “choose later” gaps or mutually impossible platform assumptions block acceptance. |
| **Responsibility** | A noncustomer contests a harmful decision; the usual custodian is implicated; the founder has authority but materially misunderstands the consequence. | Reachable, competent, conflict-aware response and remedy; preserved standing; consequence-specific assistance or narrowing. Neither a packet nor lawful approval establishes performance or informed assessment. |
| **Changeability** | Change a provider, supplier, source meaning, schema and governing purpose while work remains in flight; then remove the relevant component. | Dependency-aware migration, retained uncertainty, safe rollback, successor acceptance and removal criteria. Old effects or duties cannot disappear through new identifiers, rewritten summaries or architecture versions. |

Exact ordering, timing, durability, fencing and kernel obligations are technical conformance tests. They must match the chosen contract; they are not reasons to prefer one organizational architecture.

**Coverage must answer meaning, not accumulate links**

Maintain the original 566-question denominator and question text. Review every row’s disposition and the substantive answer it identifies. Decompose compound questions where needed: **F25-Q01’s single row contains many company jobs**, so answering its orchestration clause does not answer sales, bookkeeping, supplier negotiation or support.

Check three directions:

1. Source concern → actual answer → applicable contract → planned verification.
2. Contract or mechanism → the requirement and problem justifying it.
3. Independent scenario → all required duties, including those absent from the producer’s catalog.

Shared answers are legitimate when they explicitly cover each question’s meaning. Merely assigning many IDs to one chapter is not coverage. Examine supplemental vision, operating/consequence sectors, lifecycle stages and discovered D01–D15 independently; “566 answered” cannot conceal omissions there.

Refusal, supersession and nonapplicability require reasons and visible downstream consequences. A refusal must not silently remove the whole-company ambition. “Answered” means specified at this gate; “implemented” requires later implementation evidence. Preserve missing evidence, failed measurements, disagreements and changes of framing.

**Distinguishing a capability contract from catalog prose**

For each capability, require its purpose, actual inputs and outputs, dependencies, authority, consequences, evidence, evaluation, implementation mode, failure behavior and replacement/removal criteria. Cross-cutting contracts may be referenced, but domain-specific meaning must remain explicit.

Use the **implementation ambiguity probe**: supply one concrete valid request, one malformed request, one ambiguous outcome and one unavailable dependency. Two implementers should derive compatible observable behavior without inventing who accepts the outcome, what is promised, or who may act.

Require a complete path through intake, performed work or accepted delegation, substantive outcome acceptance, disagreement, partial completion, replacement and surviving duties. State-bearing subjects need identity, ownership, lifecycle, transitions, authorization, relations, versioning, retention and deletion semantics. They need not become separate classes, tables, services or agents.

A legal coordination capability can legitimately arrange qualified work. Its endpoint must nevertheless include accepted engagement, delivery, determination or filing evidence, missed-deadline handling and replacement. Producing a brief for an unspecified professional is only preparation. The same distinction applies to physical fulfillment, hiring, refunds and succession.

Across these cases, separately inspect authorization, delivery, customer acceptance, economic value, harm, owner attention and competence. Include an operationally competent losing venture and a profitable venture that shifts excessive work onto customers, applicants or contractors. Explicit mission subsidy remains a legitimate human value choice; it must not be disguised as profitability.

**Baselines, negative controls and EAS-R1**

Keep **S0** credible: native business tools, a shared responsibility/decision record, fixed grants, calendar/reconciliation, temporary help and accountable manual releases. Match lifecycle scope, external obligations, protection, recovery and owner allowance. Do not gift S0 an always-available expert founder or omit S1’s maintenance labor.

Retain four separate EAS-R1 comparisons:

1. **Own-rights conformance:** C2 owes its admissible bounded discriminator and governed disposition; A/S1 may lawfully defer a discretionary inquiry.
2. **Business effectiveness:** match external obligations, information, total resources, service requirements and owner allowance. Preserve differing internal inquiry deadlines when they are the treatment.
3. **Representation control:** implement the same C2 rights through case-based and shared-hypothesis representations. Equivalent outcomes challenge representation necessity, not the rights’ value.
4. **Simpler policy control:** fully adaptive A with protected discovery capacity and scheduled cross-domain review, without compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction.

Do not force another candidate or a preferred winner. Reopen the selected foundation when simpler operation produces equivalent outcomes with less total burden, federation materially improves actual domain replacement/coordination, or protected inquiry finds important neglected work that discretionary review systematically misses.

Negative controls must include a checker that passes without checking, omitted failed attempts, a changed denominator, correct output produced without required authority, duplicated evidence presented as independence, unchanged rationale despite changed influence, and blanket refusal falsely presented as safety success. Pair these with valid authorized service and harmless independent activity. Evaluate delayed unfamiliar owner recognition/intervention, not attendance, confidence or agreement with advice.

**Which unknowns may remain nonblocking**

A future empirical result can remain unknown: longitudinal competence benefit, actual business demand, stochastic reliability, comparative discovery value or sustainable maintenance cost. It needs a falsifiable protocol, appropriate baseline, measurement unit, repeated trials where relevant, stopping/reopening rule and owner. The plan must not depend on a favorable result being assumed.

Deployment prerequisites can remain unestablished: actual credentials, professional engagement, applicable determinations, external funding, accepted substitutes and operational risk acceptance. Their admission checks and unavailable-state behavior must be complete. No dependent promise may be counted as deployable before they are satisfied.

A missing foundational choice is different: no authority for a transition, no performer for required work, incompatible completion meanings, unchosen enforcement topology, impossible provider assumptions, or no way to preserve obligations across the declared failure. If implementation would resolve it by inventing policy or changing a guarantee, it blocks full-plan acceptance.

**Grounds for an adverse Phase G verdict**

Find the plan **unbuildable** when essential interfaces, dependencies or supported enforcement are absent or mutually incompatible; **incoherent** when valid local behaviors compose into unauthorized, contradictory or falsely completed company behavior; **incomplete** when material source concerns or lifecycle duties lack substantive treatment.

A foundational blocker cannot be offset by excellence elsewhere. Report its exact trigger, resulting failure and consequence, affected requirement, missing decision and discriminating evidence. Preserve D2’s conceptual closures while reopening any finding whose promised repair disappears in specification.

Only after this review can the package receive a scoped planning disposition. That disposition would still establish neither runtime conformance, deployment readiness, actual fulfillment nor the business value of the intended system.
