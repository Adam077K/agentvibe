**EAS-02: why C2 now qualifies, and what it does not establish**

The original C1 could be implemented inside fully adaptive A without changing the relevant control rights. The amendment acknowledges that overlap at [AC-foundational-distinction.md §1, lines 7–15](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#1-the-overlap-is-real). I agree with retaining that conclusion.

C2 adds a different governing commitment at [§2, lines 19–34](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#2-c2s-nonoptional-constitution):

1. A charter holder can obtain a first bounded discriminator when admission predicates hold, without a production sponsor deciding that the question merits work.
2. Protected inquiry capacity cannot be reclaimed through ordinary production optimization.
3. Inquiry continuity survives closure of originating production work.
4. Production cannot finally adjudicate the inquiry’s disposition or use ordinary work-order closure to retire it.
5. Changing those rights requires the charter’s actual amendment or emergency authority.

The strongest distinction is the combination of **a claim on bounded work and a separate authority to end that claim**. Priority settings alone would not establish it. A scheduler that merely happens to investigate quickly is not equivalent to a scheduler prohibited from denying an admissible discriminator for ordinary production reasons.

This changes permissible actions, control privileges, reserve ownership, continuity obligations, and operating cost. Those are material organizational architecture choices even when one case engine implements them.

The comparison must remain honest about composability:

| Arrangement | What distinguishes it in this comparison |
|---|---|
| **A** | Adaptive cases remain under the admitted-case authority, including discretion over non-due investigation. |
| **B** | Native domains retain administrative control and scheduling; sponsors coordinate outcomes through bounded exchanges. |
| **C2** | A shared company system contains standing inquiry jurisdiction that production cannot ordinarily override. |
| **Case engine implementing C2’s rights** | An implementation of the same governing arrangement on this axis, not evidence against case technology. |
| **Federation with equivalent inquiry charters** | A hybrid combining B’s administrative arrangement with C2’s inquiry rights. It must be identified as such. |

The candidates need not be mutually exclusive technologies. They must expose materially different commitments. C2 now does so.

The research/support/finance trace at [§4, lines 48–61](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#4-discriminating-trace-research-support-and-finance) supplies a concrete difference: A may lawfully defer the prospective investigation; C2 owes its reserved attempt. A is explicitly allowed to investigate well and is not scored as failing merely for exercising legitimate discretion. C2 also pays assessor and continuity costs when the hypothesis is false.

That is sufficient for conceptual alternative depth. It is not evidence that compulsory investigation improves company outcomes.

**Do the shared repairs erase the distinction?**

They narrow it, but do not erase it.

- Shared durable release and validity controls determine whether an effect may occur. They do not decide which business question deserves investigation.
- B’s release registry is deliberately limited to committing exact authorized bundles. Its duty resolver assigns ownership; it does not command native schedules. See [technical §3, lines 58–76](../candidates/technical-foundation-repairs-v1.md#3-bs-cross-issuer-release-and-revocation-contract) and [company §2, lines 21–31](../candidates/company-foundation-repairs-v1.md#2-b-ownership-before-sponsorship-without-a-permanent-company-planner).
- Shared grievance rights require meaningful response to affected people, including independent escalation where appropriate. They do not automatically grant every eligible internal prospective question the C2 right to its first discriminator.
- Shared protected discovery capacity establishes a resource allocation. C2 additionally establishes a holder’s claim on a particular admissible investigation and limits production’s retirement jurisdiction.

If synthesis later gives A or B all those inquiry rights, the comparison must acknowledge the resulting hybrid or equivalence. The amendment expressly requires that treatment.

**New issue EAS-R1 — P2: the comparative protocol can accidentally match away, or reward by definition, the inquiry-right difference**

Locations: [AC-foundational-distinction.md §4, line 50](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#4-discriminating-trace-research-support-and-finance), [§5, line 69](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#5-relationship-to-b-and-the-simpler-comparator), [§7, line 99](../candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md#7-acceptance-and-falsification-for-independent-rereview); [company-foundation-repairs-v1.md §6, line 86](../candidates/company-foundation-repairs-v1.md#6-semantic-maintenance-and-finding-omitted-work-are-paid-labor).

The trace makes C2’s four-hour discriminator commitment part of the treatment. The later comparison instructions call for equal inquiry deadlines. Shared repairs also give every candidate protected discovery capacity.

**Trigger → failure → consequence:** An evaluator either gives A the same binding inquiry rights while treating it as a different control, or scores A’s lawful discretionary deferral as a failure because C2 owes an earlier attempt. The experiment then measures an inconsistent comparator or rewards C2’s definition, rather than establishing beneficial business effects.

**Neutral property:** Match external obligations, information, total resources, and legitimate service requirements while preserving the governing choice being tested.

**Required specification:** Separate:

- Rights conformance: whether each candidate obeys its own declared authority.
- Business effectiveness: useful discovery, missed obligations, service, uncertainty, owner effort, and total cost.
- Representation control: the same C2 rights implemented through a case engine.
- Simpler policy control: fully adaptive A with protected discovery capacity and scheduled cross-function review, without compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction.

**Falsifier:** Equivalent rights are counted as different architectures, or C2 wins solely because A lacks C2’s internal deadline. Either observation invalidates the comparative conclusion.

This is a protocol refinement for synthesis/specification, not a remaining foundational blocker. It does not require another candidate now.
