**XSR-01 — Medium: the scope overlay leaves first-contact admission broader than the company’s discovery contract**

**Passages:** `planning/specification/scope-lifecycle-contracts.json:1769` requires institutional and performance readiness “before first outward exposure” and associates that requirement with CAP-12/13/14/40. Its case at line 1791 exercises an unsupported cold message; line 1792 supplies only a generic ordinary control. The sector overlay has related ambiguity: `scope-lifecycle-contracts.json:309` routes developer-tool activity through FormationReadiness, “ProfessionalDetermination/InteractionDetermination” and verified performance capacity. It does not say explicitly which of these is conditional on the particular activity. The more precise activity-specific qualification appears at line 1493 and in 03.

**Concrete path:** The founder authorizes a bounded interview about an observed problem. Contact purpose, recipient authority, truthful wording, privacy handling, interviewer availability and a response custodian are established. No product, price, offer or product-delivery capacity exists yet.

One implementer can allow the interview under CAP-04. Another can apply “first outward exposure” literally, require CAP-12 launch readiness and therefore require an Offer/ProductSpec and product capacity before collecting the evidence needed to define them. Both can point to normative passages. Applying the sector sentence as a universal professional gate introduces another potentially unnecessary engagement and cost.

**Consequence:** Authorized discovery becomes dependent on later commercial readiness, or implementers silently decide which normative gate to disregard. This affects F25, lifecycle stages 01/02/04, founder attention and G’s implementation-ambiguity probe.

**Required correction:** Bind prerequisites to the actual contact or promise. Distinguish research recruitment/interviews, ordinary inbound replies, bounded validation offers and standing commercial offers. State expressly that professional determinations are required when the actual activity’s prerequisites require them; neither a sector label nor a lifecycle label independently creates that requirement. Preserve real contact, disclosure, representation and response duties.

**Falsifying test:** Give two implementers the interview above and an otherwise similar message that offers a paid service with missing delivery capacity. They must derive the same admission result: permit the properly authorized interview; withhold the unsupported commercial exposure. The positive case must conduct the interview, not merely prepare its packet.

**Uncertainty/disposition:** I have not established that the architecture makes discovery impossible. A narrow reading is coherent with 03. The issue is that the scope overlay currently permits incompatible readings; this is a bounded contract clarification before freeze, not a foundation replacement.
