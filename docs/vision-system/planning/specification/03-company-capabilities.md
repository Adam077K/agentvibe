## Record and procedure contract

**SPECIFICATION.** All domain records use the kernel’s `RecordEnvelope` and exact `Ref = {record_id, record_type, revision}`. `company_id` is an administrative boundary; `principal_id` identifies the actual principal and is never inferred from venture or brand. A domain-owned record's `state` definition constrains the envelope state; other listed fields form its payload. Canonical core extensions use the kernel state and cannot redefine it. An absent optional value carries an absence reason in the relevant record’s evidence/uncertainty account. Unknown amounts, rights, available people and deadlines never become zero, false or unlimited by default. `Instant`, `Money`, `Position` and inline `SourceWatermark` import kernel types: UTC timestamps use `Z`, and money includes currency, signed minor units and exponent. Importers may normalize an explicitly offset timestamp while preserving its original bytes; ambiguous time is rejected.

**SPECIFICATION.** Every procedure runs as an admitted `WorkOrder` through S1-C02. Its inputs are exact catalog ports plus loader-controlled context, causal allowance, lease and acceptance owner. It returns staged artifact references, attributable attempt reports, observations, checkpoint and unfinished duties through S1-C03. All commands use the kernel’s `POST /v1/commands`, including `command_id`, version, company/principal, expected revisions, causal episode and authority references. Transport supplies authenticated actor identity. Kernel `accepted` means the transition is committed and independently witnessed; it does not mean an outward effect occurred or a customer was satisfied. `durability_pending` requires lookup with the same command ID; a primary row cannot supply the missing witness.

**SPECIFICATION.** The catalog's `record_bindings` is normative. `Intent` is the company field extension/view of canonical `IntentVersion`; it never independently owns endorsed purpose. `PersonMandate` extends canonical `Mandate`, and `AcceptanceRecord` extends canonical `EvidenceJudgment`. Their extra business fields retain exact canonical references and existing core states. `Party` resolves to `Principal`, `Artifact` to `ArtifactVersion`, `Decision` to `DecisionRecord`, `Permission` to `Grant`, and `Operation` to `OperationIntent` before any exact reference is persisted. A company label cannot create another authoritative record type. `Incident` and `Experiment` are native domain records with exact controlling `Case` refs; they do not replace Case admission. `ImprovementProposal` references `ProtectedChange` before promotion. Goal, Mission, Project and Workstream remain distinct domain intent/grouping subjects with case references for executable work; this file creates neither duplicate canonical versions nor aliases of them to Case. Full-schema consolidation must preserve these bindings.

**SPECIFICATION.** `company.capNN.v1` names a procedure contract, not an agent. The catalog’s `implementation_mode` is a composition: deterministic functions compute or validate; durable procedures wait and route; bounded model steps interpret or generate; actual people/professionals/services perform their respective work. A procedure may use a current accepted dependency record without starting that capability recursively. Treasury and bookkeeping can reference each other’s current observations; such semantic dependency is not an executable scheduling cycle. A real work dependency cycle opens an owned exception with its blocked outcome and next discriminating action.

**SPECIFICATION.** Catalog `dependencies` declare available capability interfaces and conditional record predicates, never a requirement that every listed capability already completed a run. Actual inputs needed for a proposed step remain explicit in its WorkOrder; absence can be reported only where its acceptance condition permits it.

**SPECIFICATION.** The first-venture path is: endorse intent and assign setup custody; create a research plan and evidence inventory, including genuinely empty observation strata; investigate authorized sources and alternatives; produce a provisional product/price and support/delivery procedure; test actual capability and establish institutional readiness; obtain authority for a bounded validation offer; then gather the first actual agreement, delivery and customer observations. No prior paying customer is required to investigate or prepare. Empty evidence cannot establish demand, customer validation or live-service experience. An unvalidated commercial hypothesis can support an explicitly authorized pilot only with real capacity, required rights, remedies and resources. No grant or risk preference for such a pilot is adopted here. A demand-dependent scale decision waits for actual evidence; unknown demand is not a universal block on the first investigation.

**SPECIFICATION.** `company.record.propose` implements the catalog's explicit seed factories. It validates typed source inputs, creates only the allowed initial state and assigns the declared custodian before admission. This supplies research plans, draft offers, campaigns, received conversations/cases, assessment protocols and operating plans; they cannot appear as unexplained inputs. It does not fabricate observed evidence or bypass endorsement. Every catalog entry has a distinct substantive acceptance condition and responsible acceptance role in addition to kernel checks. **SPECIFICATION.** Ports describe typed records produced during and at the end of a procedure. An agreement can become authoritative before the sales procedure finishes, with delivery still due. Each intermediate output exposes its actual state and unfinished duties. Terminal acceptance requires the catalog's substantive condition; “waiting on another capability” does not satisfy it. **SPECIFICATION.** Each work contract names a latest responsible start, due outcome and update deadline. ### The overlap tie-break

**DESIGN PROPOSAL.** The ordered resolver above decides which *kind* of mandate takes custody. It does not decide between two mandates of the same kind that both cover the harm, and until that is stated two implementers derive different owners from the same facts. Three candidate rules were available: the broadest covering mandate (custody by seniority), the narrowest covering mandate (custody by least sufficient authority), and founder escalation on every overlap. The third contradicts the paragraph above — escalation is reserved for genuinely missing founder authority — and the first hands a wide mandate authority over work it did not need to act on, which is the accumulation [02 §3](../02-architecture-selection.md#3-selected-logical-and-responsibility-model) refuses when it says the resolver "cannot grant resources or become a second planner."

**DECISION — least authority sufficient, then availability, then acceptance order.** On overlap, provisional custody goes to the mandate with the **narrowest scope that still covers the whole harm**. Where two such mandates are equally narrow, custody goes to the one whose **accepted alternate is currently available**; where that still ties, to the one whose **mandate acceptance is earliest**. The reason is that the narrowest sufficient mandate is the one that can act without borrowing authority it was not granted, which keeps this a custody assignment rather than a widening of permission. Each step is deterministic over records the company already holds, so the same facts produce the same owner in two implementations.

**DECISION — what the losing mandates and the assignment itself record.** The mandates that did not take custody are recorded on the resulting `ResponsibilityAssignment` as `contesting_mandate_refs`, so a reader can see that an overlap existed and which alternatives were passed over; an empty list means the resolver found no overlap, which is a different fact from the field being absent. The assignment is **provisional** until acknowledged transfer or timer expiry, exactly as the paragraph above already requires — expiry activates the accepted alternate through the existing `alternate_assignment_ref`, `deadlines` and `expires_at` payload fields, and needs no new timer. **Disputing the assignment does not suspend custody.** A contested provisional custody is still custody: the harm keeps an owner while the dispute runs, because the failure this resolver exists to prevent is an unowned queue, and a dispute that suspended custody would recreate it. The guarantee is independent of which mandate wins — custody exists either way, `unresolved_scope` is required on every `ResponsibilityAssignment`, and timer expiry activates the accepted alternate.

**SPECIFICATION.** **Fields an implementer must add.** `ResponsibilityAssignment.payload.contesting_mandate_refs: Ref<Mandate>[]` (required, empty-allowed, meaning "no overlap"); `payload.custody_basis` as a closed enum over `{promise_sponsor, service_mandate, maintenance_custodian, provisional_overlap_resolution}`, recording which branch of the ordered resolver produced the assignment; and `payload.custody_tie_break_reason` as a closed enum over `{narrowest_sufficient_scope, alternate_available, earliest_acceptance, no_overlap}`, recording which of the three steps decided it. The last is what makes two implementations comparable: without it, two resolvers that disagree produce different owners and no evidence of where they diverged. The [record registry](contracts/record-registry.json) and [record schema](contracts/records.schema.json) are the place those fields are declared; this section states the rule, not the JSON.

> **Superseded 2026-09-13.** The closing sentence — *"The record registry and record schema are the place those fields are declared; this section states the rule, not the JSON"* — stands verbatim, and **the declaration it defers to has happened.** All three fields exist on `ResponsibilityAssignment` with the exact names and the exact closed enums stated above, and **a guard compares them**: `edge.ResponsibilityAssignment.proposed.accepted.v1` — the edge that PRODUCES an assignment, which is where the resolver's answer is recorded — requires `custody_basis` present, and where `contesting_mandate_refs` is non-empty requires `custody_basis == provisional_overlap_resolution` with a `custody_tie_break_reason` that is not `no_overlap`. Without that second half the enums would record a value nothing compares, which is how a field becomes decoration. `contesting_mandate_refs` is deliberately absent from every `nonempty_fields` list, because empty is a meaning here — *"the resolver found no overlap"* — and is a different fact from the field being absent. The guard does **not** require the contest to be resolved: a contested provisional custody is still custody, since the failure the resolver exists to prevent is an unowned queue.

### CAP-42 grievance case lifecycle

**SPECIFICATION.** `GrievanceCase` has ten registered phases in [the record registry](contracts/record-registry.json). Six of them were named in no chapter of this specification, which left the record's `source_contract` pointing at a candidate repair file rather than at a contract. This subsection is that contract. Each row states what must be true to be in the phase and what the phase must not be mistaken for. It adds no phase, no edge and no predicate; the registry remains the authority for which transitions exist.

| Phase | What must be true to be in it | What it must not be mistaken for |
|---|---|---|
| `received` | An actual challenge has been captured at the accessible intake with its arrival time, the requested outcome and the originals preserved. Standing does not require an account or product access | Acknowledgement of merit. A receipt is not a determination, and the company's response deadline starts running here rather than at triage |
| `triaged` | The case has a named competent recipient **outside the disputed decision and incentive dependency**, a stated deadline and a route; the person's individual standing is recognized | An assessment of the complaint. Triage assigns and schedules; it decides nothing. Clustering repeated processing is permitted and cannot erase this person's standing, deadline, evidence or alternate |
| `investigating` | The assigned custodian is examining actual evidence against the stated challenge within a finite recorded allowance | A remedy in progress. Investigation consumes recorded operational labor, and its existence is not a reason to suspend unrelated legitimate service to this or any person |
| `awaiting_evidence` | The investigation is blocked on a named item from a named source, with a due date and a stated consequence if it does not arrive | A stopped clock. The deadline and the duty both continue; the complainant's silence is not withdrawal, and the company's silence is not a disposition |
| `remedy_authorized` | A competent authority has decided a specific remedy, its funding is **actually reserved**, and a performer and due date exist | A performed remedy or a made payment. An authorization without funded capacity and a named performer is an unowned promise, not this phase |
| `remedy_performing` | The authorized remedy is being performed by the accepted performer within the recorded scope | A completed remedy or a satisfied person. Partial or failed performance keeps or returns the case here, and no timer advances it |
| `disposition_issued` | A reasoned outcome has been communicated to the person in terms they can act on, naming the reasons relied on, the surviving duties and the route to contest | Closure, or agreement. A disposition can be issued and immediately contested, and issuing one to an unreachable address does not discharge the duty to communicate |
| `contested` | The person has challenged the disposition or the remedy, and the challenge is routed outside the disputed dependency | A fresh start or an admission. Prior reasons, deadlines and evidence survive, and contesting does not suspend an already authorized or performing remedy |
| `closed_with_residuals` | Every disposition that can be made has been made, and at least one duty, unknown effect or unpaid remedy survives — each with an actual custodian, funding and review date | `closed`, or an absence of remaining responsibility. It may not be advertised as one, and the reachable intake and competent residual custodian continue while it stands |
| `closed` | No residual duty, unknown effect or unfunded remedy remains against this case | Proof that the person was satisfied, or a bar to a further grievance. `closed → contested` is a registered edge |
