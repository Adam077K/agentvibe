# Company capabilities and substantive fulfillment

**Specification version:** COMPANY-1.0 · **architecture:** S1.0 · **base:** `6a2487a` · 2026-09-12. **Status:** Phase F authoring, unimplemented and awaiting independent review. This specifies S01 and company-facing contracts for CAP-01–46. It does not complete the remaining execution, integration, context, evaluation, full-schema or deployment tracks. No actual authority, funding, professional engagement or service capacity is created by this document.

[S1.0](../02-architecture-selection.md), [CF1](../candidates/company-foundation-repairs-v1.md), [TF1](../candidates/technical-foundation-repairs-v1.md), and the [architecture/company](../reviews/D2-architecture-evidence-company.md) and [technical](../reviews/D2-technical.md) reassessments control its boundaries. The [capability catalog](capabilities.json) supplies explicit typed inputs/outputs, dependencies, modes, authority, evidence, happy path, conformance case, failure and replacement for every [required outcome](../../coverage/capability-requirements.json). Its record definitions are domain payload/state extensions, not a competing kernel or a claim that all directive schemas are finished.

## Record and procedure contract

All domain records use the kernel’s `RecordEnvelope` and exact `Ref = {record_id, record_type, revision}`. `company_id` is an administrative boundary; `principal_id` identifies the actual principal and is never inferred from venture or brand. A domain-owned record's `state` definition constrains the envelope state; other listed fields form its payload. Canonical core extensions use the kernel state and cannot redefine it. An absent optional value carries an absence reason in the relevant record’s evidence/uncertainty account. Unknown amounts, rights, available people and deadlines never become zero, false or unlimited by default. `Instant`, `Money`, `Position` and inline `SourceWatermark` import kernel types: UTC timestamps use `Z`, and money includes currency, signed minor units and exponent. Importers may normalize an explicitly offset timestamp while preserving its original bytes; ambiguous time is rejected.

Every procedure runs as an admitted `WorkOrder` through S1-C02. Its inputs are exact catalog ports plus loader-controlled context, causal allowance, lease and acceptance owner. It returns staged artifact references, attributable attempt reports, observations, checkpoint and unfinished duties through S1-C03. All commands use the kernel’s `POST /v1/commands`, including `command_id`, version, company/principal, expected revisions, causal episode and authority references. Transport supplies authenticated actor identity. Kernel `accepted` means the transition is committed and independently witnessed; it does not mean an outward effect occurred or a customer was satisfied. `durability_pending` requires lookup with the same command ID; a primary row cannot supply the missing witness.

The catalog's `record_bindings` is normative. `Intent` is the company field extension/view of canonical `IntentVersion`; it never independently owns endorsed purpose. `PersonMandate` extends canonical `Mandate`, and `AcceptanceRecord` extends canonical `EvidenceJudgment`. Their extra business fields retain exact canonical references and existing core states. `Party` resolves to `Principal`, `Artifact` to `ArtifactVersion`, `Decision` to `DecisionRecord`, `Permission` to `Grant`, and `Operation` to `OperationIntent` before any exact reference is persisted. A company label cannot create another authoritative record type. `Incident` and `Experiment` are native domain records with exact controlling `Case` refs; they do not replace Case admission. `ImprovementProposal` references `ProtectedChange` before promotion. Goal, Mission, Project and Workstream remain distinct domain intent/grouping subjects with case references for executable work; this file creates neither duplicate canonical versions nor aliases of them to Case. Full-schema consolidation must preserve these bindings.

`company.capNN.v1` names a procedure contract, not an agent. The catalog’s `implementation_mode` is a composition: deterministic functions compute or validate; durable procedures wait and route; bounded model steps interpret or generate; actual people/professionals/services perform their respective work. A procedure may use a current accepted dependency record without starting that capability recursively. Treasury and bookkeeping can reference each other’s current observations; such semantic dependency is not an executable scheduling cycle. A real work dependency cycle opens an owned exception with its blocked outcome and next discriminating action.

Catalog `dependencies` declare available capability interfaces and conditional record predicates, never a requirement that every listed capability already completed a run. CAP-07's support dependency means the product must be supportable, not that it already has a customer ticket. CAP-12 requires verified delivery/support capacity and procedure, not an earlier sale. CAP-06 can use a proposed price range; CAP-18 can report unknown demand; qualitative CAP-04 research does not require a completed CAP-29 experiment. Actual inputs needed for a proposed step remain explicit in its WorkOrder; absence can be reported only where its acceptance condition permits it.

The first-venture path is: endorse intent and assign setup custody; create a research plan and evidence inventory, including genuinely empty observation strata; investigate authorized sources and alternatives; produce a provisional product/price and support/delivery procedure; test actual capability and establish institutional readiness; obtain authority for a bounded validation offer; then gather the first actual agreement, delivery and customer observations. No prior paying customer is required to investigate or prepare. Empty evidence cannot establish demand, customer validation or live-service experience. An unvalidated commercial hypothesis can support an explicitly authorized pilot only with real capacity, required rights, remedies and resources. No grant or risk preference for such a pilot is adopted here. A demand-dependent scale decision waits for actual evidence; unknown demand is not a universal block on the first investigation.

`company.record.propose` implements the catalog's explicit seed factories. It validates typed source inputs, creates only the allowed initial state and assigns the declared custodian before admission. This supplies research plans, draft offers, campaigns, received conversations/cases, assessment protocols and operating plans; they cannot appear as unexplained inputs. It does not fabricate observed evidence or bypass endorsement. Types without a specialized state enum use `proposed/validated/superseded/restricted`: validation is a recorded domain acceptance decision, not a generic write. Every catalog entry has a distinct substantive acceptance condition and responsible acceptance role in addition to kernel checks. C06 records that role's evidence; the producer cannot make its own evidence independent by changing a label.

Ports describe typed records produced during and at the end of a procedure. An agreement can become authoritative before the sales procedure finishes, with delivery still due. Each intermediate output exposes its actual state and unfinished duties. Terminal acceptance requires the catalog's substantive condition; “waiting on another capability” does not satisfy it. The workflow stores these milestones durably so replacing a worker cannot lose a payment, accepted promise or partially delivered order.

S1-C04 owns grants, reservations, release, conflict identity and resource bounds; C05 owns validity/lineage; C06 owns evidence/acceptance; C07 owns human/external responsibility; C08 owns independent recovery; C09 presents projections. `01-contract-kernel.md` and `02-authority-recovery.md` are companion specification contracts authored separately. This file neither assigns production credentials to a capability nor duplicates their grant/release state machines.

Each work contract names a latest responsible start, due outcome and update deadline. The scheduler calculates latest start from due time minus the supported upper duration, necessary review/handoff time and declared contingency. Unknown duration has an explicit uncertainty range; a service promise cannot depend on an unsupported bound. Provider/supplier acknowledgments renew availability only for their acknowledged scope. A command timeout, overdue timer or owner silence does not create acceptance, professional approval or discharge.

## Native business authority and meaning

Implement company-owned domain record modules for intent/portfolio, product/offers, relationships/agreements, fulfillment/support, finance/procurement, people/partners, evidence/experiments, and institutional duties. These are logical domains within S1, with their own field validators and procedures. They do not require a vendor suite or microservice per domain. Existing native tools can become authoritative sources for specific fields through a versioned `DomainAuthorityBinding`: record type/field, principal, native system and ID, semantics/version, observation route, writer, effective interval and replacement rule. One authority owns a field during an interval. Imported caches declare their source watermark and cannot accept local writes to the authoritative field.

| Native distinction | Selected invariant and write boundary |
|---|---|
| Idea, intent, strategy, obligation | An `Opportunity` proposes learning; endorsed `Intent` supplies purpose; `StrategyDecision` allocates means; `Obligation` records what is owed. Advancing an idea never implies authority to sell. |
| Product specification and offer | `ProductSpec` states intended experience and testable requirements. `Offer` is a versioned seller/audience/terms/price/delivery/support promise with explicit acceptance rules. A new specification does not rewrite a sold offer. |
| Relationship, sale, receipt, fulfillment | `Conversation` preserves actual exchanges and restrictions. `SalesAgreement` records the exact accepted terms. Payment is a separate operation/effect. `Fulfillment` records actual onboarding and delivery. None substitutes for the others. |
| Ticket, remedy, grievance | `SupportCase` tracks the actual problem and update date. Remedy consumes an explicit entitlement. `GrievanceCase` retains a person’s challenge even after internal ticket closure. A second native request ID is not another entitlement. |
| Journal, bank evidence, spend permission | Internal `JournalBatch` records balanced entries and original sources; provider/bank observations establish settlements. S1-C04 supplies spend authority and holds. A journal can record an unauthorized observed movement without retrospectively authorizing it. |
| Supplier, professional, worker | `SupplierEngagement` is accepted scope/capacity/terms. `ProfessionalDetermination` is an actual competent issuer’s scoped conclusion. `PersonMandate` binds actual identity and decision rights. A catalog listing or sent packet is none of these. |
| Measure, hypothesis, decision | `MetricDefinition` fixes units/cohort/denominator/exclusions. `MetricSeries` holds observations and gaps. A causal hypothesis or endorsed choice retains its own epistemic status. |

Native financial computation uses decimal-string minor units by currency. Each `JournalEntry` has source references, an account, and exactly one nonzero debit or credit. A posted batch balances separately per currency and ledger; it is immutable and corrected through linked reversing/correcting entries. No unsupported accounting treatment, jurisdictional rule, exchange rate or balancing entry is invented. One ledger authority owns each principal/period. Bank reconciliation preserves unmatched movements, fees, timing differences, credits and disputed classifications until supported treatment exists. A source change migrates meanings without rewriting past denominators or hiding old uncertainty.

Mapping changes that reveal overlapping entitlements or new required participants follow technical R1: fence combined scope, preserve old unknown holds, invalidate obsolete unreleased preparations, reconcile new identities and establish a common release order before resuming. A projection-only rename cannot move a customer promise, money or rights into a different principal.

## Fulfillment routes and unresolved adapters

The catalog's `fulfillment_routes` names each usable procedure boundary and its remaining integration decision; every capability references its necessary routes. A selected logical module is still unimplemented. An external adapter with no admitted implementation is explicitly unavailable, and fixtures cannot change its state to available. A real person can perform a separately admitted manual route when qualified and reachable; their operation still has exact scope, authority, first-action cutoff, captured result and unknown-effect reconciliation. A task inbox alone is not such a route.

## Operating setup and bounded autonomy

An `OperatingProfile` is setup data with explicit validation, not founder facts inferred during construction. It binds actual principal and people, endorsed intent/constraints, relevant activity/location/contract facts, domain authority bindings, available integrations, support/update deadlines, consequence grants, contact/data policies, capacity/continuity arrangements, retention determinations, reporting calendar, participation plan, economic reporting periods and closure resources. Missing values are represented as unavailable operating prerequisites. They do not block writing or testing the software against fixtures, and they do block dependent real promises. No design questionnaire is required now.

Internal preparation proceeds under existing authority. Routine work can originate from accepted duties, scheduled procedures, observed failures, protected adverse samples and an endorsed discovery mandate. New expenditure, contact, publication, contracts, consequential deployment, changed identity or private-data disclosure needs its specific current grant. Human owner preferences and material exposure remain actual setup/decision inputs; software does not choose them through a profit or risk score.

Outcome sponsorship is assigned before an offer becomes available. The first receiver of necessary orphan work takes temporary protective custody under the intake mandate. It locates the existing promise sponsor, otherwise the service mandate, otherwise the designated maintenance/discovery custodian. A bounded resolver assigns provisional custody when mandates overlap. Only acknowledged transfer moves responsibility; default timer expiry activates the accepted alternate. The resolver assigns ownership, not new grants. Escalation to the founder is reserved for genuinely missing founder authority or unresolved personal ends; ordinary cross-domain diagnosis and coordination consume recorded operational labor.

Goals have a review date, next bounded discriminator, horizon and abandonment authority. The planning horizon extends only as far as current evidence, service commitments and available control support; speculative later stages retain ranges and alternatives. At review time an untouched goal is reaffirmed with evidence, narrowed, parked with a reopening trigger or abandoned with duty disposition. Each investigation’s finite causal allowance and stopping rule prevents persistence from becoming periodic rewording of the same work. Strategic rejection distinguishes inadequate execution from a contradicted value proposition using the experiment evidence, not the producer’s preference for another build.

CAP-09 separates stable identity/voice from tone adapted to first contact, ongoing relationship, failure and apology. The actual owner accepts taste and identity. Brand/name/asset-use claims require applicable rights evidence. CAP-13 produces useful material with claims, rights, audience, disclosure and version lineage. “Approved content” is still a draft for outward purposes until exact release authority exists.

CAP-18 evaluates willingness-to-pay and alternatives against delivery, support, acquisition, recurring service, failed-attempt and closure cost. `PriceProposal` records currency, amount, renewal/cancellation effects, assumptions and authority. A subsidized purpose can be explicitly endorsed while the negative contribution remains visible.

CAP-10 implements the product/service artifact and its operating procedures. Independent CAP-11 acceptance checks actual utility, process, authorization, original criteria and missing evidence. ## Customer journey, commerce and repair

CAP-14 chooses an authorized distribution channel according to audience evidence and a bounded hypothesis. Earned attention is implemented through useful content, permitted relationship/referral work and measured audience response; a channel choice does not authorize sending. Recipients, contact allocations, current suppression, claims and metadata pass the release/disclosure boundary. Actual channel result, meaningful conversation, negative reaction, opt-out and contribution are retained separately from impressions or vanity counts.

CAP-15 qualifies a real opportunity, records objections and negotiates within standing terms. A price/term departure goes to the appropriate signatory; a model cannot create apparent agreement from an unanswered proposal. Controlled acceptance records the exact offer and potential duties independently before acknowledging an order. Payment/credit conditions remain separate. CAP-30 then executes onboarding: verify the customer’s intended recipient and permitted data, provision only entitled access, deliver necessary instructions/material, and check that the customer can actually use the service. Failure creates a support/remedy duty with an update deadline.

Publishing a standing offer itself creates a declared exposure window: capacity and authority must cover the permitted acceptance route and outstanding valid terms. If an actual counterparty acceptance arrives outside the controlled route, capture it immediately and obtain the applicable competent interpretation; a software rejection cannot erase an obligation that already arose. Preserve disputed/possible duties and resolve performance or remedy. Sales marks its complete commercial outcome only after CAP-30's accepted delivery and the specified payment conditions; agreement formation remains separately visible before then.

Fulfillment executes the actual agreed digital, human or physical service through an accepted performer. For each milestone record promised outcome, window, evidence and remaining obligations. Partial receipts do not complete the entire order. Acceptance uses the agreed criteria and counterparty acknowledgment where required; a silent customer does not acquire a new discharge meaning. Recurring service generates individual due occurrences with catch-up rules appropriate to the promise, not a backlog burst of obsolete sends after reconnection.

CAP-16 retains exact communications, relationship owner, purpose restrictions, new promises, corrections and next action. Sensitive negotiation and relationships requiring a person route to a real person; a bot signature cannot impersonate that involvement. CAP-17 retrieves the sold version and actual fault, performs authorized repair/completion/refund and checks the resulting customer condition. Refund conflict identity uses entitlement and prior unknown effects, so sales retention and support cannot pay it twice. Public mistakes create an affected-audience correction proposal, not just new copy; corrections use current authority and preserve earlier consequences.

For a defect spanning offer, implementation, invoices and supplier terms, the outcome sponsor opens one coordinating case with domain work orders and an explicit final criterion: affected bills corrected, actual service repaired or remedied, claims updated and customers appropriately informed. Domains retain native methods. Individually accepted subtasks cannot close a failed journey. Recurrence reopens product, market or supplier assumptions, including an alternative that the offer should end rather than gain more features.

## Finance, suppliers, people and institutional work

CAP-19 imports actual account observations and reconciles balances, outstanding commitments, released unknown effects and restricted reserves. Money, provider allowance, slots and minutes are separate accounts. Each `Economics` report includes observed and outstanding costs, failed attempts, acquisition, service/refunds, review, maintenance, security, migration and closure. Owner minutes remain minutes unless a legitimate declared valuation is provided. Shared overhead allocation rules are explicit and reproducible; entity/currency totals are not netted without the necessary determinations and authority. Forecast revenue cannot replenish spendable cash.

The catalog's `MoneyKnowledge` and `MinutesKnowledge` are tagged values: observed amount with original evidence and coverage, estimate with attributed bounds/assumptions, or unknown with reason and responsible next check. Unknown has no amount field; observed zero requires actual evidence. Observed-revenue/cost fields prohibit the estimate variant. Reports retain known subtotals and missing categories, while early strategy can use explicit estimates or uncertainty. CAP-21 obtains an actual scoped conclusion, conditions, uncertainties, expiry and required duties from a competent issuer; changes of activity/provider evidence reopen it. Preparing or delivering the packet does not complete the requested professional work. Missing capacity invokes the accepted alternate before the latest responsible start and remains a material readiness/continuity failure when unavailable.

CAP-24 compares make/buy alternatives including discovery, contracting, evidence access, inspection, minimums, renewals, termination and replacement costs. Actual accepted engagement states deliverable, competent provider, capacity window, price ceiling, dependencies, acceptance and backup. Verify changed payee details through an independent authorized route. CAP-25 maintains reciprocal partnership terms, opportunity attribution, relationship owner, conflicts and real joint performance. CAP-26 predeclares competence/availability and assessment criteria, obtains proportionate work evidence, accounts for applicant effort and has a contestable consequential human decision. Engagement, onboarding and access are separate from selection. CAP-27’s actual person mandate is specified in [human operation](04-human-operation.md).

CAP-40 establishes the actual principal, relevant location/activity facts, signatory, account access, name/asset rights and required registrations/professional results before transacting. Each prerequisite records dependency, estimated lead-time range and its evidence, earliest/latest possible completion, due date and responsible performer. The software can prepare before revenue but cannot defer a prerequisite required before the first payment. It asserts no universal jurisdiction, legal form, licensing threshold or setup duration. The setup packet is resolved through actual authorized action, not sample values.

CAP-33’s governance decisions change only through the legitimate authority. CAP-35 tests growth against actual delivered outcomes, due-service and closure capacity, semantic labor and owner load. The 1×/2×/4× comparison retains S1’s baseline and adverse cases. New admissions shrink before mandated review or existing-duty progress is starved. Stage-specific success differs: discovery asks whether a question was usefully resolved; validation requires real need; delivery requires fulfilled promises; maintenance requires service and sustainable carrying cost. CAP-36 pause withdraws specified new-work authority, cancels avoidable renewals, and retains active support, data, payment and reporting duties. CAP-37 follows independently durable recovery/frontier and current authority checks, including delayed first-send and unknown-effect handling. CAP-41 succession has accepted scope, actual authority/competence/access, a usable original-evidence package and rehearsal of unfamiliar duties. CAP-39 closure inventories accepted and potential promises, refunds, service, workers/suppliers, renewals, filings, records/data and grievance resources; performs each real disposition; and records surviving unresolved scope. `closed-with-residuals` has actual custodian, funding and review dates. It cannot be advertised as no remaining responsibility.

CAP-42 provides noncustomer-inclusive intake without unnecessary product access, proportionate identity checks, reasons, deadlines, actual competent escalation and funded remedy. A complaint about the current custodian routes outside the disputed decision/incentive dependency. Repeated processing may be clustered, but each person’s standing, deadline, evidence and alternate survive. After product closure the reachable intake and competent residual custodian continue as long as required duties remain. Issuer limits bound a delegate’s release power, not the person’s rights or a professional adjudication.

## Integrated conformance and source-question trace

These are unexecuted implementation acceptance cases. Each must inspect actual effect/delivery evidence or a fixture explicitly marked synthetic. A production acceptance cannot cite a synthetic professional, willing customer or bank balance as if real.

Trace these cases to [the risk register](../../registers/risks.json), version `a6598d3`: especially RISK-10–14 and RISK-17 for owner capacity, intent, institutional capacity, redress, business/human burden and interacting ventures. The catalog links affected risk IDs; this adds specification obligations without closing any risk or reproducing its likelihood claims.

1. **Successful ordinary business:** research an actual authorized need, produce/test the complete experience, establish actual readiness, publish within grant, accept a sale, complete onboarding/delivery, handle a legitimate support issue, reconcile books and retain required service duties. Fail if any handoff alone is counted as fulfilled work.
2. **Unsponsored combined defect:** introduce a correct local ticket closure, incorrect invoice and misleading offer. With founder absent, protective custody and sponsorship resolution produce actual cross-domain correction within existing mandates; record all human coordination.
3. **Noisy complaint and urgent true defect:** preserve individual rights, bounded investigation and continuing legitimate service across repeated capacity resets. False blanket rejection, unexplained broad freeze or due-service starvation fails.
4. **Actual ending:** stop a losing venture during unknown refund effect, supplier departure and noncustomer privacy complaint. Require evidence of performance or competent legitimate disposition, usable successor package and continuing funded redress. A truthful list of incapacity is a failed operating outcome, not false completion.
5. **Business value and shifted burden:** run an operationally successful losing case and a profitable case with excessive customer/applicant/contractor coordination. Report distinct economic, usefulness and individual-burden failures; no combined score rescues them.
