# Knowledge, context, evidence and evaluation

**Contract:** KNOWLEDGE-1.0 · **architecture:** S1.0 · **authoring base:** `cb1eac4`, with kernel proof amendment `e9201fa` incorporated · 2026-09-12. **Status:** Phase F specification, unimplemented and awaiting independent review. This owns S04's 107 questions. [The machine contract](work-knowledge-contracts.json) supplies per-question answers, typed relationships, command guards, decisions, planned modules and unexecuted tests. It does not claim current model accuracy, reliable retrieval, preserved human competence or an empirical architecture winner.

The [kernel](01-contract-kernel.md), [authority/recovery](02-authority-recovery.md), [company catalog](capabilities.json), [human operation](04-human-operation.md), and [work/skills](05-work-agents-skills.md) control identity, purpose, authority, acceptance and actual performance. [L05](../../research/L05-memory.md) separates retention, retrieval, correct use and temporal validity. [L06](../../research/L06-evaluation.md) separates output, process, account and business outcomes and records evaluator fallibility. These are bounded source findings and design precedents, not experiments repeated here. [G](../reviews/G-acceptance-protocol.md) and its EAS-R1 comparisons remain frozen.

## 1. Canonical knowledge and distinct memory

Use canonical `IntentVersion`, `Observation`, `Claim`, `ReasonRecord`, `DecisionRecord`, `ArtifactVersion`, `EvidenceBaseVersion`, `EvidenceJudgment`, `ValidityEpoch` and `Continuation`. The record bindings in the machine contract add fields/views or exact linking records; they do not create another truth, approval or completion authority. Every query and mutation carries company/principal, authenticated scope, purpose and current restrictions. Canonical content and prior meanings are versioned; derived indexes and summaries can be rebuilt. A signature proves attribution/integrity under its trust root, not factual truth or complete capture.

| Memory job | Selected representation and write responsibility |
|---|---|
| Working memory | Current WorkOrder plus loader ContextManifest and bounded temporary scratch artifacts. C03 proposes scratch; it cannot promote scratch to company knowledge. Expiry follows work/retention policy. |
| Episodic history | Attributed attempts, messages, observations, decisions and artifact versions. Protected capture records failures and gaps; a narrative summary links these records rather than replacing them. |
| Semantic knowledge | Company `KnowledgeEntry` links scoped canonical claims, supporting/opposing sources, valid/system times and current applicability. The knowledge steward proposes; competent C06 judgment determines accepted scope. |
| Procedural memory | Exact WorkflowDefinition, InstructionBundle and SkillVersion with tests, provenance, license, version and owner. Repetition does not turn an unreviewed habit into a procedure. |
| Intent and person preferences | Authenticated endorsed statements, explicit corrections, relevant taste examples and consented ParticipationPlan. Preserve original scope/effective time; do not infer personality, health, general intelligence or unrelated habits. |
| Search/context projections | Authorized lexical chunks, cached retrieval results and summaries with complete known derivation, omissions and expiry. C05 owns current-use eligibility; indexes are never authority. |

`SourceRecord` identifies the actual publisher/issuer or captured origin, source type, publication/observation/received times, incentives or known conflicts, retrieval/capture route, version, scope, completeness limits and verification owner. Unknown authorship or date stays unknown. An official or interested source may supply useful evidence within its scope; it does not become independent merely because it is primary. `KnowledgeQuestion` records what is unknown, why it matters, affected decisions, search already attempted, resolution predicate, custodian, latest check and stopping rule. “Not found,” “not searched,” “inaccessible,” “withheld by policy” and “insufficient evidence” are distinct dispositions.

KnowledgeEntry does not duplicate the company catalog's identity. This contract extends its source/claim/current-validity relationships and maintenance procedure. Lessons use the existing `LearningProposal`: anecdote, supported pattern and causal claim have different evidence requirements. Before retaining a lesson, state scope, rival explanation and falsifier. Rejected approaches remain searchable with their context and known limits; one failure cannot become a universal prohibition. A newcomer loads an accepted purpose/role packet and the current relevant procedures, then follows exact originals for unfamiliar or contested duties.

## 2. Context assembly and information loss

C05 implements a deterministic loader around existing `context.capture` and `context.publish`. The request names exact WorkOrder, purpose, destination contracts, requested sources and optional prior manifest; the server discovers and records actual delivered inputs. The caller's claimed read list is not trusted. The manifest includes source/capture/artifact versions, permission/purpose/validity epochs, transformations, selection reasons, byte/token allocations, actual source spans, omissions and complete known native ancestry. Model-provider traffic is itself disclosure, including tool stdout, error messages, fixtures and resumed history.

Mandatory context consists of the exact task purpose, outcome/acceptance criteria, applicable exclusions and procedure, current scoped authority references, known material contradictions, partial outputs, unresolved effects/duties and the next action. Optional background is added only when it can change the task. The loader reserves room for results and control messages using the admitted runtime's supported limits. With opaque token accounting it enforces conservative documented input-byte/launch limits and reports unknown internal usage; it cannot claim exact subcall capacity. Nothing quietly truncates a required promise, warning or permission boundary.

At capacity, remove irrelevant/redundant background first, then use attributed scoped excerpts or a validated summary. Exact consequential amounts, identities, deadlines, exclusions and material unresolved facts remain references with accessible original spans. If necessary material still cannot fit or be used reliably, split the work into bounded steps, load a smaller relevant scope or select another qualified runtime. That affects admission; it does not authorize broader disclosure or silently drop conditions. Longer accepted context is not proof of utilization, as L05's historical studies illustrate.

`ContextTransformation` records input/output refs, algorithm/model/prompt/version, spans included/excluded, intended preservation contract and known loss. A summary carries `SummaryLoss` entries for omitted alternatives, qualifiers, temporal relations, source uncertainty and unsupported reconstruction. Critical preservation checks use deterministic exact-field comparison plus an independent semantic challenge where wording matters. Compression passes only for its declared use; a customer-quote summary cannot replace the original in a taste or disputed-promise decision.

Native sessions bind exact AgentInstance/WorkOrder and previous manifest lineage. Resume can reuse a session only when every possible retained input is allowed for current recipients and purpose, current restrictions hold and the profile exposes a sufficient ancestry account. Narrowing today's tools cannot remove yesterday's data. Otherwise close the opaque route and create fresh context from the portable Continuation; record lost uncaptured influence. Captured original results retain their producer identity, while live regeneration becomes another attempt charged to the same episode.

Context outputs follow the kernel's serialization against current invalidation/deletion epochs. Source invalidation while a worker runs restricts late derivatives and dependent effects. Local snapshots can support bounded historical inspection only under current access; they cannot claim current validity. A missing source can leave a defensible limited account with an explicit reconstruction gap, but never a fabricated quotation or complete causal explanation.

## 3. Retrieval, ranking, freshness and contradiction

Select a permission-filtered lexical baseline: exact-ID/field queries over admitted control metadata first, PostgreSQL full-text indexing for explicitly admitted nonpersonal/nonconfidential material, and bounded in-memory lexical scanning for protected text. Public availability alone does not establish eligibility for a persistent plaintext index. PostgreSQL 18 documents `tsvector`, query parsing and lexical/proximity ranking; its documentation explicitly leaves application relevance to the application. GIN supplies an indexed text-search path. These primitives support the implementation choice, not our authorization, recall or usefulness claims. [Text-search controls](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/textsearch-controls.html), [index types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/textsearch-indexes.html) (checked 2026-09-12).

The initial indexer preserves an authorized original-integrity reference, record revision, byte/span boundaries, language/parser configuration and source permissions; chunks follow semantic paragraph/section boundaries with exact links to surrounding context. Protected values and low-entropy plaintext digests are not copied into an unprotected metadata index. Original text remains independently addressable. Exact identifiers, dates, account facts and active obligations use typed queries; phrase search handles wording-sensitive retrieval. Language configuration is explicit, with a simple lexical fallback and visible unsupported-language limits. There is no required vector service or knowledge graph. Add a representation only after a matched retrieval/use/cost comparison demonstrates a needed benefit, preserving the flat baseline.

Retrieval proceeds in this order: authenticate and compute the allowed principal/purpose/recipient/validity set; resolve explicit IDs and mandatory task refs; search only the eligible persistent index or authorized protected-text scan scope; rank candidates; load permitted spans; then recheck current restrictions at delivery/publication. The model never receives unauthorized snippets, counts, document titles or global ranking explanations that reveal excluded parties. Incomplete index frontiers or source watermarks are surfaced. A query against all records followed by client-side filtering is prohibited.

Rank by exact subject/identifier match, task-applicable record class, lexical relevance and source/time applicability within that class. Include linked corrections and material contrary claims even when ordinary lexical rank is low. Freshness is a use predicate or explicit warning, not a popularity bonus; repeated retrieval never renews verification. The retrieval profile pins configuration/weights/tie-breaking, initially record ID for otherwise equal candidates. Numeric relevance is a candidate-ordering aid, not probability of truth or a universal company priority score.

The initial budget inspects at most 20 candidate metadata records and loads at most eight source chunks per retrieval step, with bytes and elapsed time charged to the WorkOrder. Mandatory exact references are resolved separately and still consume context capacity. These are bounded starting policies to test, not measured recall limits. Exhaustion records the query, eligible scope, examined candidates, selected refs, omitted candidates and unresolved question; a further search requires a named expected information gain within the same causal allowance. The query may widen legitimately, but cannot repeatedly relabel the same search as new work.

Current-product, market, external-rule or uncertain niche facts require appropriate fresh source work when relevant; stable admitted arithmetic and exact existing records do not need web search. External search uses an authorized query/destination and the integration disclosure contract. Private context cannot supply arbitrary search terms through a “read-only” tool. Retrieval text stays attributed source content, separate from authenticated instruction. Citation checking resolves the exact source and tests claim support; a plausible URL or another model repeating a claim does not suffice.

Contradictions first compare entity, time, conditions, units, source authority and interpretation. Different scopes may both be valid; an authenticated correction can supersede a designated field through its legitimate update route. Otherwise retain a `ContradictionSet` and scoped competing claims. A competent assessor records settlement, qualification or continued uncertainty with reasons. New contradictory evidence or missed validity review invalidates dependent current judgments where required; it does not declare the historical source false. Protected due service proceeds on unaffected valid facts while the relevant inference waits or narrows.

## 4. Maintenance, corrections and deliberate forgetting

**Storage follows sensitivity and deletion scope, never field size.** All unrestricted human/model/source text is protected by default, including titles, reasons, queries, scope descriptions and errors. Numeric, enum and relationship values can also disclose salary, health, identity or confidential business information. Each field and derived combination needs a versioned C05 storage classification, C07 retention/duty determination where required, purpose and descendant-copy inventory. A closed type or opaque ID is not proof of harmlessness. Only a field-specific admitted control-metadata allowlist may remain plaintext; unclassified fields use the protected path. An automated classifier cannot grant an exception.

Domain schemas describe the authorized decoded payload. Use the kernel's existing encrypted durable-blob option for sensitive canonical record bytes, including short values: validate inside the trusted current-access process, encrypt under the applicable deletion/retention scope before persistent serialization, and put the exact encrypted-blob reference and ciphertext digest in GroupBody. Record postimages, JSONB projections, event payloads, statement/error logs, tracing, WAL, indexes and backups must not receive a second plaintext copy. A plaintext digest of a guessable value is itself protected; integrity references outside the scope cover ciphertext. This is a storage binding of the same canonical record, not another authority record or a reason to accept unvalidated ciphertext. Atomic reference/durability and key recovery remain the kernel/C08 contracts.

Some kernel joins and proof metadata necessarily expose stable identifiers, relationships, counts or timing. Their admitted allowlist must state which information persists, its exact physical copies and retention/deletion scope. If any necessary plaintext projection contains personal/confidential information, its WAL, replicas, indexes and backups remain covered descendants. Restrict access immediately, record the residual, and keep the corresponding deletion obligation incomplete until demonstrated purge or authorized expiry of every required copy. Crypto-erasure of a content key cannot claim to erase surviving plaintext linkage. If the requested operation's deletion commitment cannot tolerate that retention, do not admit the operation; resolve the actual duty/retention conflict without inventing sector law.

Persistent PostgreSQL lexical terms/snippets are permitted only for the admitted nonprotected class. C05 decrypts protected material only after current principal/purpose/recipient filtering and performs bounded lexical scanning or an ephemeral in-memory index. No plaintext protected query cache, term index, temporary database table or disk spill is allowed. Exact permitted metadata narrows the corpus first. Scan byte/time/memory bounds and unexamined sources are explicit; reaching them produces an incomplete search or a smaller next query, not an assertion that no relevant source exists. The profile's measured recall/use and operator cost determine whether this baseline is viable for the actual corpus.

Runtime admission must account for process memory, crash dumps, swap, diagnostic output, worker stdout, export and live key holders. Unknown persistent-copy behavior disqualifies that runtime for protected material. Structural logs contain approved codes and minimum scoped identifiers; sensitive diagnostic detail uses the encrypted capture path. Deletion fences further loads and publication, retires cached/ephemeral representations and closes or reauthorizes affected processes under C08. Claims of physical erasure or key destruction require actual evidence; merely requesting shutdown or restarting an application does not establish it. Offline exports and resumed native contexts retain their existing continuing deletion obligations.

The knowledge custodian owns bounded semantic maintenance: expiring prerequisites, source/schema changes, failed searches, newly discovered classes, unsupported repetition and correction propagation. Refresh by consequence, volatility and dependency use, not by re-reading all history on a fixed timer. A material active prerequisite receives a review deadline and actual responsible provider; overdue unverified evidence loses current eligibility for its dependent use. Low-stakes historical material can remain attributed and qualified. Time passing alone cannot renew a claim.

Forgetting has distinct operations: exclude from routine retrieval, supersede current meaning, archive, redact, or erase according to the actual retention/determination policy. `ForgetRequest` links the legitimate instruction or DeletionScope, exact sources/purpose, known derivative closure, cutoff, owner and verification. C05/ C08 enforce the continuing restriction epoch and isolated rejoin rules; the domain request cannot weaken them. Late summaries, exports, offline copies and restored indexes remain within the continuing scope. Unknown provider-held copies are recorded and follow the actual available rights/retention route; the software cannot certify erasure it cannot observe.

Promotion into durable knowledge requires a stated reusable need, source rights, scope, expiry/review and owner. Scratch remains scratch unless accepted. Memory size is controlled by compact indexes, purposeful retention, retired duplicate procedures and scoped views; deletion is not a hidden performance optimization that removes accountability. Historical evidence needed for a lawful duty and a current deletion demand requires an actual competent determination, narrow restriction and recorded residual obligation. No universal retention duration is inferred here.

Corrections preserve original words and effective time, classify changed intent versus corrected fact, and identify dependent procedures, claims, summaries and current work. An understanding check uses a new bounded case or a direct critical-field check; storing the correction alone is insufficient. Test that a later worker uses the corrected value, rejects an obsolete permissive snapshot and still reconstructs the older decision's limited context. A newcomer or affected person can open a knowledge challenge without inheriting authority to suspend the company or reopen settled personal values.

## 5. Protected evidence and account reconstruction

`RawCapture` preserves the declared observed bytes/media/digest, source/endpoint/account identity, capture interval, sequence, omissions and access/retention. Transport credentials are excluded through structural capture rules and referenced by generation; that exclusion is itself recorded. Never describe a redacted capture as every original wire byte. The integration `IntegrationObservation` points to capture, exact adapter/connection/invocation, parsed Observation refs, native IDs/cursors, transport result and gaps. Native status and parser output remain attributed observations until independent reconciliation supports the relevant outcome.

The protected evidence closure covers capture adapter, transport/parser/schema, normalization, source/semantic mappings, sample selection, test fixture, metric definition, denominator, evaluator code/model/prompt, labels and acceptance rule. `EvidenceBaseVersion` pins these transitive refs and common trust roots. C06's capture and selection paths are outside the evaluated producer's write authority. A producer cannot choose all adverse samples, delete failed attempts, alter expected answers or activate its new evaluator. Merely using a second model or service account does not establish independence when its evidence and incentives share roots.

Changed/corrupted dependencies trigger C05 invalidation and mark affected judgments stale/contested before their next consequential use. The graph stores reverse dependencies and a verified complete traversal/index frontier; a delayed background traversal cannot be the sole admission guard. Missing closure stops the dependent acceptance, while raw unaffected evidence and ordinary permitted work remain available. Revalidation uses current competent ownership and new independent evidence, not the old confident summary. Tests corrupt an extractor, denominator, label set and shared parser while keeping the producer artifact unchanged.

`AccountReconstruction` is a derived, permission-filtered view of an explicit prior witnessed Position and source coverage. Its current view binds canonical BusinessAccount fields, including inline source watermarks and omissions. It reconstructs original intent/decision, admitted work, attempts, actual observations, partial outputs, independent judgments, effects, costs and surviving duties. Captured sequence does not establish complete causation. `ReasonRecord` keeps endorsed reason, executor report, causal hypothesis and unknown influence distinct; hidden model reasoning is neither demanded nor claimed available.

Separate reproducibility promises: **record reconstruction** from surviving artifacts; **captured-response replay** with external effects simulated; **live rerun** under new current grants; **distribution replication** across controlled stochastic trials. A replay never resends a past payment/contact or overrides current deletion restrictions. Pin controllable model/runtime/configuration/tool/skill/context/dataset/evaluator versions, input state, allocation, attempts and actual observations. Unknown provider internals and unavailable versions remain limits. A reproducible false measurement is still false.

Accounts include refused, abandoned, timed-out, unobserved and failed work, plus known missing inputs/intervals. Independent ingress/work/resource counts challenge the export denominator. A gap is not inactivity. Human-facing summaries preserve critical differences and expose originals within access limits. Evaluate reconstruction using an investigator unfamiliar with the run, including deleted content and planted unobserved influences; success is a correct bounded account with honest limits, not a fluent full story.

## 6. Evaluation contracts at every level

`EvaluationPlan` is registered before seeing treatment results: subject/version, requirement/claim, unit/population, intended use, outcome and process criteria, forbidden outcomes, comparison, selection/assignment, resources, evaluator/labels, independence roots, trial/reset rules, stopping/analysis and reopening. `EvaluationRun` records every selected unit and attempt, including noncompletion, raw observations, cost/latency and protocol deviations. `Review` links a competent reviewer's attributable findings and limitations. Canonical EvidenceJudgment owns accepted scope; EvaluationRun or Review has no independent release authority. Its proposed state is unfinished assessment; inconclusive is a completed assessment that cannot establish its subject predicate; rejected is an adverse judgment; contested means disputed evidence, not automatic rejection. Later reassessment creates a witnessed revision with historical reasons retained. Acceptance of an uncertainty report establishes only that exact report criterion. Product, launch, capacity and effect gates require their exact current accepted subject/version/predicate bindings and complete evidence closure.

| Subject | Concrete evaluation and required separation |
|---|---|
| Model/runtime | Same scoped tasks/inputs/resources with exact profile; correctness/abstention, useful completion, disclosure and variation. A leaderboard cannot qualify company authority. |
| Prompt/instruction | Stable cases plus instruction conflicts and injected source text; test outcomes and actual adherence separately, including a sham wording change. |
| Tool/adapter | Valid, malformed, forbidden and ambiguous effects; independently observe destination state, parameters, retries, descendants and disclosure. Syntax success is insufficient. |
| Skill | Applicability selection, correct use, stale examples, source/license obligations, composition and selection overhead. Compare a direct procedure with the same outcome criteria. |
| Agent instance/template | Bounded useful result, honest unsupported facts, depth/allowance, tool/context restrictions and cessation; heartbeat and output volume are not progress. |
| Handoff/message | A new worker performs unfamiliar next work using exact constraints, partials and unknowns; test lost/duplicate acknowledgment and a harmful omitted qualifier. |
| Step/run/workflow | End-to-end result and process, partial failure, replay/migration, resource limits and real compensation; local accepted subtasks cannot hide a failed whole. |
| Artifact | Requirements, actual usability/fit, errors, rights and source support, using rendered/functional evidence where relevant. Taste uses the legitimate human criterion. |
| Decision | Quality against available evidence, alternatives, mandate and expressed values at decision time; judge separately from lucky or delayed outcomes. |
| Project/venture | Actual beneficiary use, accepted delivery, rework, stage-specific learning, carrying and closure costs; no mandatory positive revenue for an endorsed subsidy. |
| Business/service | Complete discovery-to-closure paths, customer acceptance, contribution, duty progress and shifted human burden; profitable harm and competent losses remain separate failures. |
| Safety/responsibility | Exact invariant/duty behavior under adverse cases, plus ordinary authorized service and genuine rights progress; blanket refusal cannot pass usefulness. |
| Attention/competence | Actual minutes, interrupts, adverse evidence exposure, delayed unfamiliar transfer/intervention and burden under the consented human protocol; attendance/confidence/agreement cannot establish competence. |
| Evaluator/account | Known-defect detection, clean-case acceptance, source/denominator completeness, blind/reranked judgments, drift and investigator reconstruction. The checker itself has an independent acceptance owner. |

Standards vary with the exact consequence and claim. Deterministic invariant checks always run at their enforcement boundary. Reversible low-impact transformations may use deterministic validation and sampled outcome review under an admitted acceptance policy. Material effect prerequisites, professional determinations and protected changes require their specified competent independent evidence. The producer may supply self-checks and answer factual questions, but cannot become sole independent acceptor by changing role labels.

Review execution records inspected evidence, actual commands/observations, findings, omissions, review duration and deciding identity. An always-pass reviewer must fail the known-defect control. Review findings are not silently overridden; an authorized owner may accept a permitted residual defect through an explicit scoped disposition, preserving dissent and expiry. Nobody can waive an unavailable lawful mandate, required professional result, core invariant or independently witnessed authority by clicking “override.” Legitimate taste disagreement can remain disagreement while the actual taste owner chooses.

## 7. Cases, trials, independence and negative controls

Maintain four versioned pools: fixed regression cases; fresh eligible production samples; withheld challenge cases; and adversarial sequences. Fixed pool revisions create a new baseline and preserve overlapping old cases for a bridge comparison. Production sampling selects from eligible intake before outcome is known, records denominator/exclusions and retains abandoned/unobservable units. Cases requiring private data obey the same purpose/recipient rules; synthetic cases remain labeled and cannot establish actual customers, professional availability or fulfillment.

Each plan states whether variation is acceptable. Deterministic contracts expect exact semantic results under the declared inputs; creative output can vary while meeting use/taste constraints; consequence bytes and grants never vary after authorization. Initial stochastic diagnostics use five attributable trials per selected case when the authorized budget permits, with reset/seed recorded where actually supported. This is a defect-discovery default, not a reliability certificate. If fewer run, report the shortfall. Qualification or improvement claims predeclare a sample plan matched to effect size/uncertainty and actual task strata; a budget too small for the claim leaves it unresolved.

Retain all trials, not best-of-many. Distinguish any-success, all-success, per-attempt, per-request and per-fulfilled-duty rates. Pair comparable candidate/baseline cases and randomize order where feasible; preserve task and period clusters. Report uncertainty per endpoint and stratum. Exact binomial inference is used only when its independence assumptions are justified; correlated sessions, customers or outages require a cluster-aware declared method or descriptive limits. A moving baseline requires bridged common cases and explicit changed populations, not a clean month-to-month percentage without qualification.

Negative controls include a sham change expected not to improve the endpoint, an unaffected outcome, and a negative claim with no supporting evidence. Positive detection controls inject a known defect, fake citation, skipped authorization, omitted failed attempt, changed denominator, poisoned skill or dropped promise. Clean valid controls detect indiscriminate rejection. Counterbalance presentation order and hide irrelevant producer identity from comparative judges when feasible. Where process/rights must be checked, supply that evidence in a separate phase rather than blindly concealing a required fact.

Independent perspective means different relevant evidence, method, measurement or competent human grounding under separate protected authority. First prefer the cheapest observation that can discriminate: actual destination state, a deterministic counterexample, original customer evidence or a scoped professional assessment. A different model family can add a tested viewpoint but is not a proof of independent error. Record shared training/provider, parser, source, rubric, labels, capture owner and incentives where known; unknown roots remain unknown. Measure joint false acceptance on controlled defects before treating panel agreement as extra assurance.

On disagreement, preserve each finding, identify whether scope/value, factual evidence, method or uncertainty differs, and obtain the cheapest discriminating test or legitimate decision. Difficult taste or forecast questions may remain unresolved without implying reviewer failure. The producer cannot select only agreeable judges. A checker drifting toward agreement is challenged with independently held defects, swapped identities/order, original-source review and false-positive controls; invalidated evaluator dependencies withdraw affected acceptance until reassessed.

## 8. Drift, reopening, economics and comparators

Reopen on material contrary outcome, changed source/model/prompt/skill/tool/schema, missed expiry, new applicable duty, failed original-source reconstruction, a naive credible challenge, or an alternative that could materially improve the purpose. C05 creates a current validity consequence; C02 assigns bounded investigation; C01/C07 decide only within their actual domains. A newcomer can obtain a reasoned disposition under S1's protected discovery and ordinary challenge rules. This does not introduce C2's compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction.

At review, distinguish still supported, qualified, obsolete, contradicted and unmeasured. Reaffirming a settled choice requires current reasoning about its premises, not its age or popularity. Values/exclusions deliberately settled by their owner retain the actual amendment route; new factual contradiction remains reportable without silently replacing those ends. Repeated equivalent challenges share investigation work and the same causal allowance while preserving each person's rights/deadlines. Every inquiry has a next discriminator and stopping rule, preventing both calcification and perpetual reconsideration.

Monitor gradual drift on fixed and fresh cases: acceptance error by stratum, omitted constraints, false refusal, selection cost, missing-source rate, correction/forgetting reuse, review minutes and customer outcomes. A metric-owner alert threshold is registered with a baseline and actual response authority before automated intervention. A sudden known invariant breach invalidates the affected scope immediately under policy; a small uncertain trend triggers bounded discrimination, not broad self-directed suspension. Counterfactual evaluator checks can reveal an instrument that stopped responding even while its average score looks stable.

Retain S0 with native tools, competent manual release and comparable external obligations, resources, recovery and owner allowance. Preserve G's four EAS-R1 tests separately: each design's own rights; business effectiveness at matched external obligations/resources/information/service; case versus shared-hypothesis representation with identical C2 rights; and fully adaptive A/S1 with protected discovery plus scheduled cross-domain review but no compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction. Do not force equal internal deadlines that erase the treatment or score lawful S1 deferral as violation of C2's rule. No ontology, blackboard or agent count receives credit by existing.

Evaluation itself consumes capture, labeling, source investigation, reviewer/professional time, owner attention, compute, maintenance and closure resources. Register the expected information value and budget before running it, then report actual cost and foregone useful work. Protected checks and due service cannot be silently starved to improve scores. If equivalent business outcomes and responsibility can be achieved with less total burden using simpler production, retrieval or review, narrow/remove the extra mechanism through the normal protected change route. Neither an elegant account nor safe permanent refusal rescues unaffordable or ineffective operation.

## 9. Implementation boundaries and conformance

Implement lexical indexing/retrieval, source/knowledge lifecycle, manifest assembly and forgetting under `system/packages/context`; protected capture/dependency closure, evaluation and accounts under `system/packages/evidence`; shared validators under `system/packages/contracts`. C09 surfaces consume witnessed projections with current access. External research/provider adapters remain in the integration track. Exact optional vector/graph systems, additional judge vendors and general model-memory products are not required dependencies of this baseline.

Conformance requires ordinary successful source use, actual useful work and the following adverse variations: same promise under different entities/times; a stale correction ranked first; a false citation repeated by two judges; deletion during late summary publication; a private term in an outward search query; hidden resumed context; a passing artifact with unauthorized process; a corrupted shared parser/denominator; an always-pass checker; a summary dropping an approval-changing exception; missing failed-run exports; and a restored index before current restrictions. Each test specifies exact expected state, evidence and allowed continuing work. Storage tests place short personal strings, a salary, a health enum and identity linkage in fields, errors and corrections; inspect GroupBody/JSONB, WAL, indexes, logs, replicas and restored backups for unintended plaintext. Then revoke the content scope, fence live holders and verify which copies remain. A remaining admitted linkage must keep deletion visibly incomplete; a permitted nonprotected search must still succeed. These are required future boundary tests, not results obtained here.

Capacity tests grow eligible/irrelevant/hostile corpora and skill libraries while holding relevant questions fixed, measuring both retrieval and correct use, false exclusion, latency, disclosure, source upkeep and owner work. Historical benchmark results do not determine our pass thresholds. Longitudinal owner/business trials and real unavailable-professional/provider scenarios remain authorized future evaluations with actual admission. Full-schema consolidation must resolve every field/view binding and executable validator; this specification does not turn the proposed module paths or unexecuted protocols into implemented coverage.
