## 1. Integration identity and admission

S1-C03 requests work; C04 alone issues consequence authority; C05 supplies current disclosure/validity; C06 captures and assesses; C08 reconciles; C07 owns external standing and actual service; C09 projects status. ## 2. Invocation, capture and truthful outcomes

The caller proposes arguments; deterministic code resolves protected account, payee, recipient, amount, target resource, claim form and scope from current authoritative records. User-supplied strings and source text cannot replace these trusted parameters. The resulting exact bytes and interpretation versions enter OperationIntent. C04 reserves money, capacity, potential duties and business conflicts before disclosure or external mutation. Gate expiry applies to the controlled transport event described in 02; neither an HTTP status nor a native exit code proves fulfillment.

RawCapture/v1 is an ArtifactVersion with protected metadata: invocation/attempt identity; direction; endpoint and native account; media type; original length and digest; captured interval; capture sequence and missing ranges; header allowlist; credential generation; encrypted blob refs; retention/access/deletion scope. Authorization headers and cookies are omitted by structural field selection before persistence, with an omission marker; business content is retained under encryption and access policy. Capture occurs before the result is offered to a model. Streaming sequence gaps, truncated output, parser failure and missing final messages remain explicit. Exact request business bytes are reconstructible from the committed envelope; no claim that redacted credential bytes were retained. Free text is protected by default; numeric/enumerated fields and relationship metadata also require field-specific sensitivity/retention classification. Canonical sensitive record bytes use the kernel encrypted durable-blob path; GroupBody/JSONB, WAL, indexes, logs and backups must not retain accidental plaintext duplicates. Persisted full-text indexes are limited to explicitly admitted nonpersonal/nonconfidential material; protected content uses permission-filtered bounded in-memory lexical scanning with no persistent terms/snippets/query cache or spill. Any necessary plaintext relational projection has explicit purge/retention accounting and keeps deletion incomplete while it survives. Parser output is a derived Observation with the raw capture and parser version as dependencies. A hostile HTML page, e-mail, tool response or test log is data, including text that impersonates a system instruction.

Default external attempt: one request, HTTP/1.1, fresh socket, no automatic redirect/retry/pooling, 30-second all-buffer gate window, request ≤1 MiB, response ≤8 MiB, decompressed response ≤8 MiB, depth-limited parse. A profile may set a shorter window or smaller bounds. A larger supported operation must be a separately reviewed profile with explicit reservations and the same gate semantics, never an invisible library default. Read retries: at most three total attempts in the causal episode with backoff 1/4 seconds and deadline check; observed 429 Retry-After defers without exceeding the deadline. Mutations retry only on positive proven-unsent evidence or a provider-specific current idempotency contract. Unknown effects retain holds and reconciliation work; changing tool or provider is not an escape from the business identity.

Circuit breaker opens after three transport failures in five minutes, probes once after five minutes using a harmless admitted read, and resumes only on valid response/profile. Authentication failure, schema drift, unexpected redirect/destination, charge mode change or capture corruption quarantines immediately. A refusal records the unmet authority/standing/capacity predicate; a provider error records what was attempted; a timeout after possible send is unknown. All consume their actual causal resources. One provider's reported success and another's disagreement form a contested evidence set, not last-writer-wins truth.

## 4. Communication and independent continuity

Authority covers exact sender identity, principal, recipients, business purpose, allowed claim/content form, language, time window, volume and disclosure. Destination-clean context prevents some disclosure; it does not prevent an invented guarantee, price, promise or credential claim. Routine automatic replies therefore render deterministic approved content forms with typed slots resolved from current accepted facts and permitted uncertainty statements. Free text is a draft requiring accountable human review or a separately admitted constrained content procedure. The model cannot change protected slots or invent claims through a stylistic field. Meaning-changing translation and attachments receive the same gate. Contradictory evidence disables the affected claim form, not all legitimate replies.

A useful routine example is a received support case: send its receipt number, actual monitored response window and the independent grievance address using a fixed form; later send a verified delivery link from the accepted ArtifactVersion. Neither says a human personally reviewed it. Machine involvement is disclosed whenever the interaction determination requires it and never falsely denied; representation and accountability remain with the identified company/mandate. No claim of guaranteed returns, legal compliance, professional qualification, completed payment, safety certification or customer consent is permitted without its exact current predicate. Company voice is a versioned style policy subordinate to these facts.

Standing grants may authorize these bounded routine messages without per-message human intervention. New audience/offer, discretionary concession, binding promise, sensitive disclosure or changed claim requires the applicable consequence decision. The actual human review time is reserved and measured; exhausting it pauses new discretionary outreach before it crowds out due service. Default planning allocation is at most 20 first-contact messages/day and 100 routine replies/day per company, further constrained by consent, actual provider/account limits and owner allowance; these are design limits, not authorization to contact anyone. Complaint/suppression signals have a separately reserved intake path, and an attacker cannot silently erase a duty by labelling it spam.

IC-HUMAN continuity admission requires a primary custodian and an accepted alternate with independent login/recovery/MFA and an understood mandate. They receive an encrypted, dated continuity export containing due duties, unresolved effects, standing, authoritative source pointers, contacts and offline instructions, but not stale grant permits. Test an actual acknowledgement and a rehearsal of a benign request before declaring the route live. A customer can complain without approval from the actor complained about. Custodian compensation, availability window, response capacity and removal/replacement are explicit resource obligations. No assumed free lawyer, accountant, proxy founder or perpetual on-call volunteer.

## 6. Capacity, money and scheduling

ResourceAccount and Reservation are kernel records; Budget is their composite, not a new ledger. Reserve each currency/principal separately. Keep subscription entitlement, provider quota buckets, native-job launches, compute, disk, network, paid calls, customer delivery capacity and human attention as separate units. Money cannot be inferred from token estimates, and an included subscription call still uses scarce capacity. Actual owner cap, operating hours and authorized infrastructure/professional spend are required setup data; the $400 subscription baseline is an assumption from L10, not an infrastructure budget or permission to spend.

Capacity policy CP1 initializes at one native N job and one X job concurrently, with separate reserved service/grievance/recovery work. Increasing concurrency is a reviewed CapacityPlan revision after measured resource and provider allowance evidence. Dispatch uses earliest real obligation deadline within class, then age; reservations prevent speculative improvement, cold outreach or repeated failed exploration from consuming the protected due-service share. Proposed planning shares are 50% due service/commitments, 20% ordinary creation/research, 15% continuity/security/grievance, 10% maintenance/evaluation and 5% discretionary exploration; the actual mandate must endorse shares and minimum absolute service capacity before use. Borrowing never removes a due-service minimum or creates authority. A grievance does not need to win a commercial prioritization score to receive the mandated response.

Pressure is the maximum of independently observed saturation dimensions, not a blended score that hides exhausted money or attention. Yellow: any reservation ≥70% of its period allowance, disk ≥70% of admitted volume, or queue p95 delay threatens 50% of deadline slack. Stop new discretionary admission at 85%, restore only below 65% for 10 min; protected work continues only within its already reserved envelope. Disk ≥90%, capture loss, witness lag beyond admitted freshness, or uncertain billing quarantines affected launch/release. Alert current owner and alternate with exact exhausted dimension, due duties and available manual/other-provider paths. A provider outage pauses its queue, permits local D1 staging and already admitted computation, and routes suitable work to an independently qualified substitute; it never silently increases spend or authority.

Allowance observations include source, observed_at, valid_until, account, bucket, interval/reset if supplied, remaining or unknown, and usage confidence. Refresh before launch and at most every five minutes while active using supported interfaces; custodian-only billing observations expire after 24 h or immediately on a relevant auth/plan/credit event. If provider has no reliable remaining value, schedule conservatively at one launch and use actual throttling plus finite episode reservations, not invented task capacity.
