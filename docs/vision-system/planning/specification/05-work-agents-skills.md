# Work, temporary agents and reusable procedures

**Contract:** WORK-1.0 · **architecture:** S1.0 · **authoring base:** `cb1eac4` · 2026-09-12. **Status:** Phase F specification, unimplemented and awaiting independent review. This owns S02's 161 questions; [the machine contract](work-knowledge-contracts.json) records each substantive answer, decision, evidence, uncertainty and planned module. It does not authorize operating resources or complete the later integration, deployment, improvement and unified-schema tracks.

The [kernel](01-contract-kernel.md), [authority/recovery](02-authority-recovery.md), [company procedures](03-company-capabilities.md), [human operation](04-human-operation.md), [capability catalog](capabilities.json), and [knowledge/evaluation contract](06-knowledge-evidence-evaluation.md) form one specification. The [frozen G protocol](../reviews/G-acceptance-protocol.md) remains unchanged. [L04](../../research/L04-engineering.md) and [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md) support separating durable work from replaceable executors; their reported demonstrations do not establish our throughput or business value.

## 1. Boundary, identity and responsibilities

S1-C01 owns endorsed ends, responsibility and legitimate deciding domains. C02 owns admitted Cases, executable WorkOrders, scheduling, workflow cursors, delegated scope and total causal allowance. C03 produces staged artifacts and attributable reports. C05 supplies permitted context and current validity; C06 independently accepts the specified outcome. C04 alone controls consequences, and C08 owns independent recovery. C07 provides actual competent human/service performance; C09 presents current permission-filtered state. A workflow, skill, model or temporary worker cannot acquire any of these authorities by containing instructions to do so.

All records use canonical `RecordEnvelope`, exact `Ref`, UTC timestamps, `Position`, resource quantities and kernel command results. `accepted` requires a committed, independently witnessed transition. It is distinct from output acceptance, effect observation, customer acceptance and duty discharge. Witness outage permits only existing admitted local computation within its surviving lease/context/allowance and the kernel's explicitly local `StageRef` save/export route. A StageRef is not a Ref, and importing it revalidates current restrictions. This document does not create another durability or receipt protocol.

The machine file distinguishes canonical extensions, domain records and derived projections. Task is canonical `WorkOrder`; agent output is `AttemptReport`; Handoff uses `Continuation` plus accepted responsibility transfer; artifacts, claims, decisions and grants retain their canonical types. The prose labels “worker done,” “step done,” and “project complete” must always resolve to a named acceptance scope. No writable aggregate flag may override its underlying judgments or surviving obligations.

Planned implementation is in the existing modular Node/TypeScript application, with protected PostgreSQL command/timer/inbox/outbox transactions. Proposed paths in the machine file are a build map, not existing modules. C03 executors remain outside protected credentials and writable registry state. Routine methods can share code and process boundaries where the kernel permits; there is no roster of permanent domain personas.

## 2. Intent, scopes, dependencies and admission

`Goal` identifies a beneficiary, measurable or explicitly qualitative outcome, exact IntentVersion, success evidence, exclusions, horizon, sponsor and review/reopen rule. A goal may exist without admitted work. C01 endorses its interpretation; C02 references it from `Case.outcome_ref`. The smallest useful WorkOrder is one independently inspectable result or discriminating observation with a finite budget and an acceptance owner. It can be a one-function repair, an actual supplier confirmation or a research conclusion; a token, arbitrary hour or persona conversation is not inherently useful work.

`Mission` groups goals around a bounded company purpose and review horizon. `Project` groups a change with deliverables, stage model, dependencies, carrying/closure costs and acceptance scope. `Workstream` groups recurring related work with its service mandate, cadence and continuity owner. Their exact parent links are optional rather than a mandatory four-level hierarchy. A small correction can reference one goal and one Case directly. Each record retains principal/venture boundaries; none grants resources or establishes legal identity. Parent membership changes do not move duties or reinterpret historical outcomes.

Decomposition starts from the outcome and surviving obligations. The responsible planner identifies the next uncertainty, produces a finite dependency graph and separate acceptance criteria, assigns actual custody and checks resource/deadline feasibility. Planning effort itself consumes a bounded WorkOrder. Its output must enable work or answer a decision; another plan with renamed subtasks is not progress. Stable known procedures can be admitted directly. Unfamiliar work starts with a bounded discriminator before a large commitment, with useful independent work continuing.

`WorkDependency` has source/target exact refs, kind `requires_output/requires_authority/requires_capacity/shared_resource/knowledge_question`, a typed predicate, evidence freshness, custodian and latest responsible resolution time. Capability catalog dependencies mean available interfaces and conditional step guards, not required previously completed capability runs. C02 validates the actual execution graph at admission: cycles in required outputs produce a named blocker and a proposed cut, such as a provisional hypothesis followed by a later validation. It cannot synthesize missing customer or financial evidence to break the cycle. Readiness dependencies are met by actual procedure/performer availability where required, not prior completed sales.

`Blocker` states exactly what cannot proceed, what observation/decision/capacity would unblock it, who can provide that result, next check and due impact. Unknown cause differs from known impossibility. Its state is `open/investigating/awaiting_dependency/resolved/accepted_limit`; only supported evidence satisfies the predicate. An accepted limit parks or narrows affected work and preserves duty ownership. Silence, a new timer, or a new worker name cannot resolve it. The outcome sponsor notices combined drift and incompatible local successes through milestone review, not by relying on workers to volunteer failure.

WIP defaults to one running WorkOrder per temporary executor and one native producer per newly admitted execution profile until a bounded capacity trial establishes a higher limit. This is an initial engineering default, not a founder attention preference or performance fact. Global admission takes the minimum of actual executor slots, resource reservations, independent review capacity and human/service availability. Protected due work keeps its reserved lane. More backlog does not create more capable people or provider allowance.

## 3. Workflow, run and step execution

`WorkflowDefinition` is an immutable versioned procedure: typed input/output ports, named steps, deterministic guards, allowed adaptive branches, stage rules, maximum elapsed/idle time, retry/compensation contracts, evidence and acceptance roles. A fixed path handles known repeatable work; an adaptive step produces a proposed next plan under the same scope and allowance. The admission owner chooses the mode using uncertainty and cost evidence, not whether the producer prefers more freedom. Unknown cases leave the fixed path through an owned exception; they do not silently bypass gates.

`WorkflowRun` pins definition, Case, causal episode, exact inputs and current step cursor. `StepExecution` pins step definition and WorkOrder; its execution projection follows the canonical order/report/acceptance records. A run follows `prepared/running/waiting/parked/acceptance_pending/accepted/failed/cancelled`. A step follows `ready/dispatched/running/reported/accepted/rejected/waiting/cancelled`. Only C02 advances the cursor, in the same protected transaction that records the step result and creates the next work intent. C06 judgment plus the required sponsor acceptance permits a run's accepted state. Actual effect states remain OperationStatus dimensions.

Each step declares an input contract, performer class, read/write resource scope, result envelope, allowed outcomes, exit predicate and next branch. Deterministic transformations execute without a model. Nondeterministic model/tool/human results are captured once as attributable data; replay consumes the recorded result only while current validity permits it. A live regeneration is a new attempt, never “replay.” External operations are proposed and released through C04, outside database transactions. Domain acceptance can require an actual professional result or customer condition; a successful dispatch does not satisfy that guard.

Stage is an epistemic/business position such as proposed, investigated, validated, deliverable or maintaining. Status is whether execution is running, blocked or finished. Workflow definitions choose relevant stages; they do not impose a coding pipeline on finance or support. A small deterministic correction can use one production step and one required acceptance check. A declared guard may establish that a stage is inapplicable, with a reason; current authority, data permission, required review and actual duty-discharge predicates cannot be skipped. New contrary evidence can move the current projection backward through a recorded invalidation/review decision, preserving earlier acceptance history.

Changing a definition creates a new revision. Existing runs remain pinned if compatible and safe. A migration plan names in-flight states, old/new step mapping, preserved outputs/unknown effects, invalidated acceptance, current grants and rollback. C02 parks incompatible runs, and C04/C08 fence affected authority where required. Resuming from the middle requires exact accepted checkpoint, available inputs, compatible code/definition and current restrictions. The system does not replay a payment or customer contact to reconstruct a cursor.

Compensation is its own typed work with authority and acceptance. It may repair a consequence while leaving history and obligations. Steps declare `no_compensation`, an actual compensating procedure, or a need for a competent decision. A failed refund, apology or supplier replacement remains an owned duty; cancelling the workflow never makes those results true. Integration tests must inspect the combined journey after individually correct steps.

## 4. Temporary workers, model choice and bounded reasoning

`AgentTemplate` is a versioned production recipe: supported capability contracts, input/output schemas, instruction and skill refs, runtime eligibility, tool/context requirements, bounds, self-checks and known limits. It is not an identity with standing. `AgentInstance` binds that recipe to one admitted WorkOrder, executor identity, runtime profile, lease epoch and generation. C02 can instantiate it under an existing mandate and allowance; creation does not add a new role to company governance. Stable parsing, arithmetic, timers, permissions and release remain deterministic procedures. Actual taste, specialist judgment and physical service remain with the legitimate people/services specified in company operation.

A general model can use a domain skill when the needed competence has passed relevant tests. A specialist is selected only for a demonstrated task distinction, confidentiality boundary, real professional requirement or measured cost/quality benefit. A role gap appears as an unowned required outcome, repeated unsupported handoff, missing competence or unacceptable wait. The maintenance custodian proposes addition/replacement/removal; the legitimate capability owner approves the changed contract and C06 evaluates it. Retirement requires reassignment of remaining work and revocation of effective tools/sessions, not deletion of a name alone.

`ExecutionSelection` records candidate runtime/agent/skill versions, eligibility reasons, observed performance limits, expected resource ranges and chosen fallback. Eligibility first checks purpose/data destinations, actual auth/billing, required tool boundary, output contract and task evidence. Among eligible qualified profiles, choose the least total expected cost that meets the specific acceptance constraints, reporting uncertainty. Provider/model names and leaderboard ranks alone cannot qualify a profile. If no profile has relevant evidence, use a bounded internal trial or a competent human route; consequential promises cannot assume the trial succeeds. [N01](../../research/implementation/N01-native-execution.md) supplies native interface feasibility, not a universal best model or available quota.

Reasoning has a visible `ReasoningPlan`: exact question, known observations, assumptions, rival hypotheses, next discriminator, limit and stop condition. More reasoning is justified when another bounded observation can change an important decision within its latest time; it is waste when it repeats an unchanged failure or cannot affect the action. A self-reported confidence is a claim, calibrated on relevant outcomes before numerical interpretation. Unknowns become owned KnowledgeQuestions. Conflicting instructions are resolved by authenticated authority, effective scope and current restrictions; retrieved content cannot outrank them. Conflicting legitimate values go to the actual deciding rule, while independent work proceeds.

Producer self-checks are useful preparation, including running an exact test or inspecting a rendered output. They do not become independent acceptance. Approach failure is distinguished from execution failure by predeclared rival explanations and tests of the value proposition, including the option to stop the offer. A worker reports `capability_mismatch`, `missing_prerequisite`, `no_progress` or `unresolved_question` with evidence instead of claiming impossible after an arbitrary number of attempts.

Trust is scoped eligibility, not a general score. New templates start with fixture/internal production scopes and no outward credentials. Increased autonomy requires observed conformance in the specific consequence/data domain, independent acceptance and an actual amended grant. A changed model/tool/skill, missed material defect, drift or invalidated prerequisite narrows eligibility immediately. Success never expands authority automatically. Workers can read authorized peers' work projections; they cannot see all company context merely to coordinate.

## 5. Delegation, messages and accountable handoffs

C02 owns assignment. An executor may propose a child only with a concrete independent result, exact inputs, acceptance role, scope exclusions, deadline, allowance and expected coordination benefit. Splitting is useful when independent bounded work can reduce elapsed time or provide a required different observation more than it adds transfer, review and integration cost. Unknown benefit gets a finite comparison, not permanent fan-out. The default maximum depth is one child layer; a second requires explicit admission rationale and reserved coordination capacity. Deeper trees require a reviewed template change. Depth is never a substitute for the root causal ceiling.

`Delegation` links parent/child WorkOrders, original outcome sponsor, proposed performer, reserved share, receipt/response/latest-result times, nondelegable decisions and Continuation. The child cannot further delegate founder values, professional standing, independent acceptance, access that it cannot lawfully transfer, or the parent's responsibility. Parent sponsorship remains until an actual accepted responsibility transfer. A parent cannot label a difficult decision “delegated” to avoid exercising its own mandate.

`WorkMessage` has kind `assignment/question/observation/objection/correction/progress/result/stop_notice`, sender/recipient identities, exact subject refs, permitted payload/artifacts, request correlation, delivery expiry, response deadline and required action. Durable transport receipt means the recipient inbox stored the message. `MessageAcknowledgment` distinguishes received, understood-scope, accepted-assignment, refused and answered. Assignment acceptance requires current authority/capacity and a check of purpose, exclusions and expected result. A receipt, model paraphrase or emoji cannot transfer duties.

Duplicate message identity/content returns its recorded result; changed content under that identity conflicts. Delivery retries reuse the same identity and consume the original causal allowance. Expiry prevents a stale assignment from starting. A worker that stops acknowledging or heartbeating loses its execution lease under the kernel protocol; the owner records unknown activity and reconciles before reassignment. No process-liveness observation grants exclusive external authority. Replies received after transfer remain attributable evidence for the current custodian, not commands from a resurrected owner.

Questions identify the exact missing fact/decision, why it matters, needed-by time, default behavior and independent work that can continue. Objections preserve challenged scope, contrary evidence and requested investigation; they do not automatically impose suspension or rewrite direction. Urgency derives from actual latest responsible time and a standing interruption rule. Nonurgent requests batch into the human participation window. No answer invokes the recorded bounded fallback, substitute or parked state; it never means approval. A founder correction produces an exact amended intent/decision or factual correction and a dependent-work invalidation/understanding check.

Result transfer carries typed outputs and partial-state flags, exact intent/criteria, warnings, amounts/units, unknowns, contradictions, source/omission refs, remaining duties, pending effects, resources used and next action. A summary is an additional derivative beside the canonical Continuation, never its replacement. The receiver checks critical predicates against originals before acting. Schema mismatch yields a quarantined report and bounded repair request; free-form explanation can accompany typed fields but cannot fill missing required evidence with plausible prose. Each transformation records losses; the next step can demand the original or stop the affected inference.

## 6. Concurrency, failure, retry and stop

`WorkspaceAssignment` pins repository/source root, base revision, permitted paths/resources, task identity and write lease. Separate standalone checkouts are the protected-source default where shared Git/configuration could violate the admitted boundary; worktrees are an allowed concurrency convenience only within a verified compatible trust scope. Worktrees alone do not isolate processes, ports, databases or network effects, as discussed in L04. One writer holds an exact mutable artifact/resource scope; nonoverlapping writers can proceed. A merge WorkOrder checks textual and semantic compatibility against the current base, tests the combined result and obtains independent acceptance. A clean textual merge is not behavioral compatibility.

Two branches can propose changes to the same product meaning without touching the same file. `WorkDependency` shared-resource and semantic-conflict refs therefore supplement path leases. C02 detects new overlap and pauses only the dependent integration; C04 applies the kernel's union-scope migration where obligations/issuers/conflicts changed. The resolver cannot silently discard either proposal. Original work and its costs remain visible.

`FailureRecord` captures error class, exact procedure/input/dependency versions, observed cause, unknowns, affected scope, attempts and proposed next discriminator. Its fingerprint uses stable semantic cause/scope plus normalized error category; cosmetic wording or a new agent ID cannot create fresh allowance. Error classes are transient internal failure, invalid input, auth/permission failure, provider capacity, stale context, invariant breach, unknown effect, competence gap and no progress. Internal transaction retries remain the kernel's bounded pure-transaction rule. External retries remain the authority/adapter contract; this document creates no generic resend loop.

For semantic production, the initial rule allows one retry after a changed input, environment or method is identified within the existing allowance. Two failed attempts with the same cause and no accepted progress open a Blocker; they do not prove impossibility. A template may justify another finite policy using actual conformance evidence. Capacity/auth failures park until a verified changed condition; repeated model calls to rediscover exhaustion are forbidden. C02 checks heartbeat separately from meaningful progress: accepted output, resolved predicate or a tested hypothesis with an honest negative result. Rework time, rejected attempts and abandoned outputs stay in the denominator.

Stop preserves five facts from human operation: request, release fence, worker cessation, external-effect reconciliation and completed remedy. C02 cancels not-yet-dispatched internal work, requests admitted worker stop and prepares continuation under its reserved closing allowance. Expired leases fence writes; they do not prove no external effect. Recovery reconciles actual OperationStatus before repeating any business action. Unrelated authorized service continues under its separate resources. Abandonment always identifies current custodian, allowed cleanup, remaining deadlines and reopening condition.

## 7. Durable schedules and capacity gaps

`Schedule` binds an accepted workflow/capability version, actual timezone/calendar rule, activation interval, occurrence window, latest responsible start, catch-up/concurrency policy, authority, resource class, sponsor and alternate. The initial scheduler is the kernel's durable SQL timer sweep plus event-triggered wakeups; no model runs merely to notice time. `ScheduleOccurrence` identity is schedule revision plus intended local/calendar occurrence and resolved UTC instant. The timezone-rule version and ambiguous/nonexistent local-time policy are explicit. A clock jump cannot create a second logical occurrence.

Every schedule chooses one missed-occurrence policy. **Individual reconciliation** preserves each due invoice, service occurrence or reporting duty and its actual deadline. **Coalesce** produces one current research/maintenance assessment with the omitted windows listed; it cannot merge distinct rights. **Skip-expired** discards obsolete promotional opportunities with a reason and no catch-up blast. **Escalate** activates the accepted custodian/alternate when a missed action needs actual competent judgment. Defaults offered by procedure kind must be explicitly adopted; the software does not invent statutory deadlines or customer agreement terms.

Occurrence states are `planned/due/admitted/running/completed/missed/disposed`; completion follows actual workflow acceptance. Missing work is not automatically marked completed when the next occurrence starts. The scheduling owner records what was intended, actual wake/start, lateness, duplicates suppressed, partial work and remaining duty. Concurrency defaults forbid overlapping instances of the same effect-bearing occurrence; independent read-only assessment can overlap only under separate input/resource admission.

Unattended work needs all prerequisites in standing mandates, available context/tools, finite budgets, acceptance and no-answer/continuity behavior before starting. Attended mode does not weaken authority or allow unavailable service. When the company has no due or authorized useful work, executors idle; planned discovery runs only within its adopted mandate. During a gap, surviving local work can checkpoint through D1, due service uses actually available independent routes, and unavailable performance remains an operational failure. Return presents the human account's actual changes, missing periods and unresolved consequences.

## 8. Instructions and reusable skills

`InstructionBundle` contains a short stable purpose/constraints contract, authority hierarchy references, typed operating procedure, response requirements, examples and change history. Task-specific facts, scope and current rights arrive through the WorkOrder/ContextManifest. No secret, reusable credential, private cross-purpose profile or supposed hidden authority belongs in an instruction. A prompt can guide interpretation; deterministic boundaries enforce permissions, schema, resource use, isolation and release. Repeated instruction noncompliance on a critical rule is evidence to change the mechanism or eligibility, not just add more emphatic text.

`SkillVersion` is a reusable procedure package, not a new agent. Required content: capability/outcome tags; applicability and nonapplicability predicates; typed inputs/outputs; finite steps/branches; dependencies and supported versions; actual tools/runtime/recipient requirements; consequence boundaries; evidence and acceptance criteria; examples and counterexamples; provenance, authorship and license/attribution/redistribution conditions; test suite and known failures; maintenance owner; review date/invalidation triggers; replacement/removal rule. Executable helpers are separately hashed artifacts with admitted worker privileges. A prose package cannot install, contact, spend or execute by being loaded.

Package layout is `manifest.json`, `procedure.md`, optional `examples/`, `tests/` and `helpers/`; the manifest references every file digest and external dependency. Export includes the exact license text/ref and source/version. Missing reuse rights prevents reuse/publication depending on them, while independent original work can proceed. A borrowed example is attributed data, not proof that its current dependencies work. Semantic version changes follow the kernel rules; accepted runs pin exact SkillVersion, InstructionBundle, AgentTemplate and RuntimeProfile refs. Updating one invalidates relevant eligibility, examples and dependent evidence through C05/C06.

Write a skill when repeated work, hard-to-reconstruct domain constraints or shared failure shows expected reuse exceeds authoring/selection/maintenance cost. A one-off procedure can remain a WorkOrder. The capability owner admits a new/changed skill after separate review of scope, licensing and outcome tests. Required tests include a valid case, malformed input, an out-of-scope case, stale example/dependency, injected instruction and an unavailable real performer. Test output and procedure-following observations are separate: a skill can be loaded but ignored, or followed while producing a bad result.

Selection starts with deterministic eligibility over capability, input shape, current versions, permission and performer requirements. Search only eligible metadata; fetch a small candidate set, compare one-line applicability and known limits, then load the selected procedure. Initial limits are at most five candidates and two full skill bodies per selection, charged to the WorkOrder. A composed plan pins a finite dependency DAG, compatible ports and unioned constraints; a conflict cannot be resolved by concatenating prompts. Overlap is owned by the capability maintainer, who can retain explicit alternative contexts or retire a redundant package.

Measure selection time, candidates inspected, tokens/bytes loaded, wrong selections, ignored steps, accepted outcomes and maintenance cost at 10/100/1000-package synthetic libraries with a stable relevant subset and adversarial near-duplicates. These are test sizes, not proven capacity. When the admitted selection budget is exceeded, narrow by exact capability/type, use a known adequate baseline or open bounded catalog repair. Do not feed the whole library into context. An unused emergency skill can remain necessary; an unused redundant skill can retire after dependency and obligation checks. Periodic review uses use/failure evidence and changed dependencies, not access popularity as a freshness signal.

## 9. Positive, adverse and capacity-limited conformance

These cases are specified tests, not executed outcomes. Each uses exact records and observes business result, authority, evidence and total labor separately.

1. A first venture conducts authorized research, drafts a supportable pilot, obtains actual readiness, sells, delivers and handles the first support issue. No cycle demands a prior sale or ticket; no empty evidence becomes validated demand.
2. Two producers independently improve product and onboarding. Their isolated edits merge, but an incompatible promise fails integration acceptance. A resolver repairs the combined journey; valid unrelated work continues.
3. A child receives an assignment, loses its acknowledgment and returns late after transfer. Same-ID lookup recovers the original receipt; current ownership and epochs prevent duplicate work/effects, while useful partial evidence survives.
4. A model heartbeats while repeating the same unsuccessful approach across quota reset. Meaningful-progress checks park the causal episode; a genuine due service and complaint use their protected resources and achieve actual progress.
5. A scheduled campaign expires during outage while separate invoices become due. No promotional burst occurs; each invoice/duty is reconciled under current authority. Local research output saved as StageRef remains visibly local until validated import.
6. A stale borrowed skill passes shape checks but its example omits a cancellation duty. An independent adversarial case detects the omission, invalidates dependent eligibility and preserves unfinished customer duties through replacement.

Removal exports exact goals, definitions, runs, work/continuation refs, source artifacts, accepted criteria, failures, skill licenses and due schedules. The successor accepts responsibility and verifies compatible replay or explicit migration before old routes retire. S0 and simpler deterministic/manual paths remain comparison baselines; added coordination, deep delegation or large skill libraries must be removed when equivalent useful outcomes require less total burden without them.

The machine contract lists allowed state edges and record-specific transition responsibilities. Its repeated shared guard is scaffolding, not a claim of 373 executable predicates. Full-schema consolidation must register the materially distinct edge predicates and typed arguments, resolve company composite Evaluation references to their actual plan/run/judgment roles, and preserve Handoff as Continuation plus accepted responsibility transfer. Those are explicit later specification obligations; no runtime or conformance freeze is claimed here.

The XSR-02 author amendment adds [56 shared verification subcases](work-knowledge-contracts.json#/verification_subcases) and exact per-question assertion joins for all 268 work/knowledge answers; `verification_bindings` identifies the stimulus and expected observation that tests each distinctive claim, including usable provider exit and bounded equivalent-issue reopening. `verification_family_refs` is now DERIVED from those bindings — it names exactly the planned test modules that exercise the answer. It read as the broad portfolio grouping until the XSR-R-01 repair, and under that reading 88 of 394 bindings cited a family their answer did not declare, so a reader following the declaration reached the wrong module. Each subcase carries its own `positive_control` and `execution_boundary` (XSR-R-02); `procedure` stays one shared text and declares that sharing in `shared_control_groups`. Recompute rather than quoting these counts: `node scripts/vision-wk-bindings.mjs verify`. These are unexecuted specifications, not independent closure of XSR-02 or of the XSR-R findings. Root coverage projections are rebuilt from the source by `vision-wk-bindings.mjs project --write`, never by hand.
