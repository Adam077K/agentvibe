## 1. Catalog semantics and ownership

**SPECIFICATION.** The nine component IDs are the logical responsibilities selected in [S1.0](../02-architecture-selection.md). They do not imply nine services, nine agents or one owner for every kind of decision. C01/C02/C05 and appropriate domain coordinators may share a protected process; the producer, authority, independent witness and evidence-control privileges retain the separations prescribed in 01–02. Records, signed responses, projections, operating processes and actual human mandates are distinct things. **DEFERRED:** The complete record/predicate catalog is a separate consolidation obligation.

**SPECIFICATION.** Every component below supplies purpose, responsibility, nonresponsibility, owner, interfaces, inputs, outputs, state, lifecycle, dependencies, trust, permissions, failure, observability, evaluation, scaling, version, replacement and removal. State/lifecycle entries explain ownership and transition conditions using the existing canonical machines; they do not create another persisted component state or competing grant/acceptance machine. Planned module paths name implementation destinations under the final construction graph; those modules are not represented as implemented.

**SPECIFICATION.** An owner is an actual Principal acting under a current accepted ResponsibilityAssignment and Mandate for the relevant scope. A job title, component ID, company namespace, agent template or authenticated account cannot supply legal standing, professional competence or consent. The exact holder, accepted alternate, available time, fees, response window and recovery access are operating setup evidence. One person may hold compatible assignments within actual capacity; producer independence, legitimate deciding parties and independent recovery custody cannot be waived to simplify the roster.

## 2. Enforcement and actual standing

**SPECIFICATION.** At the authoring boundary, these mechanisms are prose contracts. Implementation must demonstrate deterministic schema/revision/epoch checks, attenuation, serialization, protected capture, exact independent witness, bounded transport, resource accounting and all-channel isolation. Actual provider eligibility, professional performance, custodian independence, lawful standing, continuing service and an owner's practiced competence require external evidence and accepted people. Software can refuse an act whose required evidence is absent; it cannot make the underlying fact true by recording an approval.

**SPECIFICATION.** The operational placement remains OP-AWS-LINUX-1: A and R use distinct actual account/admin/recovery roots in the specified regions; R and G deliberately share one host/admin/load root; native authentication resides in N and arbitrary code in X. AWS common-provider failure and the declared admitted root compromises are outside the stronger independent-loss claim. Local processes or the development VM cannot demonstrate those roots. The [priced deployment and outcome-matched comparators](07-integrations-capacity.md#7-deployment-economics-and-comparators) retain estimated sizing and unknown actual professional/custodian/founder costs. The subscription baseline does not establish infrastructure funding or owner attention adequacy.

**SPECIFICATION.** Failure behavior follows the affected dependency and consequence. Missing required identity, witness, trusted time, disclosure lineage or exact accepted predicate denies dependent authority. D0 ephemeral work, genuinely saved D1 local staging/export and D2 witnessed transitions have different claims. Already admitted local work may continue within surviving context, lease and allowance. An offline browser draft is neither a server StageRef nor independently durable; a primary-ahead command row is not a witnessed acceptance. Actual owed service still needs an accepted competent route or a truthful missed-duty account. A system that only refuses safely has not demonstrated useful company operation.

**SOURCE CLAIM.** The [SC-POLICY-1 maintenance contract](../../research/substrate-claims.json) applies explicitly to inherited substrate reliance. Record source/version, publication date or unknown, access time, issuer interests, confidence and applicability limits; primary documentation can be interested and cannot prove runtime composition or business value. Recheck before dependency lock or operating reliance, on upstream/security/support/auth/terms change or contradiction, and no later than **2026-10-12** for this initial living reliance unless an earlier consequence deadline applies. Preserve historical claims with their original scope; stale/inaccessible/contradicted sources lose eligibility as the sole current prerequisite. Relied-on 07–08 sources must carry the same maintenance fields before operating admission, without pretending they already have entries in the shared register.
