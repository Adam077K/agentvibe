## Operator jobs and authority

**SPECIFICATION.** The operator must be able to answer: what changed, what remains owed, what useful work actually happened, what is unknown, which decision truly needs me, what happens if I do nothing, and how can I stop or correct it? The same contracts support a founder, a second owner, a bounded collaborator, a professional, a continuity substitute and an affected person. Each sees only their authorized scope. People remain actual parties with identity, competence and accepted standing; an application role is not corporate office or professional qualification.

**SPECIFICATION.** `ActorBinding` is a permission-filtered human projection with exact `identity_binding_ref:Ref<IdentityBinding>`, `principal_ref:Ref<Principal>` and `mandate_ref:Ref<Mandate>`; it is not a second identity authority. `Party` is the company label for canonical `Principal`, and `PersonMandate` is a human payload extension/view of canonical `Mandate`. The projection displays verified identity evidence, authentication method, principal, role/decision domains, scoped grants, accepted responsibility assignments, expiry and revocation from those sources. The client never supplies its own trusted actor, role, grant or professional status. Identity recovery is a separate authorized procedure with independent evidence; possession of a restored session or device does not reauthorize an actor. The kernel owns authentication/grants and provider-specific revocation; this document defines their human meaning.

**SPECIFICATION.** An `AuthorityMatrix` records each decision domain, legitimate deciding party or required combination, consulted parties, veto/consent conditions, competent assessor, alternate and absence behavior. It can grant routine product, technical, accounting or customer-remedy decisions within existing bounds. **FOUNDER CONSTRAINT.** Personal intent, values, taste and new material exposure remain with their actual owner. A second person can contribute facts, perform work or possess specialist authority without acquiring founder identity. A faster answer, majority of model votes, employment title or successful history never determines authority.

**SPECIFICATION.** Shared decisions use the declared rule: an exact proposal gathers the required endorsements for one revision, then the kernel validates current authority. If one required party changes or withdraws before authorization, it remains pending or conflicted. A domain with no valid rule cannot accept new consequential work needing one. Independent authorized work continues; the dispute packet preserves positions and latest responsible time. No synthetic arbitration persona chooses between equally defensible personal ends.

## Decision timing and no-answer behavior

**SPECIFICATION.** Generate a packet only when a current mandate cannot resolve a real decision without that person. Before interrupting, test whether the decision has already been answered within the same purpose, scope, consequence and validity interval. Reuse explicit rules; do not infer permission from repeated similar approvals. Factual questions go to competent evidence gathering; bounded implementation choices stay with their authorized owner. A genuinely missing personal intention gets one exact question with independent work continuing.

**SPECIFICATION.** `latest_decision_at` is the earliest time needed to preserve a feasible option: due obligation minus supported performance, review, transfer and contingency intervals. `expires_at` also respects grant/evidence/offer validity. Packet readiness requires exact effects, alternatives, significant uncertainty, resource/counterparty consequences and no-answer behavior. If preparation cannot establish these before the latest time, escalate the lack of readiness as the issue; do not pressure a hurried signature.

**SPECIFICATION.** The no-answer rule is explicit by case: refuse new purchase/publication/commitment; continue an already authorized procedure; invoke accepted substitute; or carry out preauthorized bounded preservation. Expiry never means consent. For due work requiring unavailable judgment, activate continuity and retain responsibility/service loss rather than calling refusal successful fulfillment. Known mandatory safety controls under standing policy act without waiting for a person where waiting would defeat them; an urgent allegation itself supplies no such authority.

**SPECIFICATION.** An answer that reveals a material misunderstanding triggers the domain-assessment path before the affected effect can rely on informed competent review. An honest preference for another design or a deliberate changed value remains a valid preference, not a factual deficit. Independently authorized work continues throughout. Decision history preserves chosen option, endorsed reasons, contrary evidence, uncertainty, original authority and what later changed; a stranger can reconstruct its bounded meaning without pretending to know every causal influence.

## Complete terminal and coding interaction

**SPECIFICATION.** The terminal workspace is a projection of a real `WorkOrder` and environment, not a disconnected conversation. It displays company/principal, venture, workspace path/revision, execution identity, actual provider/billing attestation, allowed tools/network destinations, loaded context manifest, artifact state, active leases, remaining causal allowance and current stop state. A task switch preserves all of these with an exact continuation reference; conversational recency cannot select a different account or grant.

**SPECIFICATION.** Conversation entries distinguish authenticated direction, user correction, source content, model proposal, tool invocation, captured output, decision and independently observed result. Source text and tool stdout retain disclosure/trust restrictions; untrusted instructions cannot become commands. Show the actual command/arguments, working directory, environment identity, redacted secret names, bounded permissions, start/end times and exit/timeout state. Neither exit zero nor a native model’s completion message establishes tests, deployment or customer fulfillment. Environment variables and outputs reaching a provider must satisfy the data-recipient contract; masking secrets in a transcript does not repair an unauthorized underlying disclosure.

**SPECIFICATION.** Coding work includes navigable source/diffs, base and head revisions, conflicts, generated artifacts, test commands/results/denominators, failed/skipped tests, static checks, integration/evaluation results and actual release proposals. A patch is staged and reviewed before authoritative application. Concurrent workers show separate work scope, branch/workspace, lineage, checkpoints, resource consumption and acceptance owner; adding workers cannot multiply shared grants or allowance. Merge conflicts and stale artifacts become explicit work, never a silent last-writer choice.

**SPECIFICATION.** Shell interaction is available only through the admitted execution boundary and task-scoped environment; company consequences continue through the release service. Native provider authentication is distinct from company action authority. The external integration track specifies supported native launch/clean-context/tool mediation modes. No tool, helper hook, nested shell, browser or local extension acquires production credentials because it appears inside this terminal. Activity outside the managed observation boundary is declared unavailable evidence and reconciled when discovered, not included in a complete-account claim.

**SPECIFICATION.** Interruption records completed/partial work, exact next action, evidence/unknowns/disagreements, artifacts, authority/validity versions, pending effects and causal consumption. Reopening a saved native session requires a compatible permitted route and current complete context restrictions; otherwise create a fresh session from the portable packet and label the context loss. The terminal never silently purchases metered capacity or invokes another account to avoid exhaustion. Resumption is a new attributable attempt when generation restarts. Existing captured results remain evidence with their original model/provider identity.

**SPECIFICATION.** Stop displays five separate facts: command received; future release denied; worker cessation observed; released/external effects reconciled; required remedy completed. Technical R3’s first-transport deadline and already-released revocation window are shown when material. A lost acknowledgment offers a status lookup using the same command ID, not a fresh stop or resume with invented success. Resume is blocked until current restrictions, unknown effects, evidence and actual authority permit it. An old UI tab cannot revive a released attempt or obsolete lease.

## Attention and practiced competence

**SPECIFICATION.** `ParticipationPlan` is owner-endorsed setup data: availability windows/timezone, maximum decision-batch minutes, interrupt limit, practice allocation, incident reserve, permitted assessment data, sampling policy and review date. An empty plan means no asserted attention/competence capacity for dependent promises. It does not prevent internal software development or force a questionnaire now. The interface offers understandable defaults at operating setup, but silence cannot adopt them.

**DESIGN PROPOSAL.** The fixed default policy offered for adoption uses one cycle per five declared operating days, within the chosen minutes. It offers four short encounters: original customer material, an independent taste decision before advice, an unusual financial/obligation explanation, and an intervention or recovery rehearsal. Customer material alternates ordinary and adverse/abandoned/underserved cases; the financial/obligation slot includes an adverse sample each cycle. Source eligibility is determined by access and the sampling frame; selection uses a recorded reproducible random seed within strata, outside the account producer’s write authority. Empty strata and unavailable originals are recorded, not replaced by flattering examples. **UNKNOWN:** This is a proposed schedule to test, not a proven human-learning dose.

**SPECIFICATION.** Owner-requested examples supplement rather than silently replace the required sampling record. Decline, interruption and noncompletion remain visible coverage limits; they do not establish incompetence. Revisions to the policy are explicit and owner-contestable. The system does not track unrelated browsing, covertly infer personality/health, rank general intelligence, or optimize agreement with its recommendations. Collect only relevant authorized operational and consented assessment data under a retention rule.

**SPECIFICATION.** For learning encounters, optionally capture a brief factual explanation/prediction or manual intervention before advice; record independent feedback afterward. A taste judgment is preserved as taste. At the next applicable cycle, present an unfamiliar transfer case with a different context; periodically test delayed transfer after an interval predeclared in the study protocol. Compare unaided recognition, intervention quality, false-positive assessments, owner time and reported emotional burden separately. Familiar task success cannot establish general competence. Consented longitudinal comparison, including absence and closure, is required before claiming preservation.

**SPECIFICATION.** On material domain misunderstanding, a competent assessor states the exact factual proposition/intervention, evidence and limits. The owner can contest it or request a second assessment. The relevant effect then obtains competent assistance, a narrower action, a rehearsal or a wait under the predeclared competence prerequisite. Lawful approval does not manufacture specialist qualification; a model cannot strip values authority. Unrelated work continues. If assistance is unavailable, new dependent exposure stops and existing duties invoke accepted continuity. This is an observable changed path, not another warning dismissed by a generic “approve anyway” button.

**SPECIFICATION.** Attention admission counts planned decisions, observed handling duration, interrupts, direct relationships, practice, recovery and duty-review time. Estimates remain estimates and are checked against actual minutes. The scheduler groups equivalent decision needs without merging distinct entitlements, reserves actual availability, and reduces new commitments before overload. When a batch reaches the chosen limit, remaining nonurgent decisions defer to the next declared window; their latest responsible times trigger substitute/continuity, never automatic approval. The number of decisions is not itself a universal limit: one complex material choice can consume the batch.

## Collaboration, absence, succession and departure

**SPECIFICATION.** Onboarding supplies original purpose/taste, protected exclusions, current domain responsibilities, actual access, routine procedures, unresolved disputes, evidence limits, imminent deadlines, reporting and stop paths. The newcomer acknowledges scope and performs a bounded access/understanding check. “Do not reopen” constraints identify their actual issuer and amendment route; newly discovered factual contradictions can still be reported without silently rewriting a settled personal value.

**SPECIFICATION.** A handoff includes original evidence and its permitted use, exact pending decisions, accepted duties, partial artifacts, unknown effects, deadlines, conditions for resumption, remaining resources and proposed next actions. Recipient acknowledgment of receipt differs from acceptance of responsibility. Responsibility transfers only after authority, competence and access checks and explicit scope acceptance. If rejected, the existing custodian or accepted alternate remains responsible. The requester sees the reason and continued service plan rather than hunting the next person.

**SPECIFICATION.** Absence mode is declared by authorized person or activated by a predeclared missed-contact/availability rule. Missing responsiveness cannot diagnose incapacity; it can trigger the already authorized operational fallback. The returning owner sees the difference from their last acknowledged position: direction changes, promises, actions/effects, decisions resolved/waiting, contradictory evidence, expenditures, source gaps, remaining duties and actual capacity. Reading the recap does not reauthorize stale work. A staged month-long absence tests whether real due service, refunds, professional tasks and grievances were fulfilled or legitimately disposed of.

**SPECIFICATION.** Temporary substitution, departure, sale and succession have different actual authority and continuity arrangements. Departure stops new assignments, inventories and revokes effective native access including descendants, transfers duties with accepted acknowledgment, settles fees/worker duties, restricts retained records and provides permitted exports. A record cannot be erased to evade a claim; retaining a role indefinitely cannot justify indefinite personal-data retention. Full takeover requires actual lawful standing, capable human/service resources, keys/access, evidence and exercised operation. A one-person company is viable only within demonstrated capacity; missing complementary judgment is a limitation, not an agent role to invent.

## Accessibility, faults, evaluation and source trace

**SPECIFICATION.** All surfaces require keyboard-operable inspection and commands, logical focus order, visible focus, meaningful labels, structured headings, screen-reader announcements for state changes, text equivalents for status/color, zoom/reflow without losing consequences, and alternatives to dragging or hover-only controls. Reduced motion is respected. Errors identify the field or failed contract and preserve entered safe text; they do not expose another party’s data. Critical controls remain reachable with large targets and deliberate confirmation of exact consequential terms, without relying on timed interaction or muscle-memory placement.

**SPECIFICATION.** Mobile starts with current scope, due consequences, uncertainty and available intervention. It never compresses away recipient, amount, first-send window, changed terms or unknown outcome. Complex packets persist as handoffs to a larger surface when required detail cannot be inspected usefully; they are not approved through a summary-only shortcut. Loss of connectivity distinguishes cached evidence from live authority; cached stop state stays “last observed” until independently confirmed.

**SPECIFICATION.** Conformance cases include: stale phone approving changed terms; duplicate response after lost acknowledgment; actor-switch or cross-company access attempt; interrupted terminal task restored on another permitted provider; unknown stop/released first-send window; unavailable original after deletion; a missing native source hidden by a fluent summary; joint owner/provider absence; a departing custodian’s unresolved grievance; limited owner batch with a true urgent duty; and a material competence misunderstanding with ample lawful authority. Each requires expected command/state/evidence outcomes and ordinary successful controls. Test accessibility with keyboard and assistive workflows, and actual human usability under authorized conditions; machine checks alone do not establish usefulness under stress.
