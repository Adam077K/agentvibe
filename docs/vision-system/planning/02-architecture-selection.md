The selected logical architecture is **a common authority for admitted work and surviving obligations, specialized native production, and separately protected consequence, evidence, and recovery boundaries**. Its version is **S1.0** (**S1** below), a logical specification anchor, not released software. Its organizing control is A-derived: one company case authority admits and maintains discretionary work under endorsed priorities. It incorporates the repaired responsibility and enforcement contracts, including native outcome sponsorship and independent challenge. This choice is a **design inference under uncertainty**. The evidence establishes available mechanisms and important failure modes; it does not establish which candidate runs a business most effectively. S1 minimizes independently administered coordination decisions inside the proposed company while retaining specialized domain meaning and external standing. The cost of that choice is concentrated agenda authority and potentially expensive case-model maintenance. A measured demonstration that this concentration misses important work, increases owner labor, or obstructs independently capable domains must reopen the choice.

The baseline for comparison must cover full business delivery, evidence, consequences, competence and closure. Removing these obligations would make an artificially cheap competitor. Requirements specify outcomes and authority; they do not prescribe case records, charters, blackboards or organizational titles.

| **C2** | Standing inquiry charters entitle eligible observers to a bounded first discriminator and separately governed disposition despite ordinary production priorities | Protects investigation from convenient local closure or indefinite discretionary deferral | Reserved analysis and assessor capacity can be consumed by false or low-value inquiries; continuity and appeal add permanent institutional labor |

The originals are [A](candidates/A-records-and-cases.md), [B](candidates/B-federated-responsibility.md), [C1](candidates/C-adaptive-coordination.md), and the [C2 amendment](candidates/AC-foundational-distinction.md). C1 overlaps an adaptive case implementation and is not credited as an additional foundation. C2’s distinction is genuine as a proposed jurisdictional choice: who owes a first investigation and who can retire it. Case engines can implement those rights; blackboard storage does not create them.

**Why select A-derived control now:** existing obligations, counterparty challenges, releases and shared scarce resources require explicit company decisions in every candidate. A common admitted-work authority offers one place to resolve missing ownership and retain continuation while native performers retain their own methods. This reduces the number of cross-authority contracts the company itself must administer compared with B’s independent issuer preparations. It also avoids imposing C2’s general protected inquiry allocation before evidence shows that scheduled cross-domain scrutiny and compulsory duty/grievance handling are inadequate. These are structural cost judgments, not observed performance advantages or claims that centralization is inherently simpler.

B remains the strongest replacement when actual independently administered domains are a benefit rather than simulated internal departments. It may run in one deployable application; microservices are not its defining feature. S1 keeps useful native semantics and contractual supplier autonomy. It does not keep B’s independently issued internal company grants or award exchange as the default. Introducing a genuinely independent issuer later requires TF1’s full preparation/revocation protocol or a new reviewed equivalent; ordinary remote checks cannot approximate a common authorization cutoff.

C2 remains the strongest challenge to agenda concentration. S1 must reserve discovery capacity, run cross-domain review, preserve adverse evidence, and protect actual grievance/rights duties. It can nevertheless defer or end a truly discretionary inquiry unresolved under authorized priorities, with reasons and reconsideration conditions. It has not adopted C2’s compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction by another name. If deferred inquiry causes persistent missed opportunities, duties or owner blind spots despite those controls, compare C2 directly. A lawful deferral is not itself an A conformance failure; its business consequences remain evidence.

S0 must remain a serious operating comparator. It can use narrow execution gates and independently durable records; its simplicity concerns agenda automation, not weaker consequence protection. If manual/native coordination delivers equivalent useful outcomes and recovery within the same owner allowance at lower full cost, remove unnecessary orchestration. S1 is chosen for complete specification because the intended scale creates a credible need for persistent automatic coordination; actual scale, exceptions and maintenance economics remain unknown.

## 3. Selected logical and responsibility model

S1 distinguishes an intention, a responsibility, a plan, a proposed effect and an observed result. A case coordinates work toward an outcome; an obligation survives that case; an operation can remain uncertain after both a worker and a plan stop. Accepted work cannot discharge external standing through an internal status.

Cases distinguish proposed, admitted, active, waiting-for-evidence, accepted, disputed, parked and abandoned states. Obligations distinguish reported performance from legitimate discharge, transfer and surviving dispute. Operations distinguish held, independently recorded, released, observed and unknown effect. The authority owning each transition verifies its prerequisites; a convenient common “done” flag cannot replace these distinctions.

> **Superseded 2026-09-13.** The sentence *"Obligations distinguish reported performance from legitimate discharge, transfer and surviving dispute"* stands verbatim and needs one word pinned down. **"Surviving dispute" here means a CONTESTED OBLIGATION that survives the closure of the Case carrying it — not a second state of an evidence judgment.** Read the other way it appeared to contradict [01-contract-kernel.md](specification/01-contract-kernel.md)'s *"Presentation may call contested \"disputed\"; it must not persist a second disputed verdict"*, and that misreading is what raised `gap-contradiction-disputed-vs-contested` in [phase-content-gaps.json](specification/contracts/phase-content-gaps.json). The two passages were never about one subject. What the registry enforces: `EvidenceJudgment` declares no `disputed` phase at all — `criterion.EvidenceJudgment.contested.v1` is where a challenged judgment's content lives — while `Obligation`, `Case`, `SalesAgreement` and `Commitment` each do declare one, because a duty, a piece of work and an agreement are the things that can legitimately be in dispute.

The following are logical components and authority boundaries. They are not nine mandatory services or agents. Co-location is permitted only when it preserves the declared fault and privilege separation.

Every contract specifies owner, inputs/outputs, authoritative source, lifecycle, access and consequence scope, retention, failure, observability, scaling, versioning, replacement and removal. These subjects can be records and functions in a modular application. Full Phase F must map all directive schema subjects without inventing an agent or service for each noun.

The selected production rule is deterministic computation for arithmetic, validation and known predicates; durable procedures for waiting, schedules, routing and repetition; bounded model work for interpretation and generation; capable people for human relationships and consequential judgment; qualified professionals and services for actual specialist or physical performance. Persistent model identities require demonstrated benefit over portable work records and temporary sessions. Existing roster names do not supply that justification.

Each offered customer journey has one accepted outcome sponsor. Subtasks may span product, finance, delivery and support, whose local acceptance supplies evidence to that sponsor. Necessary unsponsored work enters temporary protective custody and CF1’s bounded ownership resolver; it cannot remain an unowned queue. In S1 this function assigns a case/duty custodian under standing mandates. It cannot grant resources or become a second planner. Transfer requires acknowledged scope, access, competence and unfinished effects. When capacity is absent, admissions narrow and accepted continuity activates; the founder is not the routine reconstruction service.

**S1.1 — the five layers of the work / agents / authority / skills / context / routing layer, and their precedence (F2, 2026-09-13).** The nine logical components above are unchanged and no tenth is added. What the F2 round settles is **which question each part of that layer decides, and which decision governs when two disagree**. Named plainly: **consequence class decides who may act; a declared procedure decides how the work runs and what each step may see; a typed standing interest decides when work becomes due; an existence record decides whether a worker may be created at all; and exactly one standing party exists for each duty that outlives the case carrying it.**

*Every row is SPECIFICATION. The operative rules are in [`specification/05-work-agents-skills.md`](specification/05-work-agents-skills.md); this table fixes the layering and its order.*

| Layer | Decides | Governing rule | Where it is specified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 · consequence class** | who may act | Every action carries the **set** of routing classes it reaches, computed by one deterministic function from records the acting party cannot author, recomputed at release on the frozen bytes; **every reached class's gates apply** and no rank is computed | `05` §11; restating [`specification/02-authority-recovery.md`](specification/02-authority-recovery.md) §4 |
| **L2 · declared procedure** | how, and what each step may see | Immutable versioned steps declaring kind, read set, grants, validator and branch set; the **loader delivers the read set and records what it delivered**; a guard is a pure function and a model's output is never a jump; one effect per effect step | `05` §3 |
| **L3 · typed standing interest** | when work becomes due | Relevance is a **computed, unstoppable armed set** over typed fields after every committed transition; **no precondition may invoke a model**; all prerequisites simultaneously, unexpired, uncontradicted, at or above their trust class | `05` §7 |
| **L4 · `ExistenceJustification`** | whether a worker may exist | One of six admitted reasons, the unit it predicates on, **a test that can return false**, the alternative, a removal criterion and an expected cost. Specialized knowledge refused; attributable identity mandatory and never a reason | `05` §4 |
| **L5 · standing accountability** | who remains reachable after the work ends | A holder exists for a duty class **only** if its clock can run with no case admitted, an outsider has a right to reach the company about it, and it survives closure. It is a record — address, mandate, reservation, alternate, suspension procedure, review date — and **never produces, never releases, never decides the matter it is the subject of, and carries no model memory** | `05` §12 |

**Precedence is fixed and operative: the lower-numbered layer governs when two disagree.** Class may **raise** what a procedure permits and never lower it. An interest may arm but never admit above its effect class. An existence record may refuse a worker the class would permit but never create one the class forbids. A holder may hold but never act. **Why a layering rather than one organizing unit:** the round established that there is **no single unit of justification** — the founder's five criteria predicate on five different objects, and a criterion applied to the wrong unit returns undecidable, which reads as satisfied. Each of the five candidate designs carried its worst blocking findings on **its own routing unit**, so a synthesis that picked one unit would have inherited that unit's blind spot. Layering the units and stating which governs does not. The five criteria themselves are judged in [the F2 selection record](F2/05-selection-record.md) §2: **distinct tools or permissions** confirmed and the strongest of the five, **isolated context** refined and split into an attention sense and a confidentiality sense with only the latter justifying a worker, **independent verification** refined with half of it unreachable inside one model family, **parallel work** refined to near-inert at the current concurrency pin, and **specialized knowledge overturned** as a reason to create a worker.

**The production rule of this section is unchanged and it is what L4 defers to for people.** The six existence reasons govern **model** workers only; the five-way production rule above — deterministic computation, durable procedures, bounded model work, capable people, qualified professionals and services — remains the performer-type decision, because two of the six reasons do not transfer to a person and two reasons that apply only to people are not among them.

## 4. Control, admission and external effects

An authenticated directive, native observation, scheduled occurrence or complaint can propose or reopen work. Syntax/provenance validation establishes attribution within a boundary, not truth. The case authority applies existing duty and exploration mandates, identifies ownership and actual competence/capacity, and admits bounded work. Missing information creates a specific investigation or dependency; it never becomes assumed readiness.

The scheduler preserves capacity for due obligations, complaint assessment, authorized containment, semantic maintenance and planned discovery, then applies endorsed priorities to remaining work. Deadlines, meaningful progress, concurrency and causal episode totals constrain execution. New revisions, descendants, model replacements and provider resets do not renew a spent work allowance. Protected discovery capacity includes scheduled independent adverse samples and cross-domain review. Its discretionary agenda remains under the case authority; required safety or grievance performance remains nonoptional under its own mandate.

A work order names the outcome, input versions, permitted read set, expected evidence, executor/method constraints, budgets, checkpoint, stop rule and acceptance owner. Native performers may decompose it privately, with attenuated authority and total budgets. A model can propose another step but cannot admit its own larger mandate. Bidding/negotiation is omitted initially; deterministic capability/availability routing plus bounded comparative assessment suffices unless evidence shows otherwise.

S1 selects **one transactional logical authority per overlapping consequence scope**, including grants, reservations, conflict claims and mutable prerequisite epochs. Distinct resources retain distinct units and restrictions. Domains provide source facts and competent determinations; internal departments do not run independently issued spend authorities by default. A partition cannot create another copy of usable authority. Physically separate servers are permissible only with a demonstrated common order and fenced writer generations.

`ConflictClaim` keys identify the actual entitlement, audience allocation, environment or shared consequence independent of case/operation IDs. `InteractionDetermination` binds applicable entity, relationship, population, purpose and shared-dependency constraints. Quantified ceilings, qualitative prohibitions and professional prerequisites remain separate. Unknown material interactions narrow or block the affected effect while ordinary independent work continues. Discovery of new interaction classes is owned labor, not an assumption that the catalog is complete.

Before release, independently persist the `RecoveryEnvelope`: exact recoverable executable bytes, identities, conflict set, grants/policy/configuration versions, holds, evidence contract and potential new obligations. Zero acknowledged released records may be lost under the declared primary-host-and-backup failure. Current release state, valid evidence, disclosure contract, parameter authority and time bounds are checked in the ordered release transaction. Only controlled egress can consume its resulting attempt right. This is an internal authorization cutoff, not an atomic transaction with an external bank, publisher or supplier.

**Technical reassessment R2:** a locally committed row or successful status lookup after a lost acknowledgment is insufficient evidence of independent durability. Before any dispatch-authorizing response, establish authoritative independent durability evidence tied to the exact envelope, release decision, generation and required frontier. Failure injection must cover local commit, remote persistence, acknowledgment loss, status lookup and failover. Phase F must name the implementation’s actual proof and reconciliation behavior; “synchronous” as a configuration label does not close this contract.

Revocation before the cutoff prevents release; later revocation leaves a released operation in flight and starts cancellation/reconciliation where available. A remote acceptance window remains a declared consequence requiring an appropriate actual contract. A connector promising stricter suppression needs enforcement at the relevant provider boundary. A lost response, expired worker lease or deduplication window cannot justify blind resend, entitlement reuse or releasing money. Compensation is a new authorized operation with its own failure and remedy state.

**Technical reassessment R3:** separately bound release-to-first-transport delay, retries and delayed provider acceptance. Grants and operator views must expose the gateway generation, first-send deadline, cancellation/expiry semantics and residual revocation window. A paused gateway cannot retain an indefinitely usable first invocation merely because it has not retried. Missing timing/fencing assurance parks the effect; Phase F must specify the exact transport boundary and test expiry, revocation and restart at each interval.

Consequential parameters come from authoritative selectors: payee from the verified payee source, amount from entitlement/terms, recipient from the relevant relationship and permission record, claims from current acceptance evidence. Outward generation uses either destination/purpose-clean context with approved content forms or trusted deterministic rendering of explicitly permitted fields. Broad sensitive model context cannot produce arbitrary outward text or hidden metadata without separately authorized disclosure of the exact bytes. Provider prompts, search queries, URLs and telemetry are disclosure destinations too. A content scanner supplements this boundary; it does not establish noninterference.

## 5. Native meaning, evidence, memory and improvement

Native accounting, CRM, support, code, design and professional meanings remain authoritative in their domains. Shared meaning covers only actual cross-boundary decisions: identity, principal, obligation, authority, time, units, provenance, uncertainty and stated acceptance relations. A ticket’s closure cannot silently become satisfaction, a CRM stage cannot become settlement, and a domain label cannot decide legal responsibility. Mappings have owners, versions, examples, counterexamples, consumer dependencies and maintenance costs.

Working context is attempt-local. Episodes preserve attributable attempts; semantic claims are scoped derivatives of evidence; procedures/skills are versioned instructions with applicability, exclusions, provenance, test and retirement rules. Start with record queries and permission-filtered lexical retrieval. Graph/vector memory can be added only for a named retrieval problem and measured benefit; neither is the organizational source of truth. Raw transcripts are evidence of captured interaction, not validated company knowledge.

The loader captures all effective input lineage, including earlier context and tool metadata. Outputs stage immutably, then become usable only through atomic validity checking and derivative registration. Restriction changes serialize with that publication boundary and consequential release. Live sessions may still contain revoked material but lose publication/release rights; late outputs and native resumptions cannot launder it. Conservative lineage may overrestrict useful work and impose rebuilding cost; that is measured, not waived by a model’s assertion that it ignored a source.

`DeletionScope` persists restrictions across initial cutoff, late derivatives, in-flight exports, backups and offline custodians. Rejoin occurs in isolation before retrieval, indexing or egress. The account separately reports verified live erasure, restricted backup retention, unresolved vendor copies and justified retained records. Its minimal surviving restriction evidence needs a real retention basis. Deletion can reduce reconstructability; the account declares the resulting gaps rather than retaining everything for convenience.

Acceptance protects the **transitive evidence base**: capture, source selection, decoders, mappings, metrics, denominators, datasets, build/runtime dependencies and administration. A claim states which shared failure it is intended to survive. Independent sources and separately governed interpretation are required where surviving a production mapping failure is claimed; two models consuming the same bad parser do not supply that independence. Missing or changed semantics suspend acceptance. Original testimony can still be wrong, and independent assessors can still agree incorrectly.

The account distinguishes human-endorsed reasons, executor-reported rationale, observable inputs/actions, reconstructed causal hypotheses and unknown influence. Rationale is testimony about reasoning. It cannot establish cause merely because a transcript and receipt are authentic. Suppressed, rejected, stale, unobserved, unavailable and unresolved evidence remains visible, including denominator changes and missing intervals.

Improvement is another bounded case with original problem evidence, alternatives including removal, exact change, affected contracts, benefit/harm hypothesis, test budget, rollout, independent observation and rollback. Prompt, skill, workflow, retrieval, routing, adapter and UI changes are versioned. Protected identity, grants, evidence, incident classifications, evaluator selection and improvement controls require independently authorized changes across their dependencies. No reward or demonstrated quality expands authority. Rollback includes changed schemas, derivatives and ongoing work; external consequences still require remediation.

## 7. Human operation and failure boundaries

The owner receives changed intent, accepted/contested promises, contrary evidence, unknown effects, missing observations, capacity, costs and upcoming decision deadlines. Terminal work preserves conversation, plans, concurrent attempts, shell context, code/diffs/tests, approvals, artifacts and recoverable task switching. Mobile and accessible views offer the same authoritative decisions with observation age; offline state is stale/unknown. Stop requests distinguish requested, release denied, worker stopped, external settlement and remedy completed. There is no dependence on an animated office or transcript-derived success state.

Fixed, transparent participation includes original customer evidence, an independent taste decision, unfamiliar financial interpretation and intervention/recovery practice within owner-selected availability. Independent adverse sampling challenges the account producer’s selection. Practice completion, factual understanding, intervention performance, domain competence evidence and preference remain separate. A material misunderstanding changes the relevant assessment path through competent assistance, narrower action or refusal of new exposure. It does not transfer values authority or create an IQ score. Effective cadence and delayed transfer remain empirical questions.

Complaints have accessible intake independent of product accounts, proportionate verification, accepted custodian/alternate, substantive deadlines, reasons, competent escalation, remedy authority and resources. Noncustomers and complaints against the custodian retain standing through closure. An investigation cannot suspend valid service merely by existing. CF1’s distinct observation, contestation, investigation, expiring containment, justified suspension and direction decisions apply; TF1 bounds repeated processing while preserving genuine-case progress. A shared administrator or unavailable professional cannot satisfy independent escalation through a new role label.

The selected technical fault boundary includes primary-host rollback/loss, delivery duplication/reordering, partitions, stale workers, malicious retrieved/model content, compromised ordinary producers/improvers, and one admitted adapter within its demonstrated native permissions. Control roots, independent continuity records/keys, controlled egress and a separately reachable recovery administrator are assumed to survive as declared. Appropriate acceptance claims also require their specified independent observation/interpretation roots. Compromise of all roots, malicious ultimate administrators, unbounded provider administration and unobserved off-platform promises remain outside guaranteed containment/completeness. Actual exposure must be declared before dependent operation; this design decision grants no founder risk acceptance.

Recovery fences old egress and descendant access, seals the current registry generation, obtains complete channel membership/frontiers, restores holds and restrictions, reconciles effects/obligations, then issues fresh authority for demonstrably disjoint safe scopes. Missing frontier, key, membership, provider history or unfenced old writer contains the affected account/channel and wider principal if needed. A backup cannot certify its own completeness. `AccessGraph` discovery includes newly minted tokens, sessions, workflows, hooks, forwarding and scheduled execution; rotating one secret is not recovery evidence.

## 9. Evidence needed to retain or reopen S1

All experiments below are required future evidence, not results. Freeze fixtures and measurement rules before implementation results, use actual external observations where authorized, and count failed attempts and manual interventions. Safety, service, customer value, economics, owner competence and attention are reported separately; no aggregate score selects a winner.

For **EAS-R1**, conduct four distinct comparisons. First test each candidate’s own rights conformance: C2 must honor an admissible charter’s first discriminator and retirement jurisdiction; A/S1 may lawfully defer discretionary inquiry.
