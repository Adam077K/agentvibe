# 01 — Understand the ambition before choosing the system

**Date:** 2026-09-12. **Stage:** Understand. **Status:** framing artifact for subsequent expansion and challenge; no architecture selected and no founder acceptance inferred.

This document reconstructs the ambition from [The vision, the fields, and the sectors](../inputs/THE-VISION-AND-THE-FIELDS.md) and [The path to the vision](../inputs/THE-PATH-TO-THE-VISION.md), read independently of prior architecture plans and other research lanes. The latest directive, supplied through the orchestrator's 2026-09-12 brief, governs conflicts with those historical inputs.

Evidence labels:

- **F — Founder constraint:** a value, ambition, refusal, or current instruction supplied in these inputs.
- **I — Interpretation:** this writer's reconstruction; open to correction.
- **H — Hypothesis:** an empirical claim needing evidence, not a design entitlement.
- **U — Unknown:** something not settled by the inputs.

These labels describe where authority or support comes from. Repetition does not turn H or I into F.

## The company the owner is trying to make possible

**I, grounded in F:** A person, or a small group of people, should be able to decide what is worth doing and have the work of a whole company carried through without personally supervising every step. That includes deciding what to try, learning whether anyone needs it, building and delivering, finding customers, selling, supporting, operating, maintaining, handling money and obligations, changing direction, and closing responsibly.

The intended benefit is that what reaches customers remains connected to what the owner meant, while the owner can give a defensible account of work they did not witness. The account must include uncertainty, failed attempts, omissions, assumptions, stale information, costs, unresolved commitments, and consequences. It must help them steer and correct the company, not merely reassure them that work occurred.

The company should run much of its ordinary work within established authority. The owner supplies direction and the personal choices the system cannot legitimately invent. They remain sufficiently in contact with the business to understand it, recognize an important mistake, change its course, and stand behind its promises. Their attention is treated as finite, so excess work is reduced, deferred, or declined before supervision becomes ceremonial.

The strategy allows many attempts and expects some to fail. Its promise depends on discovering failure early and ending attempts without abandoning customers or creating a growing burden of obligations. More production is useful only when the outcomes justify the total costs and consequences.

**U:** The inputs do not choose the first business, target customer, product category, jurisdiction, legal form, staffing arrangement, spending limits, working hours, or acceptable losses. Examples in this document do not decide them. A general system can be designed while leaving deployment-specific choices explicit; operating authority cannot be fabricated from those blanks.

## Current constraints and historical material

**F:** Scope is the full responsible work system for one owner or a small team across the company lifecycle. Research and design must be independently derived. Counts, technology choices, organizing structures, and the shape of delegation are open. A fixed agent hierarchy or an architecture inherited from earlier plans is disallowed as a starting premise. No final architecture is selected at this stage, and ordinary progress must not depend on repeated founder approval.

**F:** The required planning order is **Understand → Expand → at least three competing foundational candidates → independent attacks → synthesis → complete specification → independent review against the vision without privileging the selected architecture → full implementation**. This framing does not substitute for the later stages or claim that any have passed.

**I:** Historical question counts are evidence of a substantial inventory, not a proof of completeness or a target number of components. Historical engine names, sealed-lane procedures, four-question rounds, specific tools, and a signoff step describe an earlier process. They cannot silently determine this system's topology or recreate routine approval gates under the current directive. The useful continuing discipline is to research what can be researched and ask the founder only for a decision that depends on their personal authority or values.

## Invariants to carry into every candidate

These are **F**, with **I** operational readings that still need precise acceptance criteria.

| Invariant | What a future candidate must make possible | Observable contradiction |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve intent | Keep work connected to the desired outcome, reasons, scope, and refusals; support explicit changes of direction | A local instruction changes the company's purpose without acknowledgement |
| Give a truthful account | Distinguish observation, testimony, inference, assumption, unknown, and unresolved outcome | A successful task report hides a failed action or an untested claim |
| Bound authority by consequences | Keep allowed exposure tied to what actions can affect, including cumulative effects | Past success alone grants broader spending, publication, or data access |
| Keep the owner competent | Support understanding and correction of the actual business over time | The owner cannot explain important promises, risks, or why customers buy |
| Respect finite attention | Make ordinary decisions inside authority and respond deliberately when human capacity is unavailable | The work queue grows by turning every uncertainty into an approval request |
| Cover the whole lifecycle | Support real attempts, steady service, maintenance, transition, and responsible closure | A venture can launch but cannot settle what remains owed when it ends |
| Preserve human answerability | Make responsibility and the limits of delegation understandable | An agent's completion claim substitutes for someone standing behind an outcome |
| Permit correction and refusal | Allow the company to challenge assumptions, stop work, and change its mind without falsifying history | Persistence continues only because work has already begun |

No invariant supplies its own metric, budget, jurisdiction, implementation, or universal priority ordering. Truthfulness and consequence boundaries cannot be traded away merely to make another metric improve. Remaining conflicts need explicit reasoning.

## Tensions that should remain visible

**I — Faithfulness versus learning.** Exact preservation of an early instruction may defeat the reason it was given after facts change. Free reinterpretation may erase the owner's choice. The unresolved requirement is controlled, explainable revision: preserve both what was meant then and why something different is appropriate now.

**I — Autonomy versus authority.** Routine founder approval is disallowed; personal risk appetite cannot be invented. These can coexist if authority can be established and used repeatedly within its real scope. The open problem is recognizing when a seemingly ordinary action crosses that scope, including many individually small actions that accumulate. The file does not prescribe a particular permission mechanism.

**I — Complete account versus finite attention and privacy.** Raw detail can overwhelm, and some information should not be collected. Every summary excludes something. Truthfulness therefore needs an intelligible account of relevant omissions and limits, with access to appropriate evidence, rather than a claim to record everything or expose everyone's data.

**I — Cheap failure versus durable obligations.** It may be cheap to generate an experiment and expensive to withdraw what was sold or promised. Affordability must include support, closure, correction, reputation, and time, not just production. Whether the proposed strategy remains inexpensive is H.

**I — Competence versus time saved.** Handing the owner selected work may preserve judgement but also consume the scarce resource the system promises to save. Useful contact with customers and consequences must be distinguished from repetitive review. The best dose and form are U.

**I — Stable taste versus evolving judgement.** A recognizable standard need not mean that a person can never change. A correction may express learning, a contextual exception, or a new value. Treating every correction as global drift or every habit as permanent preference would both violate the ambition.

**I — One person's intent versus other people's standing.** Customers, partners, employees, suppliers, and successors can have legitimate claims that the founder cannot dissolve by changing a goal. Small-team operation requires explicit handling of competing human instructions and authority. Neither a universal single decision-maker nor majority voting follows from the vision.

**I — Independent truth versus commercial scope.** The older text says the account is the only part worth building; the latest directive calls for a full company system. The present reading makes truthful accounting a requirement throughout the whole system. It does not narrow the task to an audit product or establish whether capabilities should be bought or built.

**I — Useful structure versus premature architecture.** The field map calls subjects “workers,” “memory,” and “orchestration” to make them discussable. Those names can also bias the answer toward a familiar agent organization. Expansion must allow alternatives that satisfy the underlying need without reproducing those nouns as components.

## Responsibilities that cannot be silently assigned to the system

**F** supplies the concern; the distinctions below are **I**. Nondelegable does not mean that the founder must personally perform every associated action.

| Responsibility | Assistance can provide | What assistance cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Decide what is worth wanting | Evidence, possibilities, consequences, experiments | That the owner endorses the end or the life required to pursue it |
| Choose personal exposure | Estimates, alternatives, cumulative exposure analysis | Consent to losses, hours, disclosure, or obligations outside existing authority |
| Stand behind a promise | Fulfillment, monitoring, records, proposed remedies | That the other party accepts a substitution, release, or altered promise |
| Maintain personal relationships where personhood matters | Preparation, scheduling, factual support | That automated attention is an acceptable substitute to the other person |
| Own taste and direction | Examples, comparisons, consistency checks | That an inferred preference overrules a deliberate correction |
| Remain answerable | Traceable decisions and evidence of consequences | A universal legal transfer of responsibility or meaningful accountability by software alone |

**I:** The system's truthfulness is a requirement to establish through evidence and challenge, not a metaphysical claim that only a person can ever verify. Human approval also does not make a false statement true. Licensing, consent, and legal capacity are external constraints to research for the actual deployment, not facts supplied by the sector labels.

**F:** Wanting to avoid intent loss, remain competent, and refuse unbounded exposure does not require proving these hypotheses. **I:** Conversely, the values do not prove feasibility, profitability, or market exclusivity.

## Missing questions and assumptions to carry forward

**U — Founder or other authorized human choices:** What kind of business and daily life should this serve? Which ends and relationships must remain personally chosen? Who may set or change limits when several people share the company? What should happen during prolonged absence, incapacity, or succession? These are not requests for an immediate questionnaire; many become necessary only when a concrete consequential choice depends on them.

**U — Researchable questions:** Which failures escape an apparently truthful account? How does observation coverage change when tools or vendors change? How can the owner contest a summary without reading its entire history? What obligations remain after a product, customer relationship, or company closes? Which forms of information must be forgotten even when retention would make reconstruction easier? What failures does aggregation create across ventures that appear individually safe?

**U — Questions about interpretation:** When is an instruction an idea, a standing rule, an authorized task, or an external commitment? What distinguishes changed intent from correction of an earlier misunderstanding? How far should a correction propagate? Who is allowed to say a goal was satisfied when outcomes depend on another person? Can the system represent unresolved disagreement without quietly choosing a winner?

**U — Feasibility assumptions:** Relevant consequences can be detected early enough to bound them; external actions leave enough evidence for a useful account; reduced supervision can coexist with maintained competence; a small team can sustain the residual human work; and responsible closure does not erase the economics of experimentation. None is established by this framing.

## Non-goals and next-stage handoff

**F/I:** The ambition does not require removing all people, approving every action, perfect knowledge, unlimited autonomy, a named stack, a fixed hierarchy, or automatic entry into every industry. It is not fulfilled by producing only reports, only software, or only a governance wrapper around unfinished company capabilities. Phased implementation is a means to test and deliver the whole scope, not permission to quietly substitute a smaller final objective.

**I:** Expand should revisit the inventory by company outcomes, lifecycle, stakeholders, failures, and consequences; add missing concerns and remove misleading divisions. Subsequent foundational candidates must differ in the assumptions by which they coordinate responsible work, not merely in names or vendors. Independent attacks should be able to reject the premises, not only find missing features. Synthesis must preserve unresolved disagreements, and the complete specification must make expected behavior reviewable across ordinary operation and failure. Those are obligations for later artifacts, not decisions made here.

An architecture-neutral recognition test is available now: give only this framing to someone unfamiliar with the repository and ask what the company makes possible, what the owner does, what it refuses, what happens when a venture fails, and what remains unknown. Their answer should describe the intended company rather than a particular implementation. **This test has not been run**, and no founder endorsement of this reconstruction is claimed.
