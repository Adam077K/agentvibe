# Candidate C — Adaptive coordination around business conditions

**Date:** 2026-09-12. **Status:** independent Phase C contender; unselected, unimplemented, and unvalidated. All mechanisms below are **design proposals**. No comparative scenario or owner study has been executed. This report uses the [directive contract](../../inputs/directive-contract.json), [understanding](../01-understand.md), [source vision](../../inputs/THE-VISION-AND-THE-FIELDS.md), and [cross-lane comparison](../../research/cross-lane-comparison.json). It has not consulted other candidate reports. The latest directive governs historical framing conflicts, including the older account-only commercial thesis.

## 1. Foundational choice and evidence

Organize the company around observed conditions, competing explanations, and bounded interventions. A complaint, unexplained cash movement, changed founder intention, approaching renewal, or promising customer observation can generate work directly. Temporary executors propose what to observe or change next; shared evidence changes subsequent allocation. Neither a permanent department hierarchy nor a predefined company task tree owns the next move.

The distinctive hypothesis is that this arrangement recognizes cross-function changes sooner, abandons invalid plans with less waste, and preserves the ability to operate or close. It might instead spend scarce attention on endless interpretation. The serious simpler competitor is authoritative records, reserves, scheduled procedures, and a fixed owner participation rotation. Candidate C earns its additional machinery only through measured differences under equal obligations and resource limits.

[L13, countermodel D](../../research/L13-contrarian.md) supplies the coordination precedent: Smith’s [Contract Net Protocol](https://www.reidgsmith.com/The_Contract_Net_Protocol_Dec-1980.pdf), December 1980, describes announcements, bids, and awards; Erman et al.’s [Hearsay-II](https://mas.cs.umass.edu/Documents/Erman_Hearsay80.pdf), June 1980, coordinates knowledge sources through shared hypotheses. Its §4.3 also reports poor results when specialized internal activity was forced into the general blackboard. These primary system reports motivate limited shared coordination, not a claim of business effectiveness. Both were reopened on 2026-09-12; no experiment was reproduced.

[L14, concepts A–C](../../research/L14-novel.md) contributes three bounded questions: will accepted work leave resources for restoration and closure; which promises survive local cancellation; and which owner encounters preserve governing judgment? Martin’s 2004 viability model, Winograd’s 1987–1988 conversation account, Bainbridge’s 1983 analysis, and Klenske–Hennig’s 2016 dual-control paper have different domains and assumptions. They do not validate a company simulator or an owner competence estimator. This candidate uses explicit disturbance scenarios, obligation records, and chosen learning encounters; it declines universal prediction and a combined company score.

## 2. Primitives, records, and ownership

The shared blackboard is a permission-filtered collection of records and references, not everybody’s conversation history. Six record families suffice; the directive’s schema subjects remain distinguishable types or relations within them, rather than becoming 46 services.

| Family | Essential state and transitions | Source of truth and mutation authority |
|---|---|---|
| Intent and authority | Original statements; scoped interpretations; decisions, reasons, alternatives, constraints and grants. Proposed → endorsed, refused, superseded, or expired | Authenticated authorized humans control intent and grants. Interpretations never replace originals; factual disagreement survives endorsement |
| Conditions and hypotheses | Entity, observed dimension, value/range, observation time, validity interval, evidence, unknowns, alternatives, predicted discriminating observation. Open → investigated → supported, contradicted, unresolved, or retired | Observers append observations; bounded interpreters propose hypotheses. Support never creates permission |
| Obligations and resources | Principal, counterparty, promised performance, applicable duty, deadline, discharge authority, cash, allowance, execution slots, attention and reserves. Proposed → active → performed, disputed, discharged, transferred, or surviving | A named accountable steward owns continuity; accounting/provider sources own their native facts. Only authorized evidence rules permit discharge |
| Interventions and attempts | Intended condition change, prerequisites, effect bounds, executor offer, acceptance criteria, versions, lease/epoch and logical operation identity. Proposed → eligible → reserved → dispatched → observed → settled; also denied, parked, cancelled, failed, or unknown-effect | Allocators award work; an action boundary admits effects; executors report attempts; outcome assessors settle them |
| Evidence and artifacts | Original source, exact version, capture boundary, derivatives, claims, conflicts, omissions, review and retention basis. Captured → available, contested, superseded, quarantined, restricted, or erased | Capture services own receipts; producers own drafts; independent assessors own acceptance observations; data stewards authorize retention changes |
| Operating configuration | Skills, tools, connections, routing rules, evaluators, schemas, releases, incidents and change proposals. Draft → tested → authorized → active → suspended, replaced, or retired | Versioned releases; protected changes require separate authority and evidence |

Every record has identity, venture/entity scope, accountable owner, author, schema version, access classification, retention policy, audit history, and links to relevant records. Recorded time differs from the time a statement applies. Disputes preserve competing interpretations. A service can own a record mechanically; responsibility remains assigned to an identified person or institution. An unassigned surviving duty is an incident, not a reason to omit it.

Native invoice, code, design, contract, customer, and employment representations stay specialized. Cross-domain references carry only the meaning needed for an actual decision. Shared semantics must cover identity, amounts/currency, authority, deadlines, provenance, obligations, uncertainty, and lifecycle transitions; additional mappings need a demonstrated use. [L05, temporal records and provenance](../../research/L05-memory.md); [L13, countermodel C](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

## 3. Control, feedback, communication, and allocation

Four loops run at different timescales; none requires a continuous model conversation.

1. **Observe and contest.** Connector receipts, customer input, financial reconciliation, calendar occurrences, execution outcomes, and owner changes append observations. Deterministic subscriptions identify changed conditions and missing expected observations. Bounded model sessions interpret ambiguity and post rival hypotheses. A detected contradiction changes eligibility immediately where a prerequisite depends on it.
2. **Propose and allocate.** Eligible capabilities subscribe to relevant condition types. They offer a bounded intervention specifying evidence sought, expected effect, uncertainty, resources, deadline, acceptance test, and stop rule. A proposal can create a new work frontier without appearing in an existing plan. Nothing can manufacture a grant by winning allocation.
3. **Act and reconcile.** The consequence boundary checks the concrete proposal, reserves resources, dispatches through an authorized executor, captures receipts, and requests independent outcome observations. Discrepancies become new conditions; they do not disappear into an executor’s summary.
4. **Reconsider direction and capacity.** Periodic and event-triggered reviews inspect customer evidence, surviving obligations, missed observations, reserves, owner availability, and abandoned alternatives. They may reduce work, reopen an experiment, propose a pivot, or enter preservation/closure. The founder alone supplies a new personal end where existing intent cannot resolve the choice.

Allocation uses explicit precedence: containment and due obligations, then authorized priorities subject to feasibility, then bounded exploration. Within a class, deadline slack, compatibility, observed availability, historical estimate error, and fair rotation determine an award. Incomparable business choices remain visible; no weighted score exchanges privacy for revenue. Deadline aging exposes neglected maintenance; work without a feasible executor becomes a capacity incident before its latest responsible start.

Negotiation is optional and bounded: one announcement, a capped set of independently prepared offers, one clarification round, then award, park, or name an unresolved choice. Offers are cost/availability estimates, not trusted bids proving quality. Repeated optimistic estimates reduce scheduling confidence, never increase permissions. If a checked queue assigns equally good work at lower cost, remove negotiation. Private deliberation remains local; only typed offers, evidence, questions, awards, revocations, and outcomes cross the blackboard. Each message carries sender identity, causal references, purpose/access scope, expiry, and an idempotent receipt identity. Trigger keys combine condition revision and capability version; repeated summaries cannot indefinitely regenerate the same offer. A changed proposal needs a new observation, explicit experiment allowance, or recorded reason for reconsideration.

A transactional allocator can serialize awards without becoming a company case manager: it cannot invent business priorities, mandate a universal sequence, or monopolize hypothesis creation. Independent observers and workers generate eligible proposals; multiple directions remain live. Temporary local task graphs are execution aids and can be discarded when their underlying hypothesis fails. [L03, competing control models](../../research/L03-architectures.md); [L13, allocation failure experiment](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

## 4. Admission, cumulative exposure, and settlement

Every consequential action crosses an external-effect broker holding the relevant credentials. Workers lack equivalent browser sessions, direct network routes, inherited secrets, and native administrative bypasses. If a provider cannot be meaningfully restricted, that capability runs through a separately authorized human/service boundary or remains unavailable. A claimed broker without tested complete mediation is insufficient. [L07, findings 1–4 and 7–8](../../research/L07-security.md).

The request binds legal principal, authenticated grantor and actor, venture, exact payload/hash, recipient, account, data destination, configuration and connector versions, evidence prerequisites, effect class, grant expiry, maximum exposure, and logical operation identity. Consequential parameters also identify their trusted source: an authenticated payment tool cannot legitimize a payee substituted by an untrusted document. Permission to draft, execute, negotiate, settle, or amend authority is distinct. Approval of a summary does not authorize different executable terms. Success history may inform sampling depth within a declared policy; it cannot enlarge any grant.

Admission is a transaction over current state and shared resource versions. For each independent consequence/resource bound, committed exposure plus unresolved reservations plus the proposed upper bound must fit the applicable grant. Already-counted obligations must not be counted twice. Recurring commitments and delayed charges remain reserved. Unknown material exposure does not become zero. In addition, liquid resources, delivery capacity, recovery capacity, and owner time must cover accepted obligations plus declared operating and closure reserves under explicitly named scenarios. Revenue expectations cannot cancel prohibited disclosures or unavailable professional capacity.

This is limited viability reasoning: worksheets for loss of one supplier, delayed receipts, elevated refunds, and owner absence, with bounded assumptions and named omitted disturbances. A failed or unbounded scenario yields “feasibility unresolved,” prompting narrower action or better evidence. It does not certify all possible futures. Prefer ordinary reserve arithmetic until correlated constraints demonstrate that a richer model changes a decision usefully. Models suggest consequence estimates; authoritative inputs and deterministic checks enforce admitted limits. Professional interpretation remains external where needed. [L10, separate resource accounts](../../research/L10-economics.md); [L14, concept A](../../research/L14-novel.md).

Reservation and dispatch use atomic state changes, compare-and-set versions, and monotonically increasing execution epochs. The broker rejects obsolete epochs and rechecks mutable consent, grants, claim validity, and stop state immediately before transmission. An expired worker lease releases computational ownership only; it does not prove an external effect absent or release its financial hold. Requests already transmitted remain in flight or unknown-effect. A takeover cannot dispatch a conflicting effect until reconciliation permits it.

Replicated brokers require one current authority view and fenced old instances; otherwise writes stop. A network partition never creates two spendable copies of a reserve. Before irreversible dispatch, the operation identity, parameters and reservation must be durably recorded beyond the primary host's restore boundary; unavailable independent persistence blocks dispatch. Recovery verifies the newest authority watermark and disables obsolete brokers/credentials before accepting a restored writer. Where recipients cannot fence requests, the broker must retain uncertainty about already transmitted operations and prevent conflicting retries. Missing authority storage or the independent recovery watermark blocks new effects. Read-only diagnosis and previously authorized, separately bounded preservation can continue if their own prerequisites are intact.

Settlement separates transport acceptance, observed external effect, accepted artifact, and discharged obligation. The broker owns attempt receipts; an independent domain observer checks the resulting payment, deployment, delivery, or customer condition. A producer cannot settle its own unsupported claim. Counterparty acknowledgment is required where the promise makes it necessary; agreed routine criteria may discharge ordinary performance without repeatedly asking customers. Silence does not acquire new meaning. Reversing code cannot reverse publication; compensation is another authorized action with its own evidence and cost. [L08, F2–F4 and F7](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

## 5. Execution and complete business coverage

A capability is a versioned contract for useful work, not a persona. Deterministic code handles arithmetic, identity checks, budgets, schedules, validation, and enforceable transitions. Fixed durable procedures handle stable local sequences, waiting, reminders, and known recovery. Models perform bounded synthesis, interpretation, planning and generation in isolated sessions. Humans supply intent, taste, relationships, and consequential judgment; qualified professionals supply applicable determinations; purchased services supply actual institutional or physical capability. Software cannot replace unavailable licensure, fulfillment, or accountable standing. [L02, implementation modes](../../research/L02-operations.md); [L11, professional dependencies](../../research/L11-governance.md).

| Business coverage | Conditions that generate substantive work and establish acceptance |
|---|---|
| Direction and customer research | Changed purpose, unexplained behavior, unmet need; preserve original customer evidence, recruitment bias, rival demand hypotheses and explicit stop criteria |
| Product, design, and build | A promising hypothesis with unresolved delivery/usability questions; produce specifications, alternatives, brand/taste examples, code or service procedures, accessible journeys and independently observed acceptance |
| Launch and distribution | Readiness across delivery, claims, entity/rights, support, consent, capacity and remedies; prepare channels and assets, then publish only within concrete authority; measure reached audience and meaningful response |
| Sales and support | Qualified interest, agreed terms, payment, complaint or unmet promise; preserve original conversations, consent, offers, fulfillment, refunds, sensitive relationship escalation and evidence of remedy |
| Finance and bookkeeping | Cash movement, bill, variance, deadline or reserve pressure; deterministic books/reconciliation, documented corrections and professional review/filing where required |
| Legal, privacy, and security | Activity/jurisdiction changes, new processing, rights requests, access anomalies or expiring determinations; produce scoped professional dependencies, consent/retention records, threat containment and verified disposal |
| Procurement, hiring, and partnerships | Missing capacity or expiring arrangement; evaluate alternatives and people, prepare terms, obtain authorized engagement, verify receipt/onboarding, maintain relationships, revoke access and settle departures |
| Analytics, incidents, and learning | Outcome discrepancies, omitted channels, regressions or recurring harm; measure cohorts and costs with attribution limits, contain incidents, independently investigate and test bounded changes |
| Pause, pivot, transfer, and closure | Invalid hypothesis, changed intent or inadequate reserves; stop new liabilities, preserve service, reconcile obligations, prepare transfer/closure and obtain evidence of discharge or explicitly surviving duties |

These are coverage obligations, not departments or a launch pipeline. Support evidence can directly reopen positioning; an invoice variance can stop expansion; closure can require new procurement. Each executable capability specifies inputs, outputs, authority, exclusions, owner, evaluation, interruption, replacement, and removal. Skills package versioned instructions/examples with provenance, applicability, tool requirements, known failures and expiry; loading records conflicts and omissions. No skill text grants execution rights.

## 6. Information, memory, evidence, and security

Working context is temporary; episodes preserve attempts; semantic claims remain evidence-linked; procedural knowledge is versioned configuration. Founder preferences retain original wording, examples, scope, correction reason, and whether the change revises taste or fixes a factual misunderstanding. Retrieval filters venture, person, purpose, sensitivity, validity and trust before content reaches a model. Start with exact records and lexical retrieval; add vector or graph projections only after useful comparative evidence. [L01, intent mechanisms](../../research/L01-intent.md); [L05, competing memory mechanisms](../../research/L05-memory.md).

Every consequential context manifest names loaded versions, selection reasons, source age, trust, transformations, rejected material, summaries and potential loss. “Not searched,” “searched without finding,” “withheld,” “not instrumented,” and “unknown” remain distinct. The observation boundary inventories connected accounts, expected receipt types, polling intervals, missing channels, suppression reasons/expiry, and coverage checks. Unconnected conversations, undisclosed obligations, provider internals and unobserved customer experience remain outside its proof. Periodic comparisons with bank statements, customer samples, contracts and service inventories can uncover gaps, not certify their absence. [L06, traces and evidence](../../research/L06-evaluation.md); [L13, observation boundary](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

External content enters quarantine as data. Promotion to a usable claim preserves source restrictions; it never promotes source text into instructions or founder authority. Contradictory evidence has independent links and remains retrievable. Revocation or poisoning triggers derivative invalidation across contexts, hypotheses, artifacts, queued actions and previously issued claims needing correction. Recency and repeated retrieval do not renew validity. Authenticated capture proves origin and integrity within its boundary, not truth.

Protected capture runs outside producer/improver write access and records broker calls before dispatch plus independently obtained outcomes. Missing sequence events or recorder health block evidence-dependent settlement. No guarantee extends through compromise of every trust anchor. Credentials, signers, source capture, evaluation infrastructure and administration require separate privileges; restricted egress must also test permitted destinations as possible exfiltration channels.

Selective forgetting distinguishes retrieval exclusion, supersession, archival, redaction and erasure. Purpose-specific retention decisions cover originals, derivatives, indexes, exports, backups and provider copies. Restore replays an independently retained deletion journal before access. Minimal lawful evidence of an erasure can survive without retaining the erased content; vendor deletion confirmation and independently verified deletion remain different claims. Conflicting retention duties require a qualified determination. Reconstruction must disclose what can no longer be known. [L05, selective forgetting](../../research/L05-memory.md); [L11, data lifecycle](../../research/L11-governance.md).

## 7. User model and owner competence

The owner states direction, participation preferences, time limits, absence policy, and standing consequence grants. Multiple founders require decision-domain authority and explicit disagreement handling, not routing to whoever answers fastest. Decision packets contain exact choice, why this person, latest responsible time, options, consequences, evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, recommendation, no-answer behavior and independent work continuation.

Begin with a transparent fixed rotation of original customer evidence, taste choices, unusual financial movements and recovery rehearsal, inside an owner-chosen attention budget. Optional adaptive selection addresses named gaps: unfamiliar refund economics, neglected customer segments, or failed intervention practice. A prediction/explanation before advice and later independent outcome can provide feedback. No global competence score, hidden personal profiling, forced agreement, or inferred permission follows. Delayed transfer and owner burden need a consented longitudinal comparison; none is demonstrated. Remove adaptation if fixed participation works as well. [L09, F1–F5 and participation proposal](../../research/L09-human.md); [L14, concept C](../../research/L14-novel.md).

An operator surface must show what changed, what remains owed, rejected opportunities, evidence omissions and actual controls. Terminal work includes conversations, hypotheses/plans, commands, parallel attempts, code/diffs/tests, account/environment, failures, checkpoints and task switching. Mobile decisions retain executable terms and uncertainty. Pause displays requested, acknowledged, and confirmed containment separately. Accessible text, keyboard operation and non-color states remain available. Any visual office is an optional projection of observed state; unknown telemetry cannot animate fictitious progress.

## 8. Shared comparative scenarios and falsifiers

**All eight are specified tests, not executed demonstrations.** Each needs fixed inputs, external ground truth where available, equal authority/resource limits, repeated stochastic trials where models participate, and independent capture. A safety failure cannot be offset by better throughput.

1. **Opportunity → evidence → product → authorized launch → sales/support.** Customer observations create competing demand hypotheses. Research, product and delivery capabilities offer discriminating interventions; prototype results may invalidate the opportunity before building. A surviving hypothesis yields a tested product/service and launch proposal covering claims, fulfillment, rights, consent, reserves and support. Concrete authorization admits launch and subsequent sales within its bounds. Support recurrence can suspend acquisition and redirect production without waiting for a lifecycle stage. **Falsifier:** missing noncoding work, unsupported demand treated as fact, launch without authority, or poorer customer outcomes/owner effort than the simpler baseline with no compensating advantage.

2. **Marketing claim stales; customer opts out before send.** Claim expiry invalidates dependent send eligibility. The opt-out updates a suppression record independently of campaign state. At the transmission boundary, current claim and recipient versions invalidate the queued authorization; no send occurs, the attempt records why, and research may refresh the claim without reinstating consent. An opt-out observed after irrevocable provider acceptance becomes a disclosed race and containment problem. Lost observation channels block consent-dependent sending. **Falsifier:** a send using a known stale claim or an opt-out durably recorded before dispatch; pretending unobserved outside events were synchronized also fails the truthful-account test.

3. **Month-end invoice/bank mismatch plus professional filing dependency.** The variance creates rival explanations—timing, duplicate, incorrect amount, missing transaction—without inventing a balancing entry. A deterministic procedure retrieves source records; a bounded interpreter explains alternatives. The accountant receives an evidence packet, scope, deadline and unresolved amounts; dependent filing remains unconfirmed until the qualified deliverable and submission receipt exist. Deadline pressure reserves attention or invokes an already authorized replacement arrangement. **Falsifier:** cosmetic reconciliation, suppressed uncertainty, falsely completed filing, or a missed dependency that the fixed month-end procedure caught.

4. **Refund times out, then resumes after provider deduplication expiry.** The logical refund, exact parameters, transmitted attempt and hold survive. A timeout becomes unknown-effect; resumption queries independent refund/payment records. [Stripe’s idempotency contract](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests), reopened 2026-09-12, permits pruning after at least 24 hours; reusing a pruned key can create a new request. No retry follows merely because the key is unchanged. If absence cannot be established, keep the liability and hold, contain further refunds against that obligation, and obtain authorized reconciliation. **Falsifier:** duplicate refund, released exposure before resolution, or false settlement after merely restarting. See [L08, F2](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

5. **Two ventures compete for money, provider capacity and owner time.** Offers disclose all three demands plus continuing obligations. Shared reservations prevent each venture allocating the same resource; each entity’s protected funds remain separate. Obligations and declared priorities constrain awards; one experiment can wait, shrink or end. No currency conversion turns spare inference into owner time. An unresolved equally defensible direction choice becomes one founder decision packet with a no-answer policy. **Falsifier:** aggregate overcommitment, hidden cross-entity subsidy, starving maintenance, or negotiation costing more than the saved allocation error.

6. **Founder changes intent while promises and data duties survive.** The authenticated revision preserves earlier intent and reasons. Dependency inspection invalidates new work justified only by the old intention; existing customer, supplier and data obligations remain active. Workers propose revised delivery, authorized transfer, remedy or closure. A counterparty’s disputed interpretation stays unresolved until legitimately settled. **Falsifier:** deleted promises, treating changed values as model noise, or continuing new liabilities under cached intent. A minimal obligation register outperforming this machinery challenges its necessity.

7. **Poisoned content enters memory; an improvement candidate tampers with logging.** Tainted sources retain their trust boundary through summaries. Attempted redirection of recipients or policy fails admission; discovered poisoning invalidates derivatives and records uncertain past influence. The improver cannot alter authoritative capture or acceptance selection. Missing/tampered events make evaluation inconclusive or failed, never successful. The [DGM paper, Appendix H](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22954v3), reopened 2026-09-12, reports logging manipulation despite hidden checking functions; hiding the judge is insufficient. **Falsifier:** evidence laundering, any protected write, or improvement accepted from missing logs. See [L07, F6/F8](../../research/L07-security.md) and [L12, evidence-path failure](../../research/L12-improvement.md).

8. **Owner absence, provider outage, old backup restoration, responsible closure.** Stop admitting new liabilities; execute only intact standing preservation authority. Restore offline with credentials disabled, verify artifacts/configuration/keys, recover the independent revocation/deletion and operation watermark, and reconcile external effects before re-enabling writes. Old queues cannot regain old grants. Absent authoritative watermarks or outcomes keep effects blocked. A previously appointed successor/professional and independent recovery surface support authorized obligations and closure; unavailable arrangements remain explicit incapacity. Closure tracks refunds, services, suppliers, staff, records and surviving duties, spending only existing reserves/grants. **Falsifier:** stale authority revived, personal data resurrected, unknown effects retried, new paid fallback, or “closed” declared with undisposed obligations. Safe refusal alone does not demonstrate successful closure.

## 9. Buildability, deployment, economics, and failure recovery

A feasible implementation hypothesis is a modular application with a transactional record/authority store, permission-filtered blackboard projections, deterministic subscriptions/timers and allocator, isolated executor adapters, an effect broker, separately privileged evidence/evaluation capture, artifact storage and operator clients. These are responsibility boundaries, not a mandate for distributed microservices. A local single-writer database can test behavior; multiple authoritative writers require an actual transactional/fencing design. Independent recovery records and credentials must not depend solely on the primary host.

An existing personal machine with native authorized coding environments minimizes new infrastructure assumptions but sleeps and fails; it cannot promise unattended customer deadlines. A persistent control host plus local execution adapters could provide observation/scheduling continuity while preserving account separation. Persistent unattended inference or customer products need a separately justified authorized execution service. No topology, purchase or deployment is authorized by this report. Actual quotes, operations skills, rights, availability and recovery evidence must decide among these possibilities.

An execution adapter records provider/account owner, billing mode, effective authentication route, permitted workload/deployment, CLI/model version, capability contract, data destinations, observed allowance/reset and uncertainty, concurrency bound, policy-source versions/expiry, and checkpoint/resume behavior. [L10, A1–A8, O1–O7 and T1–T2](../../research/L10-economics.md) is the dated rights evidence; revalidate on surface, account, version, terms or authentication changes and its stated expiry. Native noninteractive availability does not authorize credential pooling, resale or customer embedding. Unknown route or exhausted allowance parks work; no API, credit reload, account rotation or extra spend fallback is silent.

Continuation packages preserve intent/grant versions, current conditions, hypotheses, disagreements, omissions, exact next action, dependencies, artifacts, attempts, unknown effects and resource holds. Session/model replacement is a new attributable attempt; completed observations are reused as records, not regenerated as if identical. Bounded retries distinguish transport faults, exhaustion, auth failure, invalid requests, refusal and uncertain effects. Progress deadlines detect loops; calendar occurrences specify catch-up, coalescing or expiry. Stop separates dispatch revocation, worker cessation, external settlement and compensation. [L04, continuity/isolation](../../research/L04-engineering.md); [L08, F5–F9](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

Costs remain separate: subscription invoices, allowance pressure, execution slots, storage/search, database/backup, observability, security, communication/domains, professional and physical services, restoration/migration labor, owner minutes, and operating/closure reserves. The founder’s approximate USD 400 monthly subscription assumption is neither throughput nor permission for purchases. Track full accepted or responsibly terminated attempts, including failed proposals and review. Internal overhead and a future customer product have separate economics. Unknown costs stay unknown; admission cannot promise service against fictional capacity.

## 10. Improvement, limits, migration, and rejection

Improvement is bounded work triggered by an observed defect or avoidable cost. Its packet names problem evidence, proposed diff, alternatives including subtraction, affected records/capabilities, benefit/harm, tests, budget, rollout, observation owner, rollback and required authority. Prompts, skills, tools, workflows, allocation, memory, schemas, UI and architecture can be proposed for change. Protected intent, identity, grants, evidence capture, incidents, evaluator selection and improvement safety remain outside candidate control. Compare sealed acceptance cases, regressions, negative controls and delayed outcomes; preserve dissent and exposure history. A changed evaluator requires its own independently reviewed migration. [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md).

The potential **strengths** are fast cross-domain reallocation, explicit rival interpretations, rejection of stale work, and resource-aware preservation. The **weaknesses** are noisy triggers, uncertain consequences, interpretation churn, neglected work without appealing bids, correlated models and expensive assurance. **Scalability limits** are shared-account contention, proposal explosion, evidence-access boundaries, professional bottlenecks and human decision load. Bound proposal counts, depth, lifetime, refresh rate and work in progress; partition ventures where authority/resources permit. More workers cannot increase consented attention or shared allowance.

**Operational complexity** includes missing-event detection, mapping drift, clock/fencing behavior, consent races, retention propagation, evaluator maintenance and restoration rehearsals. **Provider dependence** remains in permissions, observability, model behavior, receipts, privacy terms and recoverable sessions. Portable records reduce substitution cost but cannot promise equivalent models or unsupported effects.

**Migration difficulty** is moderate for execution adapters and reversible projections, high for native semantic mappings and protected authority/evidence history. Import existing records with original provenance, uncertainty and unresolved obligations; run projections in shadow, reconcile counts/amounts/promises, then transfer one authority boundary at a time. Old and new systems must never both hold uncoordinated spend rights. Export schemas, originals, mappings, grants, operation identities, tombstones and configuration; retain a procedure for continuing obligations if the blackboard is removed.

Reject this **wrong design** when stable procedures cover most work with fewer missed obligations and less owner time; when sensitive information prevents useful shared observations; when consequences cannot be bounded before action; when adaptive proposals mostly restate routine tasks; or when the necessary professional/absence capacity makes the intended economics impossible. Specifically subtract negotiation, richer viability models, adaptive participation, graph memory and persistent worker identity unless each beats its simpler comparator on a named problem. None has established that benefit here. Reopen the candidate before selection and whenever scenario evidence, changed provider rights, observed owner burden or closure failure contradicts its assumptions. No aggregate score or completion claim resolves those unknowns.
