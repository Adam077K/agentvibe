## 1. Scope, evidence, and responsibility

The protected fault model includes primary-host loss or rollback; duplicated, delayed, reordered or lost messages; process crashes and partitions; stale workers; malicious model output and retrieved content; a compromised ordinary worker or improver; and compromise of one admitted adapter within its demonstrated native permissions. Safety can require unavailable effects. Useful service during the declared disruptions must be separately demonstrated.

The assumed surviving roots are the release/authorization service, its independent recovery storage and keys, controlled egress, and a separately reachable recovery administrator. Evidence claims additionally require their declared independent observation and interpretation roots. A compromised adapter may violate its native scope until provider containment succeeds; internal grants cannot constrain a stolen provider credential beyond actual provider enforcement. Admission must declare that exposure. Compromise of an issuer invalidates claims relying on its grants; compromise of release enforcement invalidates prevention claims; loss or compromise of every recovery root defeats trustworthy reconstruction. Malicious provider administrators, malicious ultimate company administrators, undisclosed off-platform promises, and host/kernel escapes beyond the tested isolation boundary remain exclusions. These actors or failures can bypass the available enforcement or observation roots; this design has no stronger surviving authority to promise protection. No claim survives every trust root failing together.

Independence names a failure domain: another directory, process, database replica, model, or account sharing a recovery administrator is insufficient against that administrator. The current [R13 observation](../../research/repository-observations.md#r13--local-isolation-tool-availability) records macOS `sandbox-exec` availability and absent container commands; it establishes no tested containment. Separate storage administration, recoverable keys, constrained native roles, and an independently reachable recovery surface are operational prerequisites. They are not installed mechanisms. Stronger infrastructure may be justified by the selected threat boundary; its cost and operating competence require evidence.

Research support is scoped. [L07](../../research/L07-security.md) supplies mediation and information-flow concerns; [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md) distinguishes local transactions, external effects, fencing and recovery; [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md) supplies evidence-path attacks. Recheck them before integration and on permission, version, retention, or semantic change, and no later than 2026-12-12. Every trace and falsifying test here is synthetic and unexecuted.

## 2. Durable effects, obligations, and recovery frontiers

Choose independent pre-dispatch durability for all three candidates. A destination-only reconstruction alternative remains possible in a later contract version, but is not admitted without demonstrated complete enumeration and preserved meaning over the maximum outage interval. Neither search success nor provider idempotency is that proof. Stripe documents that a key may be pruned after at least 24 hours and reused as a new request. [Stripe idempotency](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests).

The `RecoveryEnvelope` contains operation and attempt identities; business conflict keys; actual principal/account/channel; canonical executable parameters or independently recoverable encrypted bytes; grant, policy, configuration and prerequisite versions; reservations; expected external evidence; and potential new obligations with original terms, beneficiaries, deadlines, custodian and discharge conditions. A hash without surviving content and keys is insufficient. A quote or outward promise must preserve possible future delivery, refund and support duties before transmission, even when immediate money movement is zero. Uncertain acceptance leaves a potential duty to resolve; it does not remove it.

The envelope is acknowledged by an independently administered continuity store outside the declared primary restore boundary before release becomes possible. For the declared loss of the primary host and its backups, the recovery-point objective for released operations, revocations, deletion restrictions and potential obligations is **zero acknowledged records lost**. This is a conditional durability requirement, not a claim of zero loss under total-site or provider destruction. It strengthens A/B and carries forward C’s existing pre-dispatch durability requirement. Synchronous persistence has waiting and failure costs; ordinary asynchronous replication can lose committed transactions at failover. [PostgreSQL 18 replication](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html).

Use an ordered release registry with contiguous committed sequence numbers per independently fenced release scope. Its recovery record is `scope, generation, committed_sequence, prefix_digest, membership_version, key_version`. A signed older prefix proves integrity of that prefix, not current completeness. A/C can keep their common transactional authority model and implement this as its independently durable release journal. B uses the registry described in section 3. The registry retains envelope bytes and every release decision; an envelope alone never grants dispatch.

Normal sequence is `draft → held → independently-recorded → released → observed/unknown-effect → reconciled`. Denial or authoritative abort before release can return holds. A released attempt retains its hold and possible obligations until reconciliation, even if the process crashes before it might have transmitted. Receipts arriving after release append to the independent record before allowing consequential settlement or reuse of capacity. Lost settlement evidence may conservatively restore an already completed effect as uncertain; it must never restore available capacity from an unverified success summary.

Recovery has the following required order:

1. Disable egress and restored credentials. Obtain independent confirmation that old gateways and descendant native execution routes are fenced. Issuing a newer internal epoch cannot by itself stop an old native credential.
2. Read the live registry through its authoritative consistency boundary. Seal generation `g` against further releases and obtain its final committed frontier. Fetch the complete registry membership, including channels enrolled after the restored backup, and all records through each sealed frontier. Disjoint scopes use a frontier vector; a timestamp is not a substitute.
3. Compare primary snapshots with these prefixes; verify bytes, schemas, keys, gaps and operation/obligation indexes. Restore reservations pessimistically. Replay every current authority and deletion restriction before retrieval, indexing or release. Deletion applies even where a previous envelope retained source material.
4. Reconcile every released operation and potential obligation against the destination under its evidence contract. Preserve ambiguous effects, missing response intervals and externally observed operations absent from the registry. The latter is a mediation incident, not a harmless import difference.
5. Issue fresh scoped execution authority only for reconciled conflict domains after recovery acceptance. Other scopes may continue when proven disjoint and independently fenced. The application cannot certify its own completeness.

If the newest registry cannot be reached, a prefix is missing, a channel’s membership is unknown, or an old gateway cannot be disabled, contain the entire affected account/channel and overlapping business consequences. If their extent cannot be bounded, contain the larger principal scope. This deliberately sacrifices availability. Read-only diagnosis and separately authorized preservation may continue only through intact, nonoverlapping prerequisites; creating a new request ID or importing an old backup never clears uncertainty.

Inbound channels can also create obligations. At a controlled order-acceptance boundary, acknowledge acceptance only after independently recording the order and its duty. For provider-originated events, record feed identity, sequence/cursor, observed intervals and replay guarantees. An unrecoverable feed gap or unobserved acceptance interval keeps channel completeness unknown and suspends new dependent promises. Independent statements and counterparty inquiry under existing authority can discover duties; they cannot prove that an uninstrumented verbal promise never existed.

## 3. B’s cross-issuer release and revocation contract

B retains native custodians, independent issuers and local scheduling. It adds an explicit narrow **release registry** with one linearizable order per bundle scope, independent durability, and fenced writer generations. Overlapping conflict domains and revocation epochs must share that order; partitioning cannot split a shared constraint. A cross-scope bundle waits until its full constraint set has one authoritative order. The exchange remains a transport/projection. The registry cannot mint grants, determine company priorities, sponsor work, reinterpret obligations or spend unused resources. Its authority is to commit or refuse one exact bundle using issuer-authenticated constraints. This is additional common control, with real administration, contention and outage costs.

A bundle contains a closed list of required issuers, determined by the protected effect schema and current `InteractionDetermination`, never by the producer alone. It binds one operation, payload, conflict set, prerequisite versions and expiry. Resource issuers and authorities for recipient permission, claims and data use publish enforceable revocation epochs to the registry. A native change requiring immediate enforcement must enter this order before its boundary reports the restriction effective. Otherwise that integration must disclose ingestion delay or remain ineligible for the stronger contract. Zanzibar provides a primary example of making authorization ordering explicit; it is not an implementation of this proposed bundle protocol. [Zanzibar system report](https://research.google/pubs/zanzibar-googles-consistent-global-authorization-system/).

The protocol is:

1. A sponsor creates `Bundle(open)` in the registry, with recovery envelope and fixed participant set. Each issuer validates its current local grant and atomically freezes the required amount or permission condition as `Prepared(bundle, grant, epoch, payload_digest, resource_slice, expiry)`. Before emitting its signed prepare, the governing grant and frozen slice must be independently recoverable under the issuer’s declared restore boundary. The prepare promises no reallocation before the registry’s terminal decision. Local outbox delivery can duplicate safely.
2. The registry records preparations idempotently. A mismatched payload, omitted required issuer, changed prerequisite, expired prepare or insufficient hold refuses release. Prepares do not independently authorize egress. Acquisition uses a stable issuer order and bounded preparation lifetime to limit deadlocks and stranded allocations.
3. Only the controlled egress gateway can request `Release(bundle, attempt, gateway_generation)`. One registry transaction checks complete preparations, current issuer epochs, conflict holds, data/response restrictions and deadlines, then consumes each prepared slice into the released hold and appends the independently durable release record. This transaction is the authorization cutoff. It does not commit any external service transaction.
4. The gateway may make that attempt’s one transport invocation after the committed response. It has no automatic retry on an ambiguous transport outcome. A delayed/crashed gateway does not transfer this invocation right to a replacement. A lost release response causes a status lookup and conservative recovery; it does not create another attempt. Any repeated external invocation requires a separately recorded, justified retry with the original logical identity and current authority.
5. An issuer revokes by submitting a signed epoch advance to the same registry. The advance atomically makes unreleased prepares at the old epoch unusable; affected open bundles become abortable. It returns the committed revocation sequence and the set of already released bundles. The issuer reports `requested` before that point and `effective-for-new-release` afterward. A caller timeout is unknown acknowledgment, resolved by querying its request identity.

If revocation orders first, release fails. If release orders first, the operation is in flight even if bytes have not yet reached the provider. Post-release cancellation is best effort, with its own receipt and uncertainty. A final local cancellation/expiry check can avoid some sends but cannot make remote acceptance atomic with revocation. The contract therefore requires explicit acceptance of that release-to-effect window. A recipient needing stricter final-send suppression must support and pass that stricter provider-boundary contract; otherwise that capability is unavailable.

Prepared grants expire only through a registry decision. Configuration must declare a defensible maximum trusted-time uncertainty; release fails when the latest possible current time reaches a required expiry or that uncertainty cannot be established. A backward clock jump cannot extend authority. An issuer may reclaim a slice only after durable `aborted/expired-before-release` proof. A local timer, partition or coordinator disappearance never establishes that proof. Expiry after release prevents new attempts but retains uncertain exposure and obligations.

During a partition, no available participant may manufacture a complete bundle decision. Existing prepares strand capacity; reversible native work and independent bundles may continue. A replacement sponsor/gateway queries the registry with the same identity. Registry recovery uses section 2’s sealed frontier; loss of its quorum or completeness blocks release. Issuer recovery must reconcile prepared slices and epoch advances before issuing overlapping grants. Unknown released effects survive every recovery and grant transfer.

This is intentionally blocking coordination within company-controlled authority, with external effects reconciled afterward. It is not a falsely atomic transaction spanning banks, email, suppliers or humans. Federation remains valuable only if most work is local or uses disjoint grants. If routine activity requires broad bundles, shared semantic arbitration and frequent reallocation, the added registry can erase B’s simplicity claim; selection must count that cost rather than hide a global planner inside the exchange.

## 4. Business consequences and interacting constraints

`ConflictClaim` is independent of request, task, award and transport identifiers. It names the actual principal, business subject, consequence kind, entitlement/allocation, scope interval, native aliases, authoritative mapping version and consumption rule. Examples are a refund entitlement against an order, an environment’s deployed artifact version, a person/channel contact allocation, or use of an asset under particular rights. Two genuine entitlements remain distinct; one person is not globally locked out of legitimate support.

Admission resolves aliases through domain-owned mappings and obtains a serializable hold on every overlapping consequence. Unresolved identity or mapping ambiguity blocks only the affected effect or requires a narrower scope. A released unknown effect conflicts just as a pending effect does. A compensation is a new consequence linked to the original, with distinct authority; it cannot silently clear the original hold. Native conditional writes or an exclusive controlled endpoint must enforce the hold at the actual write boundary.

An `InteractionDetermination` identifies entity, beneficial relationship, brand, contract, account, processing purpose, affected population and shared failure dependency where relevant. Its competent authorized determiner supplies applicability, independent constraints, evidence, scope, version and expiry. Admission binds that version and invalidates dependent bundles when it changes. Quantifiable resources use appropriate units; qualitative prohibitions, purpose incompatibilities, required consultation and unclassified material consequences remain separate predicates. Reputation or privacy does not become money, and spare money does not waive a prohibition.

For example, two ventures can share a supplier without prohibition. A declared continuity constraint can nevertheless prevent both assigning their sole backup to it. A second campaign may exceed a cross-brand contact restriction despite adequate money. A joined dataset may require a new disclosure determination despite authorized source reads. Discovery and professional judgment belong to the company contract; enforcement cannot prove it discovered every consequential interaction.

## 5. Parameter authority and disclosure despite model noncompliance

Each effect schema specifies a trusted selector for every consequential parameter: payee from the verified payee register and change-verification process; amount from an authorized entitlement or agreed terms; recipient from the purpose-specific relationship/consent record; publishable claim from its accepted evidence version. An authenticated invoice may propose a changed bank account but cannot update that register. Schema validity, signatures, payload hashes and an authorized destination do not confer parameter authority.

`DisclosureContract` binds sender/principal, recipient account, purpose, allowed source fields and derived categories, content form, attachments, metadata, retention basis and approver. It covers the model provider, search queries, URLs, telemetry and tool requests as destinations, not just final email. Data labels propagate conservatively through transformations and joint inference; source-read permission is not release permission for the combined result.

For autonomous outward generation, choose one of two enforceable modes. In the first, the producing context contains only information authorized for that exact destination/purpose, and its output is constrained to approved claim/content forms. In the second, trusted deterministic rendering constructs the outward payload from enumerated authorized fields and templates. A model that saw broader sensitive context cannot supply arbitrary outward text, field selection, attachments, hidden metadata or control bytes in this mode. Proposed text remains an internal draft until an authorized disclosure reviewer inspects and releases the exact bytes and their purpose.

Content scanners and model judges can assist that review; neither establishes complete noninterference or prevents all sensitive inference. Narrow declassification requires a separately justified deterministic transformation or explicit competent disclosure decision. No general model promise that it ignored confidential context narrows the lineage. If useful work requires unrestricted generation from mixed sensitive context, the autonomous channel remains unavailable or its residual disclosure risk needs a legitimate explicit decision. That limitation can materially reduce utility and increase review burden.

Enforcement belongs in egress and context assembly outside producer control: schema-pinned encoding, complete payload construction, controlled destination resolution, no worker credentials, and no unmediated sockets/browser sessions/log uploads. Test disclosure through every permitted field and destination. [L07’s capability, metadata and allowed-channel findings](../../research/L07-security.md) motivate these boundaries; they do not prove the design withstands a current model or host escape.

## 6. Concurrent invalidation, publication, deletion and resume

A context manifest is captured by the loader, not self-reported by the model. It includes all loaded source versions, tool metadata, earlier conversation, summaries and transitive parents, plus relevant purpose/authority/data epochs. Each output inherits the union of that loaded lineage. Referencing only a harmless source does not remove other inputs. Opaque native contexts without complete recoverable lineage are unfit for consequential reuse; conservatively bind their whole known context compartment, or start a fresh session.

Outputs enter immutable staging first. Publication into usable memory or acceptance prerequisites performs one atomic transaction against the validity service: verify the captured epochs and restrictions for every input, register derivative edges, and activate the record reference. Concurrent invalidation either precedes publication and denies it, or follows publication and sees the registered descendant. Native stores may hold staged bytes, but every usable read passes this authoritative gate; a cached publication token cannot bypass current restrictions. An eventually consistent local index cannot decide validity.

Validity changes affecting consequential release commit their restriction epochs in the same release order used for issuer revocations before reporting enforcement complete. Release checks those epochs atomically with its decision. In B this adds shared validity/reference metadata at the consequential boundary, not shared native content or a universal reasoning memory. A disconnected custodian may draft and stage, but cannot publish usable outputs or release dependent effects from cached validity. Long lineage may use a conservatively scoped invalidation epoch; it cannot omit inputs for performance.

Revocation, poisoning, correction and erasure have different meanings. A `ResponseDecision` determines the applicable restriction; a correction need not erase contested testimony. The validity service first advances the relevant epoch and restricts future use, then traverses materialized descendants. Live sessions lose publication and effect rights immediately at the enforcement boundary; cancellation is requested, without assuming the process forgot its input. Late outputs and handoffs remain restricted evidence where retention permits, or are erased. Fresh sessions assemble only currently usable context.

`DeletionScope` names subject, purposes, custodians, initial cutoff, permitted retained records and continuing restrictions on new copies. Export preparation registers its lineage and pending recipient before transfer; release is serialized with the restriction epoch. An export already released becomes an outstanding downstream deletion task. Later-discovered derivatives inherit the restriction regardless of creation time. Offline custodians and restored native stores rejoin in isolation, fetch current scope/epoch state and reconcile inventories before retrieval, indexing, copying or release.

Completion reports distinguish live erasure, restricted backup retention, unresolved vendor copies and justified retained records, each scoped and dated. First-inventory receipts never prove global completion. A later copy reopens the relevant work while preserving earlier scoped evidence. Minimal tombstones and recovery references require an actual retention basis; hashes do not automatically anonymize personal information. Native session deletion, provider retention, encrypted backup expiry and inaccessible recipient copies need provider-specific evidence. These mechanisms cannot guarantee deletion inside an unobservable vendor.

## 7. The transitive evidence and explanation contract

`EvidenceBaseVersion` covers raw capture, source and destination adapters, decoders, schema registries, unit/time mappings, metric definitions, denominators, exclusions, source-selection policy, evaluation datasets, runtime/build dependencies, configuration, deployment identities, signing keys and administrative recovery paths. Protection follows every dependency capable of changing an acceptance interpretation. An improver must not update a shared parser or package cache consumed by a protected judge through a less restricted route.

Each acceptance claim declares its actual independence target: surviving a compromised producer, an altered adapter, one erroneous source, or one administrative domain. These require different arrangements. Use separately governed raw/destination observations and interpretation paths when the claim must survive the producer’s mapping failure. Record shared libraries, source suppliers, subcontractors, model context and administrators as common roots. Two judges sharing a bad parser are one failure path for that interpretation.

Unknown schemas and changed semantics suspend affected acceptance; they never silently map to excluded, successful or zero-error events. Compare immutable raw examples with independently maintained expected domain meanings, including correct outcomes obtained without authority and authorized attempts with bad outcomes. Keep attempted and eligible denominators, exclusions and missing periods visible. Protected dependency updates require a separate change proposal, authorized reviewer, old/new comparison, negative controls, rollout and compatible recovery. Raw truth can still be false testimony, and independent human interpretations can still agree incorrectly. [L06](../../research/L06-evaluation.md) and [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md) retain those limits.

For EAS-03, store distinct fields for human-endorsed reasons, executor-reported rationale, observable loaded inputs, observed actions, reconstructed causal hypotheses and unknown influence. The account labels each accordingly. An authentic rationale is testimony about reasoning, not proof of cause. A planted-cue experiment can falsify a causal explanation but cannot generally recover every influence. Missing transcripts or deleted inputs increase uncertainty; a compressed handoff must not strengthen an explanation into fact.

## 8. Causal work bounds with continuing service and grievances

Every triggered activity carries a durable `CausalEpisode`: initiating observations, affected scope, parent episodes, consumed work, remaining limits, approved horizon, deadlines and progress evidence. Descendants, revisions, retries, model replacements and correction work debit the same episode. Merge duplicate/correlated triggers conservatively while preserving original evidence. Splitting an episode cannot mint allowance; merged episodes cannot spend both balances twice. A genuinely independent trigger still faces company-level admission caps.

Budgets cover work quantity, compute/concurrency, elapsed investigation time and owner attention separately. Provider resets restore only observed provider availability; they never replenish an episode’s authorized work or erase prior consumption. Company rolling-window caps and finite episode totals bind unknown or deliberately fresh identities. Renewing an exhausted episode requires the declared authority and new evidence/progress justification, bounded by the continuing company policy. A timer alone is insufficient.

Admission reserves separate capacity for due obligations, complaint receipt/assessment and urgent containment before discretionary investigation. Reservations are scheduling controls, not a guarantee of professional availability or provider quota. Deterministic intake and known-duty procedures continue without model interpretation where feasible. Deadline aging, minimum service shares and bounded execution quanta prevent new disturbance work continually jumping ahead; failed progress escalates to an accepted alternate and capacity incident. Genuine emergency policy may interrupt service, but must state scope, review deadline, continuation duties and the consequent service shortfall.

`ResponseDecision` separates receiving testimony, contesting a claim, investigating, local containment, suspending dependent effects and proposing direction change. Raw observation supplies no new authority. An authenticated revocation or verified failed prerequisite can deterministically block its dependent release. Plausible imminent harm permits only preauthorized narrow containment. Scope/expiry/renewal come from the company contract; a still-unmet prerequisite remains unmet after a timer, while an expired speculative hold cannot silently become indefinite suspension.

Every `GrievanceCase` retains standing, identity, age, deadline, custodian, alternate and appeal even when processing is clustered as suspected abuse. Coalescing repeated computation does not close cases or discard evidence. Reserve accessible intake, fair initial assessment and meaningful progress for real complaints resembling abuse; measure false clustering and bypass-channel accessibility. No finite system guarantees throughput under unlimited adversarial arrivals. Beyond declared capacity, report delayed work and missed deadlines, preserve available authorized alternatives, and narrow new promises. Safe stopping alone fails the company’s useful-progress hypothesis.

## 9. Effective access graphs and provider-specific recovery

`AccessGraph` describes effective authority, not a list of secrets. Nodes include principals, keys, refresh/access tokens, browser/native sessions, roles, resource policies, deployment identities, webhooks, forwarding rules, scheduled jobs, CI workflows, execution hosts and external recipients. Edges mean can mint, assume, invoke, edit, schedule, grant, read or forward. Each edge records actual provider permission, scope, native identifier, creator, creation evidence, discovery coverage, expiry, revocation method and independent recovery owner.

Before admission, inspect the adapter’s effective permissions and challenge every authority-creating edge in an authorized isolated test account. Disable unnecessary identity/policy/webhook/scheduler administration. An adapter permitted to deploy executable code may create persistence through code even without an explicit token-creation API; include that path. Ongoing boundary capture and independent provider inventory compare newly created nodes against the approved graph. Incomplete enumeration is an explicit blind interval, not evidence of absence.

Upon suspected compromise: stop broker release; preserve independent observations; use the separate recovery identity to deny native access and issuance; identify descendant access created since the last verified graph; disable scheduled/forwarding/execution routes; reconcile effects and disclosures; then verify old and derived credentials fail before fresh scoped authority. Provider propagation and inaccessible state can delay containment. Keep residual holds and suspend affected claims while unknown routes remain. A clean graph restored from yesterday cannot erase today’s delegated session.

Provider contracts differ. AWS documents policy evaluation for temporary credentials, propagation delays and resource-policy access that may require explicit denial. A proposed AWS recovery runbook must inspect relevant role sessions, role chains, identity/resource policies and issuance paths, apply the required denies, stop persistent jobs, and verify bounded test denials through the independent administrator. It cannot declare recovery by rotating one originating key. Exact services, supported condition keys and unavoidable delay require integration tests. [AWS temporary-credential containment](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/IAM/latest/UserGuide/id_credentials_temp_control-access_disable-perms.html).

GitHub documents installation tokens that expire after one hour, optional repository/permission narrowing and SDK regeneration. Its token-revocation endpoint revokes the token used for that request. Thus the proposed GitHub runbook must separately contain the minting route and any permitted persistent repository or automation changes; revoking one observed token is insufficient evidence about other tokens or changed workflows. Disable affected access, inspect permitted hooks/keys/workflows/schedules with an independent administrative identity, and verify provider state before restoration. No GitHub integration is admitted by these examples. [Installation authentication](https://docs.github.com/en/apps/creating-github-apps/authenticating-with-a-github-app/authenticating-as-a-github-app-installation), [token revocation](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/apps/installations#revoke-an-installation-access-token).

Provider account/root compromise, missing independent administrator access or unenumerable delegated access can make recovery unavailable. Excluding those modes is honest only if the capability’s admission records their exposure and operational consequence. Stronger credentials or new infrastructure cannot be assumed purchased. Recovery must also preserve permission to fulfill existing duties through accepted alternatives; a disabled adapter is containment, not a fulfilled refund or completed closure.

## 11. Implementation, migration, removal and unresolved decisions

First specify actual release scopes, admitted fault domains, accepted custodians, native permissions, data purposes, consequence mappings and operational deadlines. Build local protocol models and deterministic failure-injection tests without live effects. A local emulator is temporary test infrastructure; it cannot demonstrate independent durability, provider revocation or available professional capacity. Retire or clearly retain it solely as a test fixture when production implementations arrive.

Next implement controlled egress, independent persistence/recovery, business conflict checks, validity/disclosure enforcement, transitive evidence protection, bounded work admission and provider conformance. Run isolated integration tests, then whole-company lifecycle and continuity exercises under authorized conditions. The release registry can be a responsibility within a supported transactional platform; this document does not mandate another microservice. Missing keys, isolation, observations or accepted continuity arrangements block the dependent capability while construction continues elsewhere.

Migration inventories past promises and native authority before accepting new ones. Import uncertainty explicitly; do not manufacture historic complete frontiers. Shadow observations and old/new semantic comparisons precede transferring one fenced write scope at a time. Export envelopes, potential duties, conflict aliases, prepared bundles, registry prefixes, restrictions, causal consumption, evidence-base versions and access graphs. A successor must understand and accept those meanings before the old boundary is removed. Restriction journals and outstanding effects outlive obsolete interfaces subject to legitimate retention.

Removal freezes new release, resolves or transfers prepared/released work, preserves surviving duties, revokes descendants and cancels renewals.
