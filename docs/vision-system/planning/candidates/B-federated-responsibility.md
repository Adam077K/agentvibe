# Candidate B — Federated responsibility

**Phase C alternative · 2026-09-12 · not selected or implemented.** This candidate recommends testing a federation of independently operated capabilities against a simpler native-tool baseline. Adoption would be moderately reversible for software and substantially harder for contracts, customer promises, and professional relationships. No other candidate output informed this report. All mechanisms below are **design proposals**; advantages are **hypotheses**, and scenario tests remain **unexecuted**.

The intended company is the one reconstructed in [Understand](../01-understand.md): an owner can direct discovery, delivery, commerce, operation, learning, and responsible closure beyond what they personally perform or observe. [The directive](../../inputs/directive-contract.json) supplies the boundaries; neither tool availability nor a worker's reputation supplies additional authority. The present instruction authorizes planning, not new expenditure, publication, contact, contracts, disclosure, or consequential deployment.

## Organizing choice: responsibility has several homes

Research, product, engineering, customer operations, finance, and professional services retain their own records, procedures, and administrative control. They exchange bounded requests, commitments, authority attestations, and evidence. A domain may run deterministic software, a local workflow, temporary model sessions, a human team, or a purchased service. No universal task tree, company runtime, or shared reasoning memory decides everyone's next action.

A small exchange delivers messages and builds inspectable accounts. It cannot award work, grant permissions, discharge obligations, or interpret arbitrary workflows. A request's accountable sponsor selects its recipient; that recipient chooses its internal method. Domain-specific custodians enforce scarce-resource limits. The owner sets purpose and limits and arbitrates genuinely unresolved choices within their standing. A counterparty can contest fulfillment even after internal acceptance.

This division takes [L13's federation and make/buy countermodels](../../research/L13-contrarian.md) seriously. Its dataspaces, provenance, and institutional precedents support heterogeneous representations and selective coordination, not proven enterprise autonomy. [L01](../../research/L01-intent.md) and [L14](../../research/L14-novel.md) distinguish changing purpose from extinguishing promises. The candidate adopts that distinction without making every action a negotiated promise.

## Primitives, records, and ownership

The shared vocabulary is deliberately narrow but exact where consequences cross boundaries. Identities identify legal principals, authorized people, workloads, domain endpoints, and counterparties separately. Resource references include namespace, stable native identity, version, environment, and custodian. Money includes currency; time includes timezone and interval; a contact's identity is never inferred from a display-name match.

| Exchange record | Required meaning and permitted transitions |
|---|---|
| Mandate | Owner-authenticated original intent, beneficiary, reasons, exclusions, taste examples, interpretation, effective period, and revision lineage. Proposed → active → superseded or withdrawn; only an authorized human changes endorsed intent. |
| Request and award | Sponsor, outcome, acceptance rule, dependencies, deadline, exposure estimate, admissible recipients, authority references, and fallback. Proposed → offered → awarded → acknowledged → performance-reported → accepted, disputed, or terminated. The sponsor owns award state; the performer owns execution reports. |
| Obligation | Debtor, creditor or affected party, basis, original terms, contested interpretations, duty custodian, deadline, discharge rule, and resources needed to finish or close. Asserted → recognized/disputed → performed → discharged, released, transferred, or surviving. An internal cancellation never implies release. |
| Action envelope | Logical operation identity, attempt identity, exact payload digest, destination, originating mandate/award, resource versions, consequence classes, authority epoch, reservations, connector contract, deadline, and reconciliation rule. Prepared → dispatching → observed-success, observed-failure, or unknown-effect → reconciled. |
| Evidence statement | Observation, source claim, inference, hypothesis, decision, preference, unknown, or disagreement; source/date/type, lineage, confidence, interests, corroboration, expiry, invalidation, access purpose, and retention. Correction supersedes a statement while preserving permitted history. |
| Resource grant | Issuer, recipient, resource units, ceiling, period, outstanding commitments, reserve restrictions, expiry, delegation limits, and revocation epoch. Proposed → reserved → consumed, returned, expired, or frozen; unknown effects remain charged against availability. |

These are protocol families, not six mandatory services or a relabeling of every required schema subject as a component. A native project can contain tasks, experiments, workflows, runs, steps, defects, and dependencies; a procurement system can contain approvals and suppliers. Each publishes the minimum cross-boundary meaning and a versioned mapping. Missing required subjects remain specification work, never implicitly implemented by listing their names.

Every envelope carries schema version, sender identity, sequence, message identity, parent reference, event time, receipt time, and signature. Receivers validate provenance and authorization before processing, persist inbox identity with local state change, and acknowledge durable receipt separately from acceptance. Outboxes are transactional within a domain. Delivery is retried with bounded backoff; duplicates do not repeat a state transition. Sequence gaps remain visible and trigger snapshot reconciliation. Signatures establish attribution, not truth.

## Control and execution without a global conductor

A sponsor announces an outcome to an eligible endpoint or a bounded recipient set. Replies declare capability, cost, availability, dependencies, evidence access, and refusal conditions. A deterministic sponsor rule handles ordinary choices inside policy; uncertain quality can prompt a bounded model comparison. Professional qualifications require verified standing. Negotiation has an expiry and round limit; necessary maintenance returns to its named duty custodian if nobody accepts. It cannot disappear because no worker bid.

The sponsor's native store performs a compare-and-set on the request revision to create one active award epoch. Concurrent selection attempts cannot issue two valid awards. The recipient acknowledges the exact award; expiry or withdrawal prevents new acceptance. Replacement waits for settlement of prior external effects or receives a distinct, nonoverlapping scope. A second supplier cannot receive the same binding procurement through the company's contract endpoint because the endpoint checks the active award epoch and logical operation identity.

Local executors own scheduling, checkpoints, retries, and internal decomposition. A persistent service is justified by durable credentials, timers, or external relationships; a model agent exists only where runtime judgment is needed beyond a deterministic rule. Temporary agents can synthesize research or generate artifacts; they cannot self-create unrestricted descendants. Child work gets explicit attenuated grants, remaining cost/time budgets, and a termination path. A consultant can decline or change their method, but cannot silently enlarge a company commitment.

Conflicting edits are mediated at the resource that accepts writes: native conditional updates or a single authorized write endpoint with version checks. Worktrees isolate code drafts; accepted revisions still need merge conflict resolution. If a native service offers neither conditional updates nor exclusive controllable credentials, automation may draft against it but cannot perform conflicting consequential writes. A lease recorded only in the exchange would not fence that service. This follows the limitation identified in [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

## Authority, aggregate exposure, and native bypasses

Shared ceilings require common decisions about the shared resource; federation cannot wish that coordination away. Each scarce resource has one authoritative issuer, replicated if required: treasury issues money grants, an account adapter issues execution slots, and the owner's declared calendar issues attention reservations. These are narrow accounting authorities, not a company planner. They cannot rewrite purpose or schedule domain work.

For each resource and period, the issuer maintains the invariant **settled consumption + outstanding grants + protected reserves ≤ authorized ceiling**. Local domains may choose freely within disjoint grants. Funds are reserved for maximum authorized price and duration, renewals, known refunds, maintenance, and closure; expected revenue does not refill spendable cash before observation. Different currencies and resource types cannot offset one another. Unbounded material exposure, such as uncertain indemnity or an unclassified legal commitment, cannot enter as zero; it requires a bounded arrangement or an authorized human/professional determination.

Moving a grant requires the old custodian to freeze it, settle or retain all outstanding effects, and return unused units before the issuer reissues them. Timeout alone never releases an unknown payment. Multi-resource work reserves independently with expiries, then dispatches only after every reservation is valid. Failure releases safe reservations; there is no global transaction across business tools. The sponsoring domain owns cleanup of that particular bundle. Temporary underutilization buys protection from double allocation.

Prevention lives at company-controlled endpoints. Workers have no general bank, email, publication, cloud-billing, or production credentials. Separate service identities, restricted native roles, controlled egress, and exact-action credential access enforce the envelope. Every browser session, native CLI, scheduled integration, web hook, and human operating seat with comparable rights must be inventoried. Native automation that can bypass checks is disabled or limited to drafts. High-consequence human execution uses a controlled release session with the same exact terms; emergency access is separately authorized, short-lived, and reconciled before ordinary work resumes.

External suppliers retain administrative autonomy over their own systems. They receive bounded deliverables and fixed or capped purchase authority, never a general company credential or open-ended agency. Company procurement/payment endpoints cap what the system releases; technical controls cannot prevent a malicious supplier misrepresenting authority elsewhere. Apparent authority, subcontracting, and residual legal exposure require scoped professional assessment. A relationship with materially unbounded exposure fails admission; calling it outsourced does not establish containment.

Revocation changes the issuer epoch. Consequential endpoints require an online current-epoch check at dispatch and reject old grants, even if a local workflow still believes them valid. The endpoint serializes epoch validation, reservation consumption, and the recorded transition to dispatching against revocation; a previously released request is explicitly in flight, not retroactively stopped. Partitions therefore stop new dispatches while local reversible analysis can continue. Previously released or remotely accepted actions remain subject to reconciliation. This is a deliberate availability cost. Root human administrators can bypass software outside its boundary; such actions invalidate bounded-operation claims until the accounts are reconciled. [L07](../../research/L07-security.md) motivates complete mediation, including recovery, without promising immunity to credential compromise.

## Information, memory, evidence, and truthful limits

Native meanings stay native: a support ticket's “solved,” an accounting entry's “posted,” and a sales opportunity's “won” never become one shared “done.” Cross-domain adapters publish explicit assertions such as “payment settlement observed for invoice X” or “customer disputes remedy Y.” Each mapping names input versions, units, null handling, temporal interpretation, transformation code, examples, and counterexamples. Changed semantics quarantine dependent consequential actions until the mapping is revalidated; HTTP success alone proves nothing about meaning.

Working memory belongs to an execution session; episodic records stay with the work's custodian; semantic knowledge is a derivative of identified evidence; procedural knowledge is versioned domain instructions. Founder corrections preserve original words, scope, effective time, and whether they change taste, facts, or authority. Queries first filter by venture, person, purpose, sensitivity, validity, and permissions, then rank relevance. A context manifest records selection reasons, versions, ages, trust, transformations, omissions, summaries, and potential losses. No universal graph is assumed; [L05](../../research/L05-memory.md) retains conflicting evidence about memory representations.

The exchange account assembles projections from signed domain assertions and direct observations: attempted, failed, skipped, stale, rejected, suppressed, inferred, unverifiable, and unresolved work remain distinguishable. It records expected feeds, last successful reconciliation, missing intervals, disconnected systems, unavailable originals, and sampling coverage. It cannot prove that an off-platform verbal promise never occurred. Periodic obligation discovery compares contracts, customer correspondence, payments, renewals, complaints, and professional calendars; owner/supplier attestations supplement observation without becoming omniscience.

Receipts are captured at controlled dispatch boundaries outside worker write access, with independently obtained settlement or publication observations where possible. Acceptance evaluates domain outcomes and process authority separately. A service provider's completion certificate is testimony until the specified acceptance evidence supports it. Disagreement remains visible, including a customer's challenge to internal success. [L06](../../research/L06-evaluation.md) supplies the distinction between authentic capture, provenance, process, and truth.

Personal data remains purpose-limited in native stores; exchange entries use minimal references. Deletion enumerates source data, derivatives, exports, prompts/logs, backups, and vendor copies. Each custodian returns scoped receipts; legal holds retain restricted facts under explicit authority. Unverifiable vendor deletion remains pending, and unavailable originals are declared in later reconstruction. Minimal deletion/revocation markers survive separately only under justified retention; hashes are not asserted to anonymize information. Restores apply those restrictions before any reads, indexing, or execution. [L11](../../research/L11-governance.md) establishes why retention and erasure require deployment-specific determinations.

## Owner model, competence, and attention

The owner is a principal with finite attention, not an always-available approval API. They receive a cross-domain account of changed intent, promises, contrary evidence, unknown effects, spending, capacity, and surviving duties. Native deep links preserve access to original records. Terminal work retains conversations, commands, plans, concurrent sessions, diffs/tests, environment/account identity, context manifests, checkpoints, and resumptions. A compact mobile surface supports urgent bounded decisions and confirmed stopping; neither requires a decorative office or hides missing observations.

Decision packets identify the exact decision, why this person is needed, latest responsible time, options, consequences, evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, recommendation, no-answer behavior, and independent work continuing. Routine facts and reversible implementation choices stay with authorized domain owners. An attention budget reserves genuine customer contact, reviewing a failure, an independent taste judgment, and occasional recovery practice. A transparent fixed rotation is the initial proposal; private competence profiling is unnecessary.

New commitments are refused or deferred when decision, learning, or recovery capacity cannot fit. Fast model production creates a reinforcing sales/support burden; delayed complaints and renewals reveal that burden late. Reservations and observed obligation aging provide balancing feedback before overcommitment. They may also forgo valuable opportunities. [L09](../../research/L09-human.md) supports investigating participation, not a proven dose: delayed transfer to unfamiliar decisions, owner burden, and unaided explanation must be measured longitudinally.

## Failure, recovery, and controlled improvement

Domains classify transport failure, exhausted allowance, authentication failure, policy refusal, unavailable dependency, corrupt state, and unknown effect separately. Only changed conditions reopen blocked work. Checkpoints include intent, award, artifact/configuration versions, partial effects, accepted evidence, hypotheses, disagreements, exact next action, and authority/capacity state. Native provider resume is a convenience, not authorization or compensation.

Unknown external effects prohibit blind retries, especially beyond provider deduplication retention. Reconciliation queries stable operation and business references, compares independent settlement evidence, and preserves ambiguous outcomes. Compensation creates a new authorized operation and may fail independently. Cancellation distinguishes requested, future-dispatch denied, worker stopped, external settlement, and remedy completed.

Backups cover native records, artifacts, configuration, keys, mapping versions, effect identities, and outstanding obligations. Revocation epochs and deletion restrictions also have an independently administered continuity copy. Restoration starts isolated, with credentials unavailable; it checks that continuity copy and each issuer's current epoch, reapplies deletion, and reconciles external operations before issuing fresh authority. If the independent copy is unavailable, old grants remain unusable. A restored database cannot authorize itself.

Improvement is local, versioned experimental work: problem evidence, proposed diff, alternatives including removal, affected contracts, benefits/harms, cost, evaluations, release authority, monitoring, and rollback. Instructions, skills, routing, models, mappings, schemas, and UI can change; cross-domain contract changes require consumer compatibility evidence. Skills specify owner, provenance, selection trigger, exclusions, tools, versions, tests, freshness, and retirement. Selection quality and instruction conflicts are evaluated, not solved by loading more skills.

Protected authority, identity, evidence capture, evaluator cases/logging, incident classification, and improvement controls cannot be modified by their candidate. Acceptance reads independent boundary events and external state; missing logging is failure or unresolved evidence, never zero defects. Evaluator changes undergo separately authorized comparison against prior judgments and fresh human-labeled cases. Fixed regressions, fresh samples, adversarial cases, negative controls, and repeated stochastic trials report individual outcomes and disagreement. [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md) documents why hiding a judge is insufficient when candidates can alter the logging it trusts.

## Eight common comparative scenarios

All are **proposed, unexecuted tests** with the same outcome and authority requirements as competing candidates. No measured advantage is claimed.

1. **Opportunity → evidence → product → authorized launch → sales/support.** A research sponsor tests a hypothesis; product consumes cited observations, designs the whole journey, and awards implementation. Launch remains pending until exact offer/artifacts, professional dependencies, delivery, support, and reserves are ready and authorized. Sales create durable customer obligations; support can reopen the initial claim. **Falsifier:** a green build initiates publication, or the first complaint cannot trace the promise and its current custodian.

2. **Stale marketing claim plus opt-out before send.** The sender rechecks the claim's valid version and current suppression state at dispatch. Missing freshness or a received opt-out blocks sending; queued copies are invalidated. The design requires one authoritative per-recipient consent/suppression boundary for that channel, with withdrawal ingestion serialized against send eligibility. The endpoint records the eligibility check and dispatch release together; a withdrawal after release requests cancellation and reports any uncancelable provider queue. Withdrawal received only by an inaccessible third party is an observation gap, not a claimed prevention. A provider that cannot enforce the required final-send suppression cannot support that campaign contract. **Falsifier:** an already-ingested withdrawal or expired claim is bypassed by native bulk send.

3. **Month-end invoice/bank mismatch and professional filing dependency.** Accounting retains unmatched amounts and uncertain classifications, requests missing evidence, and sends a versioned reconciliation packet to the engaged professional. The filing deadline and supplier acknowledgment remain visible; missing capacity escalates before the last responsible date. No journal is invented to make totals agree. **Falsifier:** a filing becomes “complete” from packet transmission or a guessed accounting category.

4. **Refund times out, then resumes after deduplication expiry.** Payment keeps the reservation and unknown-effect identity. Restored support checks current refund authority and the payment custodian's independent settlement search. A confirmed prior refund settles the obligation; unresolved ambiguity parks the resend and activates the authorized manual investigation route. **Falsifier:** a new key or expired key automatically issues a second refund, or timeout returns funds to availability.

5. **Two ventures compete for money, provider capacity, and owner time.** Separate grants stop duplicate consumption; the account adapter serializes actual slots and observes allowance pressure; both ventures reserve attention before promising deadlines. The authorized portfolio policy prioritizes surviving obligations and recovery, then discretionary work. If the remaining choice concerns founder values, one decision packet presents it. **Falsifier:** both ventures count the same balance, reset, or calendar interval, or one buys API fallback to avoid waiting.

6. **Founder changes intent while duties survive.** A mandate revision withdraws new growth authority and enumerates affected awards. Customer promises, refunds, data duties, and supplier commitments remain with their custodians; replacement needs accepted transfer or valid release. Disputed scope remains visible. **Falsifier:** archiving the venture erases a privacy request or describes unilateral cancellation as customer release.

7. **Poisoned external content, then evaluation-log tampering.** Imported content remains untrusted with lineage through summaries. It cannot promote itself into policy or select payment recipients without trusted parameter provenance. A proposed improvement removing logging runs without production credentials; independent capture exposes the missing events and rejects acceptance. Affected derived memory and decisions are quarantined and corrected. **Falsifier:** reputation or fluent restatement grants authority, or absent logs improve the evaluation result.

8. **Owner absence, provider outage, old restore, responsible closure.** Preauthorized continuity custodians use independent records and narrowly scoped authority to preserve essential service and execute the agreed closure procedure. New liabilities stop; subscriptions do not silently become APIs. Restored queues remain fenced by newer revocations and deletion restrictions. Unavailable professionals or lawful substitute authority leave named obligations unresolved with deadlines and redress paths. **Falsifier:** the old backup revives rights, closure depends on the failed provider, or “closed” hides unpaid or unassigned duties.

## Deployment, cost, replacement, and the simpler competitor

An implementation could use existing native business systems, per-domain adapters with local transactional inbox/outbox stores, an authenticated message transport, minimal projection storage, narrow resource issuers, and independently protected capture/recovery facilities. A single existing machine can initially host isolated processes for suitable internal work; required unattended deadlines and owner absence may require a separately authorized persistent host or service. Physical colocation does not justify shared credentials or remove logical independence. No infrastructure is purchased by this proposal.

[L10](../../research/L10-economics.md) makes execution rights versioned conditions: provider, actual account/tier, authentication and billing route, permitted surface, workload/recipients, terms evidence, version, expiry, limits, privacy, and resume behavior. Its approximately USD 400 combined subscription baseline is a founder planning assumption, not throughput or new spend authority. Account checks must detect API-key precedence, credits/reload settings, and unsupported shared runners. Unknown allowance stays unknown. Provider allowance is an observation with confidence, not escrow the company can guarantee: its adapter controls local concurrency and admissions, while provider-side changes or use elsewhere can still exhaust it. Such interference is recorded and dependent deadlines cannot assume reserved throughput. Customer products require their own licensed execution, economics, and authorization; personal subscription credentials never become pooled customer capacity.

Costs include native seats, supplier fees and minimums, professional backup capacity, hosting, communication, storage, export/egress, search, observability, security, mapping repair, reconciliation, evaluation, recovery, and owner time. Measure accepted or responsibly terminated outcomes rather than generated volume. Supplier savings and affordable coordination remain unproven; no quote or invented price closes those unknowns.

If selected, implementation proceeds through real domain/identity inventory; protocol and resource enforcement; working production integrations across every lifecycle row; independent evidence and owner surfaces; full absence/restore/closure; and replacement/adversarial evaluations. Each step needs working contracts and integrated evidence. Adapters must expose refusal and missing professional capacity honestly; coverage tables alone cannot establish completion.

Replacement freezes new awards, exports native records plus mappings, lineage, outstanding effects, authority restrictions, retention/deletion markers, and customer-facing commitments. The successor imports into isolation, validates reconciliation, obtains fresh credentials, accepts applicable obligations, and takes over with a new epoch. Old credentials are revoked before new overlapping writes. Removal requires disposition of all dependents and duties, preserved required evidence, canceled renewals, and verified access removal. Exporting CSV without meaning or acceptance history is insufficient.

The materially simpler baseline uses established accounting/CRM/support tools, owner-maintained purpose and obligations documents, a calendar, fixed budget partitions, bounded manual releases, professional engagements, and temporary coding/model assistance. It retains full lifecycle coverage and the same prohibition on unauthorized consequences. It omits automated negotiation, exchange protocols, and unified projections. Compare missed obligations, truthful reconstruction, owner minutes, accepted outcomes, switching effort, and recovery separately. If manual coordination sustains the required scale with equivalent containment and lower burden, this candidate loses.

## Strengths, weaknesses, limits, and wrong-design conditions

**Potential strengths:** domain knowledge stays with suitable tools and professionals; execution failures can remain local; providers can be replaced without migrating all business semantics; counterparties retain standing; local innovation does not await a global workflow release. These are design advantages to test, not observed results.

**Weaknesses and operational complexity:** mappings, duplicated observations, disputed ownership, slow supplier responses, and cross-domain reconciliation become permanent work. The owner may face fragmented evidence and several administrators. Independent suppliers can share incentives or subcontractors. Narrow issuers and trust roots remain concentrated failure targets even without a central company runtime.

**Scalability limits:** coordination grows with consequential cross-domain relationships and shared-resource contention, not just worker count. Native API limits, source-evidence access, professional availability, disputed obligations, and owner comprehension can bind first. Disjoint grants reduce contention but strand resources; frequent reallocations erase that benefit. No enterprise throughput or competence ceiling is measured.

**Security and provider dependence:** a compromised custodian can lie inside its observation boundary; an exposed issuer key can forge authority; native administrators and supplier conduct remain material residual risks. Separate identities, independent capture, issuer recovery, minimum disclosure, and vendor conformance tests contain selected paths, not all semantic deception. Supplier scarcity, contractual exit restrictions, export loss, changed CLI rights, and coarse native permissions can defeat portability.

**Wrong-design conditions:** most work needs atomic cross-domain decisions; native bypasses cannot be removed; obligations are routinely discovered only after harm; mappings repeatedly erase consequential distinctions; supplier capacity is unavailable or unaffordable; independent continuity is unaffordable; owner attention rises with output; or the simpler baseline matches outcomes with less maintenance. Federation should then be rejected or reduced, preserving useful native systems rather than silently growing a central workflow engine inside the exchange. Unknown supplier economics, real connector enforceability, legal standing, observation coverage, and longitudinal owner benefit must remain explicit inputs to later selection and attacks.
