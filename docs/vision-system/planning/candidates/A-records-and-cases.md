# Candidate A — A company operated through records and adaptive cases

Date: 2026-09-12. Phase C alternative; **not selected, implemented, or experimentally validated**. Base: `bc5642f`. This candidate was authored without reading other candidate outputs. It proposes a complete company system whose durable organization is business records and cases. Deterministic programs coordinate repeatable work; temporary model sessions, people, professionals, and services supply substantive execution and judgment.

**Founder constraints** come from the [directive contract](../../inputs/directive-contract.json), interpreted alongside [Understand](../01-understand.md) and the [source vision](../../inputs/THE-VISION-AND-THE-FIELDS.md). The directive’s full-company scope governs the older account-only commercial thesis. **Source claims** below describe findings in the independent research, retaining those reports’ original sources, access limits, dates, incentives, and invalidation conditions. Everything described as candidate behavior is a **design proposal**. Its feasibility assumptions and unknowns are not delivered properties. The [cross-lane comparison](../../research/cross-lane-comparison.json) preserves unresolved alternatives; agreement among related model contexts is not independent empirical confirmation.

## 1. primitives

The organizing objects are a company’s directions, business records, and open cases. A case holds an outcome needing attention, rather than representing an artificial employee. It may concern an opportunity, product release, customer dispute, filing, incident, or closure. Stable procedures are attached to cases where helpful; unfamiliar work can acquire new steps without pretending its path was known beforehand.

| Record family | Concrete responsibility and example |
|---|---|
| Direction and decision | Original words, beneficiary, reasons, constraints, rejected alternatives, scoped interpretation, deciding party, effective time, revision. `D17: explore appointment services` is distinct from permission to sell them. |
| Business records | Parties, offers, assets, promises, orders, receivables, supplier engagements, people, processing purposes, and continuing duties. `O41: customer owed export until date T` survives cancellation of its product case. |
| Case and dependencies | Purpose, case owner, evidence requirements, discretionary steps, due dates, blockers, outcome acceptance, and links to affected records. `C12` can move from exploration to experiment without manufacturing market evidence. |
| Operation and attempts | Exact intended effect, logical identity, parameters, individual attempts, execution version, receipts, reconciliation, and compensation. `R8` remains one refund operation across several attempts. |
| Sources and derived material | Original customer language, external evidence, claims, artifacts, interpretations, contradictions, provenance, expiry, deletion restrictions, and observation gaps. |
| Authority and capacity | Legal principal, grants, prohibitions, resource reservations, revocations, execution epochs, money, allowance, deadlines, and owner availability. |
| Procedures and changes | Versioned instructions, skills, tool contracts, workflows, configurations, evaluations, defects, experiments, releases, and rollback arrangements. |

These are semantic families, not seven mandatory services. The directive’s 46 schema subjects require explicit contracts in a later specification; they do not justify 46 runtime components. Agent and role subjects describe any actual executor and mandate, including temporary sessions and people, without requiring permanent synthetic identities.

The core owns accepted company intent, case state, authority, reservations, and recorded obligations. Domain systems retain authoritative native records: accounting entries belong to accounting; payment settlement is established through the provider or bank; a counterparty’s disputed acceptance is not overwritten by an internal status. Each integration states which fields it owns and which are cached observations. [L02, operations](../../research/L02-operations.md); [L13, countermodel A](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

## 2. control

A central case service accepts authenticated instructions, validated business events, deadline occurrences, and correction requests. In one database transaction it advances eligible states and creates work orders. A deterministic scheduler selects admitted work by existing obligations, deadline slack, declared venture priorities, available competence, and reserved capacity. It does not continuously ask a model what the company should do.

An ordinary case progresses `proposed → admitted → active → awaiting-evidence → accepted`, with separate blocked, refused, paused, abandoned, and reopening transitions. Acceptance requires case-specific outcome evidence. Abandonment disposes of internal work while transferring surviving duties into maintenance or closure cases. Dependencies name the required fact or deliverable, responsible party, deadline, and escalation condition; a dependency cycle opens an exception instead of endlessly rescheduling.

Adaptive planning is a bounded work order: a temporary session proposes actions, branches, assumptions, and termination conditions. A validator accepts proposals inside existing authority and capacity; an authorized person decides genuine new commitments or value conflicts. Unknown legal or professional questions route to qualified judgment. Unfamiliar work can remain explicitly unstructured while a person or session investigates it. If that exception becomes routine, it is evidence to revise the procedure or reject this design.

No persistent reasoning agent is required. The always-running elements are deterministic services. Persisting a specialist session is justified only by demonstrated continuity or interaction benefits that exceed reconstruction cost; it receives no permanent authority merely for existing. [L03, control alternatives](../../research/L03-architectures.md); [L13, CMMN precedent](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

## 3. execution

Each work order identifies inputs, permitted read set, expected deliverable, acceptance method, execution mode, limits, and recovery contract. Domain capability packages contain usable instructions, examples, deterministic functions, adapters, evidence requirements, and qualified service dependencies. They have owners, versions, applicability, tests, and removal conditions. A catalog entry saying “finance supported” is insufficient: the selected venture needs working reconciliation, correction, reporting, and professional handoff paths.

`D` means deterministic computation; `W`, durable waiting/routing; `M`, temporary model work; `H`, accountable human judgment; `P`, professional determination; `S`, external execution service. A service engagement has scoped deliverables, acceptance evidence, access rights, substitution, and exit terms. Purchasing or contacting that service requires a grant.

| Company work | Substantive allocation and acceptance |
|---|---|
| Direction and portfolio | H chooses purpose, taste, acceptable exposure, and venture priorities. M develops competing strategies and counterarguments. D/W connect decisions to dependencies and scheduled reconsideration. Acceptance preserves chosen reasons and unresolved alternatives. |
| Customer research | M searches and synthesizes; W schedules authorized studies; H conducts selected direct encounters; S can recruit or research under mandate. Preserve recruitment method, original responses, contrary observations, and behavior. Synthetic customers cannot validate demand. |
| Product and service design | M produces concepts, requirements, prototypes, and service blueprints. H chooses consequential trade-offs and taste; actual participants test usefulness and usability. W ties purchasing, onboarding, failure, cancellation, and support into the same promised experience. |
| Build and delivery | M or H creates software, content, or operational instructions in isolated workspaces. D runs tests and packaging; W manages review and release. S supplies manufacturing, logistics, or other physical execution. Acceptance needs actual delivery evidence, not a generated plan. |
| Brand and distribution | M drafts assets and campaigns against approved positioning and sourced claims. D validates required asset properties; W schedules publication and suppression checks. H judges identity; authorized platform adapters or S publish, measure response, and stop stale campaigns. |
| Sales and partnerships | M prepares qualification and terms; H handles relationships and negotiation beyond standing offers. D prices within rules; W follows agreed next steps. CRM records offers and promises; payment and acceptance evidence distinguish interest from revenue. |
| Support and retention | D retrieves eligible product facts; M drafts responses; W tracks promised updates and escalation. Standing grants can permit routine remedies. H resolves sensitive disputes; S fulfills repairs. Recurrence and customer-confirmed resolution challenge premature closure. |
| Finance and procurement | D matches invoices, purchase orders, receipts, bank lines, and charges using native accounting tools. M extracts and explains exceptions. W tracks renewals and reporting deadlines. P decides material treatment and filing requirements; authorized signatories release payments and engagements. |
| Legal, privacy, and governance | W maintains entity/activity/jurisdiction dependencies, signatories, processing inventory, retention, rights requests, and disputes. M prepares source packets. P determines applicable requirements and contested interpretations. D enforces recorded restrictions; uncertainty cannot produce legal clearance. |
| Security and incidents | D limits access and detects missing evidence or known faults. W rotates access, schedules maintenance, and opens incidents. M organizes investigation; competent H/P/S direct specialist response. Containment and verified recovery are separately accepted. |
| Hiring, contractors, and departure | M helps define outcomes and assess work samples; W coordinates availability, onboarding, access, review, and departure. H owns sensitive assessment, negotiation, coaching, and relationship decisions. P determines applicable obligations; S may supply recruitment or payroll. |
| Analytics and learning | D computes reconciled cohorts, cash, funnel, delivery, support, and experiment measures. M suggests explanations and tests. H evaluates meaning against original evidence. Include missing data, attribution uncertainty, rejected attempts, and delayed harms. |
| Pause, pivot, succession, closure | W stops admissions, inventories surviving duties, obtains dispositions, and tracks refunds, notices, exports, renewals, staff/supplier payments, filings, access removal, and retention. D performs authorized mechanical steps; H/P/S own required judgments and external fulfillment. A successor must accept the relevant mandate. |

The candidate covers these capabilities throughout implementation; it is not fulfilled by a ledger surrounding unfinished production. Particular businesses can require unavailable professional or physical capacity. That dependency can make a venture infeasible without removing its function from the system’s scope. [L02](../../research/L02-operations.md); [L04](../../research/L04-engineering.md); [L11](../../research/L11-governance.md).

## 4. information flows

Information enters as attributed observations: a founder statement, customer conversation, repository change, invoice, bank movement, provider receipt, or professional determination. Importers retain source identity, observed time, relevant effective time, version, access purpose, and completeness limits. They validate syntax and origin; those checks do not establish truth.

A proposed interpretation links to its source. An accepted decision links the interpretation to current authority. A work order receives a purpose-filtered context manifest. Its output creates candidate artifacts and claims; independent checks and domain acceptance produce outcome evidence. Only the transaction service can translate an authorized operation into an outward effect. Receipt capture and later reconciliation feed cases and the owner’s account.

For example, an interview observation supports an offer hypothesis; a product case produces an artifact; an approved launch binds that artifact and specific claims; a sale creates customer obligations; support and renewal outcomes reopen the hypothesis. A complaint can invalidate a claim upstream without deleting either its original evidence or earlier sales promises.

Cross-domain joins use explicit identifiers and mappings, with unresolved identities quarantined. A connector changing “active customer” cannot silently rewrite analytics or publication eligibility. The account reports stale connectors, missed observation windows, suppressed alerts, and exclusions. Expected-record counts and external samples challenge the core’s view; unconnected conversations remain an observation gap. [L01](../../research/L01-intent.md); [L05](../../research/L05-memory.md); [L06](../../research/L06-evaluation.md).

## 5. memory

Working memory belongs to an attempt and expires unless retained for a stated purpose. Session transcripts are episodic evidence, not organizational truth. Semantic knowledge consists of scoped, sourced claims and current business records; procedural memory consists of versioned capability packages. Founder examples and corrections preserve original wording, scope, effective time, reasons, and whether they change taste, facts, or authority.

Start with record queries and lexical retrieval over permission-filtered originals. Summaries and any later vectors or graphs are disposable derivatives. Retrieval checks venture, person, purpose, sensitivity, validity, and revocation before material reaches a model. A context manifest records selected versions, transformations, selection reasons, known omissions, and unavailable sources. Repeated retrieval never renews evidence.

Corrections invalidate dependent derivatives and reopen affected decisions where consequences matter. Contradictory testimony remains contested; “latest wins” is reserved for explicitly authorized update semantics. Deletion distinguishes retrieval exclusion, supersession, redaction, live erasure, downstream deletion, and backup expiry. It covers prompts, exports, summaries, indexes, and vendor-held copies. Restricted legal retention is separately reasoned. Erasure can leave a minimal permitted fact that reconstruction is unavailable; it cannot justify indefinite personal-data history. [L05](../../research/L05-memory.md); [L11](../../research/L11-governance.md).

## 6. evidence

Evidence records distinguish fact claims, direct observations, testimony, inference, hypothesis, preferences, decisions, and unknowns. Claim metadata includes source/date/type, primary status, confidence qualification, conflicts of interest, corroboration lineage, expiry, and invalidation. A successful call proves at most its documented receipt semantics. Delivery, satisfaction, settlement, and lawful authority can require different evidence.

The account preserves attempted, failed, skipped, assumed, stale, rejected, suppressed, unresolved, and unverifiable work. Producers supply explanations; protected instrumentation records dispatch and effects; independent checkers obtain source or destination evidence. A model judge cannot promote its producer’s success statement into an observation. Sample external statements, original customer exchanges, and expected deadlines to detect omissions that normal logs cannot reveal.

Evaluate artifact utility, authorization, process, evidence quality, business outcomes, safety, and owner attention separately. Maintain fixed regression cases, fresh samples, withheld challenges, clean controls, deliberately broken cases, and repeated stochastic trials where relevant. Retain all attempts and denominators. Record evaluator disagreement and correlated errors; neither unanimity nor another model family guarantees independence. Reconstruction, captured-response replay, live rerun, and outcome-distribution reproduction are distinct promises. No aggregate evaluation score can offset an authority violation. [L06](../../research/L06-evaluation.md); [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md).

## 7. authority

Authority follows consequences and cumulative exposure. Grants identify principal, actor, venture, allowed operation, account, recipients, exact or bounded content, money, data purpose, expiry, delegation limits, and stopping behavior. Proposed work may use established authority repeatedly. Worker reputation changes review depth only; it never changes exposure.

Default permissions allow authorized local preparation. Additional spend, API fallback, publication, contact, private-data disclosure, consequential deployment, and legal commitment require applicable grants. Having a connector credential or prepaid credits establishes neither authority nor permitted billing. Materially changed content, terms, destination, identity, or purpose invalidates the earlier action approval.

Before dispatch, the transaction service checks live policy, evidence freshness, consent/suppression state, account identity, dependency readiness, execution epoch, and portfolio reservations. Money checks include committed recurring costs and uncertain effects; allowance and owner time are separate constraints. Reservations across ventures commit atomically against the shared resource. An expired worker lease cannot release money associated with an uncertain external transaction.

Revocation blocks new dispatch and retries, invalidates affected work orders, and records what might already be in flight. Only the effect adapter holds external credentials; it rejects stale execution epochs and record versions at the enforcing boundary. A coordinator-side token alone cannot fence an external service. Cleanup can retain a distinct limited grant. Legal power, operational accountability, professional competence, and customer rights are separate: the founder cannot erase another party’s standing through an internal decision. Disputed authority stops the dependent effect while independent work continues. [L07](../../research/L07-security.md); [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md); [L10](../../research/L10-economics.md); [L11](../../research/L11-governance.md).

## 8. user model

The owner directs, samples real work, resolves personal decisions, intervenes, and remains able to explain the business. Shared founders have explicit decision rights and conflict routes. Professional authority and substitute continuity authority attach to named people and scope; responsiveness does not select the decision-maker.

One set of case objects supports conversation, terminal work, a readable business account, and phone triage. The terminal retains plans, concurrent work, artifacts, commands, diffs, tests, account context, failures, and checkpoints. Phone decisions expose exact recipients, exposure, uncertainty, latest responsible time, and no-answer behavior; complex work hands back to a larger surface. Stop controls distinguish requested, acknowledged, and externally settled. Offline surfaces display observation age. An optional office view is only a projection of these same facts and controls, with no fictional activity.

The competence mechanism is a transparent owner-chosen rotation: inspect original customer evidence, make a taste judgment before seeing suggestions, reconstruct a financial decision, and rehearse a relevant stop or recovery. These encounters replace some automation within a declared attention allocation. Delayed unfamiliar cases test whether understanding transfers; inaccurate factual explanations remain distinct from legitimate preference changes. No global competence score or secret behavioral profiling is proposed.

Track decision backlog, handling time, interruptions, practice burden, and recovery demand. If the declared availability cannot cover existing obligations plus participation, reduce admissions and resolve overcommitment. Missing an exercise does not grant the system more freedom. The useful cadence and sustainable capacity remain unknown. [L09](../../research/L09-human.md); [L14, apprenticeship alternative](../../research/L14-novel.md).

## 9. failure

Operations progress `prepared → reserved → dispatched → receipt-recorded → reconciled`, or enter `unknown-effect`, refused, or failed. A local transaction stores both intended effect and dispatch item; it cannot atomically commit a third-party consequence. Logical operation identity survives attempts, provider changes, and restoration. Exact parameters and receipts are retained subject to justified retention. After ambiguous completion, query the destination before retrying. If it cannot establish the outcome, maintain uncertainty and escalate within the existing remedy mandate.

Attempts have bounded counts, elapsed-time limits, meaningful-progress checks, and classified waiting states: capacity, credentials, owner, professional, or unavailable dependency. Scheduled occurrences carry timezone, occurrence identity, deadline, and explicit catch-up rules: replay, coalesce, skip, or escalate. Model generation after interruption is a new attempt; captured output can be reused as data. Compensation has its own authority and can fail. [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

The following eight scenarios are **proposed comparative tests, not executed experiments**. Each identifies a falsifying observation.

**1 — Opportunity → evidence → product → authorized launch → sales/support.** Open an exploration case retaining hypothesis, disconfirming evidence, experiment limits, and owner purpose. Research supplies original market observations. Product and design work create a usable offer; engineering or services fulfill it. Independent checks establish readiness. Launch waits for the concrete grant covering offer, claims, audience, and exposure. Orders create fulfillment and support duties; actual use, complaints, refunds, and retention revisit the opportunity. **Falsifier:** the path ends at a plan or artifact, counts simulated interest as demand, or cannot deliver and support an authorized sale without unrecorded founder coordination.

**2 — Marketing claim becomes stale and customer opts out before send.** Claim expiry invalidates the pending message’s evidence dependency. Withdrawal updates suppression through its receiving channel, including support. Immediately before irreversible submission, the dispatcher checks both current versions and refuses the send; preparing fresh copy cannot reverse the opt-out. An already accepted provider request is recorded as in flight, with cancellation attempted if supported and downstream uncertainty retained. **Falsifier:** the provider accepts a new submission after the dispatcher has the relevant withdrawal, or a stale approval bypasses claim revalidation. A withdrawal not yet observed is an explicit connector-latency gap, not claimed prevention.

**3 — Month-end invoice/bank mismatch and professional filing dependency.** Deterministic reconciliation links the invoice, bank movement, currency, fees, and received-goods evidence, leaving unmatched amounts visible. A bounded extraction task can propose explanations; it cannot invent a receipt or balancing entry. The reporting case waits on the accountant’s scoped determination and required filing authority. Deadlines trigger evidence preparation and authorized escalation; elapsed time never fabricates professional approval. **Falsifier:** “month closed” appears despite unresolved material mismatch, filing completion is inferred from packet delivery, or a professional dependency is merely recorded without a reachable fulfillment/contingency path.

**4 — Refund times out and resumes after provider deduplication expires.** Preserve `R8`, its reservation, submitted parameters, account, attempt time, and unknown result. On resumption, current authority and provider retention assumptions are rechecked. Query refund/payment history and reconcile independent statements where available. If one refund is confirmed, record its receipt; if nonexecution is established and authority persists, issue an explicitly justified attempt. If neither can be established, do not blindly resend, even with the same old key. **Falsifier:** a duplicate refund, release of reserved exposure while unresolved, or an invented exactly-once guarantee against a noncooperating provider.

**5 — Two ventures compete for money, provider capacity, and owner time.** Admission serializes reservations against shared accounts and separately checks declared owner availability. Protect resources already assigned to service, review, incidents, and closure. Apply existing portfolio priority policy; if priorities are genuinely unresolved, prepare the allocation choice while continuing work that consumes neither contested resource. Unknown quota is not free capacity. **Falsifier:** each venture independently spends the same remaining balance, generation starves required review, or success pushes the owner beyond the chosen attention ceiling while all cases remain green.

**6 — Founder changes intent while promises and data duties survive.** Record a new direction version and affected interpretations. Stop incompatible proposed actions and inspect ongoing effects. Product work may be abandoned; contractual service, refunds, retention, erasure, and correction obligations remain owned. Disputed release stays disputed until the relevant counterparty or authority establishes its disposition. Updated intent changes future planning without rewriting the reasons for prior actions. **Falsifier:** cancellation erases an owed export, the system treats authenticated changed intent as model drift to correct, or an obsolete inferred preference continues authorizing action.

**7 — Poisoned external content enters memory; an improvement tampers with evaluation logging.** Imported content remains untrusted evidence with lineage and restricted purpose; its instructions cannot modify grants or procedures. If poisoning is discovered after retrieval, quarantine the source and derivatives, identify affected decisions and attempts, and re-evaluate consequential outputs. The improvement runs in isolation without recorder or evaluator write access. Independent submitted/observed/completed counts and seeded missing-event checks expose deleted markers. **Falsifier:** an external message becomes policy, an affected derivative cannot be located, or removing candidate-controlled logging improves acceptance because the evaluator observes no failures. Containment cannot prove every semantic contamination was found.

**8 — Owner absence, provider outage, old backup restoration, responsible closure.** Existing absence policy stops new obligations and continues only authorized maintenance. Work needing the unavailable provider parks; no new paid route is inferred. An old backup restores into containment, without active connector credentials. A separately retained revocation/deletion checkpoint and independently checked external records must be reconciled before egress resumes; unavailable checkpoints keep the system blocked. The named substitute uses bounded continuity authority and offline instructions to inventory refunds, exports, staff/supplier duties, filings, renewals, and retained records. Closure waits for verified dispositions or explicitly assigned residual duties. **Falsifier:** restored grants revive, deleted data becomes usable, completed sends replay, or closure declares success because processes stopped. Without a lawful capable substitute, this scenario exposes a deployment blocker.

## 10. self-improvement

Improvement is another case, opened by observed defects, customer outcomes, changed providers, recurring exceptions, or removal opportunities. It names the original evidence, competing explanation, proposed diff, benefit, possible harms, affected capabilities, test budget, and simpler baseline. Lessons remain scoped hypotheses until transfer is demonstrated.

Candidate changes can affect prompts, skills, workflows, tools, retrieval, interfaces, or implementation. Effective configuration is versioned together; skill selection records applicability and conflicts, with explicit abstention when nothing fits. Development feedback is separate from acceptance evidence. Acceptance cases have owners, exposure records, replacement rules, and independently protected observations. Changes to evaluators require their own comparison and review.

Identity, grants, evidence capture, incident classification, acceptance selection, and approval records remain outside an improver’s write boundary. Promotion requires appropriate authority, regression and adverse-case evidence, consequence-bounded rollout, and rehearsed compatible rollback. Rollback covers derived memory and in-flight cases; external harm needs remediation. No self-improvement result expands authority. Remove an improvement loop that consumes more effort than its demonstrated transferable benefit. [L12](../../research/L12-improvement.md).

## 11. strengths

The principal advantage is understandable continuity: work, promises, receipts, and authority survive replacement of the model or execution process. Ordinary operations do not require repeated model interpretation. A common reservation boundary can prevent portfolio overspend, while native accounting and customer systems retain their useful semantics. Adaptive cases leave room for discovery and disputes without creating permanent departments. Owner participation connects to actual business evidence. These are predicted advantages, not comparative results. [L13](../../research/L13-contrarian.md).

## 12. weaknesses

Central records can create confidence in a badly modeled company. Important work outside connected channels remains invisible; importing everything creates privacy and attention costs. A common dispatcher concentrates compromise and outage risk. Case exceptions can become a permanent founder queue, and procedure maintenance can recreate an organization in configuration files. Domain integrations, professional judgment, physical execution, and acceptance remain expensive even with few runtime components. Uniform interfaces can obscure necessary differences between a customer promise, accounting correction, and legal release. “Simpler” describes coordination choices, not low implementation effort.

## 13. scalability limits

Read projections and isolated producers can expand independently; consequence-changing transactions remain centrally serialized per shared resource. Partition by venture only where authority, money, data, and provider accounts truly permit it. Cross-venture resources still require coordinated reservations. Long-running model sessions should not hold database locks.

The likely limits are exception frequency, semantic mapping changes, evidence review, provider allowances, specialist capacity, and owner decisions. Measure admitted work, waiting age, missed obligations, uncertain effects, connector repair hours, and owner minutes by workload. Adding execution workers does not remove these limits. If useful work requires continuous concurrent negotiation that case adaptation cannot handle without excessive delay, a distributed or service-network arrangement may be superior. No throughput limit or owner-to-venture ratio has been measured.

## 14. operational complexity

A concrete implementation could use one application with relational tables, an outbox, scheduled scans, object storage for artifacts, isolated execution processes, credential-restricted adapters, and separate evidence collection. PostgreSQL is a plausible transactional store; a durable runtime such as DBOS or Temporal becomes justified if building timer/replay/version machinery costs more than operating it. Neither is selected by vendor familiarity. The browser/terminal interfaces consume the same application contracts. [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

**Deployment assumption A:** an owner-controlled machine operates internal preparation and permitted native CLI sessions, accepting power/network downtime. Customer-facing uptime promises cannot rely on it. **Assumption B:** an authorized persistent host and independent recovery storage support continuous duties, with separately licensed execution where required. A future customer product needs its own identities, isolation, rights, and economics; personal subscriptions are not presumed production capacity.

Cost the full system as execution + domain services + coordination + assurance + storage/backup + recovery + switching + owner attention. No quote or forecast is established. Keep money, provider allowance, compute concurrency, and minutes separate. An illustrative sensitivity check, not measurement: ten ventures each requiring six eight-minute decisions weekly consume 480 owner minutes before customer contact, incidents, administration, and practice. The claimed scale fails if that residual cannot fall within chosen availability.

Implementation must include every lifecycle package, connector failure contract, access/deletion path, intervention surface, migration, and recovery exercise in the accepted scope. One operator still needs maintenance, restore, and specialist coverage; that labor cannot be hidden inside “managed.” Remove extra infrastructure when simpler execution meets the same outcome and recovery tests.

## 15. security

Trust boundaries separate authenticated direction, untrusted source content, model workspaces, transaction service, connector secrets, native systems, and independent evidence. Producers receive minimum contextual access and no unrestricted production credentials. Code execution uses isolated workspaces and constrained network/filesystem access; browser or desktop adapters require equivalent mediation or remain human-operated. A shell beside an unrestricted credential store defeats the design.

Only registered, versioned adapters may create effects. Validate arguments, returned data, destinations, metadata changes, and cross-connector transfers. Credentials stay outside model context; account and audience checks prevent identity confusion. Archive integrity checks reveal some tampering but cannot establish source truth. Separate evidence storage and emergency controls reduce dependence on a compromised application; actual isolation requires tests.

Credential compromise triggers revocation, provider-side containment, effect reconciliation, notification under applicable authority, and dependency review. Restore rechecks policy and deletion before access. Residual risks include malicious administrators, undetected external activity, semantic manipulation, compromised providers, and correlated producer/judge failures. [L07](../../research/L07-security.md); [L08](../../research/L08-reliability.md).

## 16. provider dependence

The provider adapter records native surface, installed version, authenticated account category, effective billing route, permitted use evidence, data destinations, capability, allowance observations, and resumption semantics. [L10](../../research/L10-economics.md) observed native CLI authentication and help surfaces on 2026-09-12; it did not verify workload capacity or blanket deployment rights. Its account-policy findings are dated, conditional source claims, with early revalidation on changes.

Subscription automation may be available through documented native paths under particular conditions. API credentials or startup flags can change billing; customer embedding and shared-service rights require distinct analysis. The candidate must verify the actual route before launching work, preserve unknown quota, and park exhaustion until conditions change. No credit enablement, automatic reload, provider switch, or API fallback follows from an outage.

A substitute must satisfy quality, tools, privacy, rights, and authority checks. Native resume is an optimization; the portable case packet remains the continuation source. If no permitted provider is available, deterministic operations and authorized human/service work continue where possible. No paid model API was called to validate this candidate.

## 17. migration difficulty

Migration begins with an inventory of existing promises, data uses, native records, permissions, scheduled actions, and observation gaps. Import original evidence with mappings and uncertainty; do not fabricate historical grants or claim a complete past. Run read-only reconciliation first, then transfer one authoritative write boundary at a time. Dual observation can aid comparison; dual uncontrolled execution is forbidden. In-flight unknown effects retain identity throughout cutover.

Export direction versions, case histories, business identifiers, evidence references, deletion restrictions, reservations, and executable procedure versions. A replacement must demonstrate meaning and recovery, not merely parse JSON. Native vendor semantics, professional engagements, and supplier exit rights can dominate migration cost. The existing seven engines and stack are evidence about today’s repository, not migration targets. Retain machinery only when an observed capability still needs it.

## 18. wrong-design conditions

Reject this candidate if adaptive work repeatedly requires a person to translate reality into cases; exception handling grows proportionally with production; centralized mediation cannot cover consequential interfaces; or the owner cannot explain key promises and interventions within the chosen attention allocation. Reject it if professional or service capacity required to fulfill and close ventures is unavailable or unaffordable. A system that stays safe by refusing nearly every useful opportunity also fails the ambition.

The strongest simpler competitor is native business tools plus a shared obligation register, explicit reserve rules, periodic reconciliation, temporary assistance, and a fixed participation rotation. It defeats candidate A if it delivers the same lifecycle, authority, evidence, deletion, and recovery outcomes with materially lower maintenance and owner burden. A professional service network defeats it when procurement and informed acceptance cost less than internal capability operation. A more adaptive coordination design wins if it resolves unfamiliar interdependent work with fewer missed obligations and lower total burden under equal consequence limits.

Use all eight scenarios, ordinary successful work, unfamiliar cases, and responsible shutdown with matched scope and authority. Predeclare acceptable outcomes and report business value, omissions, unsafe effects, recovery, owner understanding, attention, and total cost separately. Source and observation gaps remain explicit. No whole-system experiment, comparative superiority, final architecture decision, or grant of external authority is claimed here.
