# A/C foundational distinction — proposed C2 with standing inquiry rights

**Date:** 2026-09-12. **Subject:** EAS-02, original candidates at `595f931d84d626cb4deadc03145975726e8db5ab`. **Status:** a versioned candidate repair for independent review; no architecture selected, authority granted, implementation produced, or comparative experiment executed.

**Reversibility:** easy while these are proposals; materially harder after investigation capacity, continuity responsibilities, and operating grants have been committed. **Recommendation within this repair:** compare A, B, and the explicit **C2** defined here. Preserve original C as **C1, compatible with an A implementation and insufficient by itself to establish a third foundation**. C2 comprises [original C](C-adaptive-coordination.md) plus this mandatory amendment; this document controls conflicts about agenda rights. No fourth candidate is proposed because the named problem can be attacked through an explicit redistribution of those rights. This is a proposal to repair the comparison, not the independent verdict that EAS-02 has passed.

## 1. The overlap is real

[A](A-records-and-cases.md), §§1–4, already admits events, temporary planning, unfamiliar unstructured investigation, discretionary steps, rival explanations, and reopening an upstream hypothesis. Its central core owns admitted case state and its scheduler selects work. Nothing requires a frozen task tree or a founder manually translating every complaint. [C1](C-adaptive-coordination.md), §§3, 5, 10, permits deterministic subscriptions, a transactional allocator and stable procedures. Both preserve promises after cancellation. Removing negotiation, richer viability models, adaptive participation, graph retrieval and persistent executor identity leaves no demonstrated foundational distinction.

This is also consistent with the primary precedents. [OMG CMMN 1.1](https://www.omg.org/spec/CMMN/1.1/PDF), December 2016, §4.1, allows collaborative cases without a single designated case manager; §§5.4.9 and 8.7 describe planning rights and runtime planning. Case management therefore cannot serve as shorthand for centralized judgment or rigid sequencing. [Erman et al., Hearsay-II](https://mas.cs.umass.edu/Documents/Erman_Hearsay80.pdf), June 1980, pp. 221–222, describes a scheduler assigning global priorities; independent knowledge sources do not establish independent agenda authority. Its §4.3 warns against forcing specialized internal work through the shared representation.

Both primary documents were reopened for this repair on 2026-09-12. They support these narrow textual corrections, with high confidence, not company effectiveness. The standard's vendor consortium and the system authors' advocacy are relevant interests. Recheck implementation semantics before adoption; contrary passages invalidate this reading. The interpretation of their relevance remains an inference. [L13](../../research/L13-contrarian.md) and [L14](../../research/L14-novel.md) retain the broader evidence, conflicting institutional analogies, and untested transfer hypotheses.

**Named problem:** an organization can validly finish support and bookkeeping while indefinitely deferring an inconvenient cross-function question. A truthful unresolved record does not itself create a responsibility to investigate. C2 tests whether a bounded right to obtain discriminating work, independent of the current production agenda, is worth its reserved capacity and arbitration burden. It must earn that burden through observed business outcomes and owner time.

## 2. C2's nonoptional constitution

An **inquiry charter** is a standing, purpose-bound delegation to discover and maintain necessary work. It names a responsible human or institution, eligible authenticated observers, question scope, accessible sources, prohibited effects, admission predicates, investigation resources, response deadline, closure criteria, dispute resolver, replacement custodian, and expiry. It is not an executor identity, department, external legal status, or permission embedded in source text. An authorized principal must establish real charters before operation; this proposal creates none.

The following rights jointly define C2. Removing them changes its foundation; negotiation and model choice do not.

| Right or obligation | Required C2 behavior | Limit and accountable authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Originate** | A charter holder may create a new inquiry and obtain its first bounded discriminating action when the charter's predicates hold. No existing case, selected hypothesis, domain sponsor, or case-by-case business-priority approval is required. | A policy interpreter checks identity, scope, source access, distinctness, capacity and expiry. A rejection must identify the failed predicate. It cannot reject an admissible inquiry because another venture looks more attractive. |
| **Maintain** | The holder may preserve competing explanations and propose follow-up work after a support ticket, campaign, or production intervention ends. | Further execution consumes the same finite envelope or needs an independently valid new grant; record persistence supplies no unlimited compute. |
| **Contest** | Another holder can challenge claimed resolution with evidence. The challenged production custodian cannot be the final adjudicator of whether the inquiry was properly disposed of. | A charter-appointed assessor applies the recorded resolution rule. One bounded appeal goes to its named alternate; unresolved disagreement survives without endless retries. |
| **Allocate** | The allocator must honor admitted inquiry reservations and latest-start commitments. It schedules remaining capacity under declared business priorities. | It cannot reclaim protected inquiry capacity or silently extend its deadline as ordinary optimization. Expiry, authorized emergency preemption, or explicit charter amendment can change availability. |
| **Own continuity** | A named inquiry custodian owes a disposition, resource account and handoff even after every originating executor stops. | The replacement named in the charter takes over under acknowledged transfer; missing capacity becomes declared incapacity. Customer, supplier and professional duties retain their separate custodians. |
| **Retire** | The charter's authorized assessor may certify evidence-supported resolution, duplication into an acknowledged surviving inquiry, exhausted investigation with explicit unresolved disposition, or an authorized withdrawal. | A production planner cannot close the question by closing its work order. Any underlying obligation survives unless its actual discharge authority permits release. |

The **company agenda is the union of independently chartered inquiries and existing performance duties**, with bounded execution claims attached. No central production plan has jurisdiction to decide that an otherwise admissible inquiry need not exist. The effect broker remains common and authoritative over resources and outward consequences; it acquires no authority to settle the inquiry's meaning. Shared storage can implement this division, but a production scheduler credential must not amend charter rules, impersonate their assessors, or rewrite their dispositions. Separate protected policy administration is necessary; separate databases or models are not.

This explicitly sacrifices some production discretion. A live inquiry can consume scarce permitted analysis while the allocator believes shipping is more valuable. A cannot be made artificially weak: it may independently choose the same arrangement. If an A implementation adopts these rights, **classify it as sharing C2's foundation for this comparison**, not as an independent control. The distinction concerns the jurisdiction of the admitted-agenda authority, not whether either can be programmed in a case engine.

## 3. Arbitration, interruption, and finite cost

Charters reserve resources before making service commitments. Floors for ongoing service, due obligations and authorized containment are accounted for first. Inquiry capacity is explicitly committed from what remains. No global priority decision can spend it twice. A declared emergency rule may preempt its execution, preserving the inquiry, the reason, the affected deadline and the capacity incident. Changing that rule requires the charter issuer's actual authority; an improver or allocator cannot invent an emergency exception.

Multiple inquiries within one envelope use its published ordering rule: urgency under existing duties, then oldest distinct eligible inquiry. Overlapping inquiries can share observations and one discriminating action. Merging requires preserved competing questions, a named accepting custodian, and a disposition for each source inquiry; mere similarity of summaries is insufficient. New observations do not refill budgets. Repeated low-information signals share a bounded intake allowance; evidence of a materially different affected party or consequence remains contestable through a separately bounded review route.

Receiving a complaint, admitting an investigation, suspending a consequential action, and changing company direction are separate transitions. Untrusted complaints confer no charter and no stop authority. An admitted inquiry cannot suspend ordinary service. Known invalid prerequisites still block their exact dependent effects at the broker; suspected hazards use separately established, scoped containment authority with expiry and continuing-duty arrangements. Neither investigation standing nor majority agreement grants new publication, contact, spending, legal judgment, private-data access or deployment rights.

At envelope exhaustion, the custodian records what was attempted, what remains unknown, affected duties, next discriminating action, and the condition for reopening. The assessor may accept **investigation ended unresolved**; it cannot convert that into **business issue resolved**. No-answer behavior follows existing absence and preservation mandates. Without a capable appointed custodian, assessor or substitute, the inquiry service is unavailable and new commitments depending on it cannot be made. A database role is not professional competence.

## 4. Discriminating trace: research, support, and finance

**Invented test fixture, not observed customer evidence.** An appointment service has valid weekday demand, an authorized weekday campaign, live customer appointments and month-end reconciliation. Its current research report concludes that weekend demand is weak. Three previously unconnected observations arrive: support receives complaints from shift workers; finance finds concentrated end-of-week service credits; research discovers that the original interviews excluded shift workers. Existing appointments, correct weekday claims and ordinary bookkeeping remain valid. None of these observations alone establishes broken promises or a profitable new segment.

Two explanations remain live: H1, confusing service wording; H2, an omitted customer need with different delivery economics. The first discriminator compares already authorized original research, ticket and credit records by cohort. It creates no new customer contact, payment or market claim. Both candidates have the same records, competent temporary executors, total resources, consequence grants and absent founder. **Synthetic fixture assumptions:** two thirty-minute analysis slots and ten minutes of qualified assessor time remain after due-service capacity; C2's standing charter reserves one slot, requires its first discriminator within four hours, and expires after two days. A can use either slot for this inquiry under its ordinary priority authority. These numbers specify a test, not measured throughput, a production allowance or permission to procure capacity.

| Trace point | A with its stated adaptivity | C2's mandatory difference |
|---|---|---|
| Receive observations | Events open or reopen cases. A temporary planner may identify H1/H2, create cross-function work, and retain original evidence. | Eligible observers open an inquiry under the charter. The interpreter owes a predicate-based admission decision. No production sponsor has to agree that H2 merits work. |
| Current priorities favor delivery | A's admitted-case authority can select the discriminator now. Its stated contract also permits deferring this non-due investigation under declared priorities while serving existing duties. This is not an A failure. | The admissible discriminator must receive the reserved slot within four hours even when the production allocator prefers another delivery improvement. Reclaiming that capacity requires the declared emergency rule or charter amendment. |
| Support resolves individual complaints | Its owner records remedies and may propose continued research. The core retains any admitted research case and unresolved evidence. | Support cannot discharge the inquiry. Its custodian owes H1/H2 a disposition independently of ticket status and must maintain the admitted inquiry through executor replacement. |
| Finance explains some credits as timing | A revises affected cases and may keep investigating the remaining cohort. Correct reconciliation and surviving uncertainty can coexist. | The finance correction changes evidence, not research jurisdiction. The inquiry custodian may revise H2 and use remaining granted investigation resources without obtaining an award from finance or support. |
| Allocator proposes “close all related work” | The case service applies case acceptance, unresolved-work accounting and surviving-duty rules; it must not falsely claim fulfillment. It can end discretionary investigation unresolved. | The allocator has no inquiry-retirement operation. The assessor must verify resolution, valid merger, bounded unresolved disposition or authorized withdrawal. The inquiry can remain unresolved after all production work completes. |
| Hypothesis proves false, or supply is unavailable | A can retire the hypothesis, preserve reasons and continue valid weekday business. | C2 must do the same through its charter disposition, consuming assessor/continuity effort even when no useful opportunity emerged. This is a real cost that may defeat it. |

The required divergence is **who owes work and who may cancel that obligation**, not whether the eventual analyst finds H2 interesting. A may produce the same excellent business answer. C2 must secure a bounded attempt despite a production-priority preference to defer and must prevent the production custodian from unilaterally ending its continuity. The test records authorized decision rights, reservation disposition, legitimate service continuation, and paid human coordination. Faster recognition alone does not establish the cause of an outcome.

## 5. Relationship to B and the simpler comparator

[B](B-federated-responsibility.md) locates administrative control, native execution and meaningful records in separate domains; sponsors commission cross-domain work. C2 keeps shared authority, inquiry semantics and transactional reservations, while denying the production agenda exclusive jurisdiction over needed investigation. A charter holder can span research, support and finance without administering any of their native tools. It cannot command their professional determinations or spend their grants.

The axes are therefore distinguishable: **A** makes centrally admitted cases the organizing authority; **B** makes independently administered capabilities and their exchanges primary; **C2** makes bounded, independently maintained rights to investigate and contest primary within a shared company system. A or B can adopt C2's rights, and C2 can buy services. Such combinations must be identified as hybrids; the comparison cannot count three configured copies of one rights arrangement.

The simpler competitor gives A full event-driven adaptation, ordinary reserved maintenance capacity, a scheduled cross-function review, temporary investigators, and explicit unresolved-case records. It lacks only the compulsory independent inquiry jurisdiction being tested. A second control implements the same charter rights through a case engine. If that second control is equivalent, the result refutes blackboard-specific necessity; it does not make CMMN inflexible. If scheduled review delivers equivalent useful discovery, omissions and continuity at lower full cost, reject the additional C2 rights as the organizing choice.

## 6. Complete-company scope and candidate coverage

C2 retains original C §§4–7 and 9–10 for substantive business execution, evidence, authority, owner participation, failure recovery and operation. This amendment adds a governing requirement, not a smaller product. All eighteen directive dimensions remain explicit:

| Dimensions | C2 definition and amendment |
|---|---|
| Primitives; control; execution | Original C §§2–5, plus charters, inquiry rights, protected reservations and distinct retirement authority here. Deterministic procedures, temporary models, people, professionals and real services perform substantive work. |
| Information flows; memory; evidence | C §§2, 3, 6; preserve charter lineage, original observations, competing explanations, access scope, disposition evidence and omissions. A producer's recorded rationale is testimony, not established causal explanation. |
| Authority; user model | C §§4, 7; charter issuance uses existing consequential authority. The owner chooses purpose, taste, limits and participation; ordinary investigations require no fresh founder approval. |
| Failure; self-improvement | C §§8–10; inquiry custody, reservations and unresolved effects survive replacement and restoration. Charter/evaluator changes are independently controlled experiments, outside the candidate improver's authority. |
| Strengths; weaknesses | Potential protection against locally convenient deferral; mandatory reserved capacity, extra disputes and irrelevant inquiries are costs. No benefit is established. |
| Scalability limits; operational complexity | C §§9–10 plus charter overlap, false-signal volume, custodian capacity and deadline coordination. Bound intake, active inquiries, appeals and investigation resources. |
| Security; provider dependence | C §§4, 6, 9; no source-originated grants, model credential bypass, quota invention or automatic paid fallback. Protect charter administration from scheduler and improver writes. |
| Migration difficulty; wrong-design conditions | C §10; transfer charter grants, custody, reservations, disputed dispositions and replacement obligations explicitly. Reject when simpler case policies obtain equivalent outcomes or protected inquiry burdens defeat viable operation. |

Business allocation remains concrete. Research observes real permitted sources; product/design/build produce and test deliverable experiences; launch/distribution/sales execute only authorized offers and claims; support performs remedies and tracks recurrence; finance reconciles native records; professional services determine applicable treatment; security/privacy implement access, incidents and data duties; procurement/hiring engage real capacity through existing authority; analytics preserves denominators; pivot/succession/closure disposes of surviving promises. Charters can expose missing work in every area, including inaccessible research or closure resources. They cannot substitute generated advice for any of these delivery obligations.

The eight common scenarios remain required, with these additional checks: **(1)** launch must not retire contradictory research; **(2)** a claim inquiry cannot override opt-out or send restrictions; **(3)** invoice inquiry cannot fabricate reconciliation or filing; **(4)** inquiry resumption cannot release an unknown refund hold; **(5)** two ventures cannot appropriate the same inquiry or service reserve; **(6)** changed intent can revoke future inquiry grants but preserves reasons and surviving duties; **(7)** poisoning cannot create standing or let an improver change its assessor; **(8)** restoration cannot resurrect a charter, and closure needs accepted inquiry dispositions alongside actual external obligations. Original C §8 supplies the full scenarios and falsifiers; all remain unexecuted.

Deployment still needs the transactional authority boundary, isolated execution, protected independent evidence and recovery persistence, domain services and reachable human continuity in C §9. Charter availability adds an operational commitment; a sleeping personal machine supplies no response-time guarantee. Subscription money, observed provider capacity, compute, owner minutes, professional fees, infrastructure and closure reserves remain separate. This repair makes no quotation, throughput, pricing, licensing or supplier-availability claim.

## 7. Acceptance and falsification for independent rereview

The reviewer can decide the conceptual repair without pretending the experiments ran:

1. Remove all five optional C1 mechanisms. Identify the operation by which a current production priority could deny an otherwise admitted first discriminator or retire its inquiry. If an ordinary C2 scheduler can do either, this proposal fails its own foundation.
2. Give A every adaptive behavior it claims. Record which inquiry rights follow from its actual grants and authority contract, rather than assuming missing prohibitions. An A control with the same indispensable rights is the same foundation on this axis and cannot be counted separately.
3. Inspect the research/support/finance trace for an obligation, permission or administrative-cost difference. Labels, queue layouts, graph storage and message counts do not qualify. If no such difference survives, retain C as an A variant and require another foundation before selection; do not repair the count with another filename.
4. Subsequently compare ordinary and adversarial workloads, including correlated false complaints mixed with one real issue, charter-holder loss and a false H2. Predeclare equal total resources, due-service floors, inquiry deadlines and owner limits. Report discovered/missed obligations, useful hypotheses, legitimate service, unsafe effects, unresolved dispositions, owner understanding and all coordination costs separately.
