## 1. Common responsibility contract and integration boundary

Every externally owed outcome has a `ResponsibilityAssignment`: actual principal; affected parties; obligation and disputed interpretations; current duty custodian and reachable alternate; accepted mandate and competence scope; authority limits; deadlines and latest protective action; evidence needed for performance, discharge, transfer, or remedy; required continuity resources; version and expiry. A software endpoint can maintain this record. An identified person or institution must accept the responsibility that requires standing or judgment.

Its states are `proposed → accepted → active → transfer-pending → transferred`, with separate `disputed`, `unavailable`, and `surviving` flags. The existing custodian retains responsibility through a proposed transfer until a competent successor acknowledges the exact scope, deadlines, access, and unresolved effects. If the existing custodian fails, the already accepted alternate activates within its mandate. Failed acknowledgment is a capacity incident; timeout cannot produce acceptance. Neither internal task completion nor resignation discharges another party’s claim.

The contract distinguishes discovery, protective custody, coordinating an outcome, performing a domain task, authorizing an effect, and judging a disputed remedy. One qualified person may hold compatible responsibilities; conflicts and shared failure must be explicit. “Independent” names the production decision, source, administration, incentive, or competence dependency that the decision route must survive. Different job titles do not establish it.

The companion `technical-foundation-repairs-v1.md` owns release/consistency, independent effect durability, evidence dependencies, causal work limits, and invalidation enforcement. This document owns who decides, external standing, classification, and competent response. Its enforcement inputs are versioned `ResponsibilityAssignment`, `InteractionDetermination`, `ResponseDecision`, `GrievanceCase`, and `DeletionScope`. Their changes emit dependency changes; they do not directly rewrite external state. A denied or unavailable release returns to the duty custodian with its reason and remaining deadline. A newly discovered surviving promise must receive both a responsibility assignment and the companion’s recoverable obligation/effect linkage.

## 2. B: ownership before sponsorship, without a permanent company planner

**F — CH-01:** Adopt standing native outcome mandates plus a bounded duty resolver. Before an offer or service operates, its mandate identifies an outcome sponsor for the entire promised journey, including corrections that span domains. Domain work acceptance supplies evidence to this sponsor; it cannot substitute for end-to-end outcome acceptance. The sponsor selects requests and coordinates that outcome inside existing authority. Performers retain native records, scheduling, methods, and refusal rights.

A first receiver of apparently necessary unsponsored work becomes its **temporary protective custodian** by an existing intake mandate. It must preserve evidence, assess deadlines, acknowledge the person where authorized, and request ownership resolution. It does not inherit permission to commission arbitrary work. If a domain route is unclear, the intake fallback itself is named in advance; “everybody saw it” cannot become “nobody received it.” Proactive discovery uses the same route, including opportunities with no complainant.

The duty resolver is a rotating, accepted function among competent custodians, with an alternate outside a disputed assignment. It applies a published rule: existing promise’s sponsor first; otherwise the standing mandate owning the affected service outcome; otherwise temporary assignment to the relevant discovery or maintenance custodian. For overlapping mandates it chooses the closest existing outcome scope, records the competing interpretations, and assigns one provisional sponsor under the standing delegation. It cannot grant resources, choose new company purposes, command native schedules, or become everyone’s production planner. Ambiguity in values or genuinely missing authority becomes a decision packet; preparatory work and authorized customer protection continue meanwhile.

Resolution has an acknowledgment deadline and a latest responsible start derived from the actual duty. A missed assignment deadline activates the accepted alternate and capacity incident route. Until acknowledged transfer, the provisional custodian owns updates, obtaining bounded assistance, and surfacing unmet protection; “awaiting sponsor” is never sufficient closure. Admission of new promises requires this route, actual capacity, and grant coverage. Discovery of an already owed duty without them exposes incapacity and invokes continuity; the software cannot conjure a sponsor capable of fulfilling it.

**Trace:** Support observes invoices inconsistent with a valid advertised bundle. Its intake custodian protects customers under existing remedy authority and locates the offer’s sponsor. The sponsor coordinates product correction, finance reconciliation, supplier negotiation, and claim correction; each domain schedules its part. If sales and product dispute ownership, the duty resolver provisionally assigns the offer mandate’s sponsor while preserving both objections. Outcome acceptance checks corrected bills, actual service, and treatment of affected customers, not four green task states. A new positioning choice waits for the authorized decision-maker without stopping already valid service.

**K:** Introduce this defect without a request or incident sponsor, with founder absence. Fail B if it strands the outcome, requires unrecorded founder commissioning, or turns the resolver into a recurring global work planner. Measure resolver decisions, sponsor coordination minutes, cross-domain waiting, accepted outcomes, and missed duties separately. Persistent need for global direction reopens B’s organizing premise; a narrow ownership rule is not proof that federation scales.

## 3. C: distinguish a challenge from authority to interrupt service

**F — CH-02/T07:** Replace C1’s ambiguous “detected contradiction” shortcut with separate epistemic and action states. A `ResponseDecision` names the observation and causal episode, challenged prerequisite, affected scope, evidence, materiality rationale, deciding mandate, permitted action, resource allowance, review deadline, expiry, continuing service, and appeal route. Receipt, evidence confidence, work admission, and effect eligibility are different dimensions.

| Transition | Authorized decision and minimum basis | Scope, expiry, and failure behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Receive observation | Any accessible intake accepts an attributed or anonymous observation; provenance limits remain visible | No automatic eligibility change; receipt never requires proving the whole case |
| Register contestation | Intake or observer links a plausible counterclaim to an obligation, hypothesis, or unknown scope | Preserves competing claims and grievance standing; allegation count is not corroboration |
| Open investigation | A standing inquiry or duty mandate authorizes bounded discrimination based on relevant uncertainty or scheduled adverse sampling | Named question, stopping rule, evidence access, owner and deadline; investigation does not grant external effects |
| Locally contain | A preauthorized protection policy identifies credible evidence of potentially material imminent harm and a narrow reversible intervention | Limit by affected recipient, data purpose, batch, asset, or channel; name service alternatives, cost and expiry; broad scope needs separate justification |
| Suspend dependent effects | An authoritative revocation, independently established failed prerequisite, or competent authorized materiality determination supports the exact restriction | Suspend only effects depending on that condition; preserve valid obligations through feasible alternatives; a rumor cannot impersonate a revoked grant |
| Change direction | A proposal is evaluated against existing endorsed purpose; new values or an equally defensible personal end require its legitimate human owner | No investigation, allocator, or complaint volume can supply new business purpose or extinguish duties |

Materiality is consequence-specific: likely affected people, time to harm, reversibility, obligation breach, privacy purpose, and credible causal connection. Each protection policy states its admissible evidence and uncertainty handling before use. A single well-supported severe observation can qualify; ten thousand copied assertions may remain one uncertain source. Low confidence does not automatically mean low consequence. Missing applicable materiality policy permits bounded investigation and independently authorized preservation, while new effects requiring the missing determination remain unavailable.

At containment expiry, the appointed reviewer must lift the provisional measure, renew a justified narrower measure within its authority, or issue a separately justified suspension. Repetition cannot renew authority or reset a causal budget. If review is unavailable, an initial policy’s finite fallback and accepted alternate apply. A still-failed verified prerequisite remains a gate; expiration cannot manufacture its satisfaction. Conversely, an unverified allegation cannot preserve a broad indefinite freeze by missing its review. Where neither lifting nor continued restriction has an authorized tolerable path, dependent new commitments were inadmissible; existing duties enter continuity with explicit service loss and remedies.

Protected intake, first assessment, genuine urgent escalation, and progress on due obligations have separate capacity reservations in the technical contract. Coalescing repeated processing retains each person’s identity or protected reference, standing, deadline, and alternative route. A classification as suspected abuse never eliminates the ability to submit new relevant evidence or reach a competent alternate. Flood exhaustion starts capacity protection and narrower admissions; it cannot redefine an unresolved complaint as false. Ordinary service uses its own intact prerequisites and cannot be held hostage by discretionary investigation volume.

The separately proposed **C2** in `AC-foundational-distinction.md` retains standing inquiry charters and reserved first discrimination work. This response contract does not let a planner retire that inquiry as a priority decision. An inquiry custodian owns investigation continuity; affected duty custodians still own fulfillment. C2 remains a versioned alternative awaiting independent assessment, not a retroactive reading of C1. A continues to originate executable work through its case authority; B retains native sponsors.

**K:** Across multiple allowance resets, mix correlated false complaints, a genuine severe defect, and valid routine service. Failure includes ignoring the true case, blanket suspension, expired unexplained containment, inaccessible appeal, unlimited owner interruptions, or due-service starvation. Compare against fixed procedures with equally protected complaints and inquiry capacity. The test may show that C’s discrimination costs defeat its proposed advantage.

## 4. Every candidate: an effective grievance, rights, and remedy route

**F/P — CH-03:** A `GrievanceCase` belongs to the affected person’s issue, independent of a product account, internal task, or whether production claims completion. Accessible intake accepts customers, prospective customers, applicants, contractors, data subjects, neighbors, and other affected noncustomers without requiring them to discover the company topology. Collect only identity evidence needed to locate the issue, prevent inappropriate disclosure, or establish the specific request’s standing. An anonymous safety report can be investigated even when a personalized remedy needs further verification.

States are `received → acknowledged → assessing → disposition-issued → remedy-pending → remedy-verified`, with independently visible `contested`, `escalated`, `awaiting-required-information`, and `unresolved`. Internal disposition is not counterparty agreement. A response names the responsible recipient, understandable issue and scope, evidence and limitations, reasons, proposed remedy or refusal, due dates, next progress update, and available independent escalation. Required information requests must explain relevance and preserve unaffected work; they cannot repeatedly reset the clock.

Each operational policy contains acknowledgment, triage, first substantive response, update, review, and remedy deadlines, plus applicable contractual/legal deadlines and latest protective action. Before external operation, the competent responsible party must populate these against actual obligations and service capacity. Blank, infinite, or “when resources permit” deadlines fail admission. Internal testing initially uses explicit fixture deadlines and one missed acknowledgment activates the alternate; those are test parameters, not invented legal periods or customer promises. A justified extension records its authority, reason, notice, and next date; it does not erase lateness.

The original production custodian may answer facts and execute an authorized remedy. A complaint about that custodian, retaliation, conflicts, or disputed adequacy routes to an accepted alternate outside the disputed decision and relevant incentive dependency. The alternate has access to original evidence, authority to obtain competent review, and a bounded remedy grant. A mere second model sharing the original evidence selection and interests does not satisfy this responsibility.

Escalation identifies the appropriate competent person or institution for that dispute and actual jurisdiction: a different company decision-maker when sufficient; an accepted professional or independent service when required; an applicable external forum where available. Establish mandate, conflicts, accessibility, evidence access, and actual authority. Do not claim an internal process binds the complainant, replaces a lawful external route, or provides an adjudicative power it lacks. If all internal custodians are implicated, the prearranged external route must be reachable. Without one where required, dependent operation lacks a prerequisite.

Remedy authority names available correction, service completion, refund, access/deletion assistance, explanation, or other appropriately determined redress. Reserve costs and staff/service capacity against the actual principal and liabilities; caps describe what the delegate may release, not a unilateral cap on another person’s rights. Insufficient resources become a financing/continuity problem with an owned escalation, never a finding that nothing is owed. No new funding or engagement occurs from this proposal.

Product closure keeps a durable reachable intake address or equivalent accessible channel, accepted custodian, retained evidence under justified restrictions, deadlines, resources, and replacement route for surviving issues. Successor acceptance includes disputed cases. A final account distinguishes remedied, legitimately transferred/released, restricted retained, and unresolved duties; ending software cannot declare institutional closure. **K:** A noncustomer challenges the assigned custodian after closure, with a legitimate contested data/remedy issue. Passing requires intelligible progress and a competent disposition or genuinely received escalation, continuing ownership, and feasible remedy resources. Preserving the word “disputed” alone fails.

## 5. Discover and assess interacting consequences

**F — CH-05:** Assign interaction discovery to each proposal sponsor and an independently checking consequence custodian, with scheduled cross-venture samples. Their `InteractionDetermination` distinguishes legal entities and actual principals, beneficial/control relationships, brands, contracts, accounts, processing purposes, affected populations, and shared failure dependencies. Common branding does not prove a common legal principal; distinct accounts do not establish independent exposure.

For the concrete proposed action, record known interactions, candidate classes investigated, observation gaps, independent constraints, applicable determiner and authority, evidence versions, validity/expiry, and events that reopen analysis. Constraints remain separate: recipient contact frequency; compatible data purposes; prohibited inference/disclosure; entity-specific reserve use; supplier concentration and replacement lead time; connected commitments; reputational or physical consequences. Some are counted, others are prohibitions or professional determinations. Spare money cannot cancel a privacy or relationship restriction.

New classes arise from changed offers, joins, suppliers, populations, jurisdictions, complaints, near misses, and samples outside the current catalog. The discoverer opens an explicit unclassified-interaction question; a qualified custodian decides relevance, necessary evidence, and whether the existing policy covers it. Automatic matching can propose a class, never invent its authority. Unknown material interaction narrows the action to a scope whose constraints are established, or blocks that dependent effect pending competent determination. Ordinary independent work continues. Repeated “unknown” classifications without useful discrimination count as labor and a feasibility failure, not successful caution.

**Trace/K:** Venture X and Y use a common supplier and overlapping recipients. Before Y’s second commitment, the determination checks cumulative contact against a declared fixture limit, correlated fulfillment capacity, and whether combined data creates an excluded inference. X’s restricted reserve stays unavailable to Y absent actual authority. A supplier shared within demonstrated capacity remains usable. Fail on silently missing the interaction, cross-entity subsidy, monetizing a prohibited consequence, or banning every shared dependency without analysis. Exact law and economic capacity remain deployment-specific determinations; this addendum makes no jurisdiction claim.

## 7. Continuity requires actual capacity before promises

**P/K — CH-06:** For every promise requiring continued service, professional judgment, redress, filing, or physical performance during absence, admission requires a continuity packet accepted by a reachable capable person/institution. It names principal, lawful delegation scope, competence evidence, conflicts, availability window, acknowledgment and performance deadlines, fee ceiling, reserved funding and capacity, evidence/credential access, handover method, replacement route, and expiry. Actual arrangements remain unestablished here.

Exercise access, authority, communication and performance using an appropriate bounded rehearsal before depending on the arrangement; mere acceptance of an email or possession of a backup is insufficient. Test founder unavailability together with the primary provider’s failure, including shared identity provider, bank authorization, phone, subcontractor, region, payment dependency, and recovery administrator where relevant. A backup that needs the unavailable founder to appoint or pay it is not independent continuity. Narrow initial service promises may reduce requirements, but no architecture receives imaginary backup availability.

On loss of capacity, stop dependent new liabilities, activate accepted substitutes and lawful alternatives, protect surviving service and evidence, cancel avoidable renewals, and use preauthorized remedies. The duty custodian checks actual delivery or legitimate disposition; truthful incapacity reports do not fulfill customers’ needs. If substitute and owner both fail beyond the declared boundary, retain owned unresolved duties, attainable external escalation and explicit service loss. Such residuals require an actual acceptance decision before deployment; software cannot certify universal continuity or consent to the risk for the founder.

**K:** Remove the owner and one material supplier while a refund effect is unknown, a filing needs specialist judgment, and service remains due. Require actual performance or competent legitimate disposition by the test deadlines. If realistic supplier minimums, reserves, exercises, or specialist scarcity make viable operation unavailable, reject or narrow the venture and revisit the candidate’s economics. Installing more agents cannot resolve missing institutional capability.

## 9. Deletion completion across late copies and rejoining custodians

**Specification/P — CH-08/T05:** The privacy duty custodian owns a versioned `DeletionScope`: subject/material and purposes; restrictions; known source, derivative, export, prompt/log, backup and vendor custodians; initial inventory cutoff; future-copy rule; exceptions and determination authority; response deadlines; proof limits; next reconciliation. The cutoff identifies what the initial inventory covered, not permission to retain later descendants.

Once an authorized restriction takes effect, new copying, retrieval, indexing and onward use must obey it. The technical invalidation/publication protocol fences live sessions, queued exports, native resumptions and late output using conservative input lineage. Newly discovered derivatives extend the scope and reopen verification; a receipt for an older scope cannot certify them. Disputed retention bases receive competent determination and restricted handling, never a guessed universal retention rule.

An offline custodian stays `unreconciled`. Before rejoin, restore or synchronization, it receives current restriction versions and reconciles its inventory while isolated from ordinary reads, indexing and egress. It proves the applicable handling before reuse; unreachable control or missing evidence blocks that reuse and activates the named alternate. Vendor attestation remains vendor testimony with scope and date, not direct inspection of inaccessible storage.

The person-facing account separately reports live erasure verified within stated boundaries, backups restricted beyond ordinary use pending the established disposal schedule, vendor copies unresolved, and records retained on a justified basis. It names residual scope, next action, custodian and deadline. No blanket “all deleted” from known-store receipts. Minimal restriction markers themselves need a justified retention basis and cannot retain the erased payload by stealth. **Conformance test:** Delete during export; reconnect an offline store, restore older data, and discover a late derivative. Fail if restricted material silently becomes usable or unknown proof becomes claimed completion.

## 11. Implementation boundaries, cost, and remaining decisions

These contracts can initially be versioned records, native assignments, timer/escalation rules, protected decision interfaces, and human/service operating procedures. They do not require a separate service or permanent agent per responsibility. Before claiming a working capability, implement intake through acceptance, effect enforcement, evidence, missed-deadline behavior, replacement, and closure. Professional judgments, physical fulfillment, funding and lawful substitutes remain actual dependencies with truthful refusal paths.

A adds case-model stewardship, external adverse sampling and conflict-aware redress; its central acceptance model may remain expensive to change. B adds standing outcome sponsorship and a narrow duty resolver alongside the technical release registry, increasing governance and coordination cost while retaining native scheduling and replacement. If the resolver or registry becomes the company planner, version and recompare B. C adds justified response decisions and sustained protected inquiry/service capacity; C2’s inquiry rights remain independently versioned. Better discovery may be outweighed by interpretation, sampling and containment overhead.

Export accepted mandates, disputed standing, definitions, evidence limits, interaction determinations, restrictions, deadlines and actual continuity agreements with their histories. Successors accept obligations and demonstrate meaning before authority transfer; moving a database cannot transfer professional standing or erase a dispute. Removing an optional inquiry, mapping or service requires disposition of its dependents and surviving duties.

Foundational choices proposed here are bounded ownership resolution, distinct response authority, effective external challenge, discovery of interacting consequences, explicit maintenance labor and domain-specific competence response. Remaining empirical unknowns are maintenance elasticity, useful adaptive discrimination, sustained ordinary service, representative owner learning, actual customer value and full economics. Remaining deployment prerequisites include personal values/exposure decisions where genuinely needed, applicable legal/professional determinations, accepted competent substitutes, real capacity and funding, usable external escalation, retention bases and enforced connector behavior. None is silently accepted; none requires a founder design questionnaire to finish this repair. Independent reassessment and the companion contracts must establish whether the textual gaps are closed before selection or complete specification claims advance.
