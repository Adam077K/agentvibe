**The one line in the diff that was not briefed is the load-bearing one, so here is the reasoning in a form a stranger can act on.** The `design` lens now refuses "a design-quality score, where the honest output is a list of findings", and `craft`'s last check ends "and never a score". The evidence is an asymmetry rather than general scepticism: in Hertzum and Jacobsen 2003 any two evaluators agreed with each other on **5-17%** of problems while each found **18-60%** of the collective set. They were not producing noise, they were finding **different real problems** — so the **union** of a weak panel is worth having and its **average** is nearly meaningless. Scoring destroys the union by construction: it collapses each finder to one number, then averages the numbers away. The figures against the PASS/BLOCK version: a VLM judge scores **0.543** against a five-designer panel majority that is itself only **0.741** self-consistent, and severity agreement runs Spearman **0.23-0.31** — reproducible and invalid, the failure mode that looks most like a working mechanism. **How to act on it.** When a design panel hands back a number, the number is the defect: send it back for the enumerated findings. Add judges to widen coverage, never to average their scores. Do not put a design-quality verdict behind an exit code — conformance is the half that may bind. Figures and sources: `docs/03-system-design/DESIGN-CAPABILITY.md` section 6.1.
