## WATCH, in one screen

*A crew that stands the night over everything you are building, moves only where moving would change what it
does next, judges its work by contact with the world and its books against records it does not write, keeps a
book of bets and a book of debts and never ranks the second against the first, predicts you so that it can ask
you less about facts and more about what is only yours, and never once does alone what cannot be undone.*

- **Harness or organisation: a crew.** Six positions (maker, lookout, looker, judge, mouth, broker-and-relay),
  no titles, standing orders the crew cannot amend, one log, a two-way relief, one captain whose sleep is
  protected by rule.
- **Two atoms, never ranked:** the bet (the ply's fields) and the debt (owed, creditor, date). **The keel** is
  the one thing under construction per venture, never forked; **the weather** is everything else, forked wide,
  selected only by contact, powerless by argv and credential.
- **The drive** is four questions in fixed order: a debt due → done; the keel live → runs; in the weather, the
  cheapest test whose two posteriors straddle a decision's flip point; otherwise **nothing, and say why**. No
  cardinal score. Goals are opened by the founder, the crew (only as children of live bets), the world, and the
  clock.
- **Five postures per venture:** Drive · Walk · Serve · Tend · Dark. Walk stops at the first unsettled assumption
  with both branches built. At most three ventures in a making posture; a reserve of the window is the founder's.
- **Autonomy:** three exposure classes over four currencies (reversible: drill the undo; compensable: drill the
  delay; one-way: the founder's at any score); judge strength Hard / Rendered / Reactive / Blind. **The Second**
  (the founder model) may only lower what is asked, about facts, and never acts. The largest decision against
  WAKE; all three minds refused the licence.
- **Ten stores:** the Log (the only truth), the Wake with its graveyard, the Continuo, the Book (written by
  surprise, bi-temporal), the Lessons (trajectory analysis only, compiled into reflexes at the third sighting),
  the Bench with licences, the Hands, the Second, **the World Ledger** (books reconciled against the bank, the
  processor, the git host, the mail log, the registrar; a metric with no outside owner may never promote), the
  Treasury with **the refusal ledger** (every stop priced).
- **Four surfaces:** the desk (phone, decisions only, typed fact/preference, defaults only for reversible drilled
  items, 72-hour heartbeat), the bay (Mac, a strip board, cocked-out strips), the room (second monitor, the honest
  office simulation, never a control surface), the dailies (the raw work at the founder's hour). Terminal stays
  primary input; voice is the originating channel, read back before it binds, never for approval.
- **The watch** replaces the night: five hours, five a day, odd so the phase drifts; settle · make · prove ·
  forge · consolidate · relieve; crash-only; the relief has four phases and the outgoing watch audits the
  incoming one.
- **The door:** every outside part enters as a bet on probation with seven tests; ninety days uncalled and it
  leaves; taint narrows a cell that read outside content. First ten of the 177: ccusage, beads, projectmem,
  graphiti, dbos-transact, restate, pyribs, promptfoo, inspect_ai, claude-code-router.
- **Storage:** files in git, one house repo and one repo per venture, the Log as truth, blobs by hash off-machine;
  what no backup restores is credentials, so the disaster plan is a credential plan, drilled.
- **The field map:** ~half of a company's jobs are already deterministically checkable by instruments the world
  gives away; a third measurable but slow; a sixth the founder's forever. Security block all Hard, wired on day
  one.
- **Health:** three lines at once: fact questions fall, preference questions rise, founder-originated shipped
  work stays above a floor (the capture check).
- **Cost:** no number; the formula and its dominant term (do siblings hit the cache); ten real moves on an API
  key before any estimate is believed. The terms question on unattended `claude -p` is the founder's.
