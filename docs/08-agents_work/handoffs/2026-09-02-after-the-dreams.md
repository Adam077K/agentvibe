## WAKE, in one screen

*A company that runs while you sleep, judges its own work with instruments it built, predicts you before it
asks you, and earns every freedom by measurement rather than by permission.*

- **The inversion:** nothing upstream of the work may say no; everything downstream must produce a number.
  Dark zone (fork, mutate, no gate) → light zone (selection by contact with reality only) → the founder
  retires branches. The founder is a retirement stage, not a gate.
- **Organs:** five stores (the Wake, the Book, the Bench, the Hands, the Understudy) · three engines (the
  Orrery, the Compiler, the Vigil) · one boundary (the Line: undo cost in money, time, reputation, law) ·
  one interface (the Edition, one page at 05:00).
- **Seven laws:** the dark zone may not say no · only contact selects · autonomy is computed, never configured
  · the Book is written by surprise · ask preference, never permission · the fifth is a floor · nothing judges
  its own homework.
- **Autonomy = one function, three arguments:** the Line (undo cost) → identity questions stay the founder's
  → the strongest instrument's measured strength (Hard / Rendered / Reactive / Understudy calibration) → act
  silently / with receipt / stage / fork. A discriminator's AUC licenses generating; only a live hit rate
  licenses acting silently.
- **The unit:** the ply (parent, wager, undo, judge, budget, death, outcome), worked by a mortal cell.
- **The night:** settle → germinal centre, three rounds → replay → forge one instrument → consolidate → write.

- **The fifth:** a fifth of every night on work the Understudy is confident the founder will hate. A floor.
- **§22:** what survives of the existing harness (ledger as the Book's substrate, the check suite as the Bench's

## What WAKE does not yet cover — the next round's question

Checked by the CEO against the founder's last message. Six territories around the machine are undesigned:

1. **The drive.** A posture per project, set by the founder: forward-always (startup) · walk-with-me
   (project) · wait (research) · quiet (client). Each posture fixes who may open a goal, the night's length,
   the budget, and when it may interrupt. This is the answer to "not one system on everything, burning tokens."
2. **The founder's environment.** Terminal, phone, Mac, and the window into the night: the tree of plies alive,
   cells born and dying, the map filling. A view of the Wake, not a task board. Office-like simulation is the
   founder's stated wish; WAKE's stance is that a dashboard nobody acts from is a diary; the review found the
   founder's revealed channel is rendered pages. Design to the founder's taste, name the tension.
3. **The fleet.** Many projects, one founder, one budget, four live sessions today across three repos. What
   one WAKE may say to another; what sessions share and never share.
4. **The door for outside parts.** How a repository, a skill or a tool from the world enters: through the
   hand-admission test with a test attached, or not at all. Then sort the 177 catalogued repos through it.
5. **Storage.** Where every store physically lives, in what form, at what size, what survives a dead machine.
6. **The field map.** The founder's last addition: a company needs knowledge in dozens of fields — setting up a
   database, using tools correctly, never leaking an API key, a design system, a product, customer
   communication, a marketing video, promotion, and every other job. WAKE's answer in principle is §9: a field
   is entered by learning to judge it first, and a license is the public record of how well. What is missing is
   the inventory: every job a company needs, each with its ground truth, its instrument class (Hard / Rendered
   / Reactive / Blind), the outside parts that could enter through the door for it, and the security fields
   (keys, secrets, migrations) as Hard instruments from day one. The founder's question underneath it: is this
   a harness for vibe-coding with agents, or an organisation? WAKE's answer is an organisation with no roles,
   where domain expertise is a license, not an identity; say that back, and design the map.

Then WAKE is the whole system and the spec is re-derived from it (§22 is the migration table).

## Things the founder may have missed — a checklist to raise, not decide

- Money in: pricing, billing, churn, cohorts. No territory covers the only rung-4 signal. The offer and the
  customer as artifacts (Fable 4.4).
- Obligations, people, consent, data-protection duties, erasure — an append-only never-expiring store cannot
  meet them (Fable 4.8). Identity and standing; disclosure of machine-made work, undated so far.
- **Unattended `claude -p` on a subscription may be a terms question** (Fable 1.17). An API key answers it;
  both reviewers estimated cost and disagreed by 10× — run ten moves and read `total_cost_usd`.
- The always-on host (deferred by the founder to after one measured overnight); the second human; succession.
- Reconciliation of the register against the world's own records (Stripe, git host, analytics, mail log) —
  the anti-Goodhart mechanism every outside party asks for first (Fable 5).
- Whether the Understudy converges or learns to flatter; a founder who rubber-stamps corrupts calibration at
  the source (WAKE §19).
- Defending the dark zone against every engineer who wants to put a check in it (PLY).
- A second model family for judge seats; `gemini` is installed and one run was authorised in principle.
- Voice as an input: the founder speaks by voice; WAKE's one voice out is designed, voice in is not.
