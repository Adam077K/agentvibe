# Round 5 — worked scenarios (two writers, one file)

Follow `docs/vision-v3/_process/prompts/R5-writer-common.md`. Now that files 01–11 and 16–17 exist, **read the ones your
scenarios touch** (range-read; they are 45–115 KB): the scenarios must use their mechanism names exactly and must be
*consistent* with them — a scenario that needs a mechanism no file defines is a finding; put it in a closing
"Gaps found" list rather than inventing the mechanism silently.

Each scenario, same template:
- **Situation** (one paragraph) and **autonomy level / Charter** in force.
- **Timeline table**: time · what happens · authority acting (Want/Fund/Do/Check/Know/Hold/Brake) · agents launched **by
  title**, family (Claude/Codex) and model · skills loaded · cost so far.
- **Budget**: forecast vs actual, reserves touched.
- **Memory writes**: what was deposited, where, with which label/boundary.
- **Approvals and founder contact**: every contact with its Class × Reach, on which surface, and founder-minutes spent.
- **What the founder sees**: the actual lines on the actual surface (short mock).
- **What could go wrong here** and which mechanism catches it.
- **What the organisation learned** (Priors, Standing Orders, skills, Backlot) and how next time is faster.
Numbers are illustrations unless measured — mark them. Mermaid sequence diagram for at least every other scenario.
