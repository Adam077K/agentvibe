# Round 2 — specialist seat (common brief)

You design **one part** of the chosen organisation, deeply. Thirteen other seats design the other parts in parallel.

## Read, in order
1. `docs/vision-v3/_process/SEAT-CONTEXT.md` — rules and mechanics.
2. `docs/vision-v3/00-FOUNDER-DIRECTION.md` — binding.
3. `docs/vision-v3/r1-concepts/R1-SYNTHESIS.md` — **the carried-forward architecture: spine, grafts, vocabulary.** Design
   inside it. If you believe it is wrong for your part, say so in a section "Challenge to the synthesis" and propose the bigger
   or better alternative — never a smaller one.
4. The Round 0 briefs (`docs/vision-v3/r0-outward/`) and concept docs (`r1-concepts/`) relevant to your seat.
5. Prior material relevant to your seat: grep v1 (`docs/vision-system/`, explorer data in
   `docs/vision-system/planning/site/explorer/data/`), v2 (`docs/vision-v2/`), and the engineering specs
   (`docs/vision-v3/engineering/`). Mine what they got right; you are not bound by them.

## Your document (15–30 KB, Markdown) at the path in your brief
1. **Summary** — the design in ten lines.
2. **The design** — mechanisms, each with a name, what it does, its data shape (YAML/TS/JSON schema), and its trigger.
3. **Diagrams** — at least two mermaid diagrams (flows, state machines, sequences).
4. **Interfaces** — what your part needs from, and gives to, the other parts (mission engine, agent organisation, autonomy,
   memory, skills/tools, surfaces, engineering, economics…).
5. **Worked examples** — at least two, concrete (who launches by title, which model, time, cost, memory writes, approvals).
6. **Ideas the founder did not ask for** — at least five, specific.
7. **Risks** — each with a design answer, never a cut.
8. **Open decisions** — at most three, each with your recommendation.

## Mechanics
- **Write the file early** (skeleton with all headings) and fill it section by section with Edit, so running out of turns
  never loses the work.
- Return a **≤200-word summary**: the 3–5 decisions that matter most for the architect, and any challenge to the synthesis.
