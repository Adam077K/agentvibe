# Round 1 — concept advocate (common brief)

You are the **advocate** for one whole-system concept of the agentic organisation. Four other advocates are designing
radically different concepts in parallel; you do not see theirs. A judge panel (Claude + Codex) will score all five on:
**ambition reached · founder leverage · handling unknown work · speed · robustness · learning over time.** The carried-forward
architecture will be the best *combination*, so make your concept's strongest mechanisms unmistakable and portable.

Read first: `docs/vision-v3/_process/SEAT-CONTEXT.md` (rules), `docs/vision-v3/00-FOUNDER-DIRECTION.md` (binding), and the
Round 0 briefs in `docs/vision-v3/r0-outward/` (all five; skim, then go deep where your concept needs it). You may grep v1/v2 and
the engineering specs for material; you are not bound by them.

## Your document (≤12 KB, Markdown)
1. **The idea in one paragraph** — and the single organising principle.
2. **One mermaid diagram** of the whole system.
3. **How work flows** — from founder intent (or self-generated initiative) to done, for work nobody has a playbook for.
4. **The founder's seat** — what he sees, decides, never touches; autonomy per project.
5. **Agents** — how they are defined (title + expertise, never personal names), composed into teams, launched on demand;
   Claude Code and Codex as equals; where hybrid specialties come from.
6. **Coordination and non-interference** — how agents avoid hurting each other's work.
7. **Memory and learning** — how the organisation gets measurably better every week.
8. **A worked day in the life** — hour by hour, across ≥3 ventures, at least one fully autonomous.
9. **Where this concept is strongest, and where it is weakest** — honest; each weakness gets a design answer, never a cut.
10. **Portable mechanisms** — the 5–8 mechanisms from your concept that should survive even if the concept as a whole loses.

Be concrete: numbers, named mechanisms, data shapes. Exceed the founder's words.
