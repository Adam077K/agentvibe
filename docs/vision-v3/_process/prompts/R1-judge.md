# Round 1 — judge (two independent judges: one Claude, one Codex)

Read `docs/vision-v3/_process/SEAT-CONTEXT.md`, `docs/vision-v3/00-FOUNDER-DIRECTION.md`, and all five concepts in
`docs/vision-v3/r1-concepts/` (C1-market, C2-cofounder, C3-swarm-codex, C4-lab, C5-studio). Skim `docs/vision-v3/r0-outward/`
for evidence where a concept's claim depends on it. You judge independently; the other judge's scores are not visible to you.

Score each concept 1–10 on each criterion, with a one-line justification per cell:
1. **Ambition reached** — does it reach "one founder out-builds a company of thousands", or does it quietly shrink?
2. **Founder leverage** — decisions and output per founder-hour; altitude of what reaches him.
3. **Handling unknown work** — no playbook, new field, novel venture type.
4. **Speed** — idea to shipped/validated, and reaction to events.
5. **Robustness** — failures, runaway cost, collisions, drift, adversaries, provider outages.
6. **Learning over time** — measurably better every week; transfer across ventures.

Your document (8–12 KB):
1. The score table (5 × 6 + total), with justifications.
2. For each concept: its single strongest mechanism and its most dangerous flaw.
3. **The best combination** — the architecture you would carry forward: which concept is the spine and which mechanisms
   are grafted from each of the others, with the reason. Name the conflicts between grafts and how to resolve each.
4. What *all five* missed — at least five ideas none of them had.
5. Evidence checks — any concept claim contradicted by the Round 0 research.
