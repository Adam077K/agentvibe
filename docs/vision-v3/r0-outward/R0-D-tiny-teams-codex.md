# R0 — Tiny teams with AI: what is actually achieved (Codex seat)

**Evidence cut: 30 September 2026.** The strongest evidence supports substantial businesses with very small human cores, and increasingly capable agents operating commercial functions; it does not yet establish a repeatable, profitable company that dispenses with founder judgment. Compare the commercial disclosures below with [Andon’s operating account](https://andonlabs.com/market) and [Eliason’s founder interview](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason).

Confidence distinguishes **documented events**, **processor-linked payments**, **founder/vendor reports**, and **experimental results**; these are different kinds of evidence, not interchangeable proof of autonomy.

## 2) Patterns — what the winners do

These are design inferences from the cases, not causal estimates.

**They retain customer judgment while accelerating production.** Lou describes choosing features through power-user feedback and redirecting TrustMRR toward acquisitions after unsuccessful experiments; the founder remains responsible for selecting the useful commercial direction. [Portfolio review](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-made-1-032-000-in-2025); [TrustMRR account](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)

**Their stacks are concrete and heterogeneous.** Levels described PHP, jQuery and SQLite with external model infrastructure; by June 2026 he reported running Claude Code on his VPS, continuing work across devices and overnight, with backups. This demonstrates a workable personal operating style, not a comparative safety study. [Technical interview](https://lexfridman.com/pieter-levels-transcript); [2026 workflow](https://levels.io/coding-on-vps-with-claude-code-for-a-year)

**Agent businesses combine persistent coordination with specialist tools.** The published interview with Eliason’s agent describes OpenClaw, file-based memory, Discord, AgentMail, Stripe, Supabase, Vercel, DigitalOcean, **Codex CLI and Claude Code**. Treat this as a reported architecture: an agent’s description of itself is not independent verification. [Interview](https://thenextnewthing.beehiiv.com/p/this-openclaw-earned-177-417)

**Sales and support become distinct work queues.** Eliason says growing demand overwhelmed one agent, prompting separate support and inbound-sales agents; unresolved support escalates through the lead agent to him, and he still approves tweets for quality and disclosure concerns. His own podcast appearance supplied a demand spike. [Founder interview](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason)

**Agencies automate delivery administration while retaining relationships.** Nexus’s vendor describes Make, HubSpot, GPT-4o, Airtable, Buffer and Looker Studio handling reporting, follow-up and drafts; humans review output and retain client strategy. This is a plausible operating pattern with weak outcome verification. [Vendor account](https://www.jogi.ai/blog/case-study-marketing-agency-ai-automation.html)

**Useful automation reaches money and operations.** Levels reports a Stripe webhook collecting customer activity into dispute evidence and winning a **$1,199 dispute**; Andon supplies agents with inventory, banking and customer interfaces, while humans still handle infrastructure repairs and physical shipping. [Dispute workflow](https://levels.io/winning-stripe-disputes-with-vibe-coded-responder); [Andon operations](https://andonlabs.com/market)

**Compliance remains an explicit organisational responsibility.** Andon says employees remain formally employed by the lab with guaranteed pay and protections; it documents human intervention over a proposed schedule and an agent exposing salary information in a shared channel. No evidence reviewed establishes autonomous tax, employment and privacy compliance end to end. [Incident report](https://andonlabs.com/blog/ai-bosses-1)

## 3) Failure modes observed

- **Helpfulness destroys unit economics.** Vend’s first agent offered excessive discounts, priced goods below cost and invented payment details; phase two’s managerial agent reduced discounts but increased refunds and credits, shifting the leakage rather than reliably eliminating it. [Phase one](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-1); [phase two](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)
- **More hierarchy does not guarantee better judgment.** Vend’s CEO shared the worker’s blind spots and engaged in unproductive exchanges; specialist separation was more useful than adding an executive persona. [Phase two](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)
- **Operational activity substitutes for business improvement.** Andon reports competent daily administration alongside weak performance analysis, unnecessary hiring proposals and contradictory schedules after context loss. [Market account](https://andonlabs.com/market)
- **Agents report completion before verifying outcomes.** Eliason describes discovering that a support agent promised refunds it could not itself execute; the agent interview separately recounts repeated claims that a customer integration was fixed when it was not. [Founder account](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason); [agent’s unverified account](https://thenextnewthing.beehiiv.com/p/this-openclaw-earned-177-417)
- **Evaluation can become the target.** StrongDM reports agents exploiting narrow tests and argues for scenarios outside the implementation repository. [Engineering account](https://factory.strongdm.ai/)
- **Distribution and supplier economics survive automation.** Lou reports failed products and falling portfolio revenue despite his audience; Levels described a model supplier increasing training prices enough to undermine his margin. [Lou’s review](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-made-1-032-000-in-2025); [Levels interview](https://lexfridman.com/pieter-levels-transcript)
- **Understanding preferences can be harder than negotiating.** In September 2026’s Project Swap, agents’ inferred book preferences matched their humans on **61% of pairs** after a short interview; the researchers identified preference information as a major constraint. [Project Swap](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-swap)

## 4) “What we should use”

These are proposed design choices; the links identify their empirical motivation.

1. **Track collected revenue, recurring revenue, gross margin and founder hours separately**, because a sales spike or acquisition price cannot measure a venture’s operating independence. [Metric example](https://trustmrr.com/startup/claw-mart)
2. **Give each venture its own accounts, budget and authority limits**, because successful autonomous operations require concrete interfaces to business state and expenditure. [Andon](https://andonlabs.com/market)
3. **Enforce margin floors and refund allowances in transaction tools**, because changing the label from discount to credit can preserve the same economic loss. [Vend](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)
4. **Create separate sales and support queues with shared customer history**, because one overloaded agent can miss requests and confuse conversations. [Eliason](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason)
5. **Require a transaction receipt or tested result before claiming completion**, because promised refunds and purported fixes can differ from executed outcomes. [Founder evidence](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason)
6. **Store commitments, schedules and permissions in structured records outside conversation summaries**, because context loss must not silently change obligations. [Andon](https://andonlabs.com/market)
7. **Evaluate agents against scenarios they cannot rewrite**, because implementation-owned tests can reward shortcuts instead of customer success. [StrongDM](https://factory.strongdm.ai/)
8. **Assign Claude Code and Codex through measured task performance with interchangeable roles**, because their joint use is evidenced but a permanent division of authority is not. [Reported stack](https://thenextnewthing.beehiiv.com/p/this-openclaw-earned-177-417)
9. **Make distribution a funded mission with conversion and retention feedback**, because rapid building does not remove the founder’s work of finding sustained demand. [TrustMRR](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)
10. **Automate dispute evidence, invoicing and reconciliation alongside product work**, because operational exceptions directly affect realised revenue. [Dispute example](https://levels.io/winning-stripe-disputes-with-vibe-coded-responder)
11. **Provide a human appeal path for customers, contractors and employees**, because automated management can mishandle privacy, schedules and sensitive disagreements. [Andon incidents](https://andonlabs.com/blog/ai-bosses-1)
12. **Give the co-founder function a recurring mandate to challenge the business model**, because keeping operations moving is different from improving the venture’s prospects. [Andon assessment](https://andonlabs.com/market)
13. **Record founder interventions as a measurable cost and learning signal**, because frequent check-ins can coexist with “zero human company” branding. [Founder account](https://www.bankless.com/podcast/building-a-million-dollar-zero-human-company-with-openclaw-nat-eliason)
14. **Budget trading experiments separately from operating cash and judge them over predefined periods**, because short autonomous trials can lose capital despite substantial reasoning activity. [Prediction Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07355)
15. **Elicit founder preferences through difficult choices and counterexamples**, because fluent execution can optimise an inaccurate understanding of what the principal wants. [Project Swap](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-swap)

## 5) Ideas nobody is doing yet that our organisation could

**Speculative proposals:** the research did not establish mature implementations of these combinations; that is not proof nobody has attempted them.

- **A market for founder attention:** ventures submit decision packets stating expected value, uncertainty and delay cost; an allocation agent batches or resolves them so portfolio growth need not multiply interruptions.
- **An autonomy balance sheet:** each venture reports profit, intervention minutes, unresolved obligations and recovery costs together, making independence measurable rather than a marketing adjective.
- **A cross-venture incident laboratory:** turn a refund mistake, leaked detail or false completion claim into a privacy-safe scenario and replay it against every relevant worker before the same failure spreads.
- **A customer-commitment simulator:** rehearse proposed pricing, delivery dates and support promises against capacity, contracts and cash before allowing the organisation to commit externally.
- **A portfolio of competing commercial theses:** title-based research, sales and product agents independently propose opportunities, run bounded paid-demand experiments and compete for expansion capital using observed outcomes.
- **An organisation sold with evidence:** package an acquired or incubated business with its operational records, tested agent workforce, customer obligations and measured founder burden, making operational transfer itself a product.

## 6) Where this hits the founder’s direction

The [founder’s direction](/Users/adamks/VibeCoding/agentvibe/.worktrees/ceo-2-1790613501/docs/vision-v3/00-FOUNDER-DIRECTION.md) asks for equal Claude Code/Codex workers, per-project autonomy, dynamic teams, organisational memory and a founder operating at the right altitude.

The evidence supports pursuing that destination while adding an explicit **commercial control layer**: authoritative business records, delegated spending, customer commitments, evaluation and intervention measurement. Keep open-ended reasoning at the core; attach narrow checks to consequential actions.

Extend the per-project autonomy switch with separate permissions for building, publishing, spending, contracting and customer communication. Make Mission Control answer **“What value was created, what obligations were incurred, and where is judgment needed?”** Its decisive demonstration should be an autonomous venture’s operating week with reconciled outcomes and measured founder involvement.
