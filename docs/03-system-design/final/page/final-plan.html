Direction is cheap and written down. The Watch is continuous and nearly free. Work is expensive and disposable. Nothing is believed until something outside the model says so. Everything lands as files you can read.

A day with it

06:40
Nothing rings unless a wake-me line fired. If one did, one line.
07:20
The briefing, one page you open. What moved, with the evidence. The raw work: the render, the diff, the email that would go out, staged. Two whiches, both built. What I could not check. What it cost, per window. You tap two, overrule one by typing a sentence; by 07:31 it is a rehearsal case.
09:00
The Floor. A terminal on the venture you care about. Sterile: nothing interrupts, nothing new on your window, the venture held whole. Your own browser and send button are here and nowhere else. When you leave, the queue is one paragraph.
lunch
Decide has one item: two landing pages, both live, the cost of each. One tap.
18:00
You are the bottleneck on one thing; it has cost four days.
night
Obligations first. Then, under each driven venture's ceiling, runs are born, work however they like, are checked by something outside the model, hand back seven fields, and die. Routine work burns the Gemini window and never touches yours.

The thirty decisions

Every place v3 and Keel disagreed, or one was silent. The full row, with the one-line why and the losing image kept by name, is §1 of the plan.

  1. the base, the organs, the names
    Keel is the base; its words stand KEEL
    lost: the OS-parts map and the ten named stores

    Your verdict on v3: the engines and mythologies were taken straight from other frameworks.

  2. the atom of work
    The Intent with a done-test, not the bet KEEL
    lost: p, V, L, the flip point, half-Kelly stakes · kept: the kill sentence as the done-test; death as the ceiling; "would it change a next act" as a Desk question

    A probability and a payoff are estimates by the interested party with nothing to check them; v3's own calibration section prints "insufficient" below fifty resolutions.

  3. promises with due dates
    Obligations, read before any Intent V3
    lost: the names debt and Continuo

    Keel is silent on statutory clocks and renewals; a renewal is not a goal and has no done-test to invent.

  4. the unit of unattended operation
    The Run, resumable; the window is a fuse KEEL
    lost: the five-hour watch with phases and the four-phase relief

    Resume-not-restart makes a closed window cost only the unfinished work.

  5. a nightly document
    None. You write charters and intents, nothing else KEEL
    lost: night orders, signed nightly

    "If it needs more than this, the design has failed." The briefing's what I would do next carries tonight's intent.

  6. how many kinds of worker
    Three shapes and loadouts KEEL
    lost: six positions · the curator is a maker loadout that may write only memory files

    Separated by irreducible properties: must hold context · must be parallel and stateless · must not edit what it judges.

  7. who performs an outward act
    Staged, one tap sends; a widened class runs through the Sender, a program with no model NEW
    lost: the mouth, broker and relay as three processes

    Keel never says what executes a widened act; v3's mechanism is the only one where the thing that decides cannot be the thing that sends.

  8. names
    Labels, not names: what it makes and which window it burns BOTH
    lost: named agents with careers

    You decided it twice, on two pages.

  9. how a field is known
    Field kits with horizons; rehearsal sets gate what runs unattended KEEL
    lost: the Bench, licences, the five-step apprenticeship

    The lighter form of the same discipline, starting from transcripts the machine already has.

  10. the 134 skills
    A holding directory; re-entry only as examples, an anchor or a rehearsal case BOTH
    lost: Keel's flat refusal; v3's reflex as a third form

    You decided the holding directory on v3's page; Keel's container rule says what the door may admit.

  11. memory
    Six stores, deltas only, a night curator, transcripts mined locally KEEL
    lost: the Book written by surprise, the Second, reflexes, the Wake · kept: five fixed questions for a failure; the nightly reconciliation

    The thing that acts never edits memory; context collapse has a paper; you confirmed local mining.

  12. the permission axis
    The envelope's three lists and the reversibility door test KEEL
    lost: the Line, τ thresholds, fused defaults · kept: an undo is drilled or the door is one-way; v3's one-way list is the default never list

    Reversibility is close to a fact; importance is argued; asking is what you refused.

  13. what makes a grant real
    argv per shape, a managed settings file, peer isolation by a checked-in file, a nightly probe V3
    lost: the names cell contract and dispatcher

    Measured facts bind and Keel is silent on the seam. A capability nobody probes is a memory of one.

  14. many ventures
    Four tempos, at most two driven, a WIP limit per venture KEEL
    lost: five postures, weekly allocation arithmetic, half-Kelly · kept: Serve as a charter pattern; the harness as a venture with a ceiling

    Little's Law, and your answer: two.

  15. the surfaces
    Floor · Balcony (five views) · briefing · voice · a room through the door NEW
    lost: the desk's fused defaults; the bay as a build; Keel's refusal of the room

    Keel's two surfaces over one state; v3's contents where richer; the room is your decision.

  16. you on the Floor
    Sterile: no interruptions, nothing new on your window; obligations and routine work continue; your venture held whole NEW
    lost: v3's "the weather keeps running"; Keel's global hold

    With three windows the routine work does not compete for yours, and an obligation due does not wait for a conversation to end.

  17. voice
    Input only; a text read-back; nothing binds by voice; the verbatim transcript kept BOTH
    no losing image
  18. when you are woken
    A wake-me list with numbers, three interruptions a day, a measured acted-on rate KEEL
    lost: the five bell conditions and the emission budget; the five become the default template

    ICU alarms: 74–99% irrelevant produces trained inattention, not annoyance.

  19. the key or the window
    Three subscription windows routed by difficulty; no metered key; a 30% reserve per window KEEL
    lost: an API key first, the batch tier, the cost formula, kept as a row for the day a key exists

    You answered it. Three fuses fail independently, which one account never did. The terms reading stays open.

  20. where it runs
    A LaunchAgent, lid open on power, an always-on box after the first measured overnight KEEL
    lost: the LaunchDaemon · kept: a tick that exits, coalesced firings, a process-group kill, push after every run, the credential plan

    The Watch must run as your user to reach the subscription credential.

  21. the logbook's schema
    JSONL with OpenTelemetry gen_ai names; an intent id and a run id on every row BOTH
    lost: a bespoke row schema
  22. how work is known to be good
    The anchor ladder for work; the contact rungs 0–5 for a venture's progress NEW
    lost: Hard · Rendered · Reactive · Blind as a taxonomy

    Keel measures whether the work is true; nothing in it measures whether the venture is becoming a business.

  23. rehearsal
    Known-answer cases, re-run on a cadence KEEL
    lost: the shadow venture with fixtures · kept: a hand's dry branch is its rehearsal

    Three to five cases from your own past, at zero cost, beat a fixture world per hand.

  24. self-improvement
    Three loops; horizons on everything; a redirect logged as a defect in the brief NEW
    lost: the forge, the fifth, the weekly refusal review as a ritual

  26. succession

  27. the name and the command
    Keel; the command is keel; the tree root is keel/ KEEL
    lost: house, StartupOS, house seat, bin/warroom
  28. the first move
    The logbook · transcript mining · one real venture driven with a rung-1 anchor KEEL
    lost: forty judged artifacts; the synthetic fake company; adopting beeond in Walk

    None of the design is worth more than the first night of evidence on a real thing. Which venture is yours.

  29. consensus between families

  30. which provider stands which shape
