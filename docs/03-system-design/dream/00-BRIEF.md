# Dream the system · 2026-09-02

**From the founder, in their words.** *Start fresh. Design the perfect agentic system. Take some context on what to
build and what I want, but no guidelines, no limits. Let it dream, let it envision, let it take ideas, shape it to
a new reality, to the new world we are entering now. A full agentic system that no one has seen before.*

What the founder wants it for, in their words across three days: *a system that helps me build a company, a
startup, or any project of its kind. It can handle any task. It runs twenty-four seven, always with the founder.
It has all sorts of layers: the memory, the contexts, the saving of tokens, the way the agents get the right
context and the right knowledge for each task, and the research. Breadth beyond code: design, video, content,
social, marketing, customer, data, testing, CI. Give the agents the tools, the way, and the understanding of how
to learn. It lost creativity to playbooks, and everything built so far is a stopping mechanism.*

## There is no floor
No existing decisions bind. No existing architecture binds. No section template. No length. No rule that a
mechanism must be enforced by something. Dream first; the machine that exists today is not the starting point.

If you want a parts bin, one exists and you may open it or ignore it: `docs/03-system-design/STARTUP-OS.md` (a
spec of the current harness at full scale) and `docs/02-competitive/expansion/` (177 open-source projects, 128
mechanisms, every hand measured). It is material, not a map. Reading it before you dream will anchor you; if you
open it, open it last.

The world is yours to research: the web, papers, products, other fields. Take ideas from anywhere.

## The one line that is not a design limit
Research only. Nothing you do may leave this machine: nothing published, sent, spent, posted, shared or deployed,
and no tool that acts on the world. Write only under `docs/03-system-design/dream/`.

## What we would love to read, in any form you choose
A name for it. The world it lives in and the year. What it is. How it thinks, remembers, learns, researches,
finds the right context for a task, acts, checks itself, grows, and lives with its founder day and night. What
the founder's life is like with it. What makes it something nobody has seen. What it would take to exist. What
it would do in its first week with a founder who has only an idea. Write to `docs/03-system-design/dream/2026-09-02-<your-name>.md`.
Return ≤ 200 words: the name, the picture in five sentences, and the one idea in it you are proudest of.
