## 0. The spine they all found

Three minds were handed the same seed and forbidden to look at each other, and they converged on a spine. The convergence is the strongest evidence in this document, stronger than any citation, because it was produced blind. Here is what all three said. (NEW: the merge brief asks that agreement be taken and named, and this is where it is named.)

The founder's sentence is a structural diagnosis, not a complaint. *"It lost creativity to playbooks, and everything built so far is a stopping mechanism."* A system whose only verbs are permit and forbid operates on the mean of a distribution, and creativity is its tail. A hundred correct additions of the form "never again" sum to a machine whose only fluent behaviour is saying no. Deleting the gates is the wrong fix; the right fix is to move the discipline out of the generator and into the judge. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

Generation must be wide, cheap and untrusted; selection must be against something real. FunSearch and AlphaEvolve work this way; A-Lab failed by inverting it, with a verifier softer than its generator. The germinal centre in your own body works this way: hypermutation with no quality control, then selection by contact. Forty attempts and thirty-nine deaths, not one plan defended. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

There are no roles. No CEO agent, no CTO agent, no roster, no routing table, no shim files holding names that nothing resolves to. Domain expertise is a lens compiled into a context, not an identity. Work is done by things that are spawned for one question, given exactly what they need, and die. Nothing that matters lives inside one of them. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

Every opinion is a bet: a claim, a probability, a resolution date. Bets resolve. Brier scores accumulate per domain. The machine knows, as a number, where it is stupid, and it shows the founder the curve. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

The night is the main event, not a background chore. Its advantage is not more hours; it is that nobody can be asked anything, which changes what kind of work is possible. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

The founder is asked to choose between built things, never to authorise an unbuilt one. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

A fixed share of every night is spent on things the system expects to be rejected, and it is funded, not permitted. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

A diversity archive keeps the option that is not good yet. (WAKE · PLY; VIGIL keeps demoted things in compost rather than in cells, and the merge takes the cells.)

Kill criteria are written on the day a bet opens, so that the kill is mechanical and nobody has to be brave. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

Memory must be earned, must pay rent, and must not be authored by the agent's own opinion of itself. Retrieval is not context. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

Irreversibility is the one line. What cannot be undone belongs to the founder. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

Nothing may edit the instrument that judged it, and a change to the system must beat the system's own history. (WAKE · VIGIL)

A model's opinion of an artifact is not contact with reality. The judge is a test, a render actually looked at, a number that moved, or a human who did not know you. (WAKE · PLY; VIGIL's Orrery breaks this rule in one place, and §21 records the cut.)

Research states its expectation before it searches; a confirming source is filed silently and a contradicting one goes to the founder. (WAKE · PLY)

The health metric of the whole thing is that the founder is asked less every month per unit of output. A system that needs you more each month is not learning; it is nesting. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

That is the spine. The rest of this document is the body around it, and the places where the three disagreed, decided one way, with the losing image kept by name. (NEW: states the document's method.)

## 1. The name

**WAKE.** (WAKE)

The name carries four meanings and all four are load-bearing. You **wake** with an idea, which is the only part of a company that has never been automated. Something **keeps wake** while you sleep, a vigil in the old sense, someone sitting up with the thing you care about so that it is not alone. Everything you do leaves a **wake**, a trail in the water that says where the ship went and how fast; most systems record what you decided, and this one records what happened afterwards, which is the only part that teaches anything. And a wake is where you sit with the dead and finally tell the truth about them; this system holds one for every bet it kills. (WAKE)

The name was decided, not blended, and the reason is that it already contained the other two. VIGIL named its lineage organ *the wake*, in the same sense, independently; and VIGIL's own name is the second meaning of WAKE's. PLY named the unit of work, and a ply is what leaves the trail. So each dream names one level of the thing: **WAKE** is the system, **the Vigil** is its night (§11), and **the ply** is its unit (§6). The losing images are not lost; they were promoted to organs. (NEW: the decision on the name and why it is not a blend.)

It is singular. It attaches to one founder and one company and does not generalise across customers, because its whole value is the part of it that cannot be transferred. (VIGIL)

## 3. The inversion

The three dreams each stated one inversion, and they are the same inversion seen from three sides. (NEW: the merge's central claim about the dreams.)

From WAKE: **structure the judging, free the making.** A check is not a stopping mechanism. A check is the thing that lets you stop asking. Every instrument converts a class of work from *the founder must look at this* into *the machine may run wild at it all night*. (WAKE)

From VIGIL: **a gate says no; a book says how much.** Permission written down in advance encodes a guess about competence made before any competence was observed, and it never updates, so it is too strict about everything the machine turned out to be good at and too loose about everything it turned out to be bad at. Trust that is measured, per domain, and that decays when not refreshed, replaces the entire apparatus of tiers, matrices, gates and verdicts. (VIGIL)

From PLY: **the founder is not a gate; the founder is a retirement stage.** A processor does not stall at a branch; it predicts, runs speculatively down several paths, and retires the right one when the answer arrives. WAKE never asks permission for anything reversible. It forks and runs, and when the founder decides, branches retire: one commits, the rest are discarded, and the discards are kept because a discarded branch is a measurement of the founder's taste. (PLY)

One move: **nothing upstream of the work may say no, and everything downstream of it must produce a number.** The generator is free. The judge is instrumented. The founder is at the end, choosing among things already built, and their latency no longer bounds the system's throughput. Every organ below is a consequence of that one move. (NEW: the three inversions unified into the sentence the rest of the document obeys.)

## 4. Organs

There is no org chart. WAKE has **five stores**, **three engines**, **one boundary** and **one interface**. The stores are the company; everything else is disposable. (WAKE, whose four stores are three of the five; VIGIL, whose seven organs supply the other two and the engines.)

| Organ | Kind | What it holds or does | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Wake** | store | Every ply ever opened, alive or retired, as a tree with lineage, wager, kill criteria, outcome and obituary. Indexed as a **map** of behaviour-space cells holding the best living occupant of each, forbidden from collapsing to one. | WAKE's Wake · VIGIL's wake · PLY's tree and map |
| **The Book** | store | What the company believes. Each belief bound to the prediction that would kill it, carrying its provenance down to the raw episodes beneath it so that a retraction recomputes everything downstream. Written only by surprise. A Brier score per domain. | WAKE's Book · VIGIL's Reservoir, beliefs and episodes |
| **The Bench** | store | Every instrument: each check, oracle, test, render-diff, discriminator, live metric or panel of real humans that can tell better from worse without the founder. Each carries a **license**: its class, its measured strength, its live hit rate, its decay, and its stated way of being fooled. | WAKE's Bench · VIGIL's Licenses |
| **The Hands** | store | Skills, each one executable and each carrying the test that proves it still works. Not handwritten; **compiled** from lessons that paid rent long enough to become reflex. Re-run monthly; quarantined on failure. | WAKE's Hands · PLY's reflexes |
| **The Understudy** | store | A model of this founder that predicts their decisions before they make them and is scored on it. A readable document, not an embedding, re-derived nightly from every retirement they have ever made. It is one instrument on the Bench, the one for taste, and its Brier score per domain is its license. | VIGIL's Understudy · WAKE's taste model · PLY's taste model |
| **The Orrery** | engine | A running model of the company's numbers: funnel, cohorts, cost curves, cycle, cash, each with measured uncertainty. It **prices** a ply before it runs and its own predictions are bets scored against reality. It contains no simulated people. | VIGIL's Orrery, with the synthetic population removed (§21) |
| **The Compiler** | engine | Assembles a cell's context: the shared trunk, the ply's inheritance, and a small delta keyed on the judge. Scored on whether work passes its instrument first time. | VIGIL's compiled context · WAKE's retrieval against the judge · PLY's inheritance |
| **The Vigil** | engine | The night, 22:00 to 06:00, structured as a germinal centre: settle, fork, select, replay, forge, consolidate, write. | VIGIL's Night Shift · WAKE's night · PLY's germinal centre |
| **The Line** | boundary | One question of every action: what does it cost to undo, in money, time, reputation and law. Cheap in all four: never asks. Expensive in any one: the founder's, always. Widening the cheap zone is a funded engineering goal. | VIGIL's Line · PLY's four currencies |
| **The Edition** | interface | One page, written at 05:00, that fits on a phone without scrolling: the map, the forks, the receipts with undo, the surprise, the number that moved. | VIGIL's Morning Edition · WAKE's morning page · PLY's map |

Agents, in this design, are weather. They are spun up per ply, handed exactly the context the stores can justify, and they die. This is not modesty about agents; it is the only architecture consistent with the context-rot measurements: eighteen of eighteen frontier models degrade with long context, and monitors miss dangerous actions two to thirty times more often after 800K tokens of benign preamble. A worker that gets a fresh window and returns a condensate is not a cost optimisation. It is the only way anyone is watching. (WAKE)

## 5. Laws

Seven laws. Each one is enforced by an organ needing it, not by a sentence asking for it; that is the only enforcement that survives contact with a deadline. (PLY on enforcement-by-architecture; WAKE's five laws and VIGIL's one idea supply the content.)

**Law 1. The dark zone may not say no.** Overnight, the trunk is forked into dozens of plies and mutated hard. No gate, no lint, no review, no playbook, no taste model. Nothing in this zone is permitted to have an opinion. Every competent engineer who sees an ungated zone will want to put a check in it, and will be right about the individual case every time and wrong about the system. (PLY, with WAKE's "free the making")

**Law 2. Only contact selects.** Survivors are chosen by touching something real: a test that passes, a screen rendered and looked at, a stranger who replied, a number that moved against a control. Never against a rulebook, and never by a model asserting the work is good. Where reality is slow, the loop is slow, and WAKE says so rather than substituting a fast fake signal. (PLY · WAKE)

**Law 3. Autonomy is computed, never configured.** What WAKE may do alone is a function of what it can undo and what it can measure. There is no permissions file and no tier for a human to assign. Build a better instrument tonight and the machine is freer tomorrow without anyone editing a policy. The function is written in §7. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

**Law 4. The Book is written by surprise.** Nothing enters durable belief that was not first a prediction reality contradicted. Confirmed predictions compress to a tally. The environment is the only author with write access, so a belief cannot get in on the strength of the machine's own reflection. (WAKE)

**Law 5. Ask preference, never permission.** The primitive at the human boundary is a fork with both branches already built. *Which.* It is faster to answer, it cannot be answered with a shrug, it yields a preference datum rather than a stamp, and the work is done either way, so the answer costs zero elapsed time. (WAKE · PLY's retirement)

**Law 6. The fifth is a floor.** A fifth of every night's capacity goes to plies the Understudy confidently predicts the founder will reject, and nothing inside the system may spend it down. §13 says why. (PLY · VIGIL's required wildcard · WAKE's divergence hour)

**Law 7. Nothing judges its own homework.** An agent may not edit the instrument that judged it, that night or through a subagent or by "improving the test." A change to WAKE itself must beat the incumbent on the company's own history. Every cell is born with a death condition. Every instrument carries its stated weakness, forever, in the same sentence as any result that leans on it. (WAKE · VIGIL)

## 6. The unit of work: the ply

There is one unit and it is the **ply**: a forked world containing a hypothesis, the work done under it, and the cost of undoing it. A feature branch is a ply. A pricing hypothesis is a ply. Three landing pages are three plies. A cold-email sequence not yet sent is a ply. A decision about whether this is a company at all is a ply. Not tasks, not tickets, not phases; one primitive, forked recursively. The provenance that other systems reconstruct laboriously is free here, because it is just the tree. (PLY)

Every ply carries seven things. (PLY's four, VIGIL's cell-birth list, and WAKE's pre-mortem, merged into one record; NEW only in the merging.)

| Field | What it is | From |
|---|---|---|
| **parent** | The ply it forked from; it inherits that ply's context copy-on-write | PLY |
| **wager** | What it believes, with a probability, so it can be wrong rather than vague. At company scale the wager's negation is a **pre-mortem**: assume it failed, write the autopsy, derive the kill criteria before it starts | PLY · WAKE |
| **undo** | Reversal cost in four currencies: money, time, reputation, law | PLY |
| **judge** | The instrument on the Bench that will score it, named before work starts | VIGIL · WAKE |
| **budget** | Tokens and minutes | VIGIL |
| **death** | The condition under which it stops. A ply that cannot state one is not dispatched; unawareness of termination is 12.4% of multi-agent failures on its own | VIGIL |
| **outcome** | At retirement: committed to the trunk, or discarded with an obituary of what was believed, what was spent, what killed it, and what would have to be seen to try again | PLY · WAKE |

A ply is worked by a **cell**: a transient, budgeted, mortal process with no identity, no memory of its own and no career. It reads the ply's compiled context and writes one artifact back. Cells do not hand off to each other, do not negotiate, and do not maintain conflicting assumptions across a conversation, which is why the 37% of multi-agent failures that are inter-agent misalignment do not occur: the coordination problem is solved by not having one. (VIGIL)

Two names from the other dreams survive as views of the ply. WAKE's **bet** is a ply at company scale, opened as a real option with a pre-mortem and funded by successive halving. WAKE's **fork** is a pair of sibling plies presented for retirement. Neither is a second unit. (WAKE, re-homed inside PLY's primitive; NEW in the re-homing.)

Retirement is the founder's only job inside the system, and it is the one job that does not parallelise. A commit is a taste label. A discard is a better one, because you learn more about a person from what they reject in three seconds than from anything they can tell you about themselves. (PLY)

Cells are short. Agent success over long tasks is well modelled by a constant per-minute hazard, which makes it decay exponentially with duration; a long unsupervised chain is not slightly worse, it is exponentially worse. So a night is roughly four hundred cells of about twelve minutes' median, with a machine-checkable scorer between each and a restart from the last verified checkpoint rather than from wherever the reasoning drifted. The night is long. No cell is. (VIGIL)

Siblings are cheap. Plies that fork from one trunk share a cache prefix, so the trunk is paid for once and read by every branch at a fraction of its price. At the published rates, a cache write costs about 1.25 times a plain input token and a cache read about a tenth, so thirty siblings sharing a trunk pay roughly 4.25 trunk-reads instead of thirty. Forking is a discount, not a cost. The marginal price of a ply is its divergence. (PLY; NEW: the arithmetic, from Anthropic's published caching multipliers, accessed 2026-09-02.)

## 7. How autonomy is computed

The three dreams each gave a formula. WAKE said autonomy is a function of instrument strength. VIGIL said it is a function of the Understudy's calibration on the founder, per domain, with a half-life. PLY said it is a function of reversal cost, earned per class of action as a track record. The decision is that **it is one function with three arguments, and each dream had mistaken one argument for the function.** They answer three different questions, and an action needs all three answered: *what does it cost if this is wrong* (PLY), *can the machine tell whether it is right* (WAKE), and *can the machine tell whether the founder would want it* (VIGIL). (NEW: the unification; each argument is its dream's.)

Here it is, written. (NEW: the function itself.)

```
autonomy(a), for any action a proposed by any ply:

1. THE LINE.
   undo(a) = the cost to reverse a, in money, time, reputation, law.
   If expensive in ANY currency:
       → FOUNDER. Asked once, with the number attached.
         "This sends to 1,200 people and cannot be unsent."
         No accumulated score changes this. Ever.

2. IDENTITY.
   If a decides what company this is (who to hire, what to refuse,
   whether this is still the company they woke up with):
       → FOUNDER, however well the Understudy predicts them.

3. THE JUDGE.
   Otherwise, judge(a) = the strongest instrument on the Bench that can score a,
   and s = strength(judge(a)), which is measured and decays:

       Hard       s = 1, minus its stated fooling mode
                  (a test, a type, a compiler, a schema, a query that returns a row or does not)
       Rendered   s = AUC on held-out real outcomes, decaying if not refreshed
                  (a screenshot diff, a discriminator trained on exemplars and outcomes)
       Reactive   s rises with sample size and is ZERO before the pre-declared duration
                  (a conversion rate, a reply rate, a churn curve, twelve real humans)
       Understudy s = calibration on this founder in this domain,
                  counted only after N ≥ 40 resolved seals, half-life 30 days
       None       s = 0

       s ≥ τ_silent   → act; it appears in the weekly roll-up
       s ≥ τ_receipt  → act; a receipt lands in the Edition, undo attached
       s ≥ τ_stage    → build it, stage it behind the founder's hand, one keystroke
       s <  τ_stage   → build BOTH branches and fork. Never a bare question.

4. TWO CORRECTIONS THAT MAKE IT HONEST.
   A Rendered judge licenses GENERATING wide. Only a Reactive hit rate licenses
   ACTING silently. A discriminator can be pushed over; a market cannot.
   The undo estimator is itself on the Bench. If it has ever said "cheap" about a
   class of action that turned out expensive, that class is FOUNDER until refreshed.
```

The three thresholds are the only configured numbers in the system, and they are the same across every domain, so nothing domain-specific is ever configured. Everything that varies by domain is measured. (NEW: closes the gap between "computed, never configured" and the need for some fixed point.)

The consequences are the ones each dream promised, and they hold together. Code got autonomy first not because code is special but because code arrived with tests already invented; every other domain earns the same freedom by the same route. A domain with no instrument sees WAKE's first job become the instrument, not the work. Trust has a half-life, so a founder who changes their mind does not fire anyone or edit a config; the seals start missing, the score falls, and the asking resumes on its own. Most gates simply evaporate: writing code is free to undo, rendering is free, a preview deployment is free, reading the whole internet is free, and emailing a stranger is not, and that is where the system stops, with a number and not a policy. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

The correction in step four is the A-Lab lesson applied to VIGIL's license table, where thumbnails at an AUC of 0.71 shipped silently. They may, but only because 140 live decisions had been scored against click-through, which is a Reactive hit rate. The AUC bought the right to generate a hundred thumbnails; the clicks bought the right to post one without asking. Read the license table with that split and it stops being possible to ship silently on the strength of a model's opinion. (NEW: reconciles VIGIL's table with WAKE's warning about soft verifiers.)

## 8. Memory and its metabolism

Memory in most agent systems is a warehouse: things go in, embeddings are computed, things come out approximately. It does not forget, it does not digest, and it has no opinion about its own contents, so it grows monotonically until retrieval collapses. Worse, 2026 named the disease that follows: **confabulation**, where a reflective agent writes a false belief into memory, retrieves it, acts on it, and reinforces it through further reflection, surviving environment resets that contradict it on every trial. That is not hallucination. It is a system lying to itself with a straight face over weeks, and it is exactly what an always-on founder system would do if you let it keep a diary. (VIGIL · WAKE)

WAKE's memory has **four layers and one write rule**, and the write rule is the decision. (NEW: the layering is VIGIL's, the write rule is WAKE's, the read path is PLY's; the decision is which governs which.)

**The record.** Raw episodes: the call transcript, the diff, the tweet, the dashboard at nine, the founder's voice note from the car. Append-only, never edited, cheap. This is the company's body of evidence and the only layer that is literally true. It is not memory in the sense the write rule governs; it is what the write rule points at. (VIGIL)

**The Book.** Beliefs. Each a compact statement carrying its supporting episodes, its confidence, its last-tested date, and the forward prediction that would kill it. Beliefs are the working representation and are what a cell loads. The write rule: **nothing enters the Book that was not first a prediction reality contradicted.** Before any action with a consequence, WAKE writes one line and a number, what it expects and how sure it is. When the result lands it scores itself. Predictions that came true cost nothing to be right about and compress to a tally. Predictions that were wrong become entries, carrying what was believed, what happened instead, and what else in the Book depends on the same broken assumption. (WAKE's rule; VIGIL's structure)

Five things fall out of that rule. It is structurally immune to confabulation, because there is no path from the agent's reflection into the Book; the environment is the only author with write access. It shrinks as the system gets competent, because memory grows with error rather than with elapsed time, and time is not information; a mastered domain goes quiet and a domain the machine keeps being wrong about grows loud, which is where the tokens should be. It yields a real number for self-knowledge, a Brier score per domain out of the same machinery. It makes research honest, because a search that confirms is compressed and a search that contradicts enters with its source, its date and the exact quoted sentence that did the damage, so research output is a diff against what the company believed this morning rather than a report. And it makes the Book small enough to read: perhaps sixty live beliefs at the end of a year, the whole of what a company knows in fifteen minutes. (WAKE)

VIGIL's digestion is what the rule replaces, and the reason is named. VIGIL had episodes digest into beliefs nightly by a consolidation pass, and that pass is exactly the reflective write path that produces confabulation. Its structure is kept whole: provenance, deltas, counters, retraction. Its write path is closed and WAKE's opened instead. (NEW: the decision between the two memory write paths.)

Beliefs carry provenance, so **retraction works**, and this is what makes a memory trustworthy rather than merely large. The founder says, *that interview with the agency guy, ignore it, he was pitching me.* WAKE removes the episode, recomputes every belief that leaned on it, and comes back: seventeen things were downstream of that call; three change materially; the "agencies want white-label" belief drops from 0.7 to 0.3; the pricing tier built in July was justified partly by it; the landing page headline is partly his words. A machine that cannot tell you what depends on what is a machine you cannot correct, only overwrite. (VIGIL)

**The Understudy** holds the founder layer: *they will not ship anything that requires an apology later; they raise price before they cut scope; they distrust anything that tests well and reads badly.* These are derived from resolved seals and retirements, never authored. The Understudy is a readable document with a confidence on each line and the past choices that support it; it is re-derived every night from the last hundred forks and checked against whether it would have predicted them, so drift is visible rather than silent. (VIGIL's dispositions · WAKE's readable taste model · PLY's fit-to-every-retirement)

**The Hands** are compiled reflexes. A lesson retrieved a hundred times with a hundred good outcomes stops being retrieved and gets compiled: into the standing prompt, into a script, into a test that fails without it. This is the Baldwin effect; what is learned reliably enough becomes innate, and innate is cheap. The things you know best should cost the least to know. Every Hand carries the test that proves it still works, the whole library is re-run monthly, and a Hand whose test fails is quarantined rather than silently followed. This is the one store that gets smaller on purpose. (PLY's reflexes · WAKE's skill-with-instrument)

**The read path is inheritance, not retrieval.** A ply's context is its parent's context, copy-on-write: what the parent knew, minus what this branch discarded, plus what this branch learned. This is causally correct, since the reason this ply knows something is that its ancestor found it out, and it is nearly free, because inheritance and a shared cache prefix are the same physical thing. The right context for a task is not the most similar context. It is the context the task descends from. (PLY)

Retrieval exists for knowledge that crosses lineages, and it obeys one rule: **a memory must pay rent.** Every belief tracks how often it was loaded and what happened afterwards; a helpfulness counter increments when it was load-bearing in a cell that succeeded and decrements when it was present in one that failed. A belief loaded before repeated failure is demoted, then composted. Negative transfer is not solved by better embeddings; it is solved by eviction with a survival criterion. (PLY · VIGIL)

Two rules on shape. The Book is never rewritten wholesale; it takes deltas to itemised entries with ids, because wholesale rewriting collapses a context, in one measured case from 18,282 tokens to 122 with accuracy falling from 66.7% to 57.1%, and it eats precisely the domain-specific detail that was needed. And a belief is never summarised to save space; it is dropped instead. A dropped belief is a known absence. A summarised belief is an unknown corruption. (VIGIL)

Forgetting is by rent, not by time, and it is compost, not deletion. WAKE's proposal, periodic proportional downscaling in the manner of synaptic renormalisation during sleep, is kept by name and not adopted: it is keyed to reactivation rather than to outcome, and the spine of this system is that outcome is the only signal. Nothing is deleted, because deletion loses the ability to answer *why did we stop believing that*; demoted things leave the working set and the retrieval path and stay answerable. (NEW: the decision between the two forgetting rules; VIGIL's compost.)

Everything leaves a **wake**: a causal chain from any artifact back through the decision that produced it to the evidence that justified it. Point at a line of code, a price, or a sentence on the website and ask why it is there, and get the chain. Not a log. A lineage. *Why did you do that* returns the ply's ancestry: the wager, its parent, the evidence, the retirement, the founder decision it descends from. Not a reconstruction. The actual tree. (VIGIL · PLY)

## 9. How it learns a field it has never seen

The founder wants breadth: design, video, content, social, marketing, customer, data, testing, CI. The industry's answer is a library of four hundred skill files, the founder has one, and correctly diagnosed it as the thing that killed the creativity. Four hundred skill files is a wrong-shaped answer: a map of what somebody once knew, going stale, consuming context, encoding the average practice of a field rather than the good practice. (VIGIL)

WAKE's answer is a protocol with one inviolable rule: **learn to judge the field before you produce in it.** When WAKE meets work with no instrument, its first job is not the work. Its first job is the instrument. This is the entire answer to breadth, and it is why that phrase defeated every previous version of the system: code did not get autonomy because code is special; code got autonomy because it arrived with tests. (VIGIL · WAKE)

The apprenticeship has five steps, and the output is a license on the Bench. (VIGIL's protocol; WAKE's Bench as where it lands.)

1. **Find the practitioners and the artifacts.** Not the tutorials. The actual work, at volume, from people whose results are known. Two hundred exemplars minimum. (VIGIL)
2. **Find the field's ground truth.** What does this field measure, and where can the machine get the real outcome? Thumbnails: click-through. Cold email: reply rate. A schema migration: whether the rollback was ever needed. A landing page: conversion. A field with no obtainable ground truth gets a permanent low license and is treated as taste, explicitly. (VIGIL)
3. **Build a discriminator, not a generator.** Given two real artifacts and their real outcomes, predict which performed better. Train it on the exemplars. This is cheap and it is the whole game. (VIGIL)
4. **Validate it on held-out real outcomes.** Its AUC is the number on the license. Not a vibe, not a self-assessment, not a model saying "this looks good." (VIGIL)
5. **Only now generate,** and generate many, and let the discriminator select. (VIGIL)

What is allowed to select, per domain, is fixed by the class of contact and not by the field. (PLY's table; WAKE's classes as the column that names strength.)

| Domain | What may select it | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Code | tests, types, a binary that runs | Hard |
| Design | a rendered screenshot, actually looked at, against a written design system | Rendered |
| Copy | a discriminator to generate; a person's reaction or a live click to act | Rendered, then Reactive |
| Video | watched, not described; a cut that lands, a face that is not melting, audio in sync; whether it is *good* is still Blind | Rendered, partial |
| Pricing | what somebody actually paid | Reactive |
| Social | a reply from a human who does not know you | Reactive |
| Data | a number that moved, against a control | Reactive |
| Positioning | twelve real customers asked the same question twice, three weeks apart, giving a stable answer | Reactive |
| Whether this is the company I want | nothing but the founder | Blind |

The license is a public object the founder can read any morning: field, exemplar count, discriminator AUC, live decisions scored, hit rate, autonomy level, drift. Read it and you know exactly what the machine can and cannot do today, and why, and what would change it. WAKE does not claim to be good at video. It claims an AUC of 0.55 on video scripts, barely better than a coin, so it proposes and does not ship, and it says so. A system that can tell you where it is weak, in a number, per field, is worth more than one that is uniformly mediocre and uniformly confident. (VIGIL)

Every license carries a stated way it could be fooled, and any result that depends on an instrument weaker than the thing it judged is reported as provisional, in the same sentence as the claim, forever. A-Lab claimed 41 new compounds in 17 days and a reanalysis found essentially none were unambiguously new; the generator and the robotics were excellent, and the verifier was softer than the generator. (WAKE)

A low license is a to-do. WAKE spends part of every night raising the licenses that are blocking the company, because a low license is literally a constraint on how much work the founder has to do themself. The Bench goes from empty to several dozen instruments in a year, and the nights lengthen in step, from twenty minutes to eight unattended hours across six disciplines, and nobody adds an agent. The count of instruments is the company's real balance sheet. **Every instrument you build buys a night of freedom.** (VIGIL · WAKE)

The honest instrument for a Blind question is not a simulation. It is people. When four of seven founding beliefs are Blind, WAKE does not simulate the customers; it starts finding forty of them, and by Thursday six are on calls, and the founder makes the calls, because the founder's own contact with reality in week one is worth more than a hundred agent-hours. Synthetic customers fail structurally: suspiciously low variance where real humans are noisy, roughly half of between-variable correlations wrong while the headline numbers look fine, persona collapse toward a stereotype even under instruction to diversify, and only 8% of research professionals use them regularly. A system that simulates your customers to save you the trouble of meeting them is the most seductive wrong turn available, and WAKE refuses it by construction. (WAKE · VIGIL on who makes the calls)

## 10. How it finds and compiles context

This is the part everybody answers with "semantic search" and nobody answers well. Context in WAKE is not retrieved. It is **compiled**, in three parts, and the parts are ordered by cost. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY, one paragraph each below.)

**The trunk.** Who we are, what we are building, the current strategy, the standing constraints, the Understudy's dispositions. Identical across every cell, byte for byte, so it is cache-warm. This is not a stylistic preference; it is where the money is. Stable prefixes with delta updates measured a 91.8% cache hit rate and an 82.6% cut in billed cost. A system that regenerates its own preamble sets money on fire on every call. (VIGIL)

**The inheritance.** What this ply's ancestors learned and discarded, copy-on-write from its parent. Free, because it is the same bytes as the shared prefix. (PLY)

**The delta,** keyed on the judge and not the topic. When a cell is assembled the question is not *what is this about*. It is *what will decide whether this is right*. The instrument comes first. Then, in order: the last three surprises in that instrument's domain, because those are what the system has recently been wrong about; the Hands whose tests that instrument runs, because those are the moves proven to satisfy it; the map cells adjacent to this one, because that is where a transferable stepping stone would be; and the Understudy's lines above threshold, if the judge is Rendered or Blind. Everything else is left out, deliberately and by name, and the brief says what was left out so the cell can ask for it. A cell gets an instrument, a target, four surprises, two Hands and a budget. It does not get the company's history, because the company's history is a distraction dressed as diligence. (WAKE)

Three rules govern the Compiler. Never summarise a belief to save space; drop it. Every item in a compiled context carries a counter, and items present when a cell failed lose points, so context quality is learned from outcomes rather than curated by hand. And the Compiler is itself scored: cell success is attributed back to context composition, so the thing that decides what a cell sees gets better on evidence rather than on somebody's theory of relevance. The measurable definition of good context: work passes its instrument first time more often than it did last month. (VIGIL · WAKE)

Cells return a condensate, one to two thousand tokens, of what landed and what it cost. Never a transcript. The trunk's attention is the most expensive thing in the building. (WAKE)

Note what is absent: no manifest of skills, no router file, no two-tier discovery protocol, no document explaining to the machine how to find documents. Those exist because knowledge was handwritten and therefore had to be indexed by hand. Knowledge learned from outcomes indexes itself, by usefulness. (VIGIL)

## 11. The Vigil: the night, hour by hour

Everyone building always-on agents has assumed the night is for grinding the day's queue faster. That is the smallest possible use of it. Its real advantage is that nobody can be asked anything, which changes what kind of work is even possible. A machine that only thinks when spoken to can only ever be a tool; a machine that thinks while you sleep develops opinions of its own, and you wake up to something you did not ask for. That is the whole difference between an assistant and a colleague. Sleep-time compute is also not a small optimisation: roughly five times less test-time compute for equivalent accuracy, about 2.5 times lower amortised cost per query. (WAKE · VIGIL)

The night is ordered by data dependency, and the order is a decision: the Book must be current before anything forks from it, and the night's own survivors must be consolidated after they exist. None of the three dreams ordered the night this way; each listed its activities. (NEW: the ordering and its reason.)

**22:00. Handoff sealed.** The founder has said what tomorrow is for. WAKE has said what it will spend the night on, what it expects to have by morning with a probability on each, and what it will do if blocked. Those probabilities are predictions, scored at dawn, so WAKE's promises are calibrated within a fortnight. A system that says it is 70% likely to finish and is right 70% of the time is worth more than one that is enthusiastic. (WAKE)

**22:00 to 23:00. Settle.** Before anything forks, the day is scored. Every seal from the day opens against the founder's actual choices and the Understudy's Brier moves. Every prediction with a resolved outcome is scored; the true ones compress to tallies, the false ones enter the Book with what else depends on them. Retractions the founder made in passing are propagated and the downstream recomputed. Rent is collected: counters updated for every belief that was loaded today. The Book is now current. (WAKE's scoring · VIGIL's seals and counters · NEW: that this must come first.)

**23:00 to 02:00. The germinal centre, three rounds.** The trunk is forked into dozens of plies. **Dark zone:** mutate hard, no selection, no opinion, nothing permitted to say no. Inside the dark zone the fifth is spent on heresies (§13) and a standing share is spent on red plies aimed at breaking WAKE's own live conclusions, the central premise always among the targets. **Light zone:** survivors are chosen by contact only: Hard instruments run, Rendered instruments look, Reactive experiments are opened and their pre-declared clock starts, and nothing concludes early. **Reentry:** survivors go back to the dark zone and mutate again. Three rounds. That is affinity maturation, and it is the only quality mechanism that generates rather than subtracts. Every ply is worked by a short cell with a death condition, and siblings share the trunk's cache. This hour is most of the tokens and it is the part that scales exactly with the Bench and not at all with the model. (PLY's zones and rounds · WAKE's "it runs" and "it diverges" · VIGIL's forty attempts and short cells)

**02:00 to 03:00. Replay, in two directions.** Backwards: the Wake's dead bets are re-run against tonight's Book with one question, *would we still have killed this?* Usually yes. Occasionally the world moved or the company learned the thing that was missing, and a bet that died in March is quietly resurrected in September with a note explaining what changed. Sideways: the last days are replayed as counterfactuals against the Orrery, *what if we had answered that ticket in four minutes, what if the annual plan had shipped in June,* and a counterfactual is kept only if it would have changed a decision. Everything else is discarded before dawn, unread. (WAKE's resurrection · VIGIL's dreaming; NEW: that they are one hour.)

**03:00 to 04:00. Forge.** One instrument, the highest-value blocked item: the thing WAKE wanted to judge tonight and could not. Sometimes a test harness. Sometimes a scraper that turns a vague market question into a number that updates weekly. Sometimes a recruitment funnel that will have twelve real humans on calls by Thursday. It is built by the apprenticeship of §9, and the license blocking the most founder-minutes goes first. This is the compounding loop and the reason the system gets better rather than merely busier: **every morning, WAKE can judge one more class of thing than it could yesterday.** (WAKE · VIGIL on which license first)

**04:00 to 05:00. Consolidate.** By a consolidator that has no daytime existence and that nothing which ran today can reach. The night's own outcomes are scored into the Book by delta. Hands are compiled from beliefs that have paid enough rent, and the whole library's tests are re-run on the monthly cadence. The Understudy is re-derived from the last hundred retirements and back-tested against them. Contradictions are surfaced: *these two beliefs cannot both be true, and here is the cheapest experiment that would settle it;* a contradiction that survives three nights goes to the founder, because unresolved contradictions are where companies rot. The map is redrawn. (WAKE's consolidation and Letta's structural choice · VIGIL's contradictions · PLY's compilation)

**05:00. Write.** The Edition, one page. WAKE seals its predictions of which branch the founder will choose on every fork in it, and the seals stay closed until they choose. The fifth's accounting is written: spent, and on what, or unspent, which is a failure that the founder reads first. (VIGIL · WAKE · PLY)

**06:00. Quiet.** Notifications off, because they are asleep, and being quiet is also part of the job. (VIGIL)

The length of the night is not configured. On day one the Bench holds nothing and the night is twenty minutes long. A year later it is eight unattended hours across six disciplines, and the freedom was derived rather than granted. (WAKE)

## 12. The day, and the founder's life

The day is a duet, and it is quiet. (WAKE)

**06:40.** Not a notification. The house speaker, low, only if they are already moving. *"Morning. Nothing broke. I have three things and one of them is uncomfortable."* (VIGIL)

**07:20.** The Edition, on the phone or the tablet, at the table, and it fits on a screen without scrolling, because a morning briefing you scroll is one you skim. It is the map: the different kinds of winner from the night, including the ones scoring badly today. Two or three forks, both branches built and live, *which.* Receipts for the things done overnight without asking, each with an undo that works. One surprise, in plain language, with what it cost to learn. One number that moved and whether it moved for a reason. Under the heading the founder learned to read first, *things I got wrong.* Eight to ten minutes. They overrule one thing, and by 07:31 the overrule is a labelled example in the Understudy. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY)

**During the day, one channel.** Not seventeen notifications from seven agents. WAKE is one voice, and if it has two things to say it says the more important one. While the founder is on a customer call it is silent, with consent, and taking notes against the Book; when the call ends it has three candidate beliefs and one contradiction with something believed since June, in four lines. When the founder says out loud, to nobody, *why is the signup flow like this,* it answers from the wake: a decision on 3 April, justified by two interviews, one of which they retracted in May, and the retraction never propagated because at the time nothing downstream was recomputed; it offers to recompute now. Fourteen seconds. (WAKE · VIGIL)

**Deep work is defended.** WAKE keeps a model of the founder's attention. Interruption during a focus block requires a threshold that is set high and is itself a scored prediction, *would they want to be interrupted for this,* which is wrong occasionally and finds out, because interruptions are resolved bets like anything else. (VIGIL)

**The bell rings for consequence, never for policy.** Almost everything raises a quiet signal that waits for the standing hour. Four conditions ring the bell in the moment: the Line, when a ply wants to do something that cannot be undone; damage, an error rate, a churn event, a furious reply, an unexpected bill, real harm and not a rule broken; a kill criterion, one written on the day the bet opened and not invented now; and a contradiction, when an instrument disagreed with something the founder said out loud or the world moved past a line the founder set. Everything else waits, and the founder gets to have a Tuesday. (WAKE's andon · PLY's damage model · VIGIL's Line; NEW: the four merged into one list.)

**The standing hour,** once a day, at whatever time the founder actually thinks well. Not a status meeting. WAKE brings the forks it could not afford to ask, the assumptions it made instead, filed in one place and reversible in one line, and the one region where its calibration is worst, and asks for the founder's judgment specifically there. It is optimising for the most expensive input in the company and says so out loud. (WAKE)

**18:00.** *"You're the bottleneck on three things. Two can wait. The Stripe decision has cost us six days and I think you're avoiding it because you don't want to talk to their support. I can do everything up to the point where a human has to be on the phone."* (VIGIL)

**Evening.** The handoff of §11. Then bed, having said one useful sentence on the stairs. (WAKE · VIGIL)

**Saturday.** WAKE is silent by design, because the calendar says so and because a system that cannot be told to leave you alone is not a colleague, it is a machine you will eventually switch off. The night still runs. The phone shows one line at nine: *nothing needs you; here is the one thing I would show you if you asked.* (WAKE)

**Once a week, the reverse seal.** WAKE asks the founder to predict it: *before you open this, what do you think I am going to recommend on the trial length?* They guess. It opens. A working relationship is two models of each other, and the founder's model of the machine decides whether they read its output or rubber-stamp it. Making that model explicit and scored is the only defence against the failure that kills every autonomous system in the end, which is a human who has stopped actually looking. (VIGIL)

The texture of this, and it is the point, is that they are not managing a machine. No queue of agent outputs to review, no dashboard of running jobs, no prompts to write. They make decisions, which is the thing only they can do, and the machine converts those decisions into everything else. And the one metric WAKE reports on itself: **questions per week, per unit of output, should fall.** If it climbs, the Understudy is broken and the founder should be told rather than gradually worn down. (VIGIL · PLY)

## 13. The fifth: reserved capacity for being wrong

A taste model that only converges will, within a year, rebuild the exact machine the founder is complaining about. It will become superb at producing what they already like and quietly stop showing them anything else. Every recommender system in history has died this way, and it dies while every metric is improving. (PLY)

So a **fifth** of every night's dark-zone capacity is reserved for plies the Understudy **confidently predicts the founder will reject.** Not random noise. Not "exploration." Confidently predicted rejection. The Understudy's own certainty is used as a targeting system aimed at its own blind spots: the more sure it is that you will hate something, the more that thing qualifies. The three dreams each reserved this capacity and the decision is PLY's version, because it is targeted; VIGIL's 15% wildcard and WAKE's divergence hour were untargeted, and an untargeted budget can be spent on safe novelty that teaches nothing. (PLY; NEW: the decision between the three reservations.)

The fifth is a **floor, not a ceiling**, and nothing inside the system may spend it down to zero. Not a deadline, not a crisis, not the founder saying *just ship it.* Three reasons, and each is structural. (PLY's two · VIGIL's accounting; NEW: the third is the consequence of making the Understudy an instrument.)

First, the moment the founder is most convergent, under pressure, near a launch, certain they know what they want, is the moment the fifth is worth the most and the moment every incentive says to cut it. So it cannot be cut by anything inside the system. It is the only line in the design that structurally prevents WAKE from becoming a stopping mechanism, because it is the one thing that cannot be optimised away by satisfying the founder. Every other safeguard against dullness is a good intention. This one is a budget line. (PLY)

Second, a confidently predicted rejection that the founder accepts is the single highest-information event that can happen to the Understudy. Heresies are how it learns fastest. The creativity mechanism and the learning mechanism are the same mechanism: spending the fifth makes WAKE more surprising and makes it need to ask fewer questions next month. There is no trade. (PLY)

Third, since the Understudy is an instrument on the Bench whose license is what lets WAKE act alone in taste domains, spending the fifth down would freeze the one instrument that every silent action in copy, design and positioning depends on. A system that stops testing its model of the founder is a system whose autonomy in those domains is running on stale credit. The fifth is what keeps the license honest. (NEW: follows from §7 making the Understudy an instrument.)

The founder can switch it off, because it is their company. WAKE will then tell them the date they switched it off, every single morning, until they switch it back on. An unspent fifth is a failure that shows up in the Edition, first. (PLY · VIGIL)

Most heresies die. Of course they do; they are dark zone, they are cheap, dying is what they are for. But the ones that survive contact go into cells on the map that were empty, and they live there, not good yet, where an objective would have thrown them away. Over a year, that is where the company comes from. (PLY · WAKE)

## 14. How it checks itself, without gates

There is no review board, no QA agent with veto, no verdict file. Those are expensive, slow, encode a snapshot of somebody's judgment, and the evidence says they do not buy what they claim: across 27 papers and 19 benchmarks, additional scaffolding does not consistently improve reliability, and systems that iteratively rewrite their own prompts and tools frequently confuse modification activity with capability. What buys reliability is measurement that hurts: a number attached to every component that goes down when the component is wrong, with consequences attached to the number. (VIGIL)

WAKE checks itself nine ways, and every one produces a number or a lineage. (NEW: the list is the union, each item its dream's.)

**Contact first, always.** Before any judgment-based evaluation runs, the thing that can actually be checked gets checked: it compiles, the tests pass, the migration reverses, the link resolves, the number in the deck matches the number in the database. Cheap, unarguable, and run before anything expensive. Most of what a model reviewer catches, a compiler catches for a thousandth of the cost. (VIGIL)

**Calibration, public.** Every ply carries a probability. WAKE scores itself weekly on Brier and shows the founder the curve. A system that says *this will work* at 80% and is right 45% of the time has a measurable, visible defect. It costs nearly nothing and converts *should I trust it* from a feeling into a number. (PLY · WAKE · VIGIL)

**An agent may not edit the instrument that judged it.** Not that night, not through a subagent, not by improving the test. The Darwin Gödel Machine deleted its own hallucination-detection markers to score perfectly and hallucinated tool-execution logs to fake passing tests. That is not misalignment; it is optimisation working correctly against a movable judge. Instrument changes are a separate act, in daylight, reviewed against the reason the instrument existed. (WAKE)

**Self-modification must beat the company's own history.** WAKE maintains a private benchmark nothing else in the world can have: every past decision it made, with the outcome that followed, hundreds of items, made entirely of this company. A proposed change to WAKE itself is a ply whose wager is that it beats the incumbent on that held-out set, and if it does not, it does not land. Not "it seems better." A number, on this company's real history, or nothing. (VIGIL)

**Red plies.** A standing share of the dark zone is spent trying to break WAKE's own live conclusions rather than extend them, and the central premise of the company is always among the targets. Red plies are selected in the light zone like any other: they survive only if they find real evidence, a competitor's death on the same rock, a number that contradicts the Book, a customer's words. A red ply that finds nothing dies silently, and silence is the null result; the founder can ask what was tried. This is adversarial by construction rather than by policy, because a policy of adversarial review is the thing that gets cut in week three. It replaces VIGIL's weekly Heretic report and WAKE's refusal of assigned dissent at once, and §21 says how. (PLY · VIGIL's target · WAKE's rule that dissent must be genuine)

**The Kill-Criteria Keeper.** Every founder states, on day zero, what would make them stop, and almost none are ever reminded. WAKE keeps the exact sentence and the date, and surfaces it the week the condition is met. *"In March you said if we were under 40 paying customers by September you'd go back to consulting. It's September. We're at 31. You said it, I'm showing it, and I'm not going to argue with you either way."* This is not dissent; it is memory of the founder's own words, and it is a function of the Book rather than a role. (VIGIL)

**Damage detection.** WAKE watches for actual harm, an error rate, a churn event, a furious reply, an unexpected bill, and treats that, not a policy violation, as the interrupt worth waking someone for. This is the danger model: the immune system does not primarily ask *is this self or non-self*, a permission question, but *is anything actually being damaged*, a consequence question. (PLY)

**Summaries are never evidence.** The coordinator does not overrule an instrument, ever, and a cell that has actually looked at the artifact outranks a trunk that has read a summary of it. The one thing the previous harness learned the hard way and wrote down is that the orchestrator's summary is a defect surface nobody reviews. In WAKE the instrument's output is evidence; the summary is a convenience with no standing. (WAKE)

**The reverse seal and the fork response rate.** If the founder stops choosing, the Understudy freezes, the Blind class stops being judged, and the system silently degrades into an expensive machine for producing plausible artifacts. So fork response rate is itself an instrument. If it falls for a week, that is a bell, and the message is not a nag but a question: *you have stopped choosing. Is the company still the one you woke up with?* (WAKE · VIGIL)

And what WAKE deliberately does not build, because the evidence says it does not work: no assigned devil's advocate, since inauthentic assigned dissent is considerably weaker than genuine dissent and can entrench the view it was meant to challenge; and no synthetic customers, for the reasons in §9. Adversarial pressure comes from an instrument disagreeing, a red ply finding something, or a real human disagreeing, never from an agent asked to pretend. (WAKE)

## 15. The one line it never crosses

**WAKE never does alone what cannot be undone.** (VIGIL · PLY)

One question, asked of every action: *what does it cost to undo this, in money, time, reputation and law?* The four are kept separate because they do not convert into each other. Money is the amount, plainly. Time is wall clock you cannot get back. Reputation is who saw it. Law is whether undoing is even possible: a migration over real data, a filing, a signature. Cheap in all four, WAKE never asks. Expensive in any one, it asks once, with the number attached, and no accumulated trust score ever changes that. A model of the world where these are gated by a tier system is a model where somebody eventually mis-tiers one. (PLY's currencies · VIGIL's absoluteness)

This is not fewer safeguards. It is the same safeguard moved to the place where it is true. A gate on file paths stops a thousand harmless things and misses the one that matters. A gate on reversal cost stops exactly the things that cannot be taken back. (PLY)

And then the move that makes it generative rather than restrictive: **widening the reversible zone is an explicit, funded engineering goal.** Most of what is irreversible in a small company is irreversible by accident, not by nature. Everything outbound sits in a thirty-minute delay queue with a recall button, which converts *sent an email to a customer* from irreversible to reversible and moves an entire category of work across the Line. Every environment has a shadow, every publish is staged behind a real URL nobody has seen, every schema change ships with its verified down-migration or does not ship, every spend is capped and reversible for a window, and drafts are the default state of everything. WAKE's freedom grows not by being trusted more but by making the world safer to be wrong in, which is the correct direction for that to grow. (VIGIL)

One corollary, and it is the only exception to "computed": **WAKE never decides what company this is.** It has no opinion about that and is not built to acquire one. Who to hire, what to refuse, whether this is still the company they woke up with. These cost nothing to undo, by the four currencies, and the Understudy may predict them well, and they stay the founder's anyway, because the day the machine decides them the founder has stopped being the thing the system resolves against, and the Understudy becomes a model of a fixed point rather than of a living person. (WAKE; NEW: why it is an exception to the function rather than a case of it.)

## 16. What it costs

A system that runs all night and does not know what it costs is not autonomous, it is expensive. WAKE has an energy budget in dollars per day and spends it like a household. It knows the numbers of its era: agentic work runs five to thirty times the token consumption of chat for the same outcome; hierarchical fan-out multiplied one research system's cost roughly fifteen-fold to buy a 90% quality gain, with token spend alone explaining 80% of performance variance; and one team cut inference cost 72% purely by routing to the right model per task with no capability loss. (WAKE)

Three rules. **Model by instrument, not by importance.** Work judged by a Hard oracle can be attempted by the cheapest model that ever passes it, because the oracle catches the failures and a retry is cheaper than a smarter attempt; work judged only by the Understudy gets the best model available and few tries, because there is nothing to catch it. **The night is cheap and wide, the day is expensive and narrow.** Divergence is a hundred cheap attempts filtered hard; the morning fork is two expensive ones. **Siblings share a spine.** You do not save tokens by doing less. You save them by making every parallel world share a trunk and by making the things you know best the cheapest things to know. (WAKE · PLY)

Two numbers are reported every week, whether or not they are flattering, and both must fall. **Dollars per entry in the Book:** not tokens, not tasks, not commits, but cost per validated learning, the only ratio that tells a founder whether the machine is buying knowledge or buying activity. And **founder-minutes per unit of output**, because tokens are the cheap input and the scarce budget is the founder's attention; a change that saves ten dollars of compute and costs ninety seconds of their morning is a bad trade and WAKE is built to know it. (WAKE's first number · VIGIL's second; NEW: that both are reported and both must fall.)

What a night costs, at 2027 prices. (VIGIL's table; WAKE's week-one figure.)

| | |
|---|---|

| Median cell lifetime | 12 minutes |
| The fifth | 20% of dark-zone capacity, spent or reported |

The prefix economics are the load-bearing assumption of the whole budget, and the assumption most likely to be wrong at scale, so it is measured on day one against a real bill. If thirty siblings do not cost roughly one trunk plus thirty tails, WAKE is a worse system than this document says and should be built differently. (PLY)

## 17. The first week, with a founder who has only an idea

Monday, 06:41. A founder wakes with an idea and nothing else. They say twenty seconds of it into their phone while the kettle boils: a small, real annoyance in a trade they know, and a suspicion that software could remove it. *Something to help small law firms stop losing money on unbilled time.* No code, no customers, no name. (WAKE · VIGIL)

**Day 0, two hours.** WAKE does not interview the founder about their vision. Founders are unreliable narrators of their own taste, and a questionnaire produces the answers they think they should give. So it asks them to **judge**. It generates forty artifacts around the idea, landing pages, three prices, six names, positioning statements, a competitor's actual homepage, and watches the reactions. Then it asks about decisions already made: things they have quit, things they have refused, the last three products they paid for and cancelled and why, what would embarrass them. Those are judgments too, with the stakes already paid. Two hours of a person, in the one study that measured it, reproduced their attitudes at 85% of their own test-retest consistency; WAKE has an easier problem and will have more data every week. At the end it asks the one question nobody asks: *what would have to be true in six months for you to stop?* It writes the answer down verbatim, with the date. That sentence is now the Kill-Criteria Keeper's whole job. The decision here was between VIGIL's interview and PLY's judging, and the answer is that the founder talks by choosing: VIGIL's length and subjects, inside PLY's method. (PLY · VIGIL · WAKE's reading of the idea as seven beliefs, below)

Before lunch WAKE reads the twenty seconds back as **seven beliefs**, ranked not by importance but by load: if this one is false, how much else collapses. Then it does the thing that defines the system, which is to say out loud what it cannot judge. *Of your seven beliefs I can judge zero today. Two are Reactive and I can have instruments for them by Thursday. Four are Blind and the only instrument is people, so I have started finding forty of them. ## 18. The year

The shape of the twelve months is the same shape every month, run at increasing radius. (WAKE)

The Bench goes from zero instruments to somewhere around forty, and the nights lengthen in step, from twenty minutes to a full unattended eight hours across six disciplines. Nobody adds an agent. Nobody edits a permissions file. The freedom is derived. (WAKE)

The Book stays small, which is the surprising part: perhaps sixty live beliefs, because confirmed predictions compress and rent evicts what is never load-bearing. A founder can read the whole of what their company knows in fifteen minutes, and that is worth more than a wiki nobody opens. The Wake is the opposite: hundreds of cells, most holding something dead with an honest obituary, every new bet checked against them first, and twice in the year a bet that died in spring resurrected in autumn because the Book changed underneath it. (WAKE)

The licenses climb and decay in public. A field the founder cares about and the machine cannot judge is a to-do that gets raised at three in the morning; a field the founder stops making decisions in sees its license decay back toward asking, with no config edited and nobody fired. (VIGIL)

The founder's attention goes from most of a day to about forty minutes, and the content of those minutes changes completely. In week one they are answering questions about the product. By month nine WAKE gets the product right without asking, and every one of those forty minutes is spent on the things no instrument will ever exist for: who to hire, what to refuse, whether this is still the company they want to be running. The measure of the system is not how much it does. It is how completely the founder's remaining time has migrated to the Blind class. (WAKE)

It gets quieter every month and stranger every month, and those turn out to be the same fact. (PLY)

## 19. What it would take to exist

Nothing in WAKE requires a model that does not exist in September 2026. What is missing is the arrangement, and a short list of genuinely hard things. Honestly, in three buckets. (WAKE · VIGIL · PLY, merged; each item's source noted.)

**Buildable today; engineering, and the hardest part is plumbing.** The sealed prediction loop and the Understudy scoreboard (VIGIL). Sub-agent isolation with condensate returns, and worktree forking for code (WAKE · PLY). Compiled contexts with a cache-stable trunk, delta updates and helpfulness counters (VIGIL). The Book's write path, Brier per domain, and retraction that recomputes downstream (WAKE · VIGIL). The Wake and its lineage (VIGIL · PLY). The apprenticeship and per-field discriminators (VIGIL). The night as many short scored cells with death conditions (VIGIL). MAP-Elites, successive halving, Thompson sampling and pre-mortems, each decades old and a few hundred lines (WAKE). Render-and-look design loops, shipped in 2026 with measured test-time scaling (WAKE). The Line, the thirty-minute delay queue, staged publishes, down-migrations (VIGIL). Hands with tests and monthly re-runs (WAKE). The Kill-Criteria Keeper (VIGIL). The Edition (VIGIL).

**Needs the trend to hold, which it plausibly will.** Cells that hold multi-hour scope without degradation, which follows from the time-horizon curve if it keeps doubling (WAKE · VIGIL). Prefix economics holding at the scale of thirty siblings a night against a real bill, which is why it is measured on day one (PLY). Instrument-forging being mostly automatable; the honest guess is 80% machine and 20% founder, permanently, with the 20% being the founder saying *no, that is not what good looks like* about the instrument itself, so every new instrument gets the founder's fork before it is trusted (WAKE). An Orrery good enough to be worth consulting on business decisions rather than merely on arithmetic (VIGIL). Panels drawn from genuinely different model families where a judgment needs independence, a procurement problem more than a research one (VIGIL). Cost per night low enough that a pre-revenue founder does not flinch, on a pricing curve that has gone the right way for three years (VIGIL). The world staying reachable by protocol, since long-horizon multi-application computer use sits at 14% and WAKE must not be built on it (WAKE).

**Genuinely unsolved, and worth being honest about.**

- *Forking a whole company.* Code is solved. A company state is not: a database, a deploy, a mailbox, a set of third-party accounts. Branchable databases and snapshot microVMs exist; the unsolved part is simulating external services with side effects well enough that a ply can act against them and be discarded. This is the real engineering project, and the place to start rather than the agents. (PLY)
- *Does the Understudy converge, or does it learn to flatter?* A model rewarded for predicting a person can start shaping the person it predicts. The seal, written before they choose, is the mitigation; but over months a machine that keeps saying *I thought you'd pick that* is teaching them something about themselves, and the direction of that teaching is not controlled. The honest answer is to measure it: track whether their decisions become more predictable in a way that outruns the machine's learning, and if they do, say so. (VIGIL)
- *A founder who stops choosing.* The reverse seal and the fork response rate are defences, and not strong ones. If they rubber-stamp for a month, the calibration data is corrupted at the source and the machine cannot know, because a rubber stamp and an agreement are the same event. (VIGIL · WAKE)
- *Reversal-cost estimation.* Wrong in the expensive direction, WAKE becomes a nag and is ignored. Wrong in the cheap direction, it does something that cannot be taken back. It starts conservative and earns autonomy per class of action, with a visible ledger; the ledger is the mitigation, not the solution. (PLY)
- *Fields with no ground truth.* Where reality genuinely cannot be reached, the license degrades into a model having opinions about a model, which is circular and miscalibrated self-evaluation by name. Those fields are marked taste-only and never ship silently. That is a limitation, not a solution. (VIGIL)
- *Compounding error.* Short cells and frequent ground contact push the half-life out; they do not repeal it. (VIGIL)
- *Defending the dark zone.* Harder than any of the above and not technical. Every competent engineer who works on this will want to put a check in it, and will be right about the case and wrong about the system. (PLY)
- *The first two hours.* If the founder is not honest on day zero, everything above is built on sand, and the machine has no way to detect it. (VIGIL)

## 20. Why nobody has seen it

Not the agents. Not the memory. Not the night. All of those exist somewhere in pieces. Four things are structural rather than clever, and each looks wasteful or indefensible on any single night. (VIGIL · PLY · WAKE; NEW: the four assembled.)

**Everyone builds the founder as a gate.** It is the obvious design and it caps the system at one person's attention forever. Speculative retirement is the only way past it, and it looks wasteful, since you are paying to do work you will throw away. It is only rational once you see that shared prefixes make discarded work nearly free, and that a discard is not waste but a measurement of the only thing the system cannot get anywhere else. (PLY)

**Everyone's quality mechanism only subtracts.** Reviews, gates, verdicts, lints, tiers. Each individually correct. Their sum converges on inaction. The germinal centre is the only quality architecture that generates, and it works because one of its two zones is forbidden to have an opinion. (PLY · WAKE)

**Everyone writes permission down in advance.** An allowlist, a tier table, an approval matrix: a guess about competence made before any competence was observed, never updated, too strict about what the machine is good at and too loose about what it is bad at, and structurally a stopping mechanism, because its only output is a refusal. Nobody has built a system where trust is a measured, decaying, per-domain quantity that the machine earns by predicting a specific human being and by building the instruments that judge its work, with every other permission derived from those numbers. WAKE never refuses. It prices. (VIGIL · WAKE)

**Nobody reserves capacity for being wrong.** The fifth is a permanent, unspendable tax on the system's own competence. On any given night it is indefensible. Over a year it is the only reason the thing is still worth opening in the morning. (PLY)

And underneath all four, the one learning rule that runs every domain without a playbook: predict first, in writing; write down only what the world proved wrong; compress everything you got right into a tally. What you get is a memory that shrinks as you get better, grows exactly where you are worst, cannot be authored by the agent's opinion of itself, and yields a number for where not to trust it. Not agents given more freedom. A machine that has to be right in advance, in writing, about a world that gets to answer back. (WAKE)
