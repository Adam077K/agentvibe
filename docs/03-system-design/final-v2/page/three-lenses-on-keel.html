A1P1The night has no runtime on this machine, and R5 was read backwards
Where
§15.1a, §15.2: The Mac was never off long enough for a cloud lane to have bought anything back this week.
Problem
Measured: MacBook Air M4, 16 GB, on battery at 71%; pmset -g custom shows sleep 1 on AC and battery; 391 maintenance sleeps, 74 back-to-sleep and 24 clamshell sleeps in seven days. One sleep every nineteen minutes. The plan read “asleep 40.7 h” as no tail for a cloud lane; a practitioner reads it as never awake unattended. O15's refusal fires every night. “Battery” appears once in 960 KB, inside a deferral.
Why it matters
§0.5, §3.3, §4, §6, §16.4 and the overnight-versus-bounded premise describe work that cannot start on the founder's hardware. Every unattended run orphans at the first maintenance sleep and reads as the system being broken.
What would change
Buy the always-on box before the first night, not after. Or gate the Watch on a founder act stated as a daily habit: pmset -a disablesleep 1, on AC, lid open. Or redefine the first night honestly as the hours the founder is at the desk and not typing.
How we'd know
Dispatch one child at 01:00; at 07:00 count maintenance sleeps during the run and whether the reconciler wrote orphaned.

B1P1The plan is a rung-4 artifact by its own ladder, and its process optimises the document, not the system
Where

Problem
584 ABSENT marks, 170 mechanism lines, 28 named programs of which 26 do not exist, 38 stores and no keel/ directory. B3P1The founder's adjudication rate is the system's throughput bound, and it is unmodelled
Where
§3.3, §2.7: Staged as a which, with both options built and left for the founder, who answers with one tap.
Problem
Every question outside the envelope becomes a which; every which builds two options and waits on one human. If whiches arrive faster than one founder resolves them the queue grows without bound, and standing intents plus ten ventures guarantee the arrival rate. The plan budgets rings and tokens and never budgets whiches. Building both options burns half the capacity on the unchosen half by construction. Away mode drains the queue without a decision.
Why it matters
The scarcest resource is founder decisions per day. At scale the morning is an adjudication queue, which is the inverted architecture §0.1a fears, arriving by a route nobody named.
What would change
Treat founder decisions as a capacity like the window: measure decisions per day, hold a which budget per window, refuse to open a which when the backlog exceeds throughput times horizon. Build one option and a written second unless a ten-word summary cannot separate them.
How we'd know
Whiches opened versus answered per week. A growing backlog is the founder as bottleneck.

How we'd know

The vision, from each chair

A · the practitionerA company that keeps working when the laptop closes, spends the founder's attention as its scarcest input, and can show any number against a record it did not write. If I had the founder's hands for a week: buy the box; write the log and the launcher; run one builder against one real venture intent through one anchor; read the transcript in the morning; then decide what the second program is.
B · the outsiderA measurement instrument wrapped around one person's judgement. The asset is the ledger of what this founder decided, what was tried, what failed and which checks predicted which outcomes. Every serious runtime will ship dispatch, teams, messaging and a fleet page; none will ship a decade of one founder's labelled judgement with provenance. Build for the asset, and let the vendors build the rest.
C · the strategistA company kernel of four things on whatever runtime is current: direction, record, truth, taste. Everything else is an adapter to a vendor surface that will exist within a year. The moat is a year of records, anchored, hash-chained, cross-venture, with the world's own replies attached. No vendor ships that and no competitor can copy it, because it is made of what happened.
Caveat, in every lane's own words. One model family, one agent per lane, three lanes of the same family. Convergence here is convergence of one family's reasoning, not independence.
