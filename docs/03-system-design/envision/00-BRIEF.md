# Envision the whole system · 2026-09-02 · round two

**From the founder, in their words, at the start of round one.** *Start fresh. Design the perfect agentic
system. Take some context on what to build and what I want, but no guidelines, no limits. Let it dream, let it
envision, let it take ideas, shape it to a new reality, to the new world we are entering now. A full agentic
system that no one has seen before.*

What it is for, in their words across three days: *a system that helps me build a company, a startup, or any
project of its kind. It can handle any task. It runs twenty-four seven, always with the founder. It has all
sorts of layers: the memory, the contexts, the saving of tokens, the way the agents get the right context and
the right knowledge for each task, and the research. Breadth beyond code: design, video, content, social,
marketing, customer, data, testing, CI. Give the agents the tools, the way, and the understanding of how to
learn. It lost creativity to playbooks, and everything built so far is a stopping mechanism.*

## The order of work — this is the method

1. Read only this brief.
2. **Write your spine first.** One page: what the system is, before you have read anything else. It is the
   first section of your own file, headed *Spine, written before reading*. Never edit it afterwards; if you
   change your mind later, say so below it, and say what changed it.
3. **Then learn from the systems already designed**, as much or as little as you want, as context and never
   as a floor. In rough order of strength:
   - `docs/03-system-design/dream/2026-09-02-THE-SYSTEM.md` — **WAKE**, round one's answer, merged from three
     sealed minds; the three dreams beside it, `2026-09-02-dream-{1,2,3}.md`.
   - `docs/03-system-design/review/2026-09-02-FABLE-REPORT.md` — two reviewers' findings on the older,
     anchored spec, with measurements of what this machine actually does.
   - `docs/03-system-design/STARTUP-OS.md` — the anchored spec, 22 territories at full scale; and
     `docs/03-system-design/vision/2026-09-02-THE-PICTURE.md` — the company fully grown.
   - `docs/02-competitive/expansion/` — the catalogue: 177 open-source projects and what to steal
     (`open-source.md`), every MCP, CLI and hand measured (`hands.md`), 128 mechanisms (`concepts.md`);
     `docs/02-competitive/reference-systems/` — five systems studied in full.
   - `docs/08-agents_work/handoffs/2026-09-02-after-the-dreams.md` — the arc, and under *Measured facts that
     bind*, eleven facts about this Mac and this runtime. Facts about the ground, not a design.
4. **Research the world.** The web, papers, products, open-source repositories, other fields: a trading
   floor, an ICU, an air-traffic room, a newsroom, a film set, a game studio, a monastery, a ship.
5. **Dream the whole system.** Everything WAKE covers and everything the founder named that it does not. Name
   what you take from a prior system and what you refuse of it, and why.

## There is no floor

No existing decision binds. No existing architecture binds. No section template. No length. No rule that a
mechanism must be enforced by something. The prior systems are context to learn from, never the starting
point; the machine that exists on disk today is a parts bin.

## The one line that is not a design limit

Research only. Nothing you do may leave this machine: nothing published, sent, spent, posted, shared or
deployed, and no tool that acts on the world. Do not read `docs/03-system-design/envision/2026-09-02-mind-*.md`
other than your own; the minds in this round are sealed from each other. Write only your own file.

## What we would love to read, in any form you choose

A name. The world it lives in and the year. What it is. How it thinks, remembers, learns, researches, finds the
right context for a task, acts, checks itself, grows, and lives with its founder day and night. **How it
decides what to do next, and when to do nothing: a startup that must move forward always, a project that
walks with the founder and waits, a piece of research, a client's work; who opens a goal; how it never burns
tokens without direction. The founder's environment: terminal, phone, Mac; seeing the agents work; giving
tasks and seeing progress; whether an office-like view is the right picture or a different one is. Many
projects at once, one founder, one budget; what they share and never share. How outside parts enter: a
repository, a skill, a tool, from the world; which of the 177 you would take. Where everything is stored, in
what form, and what survives a dead machine. Every job a company needs, from a database to a marketing
video to not leaking a key, and how the system handles all of them as one organisation.** What the founder's
life is like with it. What makes it something nobody has seen. What it would take to exist. What it would do
in its first week with a founder who has only an idea.
