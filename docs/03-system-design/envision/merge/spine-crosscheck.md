# Spine crosscheck — three sealed minds

*Checker's note. Source is the "Spine, written before reading" section of each of the three envision
documents and nothing else: `2026-09-02-mind-1.md` lines 1–90 (**THE HOUSE**, M1),
`2026-09-02-mind-2.md` lines 1–104 (**CONTINUO**, M2), `2026-09-02-mind-3.md` lines 1–100 (**WATCH**, M3).
Each spine was written after reading only the one-page brief and before opening anything else, so an
agreement here is blind agreement — three minds arriving separately, not three minds copying.*

*Convention in the tables: a cell reading `—` means that spine does not assert the claim. A cell
prefixed `(≠)` means the spine says something about the same object but **does not** assert the claim,
and is quoted so the difference in words is visible; such a cell is not counted.*

## 3. Unique to one spine

**M1 · THE HOUSE**
- **Silence executes the default.** "Every question to the founder carries a recommendation, a default, and a deadline; silence executes the default." No other spine gives silence any authority.
- **A ring-fenced, non-reallocatable exploration budget** — "A company spending nothing on exploration is dead in eighteen months and comfortable for twelve of them."
- **Roles collapse into *powers*, enumerated:** "who may read, who may write, who may spend, who may touch the outside world, who may change the house itself."
- **Classify risk by undo cost, not by a risk table** — "Classify by *undo cost*, not by a risk table someone invented."
- **A settled bet that changed nothing is a defect** — "a bet we paid for twice."
- **Proximity to lethality as a ranking term** — "weighted by how near it sits to the company's throat."
- **A *Client* pace with an audit trail** as a first-class mode.
- **Surprise as the stored asset** — the world's disagreement is what memory is for.

**M2 · CONTINUO**
- **A closed vocabulary of exactly six nouns** (Bet, Evidence, Move, Hand, Belief, Mandate) — "Six nouns, and no others"; everything else is demoted to a lens.
- **A standing auction with a cheap appraiser**, clearing against a budget, as the whole scheduler.
- **Relentlessness as a bet that refuses to close** — "It will keep proposing moves, get outbid, wait, and propose again cheaper — for weeks."
- **A nightly consolidation** that compresses episodes into beliefs, kills dead bets, opens new ones, and **rewrites its own prompts and tools**.
- **Negative memory as its own room** — "without it a system re-buys the same lesson forever."
- **The durable structure is the list of functions a company must do or die** — "be found, be wanted, be paid, be built, be kept working, be legal, be solvent, be liked."
- **"Agents are cheap; hands are not."**
- **Approval bound to the exact bytes**, never to a category.
- **k attempts that are *sealed from each other*** — not merely parallel, but blind.
- **The meta-diagnosis:** "they are designing a better *harness*… when the missing thing is an **economy**."

**M3 · WATCH**
- **Three kinds of goal — question, bet, commitment — as the entire autonomy policy.** "This single line answers autonomy without a policy engine."
- **The escapement test:** "If every branch of a result leads to the same next action, the work is decoration and does not run."
- **"Invented work is worse than silence."**
- **Wake conditions attached to each commission**, and a captain "woken by rule and not by whim."
- **One door for admitting capability** — quarantine, declare, try on one real task, and record it "by what it can reach rather than by what it claims."
- **Checks observe artifacts, never reports about artifacts** — "Render it, run it, click it, look at it, try to break it, show it to a mind that did not build it."
- **Non-technical review lenses at equal rank with correctness** — secrets, voice, comprehension, legality, looks — "because a company dies of them equally."
- **Lessons bound to a retrieval trigger and evicted if they never fire.**
- **"A fact with no expiry is a future lie."**
- **The founder's corrections named as the highest-value signal in the system.**
- **The architecture test:** "If a mechanism cannot be stated as 'this makes something happen sooner or better,' it is not architecture, it is anxiety."

## 4. The founder model — what each spine says about taste, and whether taste licenses autonomy

**The short answer: two of three spines hold an explicit model of the founder's taste; none of the three
lets that model license the system to act alone. In all three, authority to act comes from a standing
instruction plus the reversibility line — never from "the system knows what you like."**

**M1 · THE HOUSE — no model of taste at all.** The word does not appear in the spine. What M1 has instead
is a standing dial and a default-execution rule:

> "*Hunt* moves always, to the cap. *Walk* acts only inside the founder's turn. *Hold* watches and reports
> and does not act."

> "**Every question to the founder carries a recommendation, a default, and a deadline; silence executes
> the default.** That single rule is what lets a nocturnal system stay honest with a diurnal human."

M1 is therefore the **only** spine in which the system can take an unapproved action on the founder's
behalf — and the licence comes from a pre-declared default and an elapsed clock, not from any belief about
what the founder would have wanted. Notably, M1 also has the closest thing to a *counter*-model of taste:
the founder may overrule the portfolio allocation "and the system will tell them what it cost."

**M2 · CONTINUO — taste is a first-class asset, and it is deliberately kept out of the authorisation path.**

> "The system holds **taste** — exemplars, references, things the founder loved and hated — as a
> first-class asset alongside code."

Taste in M2 is a *selection* instrument: it sits beside standards, and standards judge the k sealed
attempts. Authority to act is a different object entirely — the **Mandate**, "the founder's standing
instruction for one concern: how hard to push, what may be spent, what may never be touched, and whether
to move without asking." And at the irreversible classes the mandate itself is not enough:

> "the founder's approval is attached to *the exact bytes* being sent, not to a category."

So M2 answers the question explicitly in the negative for money, identity and publication: no accumulated
model of the founder substitutes for the founder, byte for byte.

**M3 · WATCH — taste is a memory organ learned from corrections, and it is explicitly not a licence.**

> "**taste**: the founder's standards, voice, and refusals, learned from their corrections. *The founder's
> corrections are the highest-value signal in the entire system and nearly every system throws them away.*
> WATCH treats 'no, not like that' as the rarest training data it will ever get."

> "By day seven the system knows how this founder says no, and that is the asset."

M3 goes furthest in *building* the model and is the most explicit that it confers no authority. The
licence line is drawn on reversibility instead, and it is drawn hard:

> "A **commitment** is irreversible or visible to the world — money moved, a message sent, a thing
> published, a schema migrated — and only the founder signs one. This single line answers autonomy
> without a policy engine."

> "The night belongs to questions and bets. Commitments queue for morning."

**The convergence worth naming.** All three spines separate *how well the system works unattended* from
*what it is allowed to do unattended*, and all three put the second under founder control by a standing,
pre-declared instrument — M1's drive dial, M2's mandate, M3's charter. Where taste exists (M2, M3) it
improves the quality of what the system opens and how it judges its own attempts; it never widens what
the system may sign. The one live disagreement in this area is narrower and sharper than taste: **may the
founder's silence itself act?** M1 says yes by design; M2 and M3 say no at the irreversible line.
