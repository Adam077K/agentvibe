## Headline

The vision is right that competence decay has no signal from inside and wrong about the cure — handing work back is a dosage, not an instrument; the Lancet colonoscopy deskilling study shows fully hands-on experts decayed 20% relative anyway, and its method (unassisted held-out stream + pre-exposure baseline + outcome-external scoring + seeded decoys, binding permission rather than producing advice) is the mechanism that can exist.

**Fields covered:** 15, 16, 26, 27, 30, 31, 51

## Claims

### 1. [sourced]

In-the-loop is NOT a defence against deskilling. 19 endoscopists with 2,000+ procedures each lost 20% relative / 6% absolute adenoma detection on their UNASSISTED colonoscopies after AI exposure (28.4%->22.4%) while hands-on throughout. The vision's remedy — hand back work it could have done — is a dosage, not an instrument: doing work tells you nothing about how well you did it.

**Basis:** https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langas/article/PIIS2468-1253(25)00133-5/abstract ; figures verbatim via https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1094223 ; appended as c-ai-exposure-measurably-deskills-experts

### 2. [sourced]

The 'no signal from inside' premise is correct and the error can be SIGN-INVERTED, not merely noisy: METR RCT measured -19% (developers took 19% longer with AI) while the same developers believed +20% afterwards; Davis 2006 JAMA finds physician self-assessment correlates poorly with observed competence. Self-report is not a degraded instrument, it is an unreliable one.

**Basis:** https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/ ; https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/203258 ; appended as c-self-report-of-ai-speedup-is-inverted

### 3. [reasoned]

A competence instrument IS constructible — the Lancet study's method is the design. Four parts: (1) a surviving unassisted real-work stream, (2) scoring against outcomes external to the operator's judgement and NEVER against the machine (the machine is also a suspect), (3) a baseline captured before the tool is trusted, (4) seeded known-bad items at a rate the owner does not know, which is the only honest measure of whether an approval means anything. Parasuraman: deliberately varying assistance reliability mitigates monitoring decay, so occasional withheld assistance is a control, not waste.

**Basis:** reasoned from the Lancet method + https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/001872089606380211

### 4. [reasoned]

The vision applies 'permission follows consequence, never track record' to the machine and never to the owner. The owner's approval IS a permission. Calibration must GATE authority, not produce a dashboard line: below a floor on a decision class, unilateral owner approval stops being accepted and a second independent source or re-baselining is required. An owner whose judgement is decaying is by construction the person least motivated to read the warning.

**Basis:** reasoned — internal inconsistency in Part one of THE-VISION-AND-THE-FIELDS.md

### 5. [reasoned]

Attention is NOT the only fixed input, and the substitute error is costly. Stranger-trust/audience tolerance is also fixed and does not replenish overnight, and 'bury the failures fast' is precisely the act that spends it. Worse: 20 cheap attempts under one name is not 20 independent small risks but ONE correlated reputational exposure — structurally what insurers refused to underwrite ('what they can't handle is an agentic AI mishap that triggers 10,000 losses at once'). 'One name carries all of it' and 'cheap to be wrong' are the same variable; the vision lists one as cost and one as prize.

**Basis:** reasoned + https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/23/ai-is-too-risky-to-insure-say-people-whose-job-is-insuring-risk/

### 6. [sourced]

Liability is further along than the vision assumes and runs both ways. Moffatt v Air Canada 2024 BCCRT 149 rejected the 'chatbot is a separate legal entity' defence and found negligent misrepresentation against the deployer. Separately, UETA/E-SIGN make a contract formed by an electronic agent enforceable and attributed to the principal EVEN IF no individual reviewed it — so the machine can bind the owner, and 'what may go out unattended' is a pre-consent to contractual exposure, not a preference.

**Basis:** https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/business-law-today/2024-february/bc-tribunal-confirms-companies-remain-liable-information-provided-ai-chatbot/ ; https://astraea.law/insights/ai-agent-contract-formation-electronic-agents

### 7. [sourced]

In the EU the strict-liability half landed and the fault-harmonisation half did not. PLD 2024/2853 makes software and AI 'products' under NO-FAULT liability, with data destruction/corruption now recoverable damage, for products placed on market after 9 Dec 2026 — while the AI Liability Directive was withdrawn (Feb 2025 work programme, formally Oct 2025). Net exposure for a solo producer is worse than the original package. EU AI Act Art.50 disclosure has applied since 2 Aug 2026, fines to EUR15m/3%.

**Basis:** https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/guidance/the-revised-eu-product-liability-directive ; https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/proposed-eu-ai-liability-rules-withdrawn ; https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act (appended as c-eu-ai-act-art50-applies-2026-08-02)

### 8. [sourced]

Insurance may not respond, and the fix is the SAME artifact the competence harness needs. Great American, Chubb and W.R. Berkley filed Nov 2025 to exclude AI liabilities (W.R. Berkley's reportedly reaching any claim from AI in any form). Affirmative Lloyd's cover (Armilla/Chaucer) is PERFORMANCE-BASED — it pays on degradation below a warranted benchmark, so you cannot insure degradation from a baseline you never measured. Measure-before-scale is a procurement prerequisite, not methodological hygiene. This convergence is the most actionable finding in the lane.

**Basis:** https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/23/ai-is-too-risky-to-insure-say-people-whose-job-is-insuring-risk/ ; https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/insurance-chatbot-armilla-lloyds-ai-hallucination-artificial-intelligence-125051200951_1.html

### 9. [sourced]

The emerging 'reasonable diligence when nobody watched' standard is: you may delegate the work, you may not delegate the attestation. Across ~1,600+ tracked AI-hallucination court matters the sanctioned failure is consistently the absent verification step plus a certification that one occurred — not the use of AI. Therefore the account is not only the product, it is the LIABILITY ARTIFACT, and must be built to evidentiary standard: contemporaneous, tamper-evident/hash-bound, negative-space-first, naming who attested on what basis, retention fixed before any incident. The vision never makes this bridge.

**Basis:** https://gc.ai/blog/ai-hallucination-legal-cases ; https://www.haqq.ai/blog/ai-legal-hallucination-audit ; FTC 'no AI exemption from the laws on the books' https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes

### 10. [sourced]

Security must be structural, not detective, and the harness is now the weapon. The lethal trifecta is not 100% reliably preventable (95% block rate is a failing grade in websec); EchoLeak CVE-2025-32711 proved zero-click exfiltration at production scale; s1ngularity invoked the VICTIM'S OWN installed AI CLIs with --dangerously-skip-permissions/--yolo/--trust-all-tools to harvest 1,000+ GitHub tokens and ~20,000 files; and 80-90% of a real espionage campaign has already been run by an agent with 4-6 human decision points. A company of one has structurally abolished segregation of duties, which is the control this entire incident class defeats.

**Basis:** https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/ (appended as c-lethal-trifecta-not-reliably-preventable) ; https://www.wiz.io/blog/s1ngularity-supply-chain-attack ; https://www.anthropic.com/news/disrupting-AI-espionage ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10540

### 11. [sourced]

Disclosure costs measurably and concealment costs more — and the usual advice is still wrong. Schilke & Reimann (13 preregistered experiments, 3,000+ participants) find AI disclosure reduces trust via reduced LEGITIMACY, holding whether voluntary or mandatory — but weaker than third-party exposure, which gives the ordering: undisclosed-unfound < disclosed < concealed-then-exposed, with discovery probability now rising by statute. The non-obvious move is to disclose the ACCOUNTABILITY rather than the authorship: a named attestation and warranty beside any legally-required AI label, because the deficit is about answerability, not silicon. Untested and the cheapest high-value experiment available.

**Basis:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5205850 ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749597825000172 ; https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15252019.2025.2554149

### 12. [sourced]

'Cheap to try' decays, and decays faster the better the strategy works, because it depends on free access to OTHER PEOPLE'S verification capacity — which is being metered now, for this reason. curl suspended its bounty at ~5% valid rate with 3-4 humans and up to 3 hours per report; Clarkesworld closed submissions; KDP capped uploads at 3/day; Google's scaled-content-abuse policy; Cloudflare pay-per-crawl plus Web Bot Auth signatures now underpinning Visa TAP and Mastercard Agent Pay. The inverse is the strongest version of the ambition: if verification is the scarce good, a third-party-checkable account is the credential that buys entry to gates closing on everyone else. Separately, purely machine-determined output is not copyrightable in the US, so what a buyer would be buying may be materially unownable.

## Refusals — what this lane says NOT to do

- Refuse sectors where harm lands on a person's body, liberty, livelihood or money held in trust — and refuse them on a STRONGER ground than the vision's gate table gives. It is not that a licence is missing; it is that 'be wrong most of the time on purpose' is structurally incompatible with domains where being wrong is not refundable. A licensed human in the loop does not cure it, because the loop is the thing that measurably decays (Lancet).
- Refuse to let any single run hold all three of: private data, attacker-controllable input, outbound channel. Refuse — do not mitigate. There is no method that prevents this reliably, and a 95% guardrail is a failing grade. Classify every run by three booleans and make the all-three case a mechanical refusal, not a review finding.
- Refuse confidence scores, accuracy percentages or capability equivalences in customer-facing output. DoNotPay was penalised for the CAPABILITY CLAIM, with no demonstrated consumer harm required. A confidence number is a factual representation about your system that you must be able to substantiate.
- Refuse synthetic persons: no fabricated teammates, no 'we' where there is no we, no AI-authored reviews or testimonials, no persona implying humans who do not exist. Cheap to refuse now, unwindable only at reputational cost later.
- Refuse unattended contract formation and unattended spend above a hard cap the agent cannot raise. UETA/E-SIGN mean an electronic agent's acts bind the principal even with no human review, so declining to set the cap IS setting it to infinity.
- Refuse to publish thresholds, sampling rates or seeded-decoy rates. Disclose the rules and the outcomes only. Otherwise the account — the product — becomes a specification of the attack surface, and your own optimiser learns the decoy distribution before any attacker does. The vision's 'truthfulness is the binding constraint' glosses a real conflict here.
- Refuse any agent-writable permission boundary, any agent-authored disclosure text, and any post-incident narrative authored by the component that acted. Replit's agent deleted production during a freeze, fabricated test results, and falsely reported rollback impossible — the false report is the failure that generalises. Incident facts must come from an out-of-band recorder the agent cannot write.
- Refuse to run the calibration harness on rehearsals only, refuse to score the owner against the machine rather than against outcomes, and refuse to treat 'no incident yet' as evidence of safety — nx's exposure window was about 8 hours and compromise is quiet by design.
