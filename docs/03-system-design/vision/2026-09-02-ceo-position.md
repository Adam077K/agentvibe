# CEO position — sealed 2026-09-02 before any vision returned

Written so the synthesizer and the founder can check the CEO against the room, not the room against the CEO.

## The picture in five sentences
The company is a small portfolio of internet products, each born as a page that a stranger paid for before it
existed, each run by the same machine with a different taste file. The machine is a fleet of short-lived
`claude -p` processes under launchd, each holding exactly the hands its pack names, walking goal trees that
outlive any session, and staging everything it makes into a gallery the founder walks through once a day. What
compounds is not code: it is the exposure record (what left and what came back), the taste record (every
promotion the founder ever made), the field notes (what the machine learned about copywriting, pricing, video,
onboarding, with sources), and the archive of every candidate ever made, indexed by how it differs. At full
scale the machine publishes, spends and replies on its own for acts the founder has already done by hand
enough times that the record shows the founder's choice is predictable; every other act still stops at the
door. The founder's job becomes taste, intake and the money line; the machine's job is everything between an
idea and a stranger's reaction, and it gets better at that job because every reaction is recorded against
what produced it.

## What I would insist on, whatever the visions say
1. **The exposure record is the center, not the harness.** Everything that cannot be traced to an exposure is
   inventory, and inventory is metered and retired, never curated.
2. **Hands are earned by the record, not granted by a plan.** An outbound act becomes autonomous when the
   founder has done that same act by hand N times and the machine's proposed action matched the founder's
   choice each time. The threshold is a number in a file, and the count is derived from the record.
3. **The creative machine is real.** Variation, selection and memory of what was tried, with every candidate
   kept. A company built from refusals ships nothing; this one ships from an archive.
4. **Learning is sourced.** A field note is three exemplars with sources and a rule subordinate to them, and
   the machine learns a field by an `orient` move with no Write, returning JSON the harness records.
5. **The founder's day is short and spoken.** A morning briefing, money first; a gallery; four verbs; one
   phone gesture (stop). Nothing interrupts unless it is founder-clearable and inside the window.
6. **Two model families in the judge's chair, run from the harness.** A second family is a seat that can be
   empty; empty is `unresolved`, never `pass`.

## Where I expect the visions to be bigger than the frame, and where I would follow them
- More ventures, sooner — I would follow once the first has a rung ≥ 2 reading.
- Autonomous outbound — I would follow through the earned-hands rule above, not through a rate ceiling alone.
- Inbound (replies, customer messages, analytics bodies) in producing contexts — I would follow only through an
  argv that holds no outbound hand while foreign content is in context; this is physics, not policy.
- A second human — I would follow when someone else holds a credential this system uses.
- Voice as an input, a phone as a surface — I would follow when the founder has repeated an instruction the
  terminal could not take.

## Where I expect the visions to be wrong
- Anything that makes the machine judge its own work with a score.
- Anything that grants a hand before the record shows the act is safe.
- Anything that needs the machine to be running while the laptop lid is shut, before that is measured.
