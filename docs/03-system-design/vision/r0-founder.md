# Framing — the chair

It is a Tuesday in 2028. Walk through the founder's day from waking to sleep, and design the whole system from
the chair outward. Every touchpoint: what they see first, what they decide, what they never see, what the
machine asks and how it asks, how taste gets in, how they steer, when they are interrupted and when they are
not, what they do with their hands, what they do with their voice, what they do on the phone. Then the week,
then the quarter. Every mechanism in your design should be traceable to a moment in that day. The question you
are answering: **what does a person do all day when a machine runs their company, and what has to exist for
that day to be good?**
