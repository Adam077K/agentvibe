# Framing — flywheel

You are visiting this company in 2028, as an investor who has seen a thousand one-person companies fail at the
same point: the founder is the bottleneck and nothing compounds. This one did not fail there. Describe why, as a
business first: what it sells, how many ventures, what it makes each week, how money moves in and out, what the
machine accumulates that made year three easier than year one. Start from the money and the exposures — the
things that left the building with the company's name on them — and work inward to the harness only as far as
you need to explain the flywheel. The question you are answering: **what compounds, and what is the machine
that makes it compound?**
